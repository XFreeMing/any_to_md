1. The assumption of this book is that the main demand for justice is the demand to remedy illegitimate inequalities. (Wolff, 2006)

> 这本书的假设是，对正义的主要要求是纠正非法不平等的要求。

- remedy: 纠正
- assumption: 假设
- this book: 这本书
- main demand: 主要要求
- for justice: 对正义的
- demand to: ...的要求
- illegitimate inequalities: 非法不平等

2. They preferred to believe that evil stemmed from faulty institutions and poor education, both of which could be remedied, rather than from a defective human nature. (Perry, 2010)

> 他们宁愿相信邪恶源于有缺陷的制度和糟糕的教育，这两者都可以被纠正，而不是源于有缺陷的人性。

- remedied: 纠正
- they: 他们
- preferred to believe: 宁愿相信
- evil: 邪恶
- stemmed from: 源于
- faulty institutions: 有缺陷的制度
- poor education: 糟糕的教育
- both of which: 这两者
- could be: 可以被
- rather than: 而不是
- defective human nature: 有缺陷的人性

3. Political and social structures and rules, short of the state, can be devised to remedy the defects of the state of nature. (Wolff, 2006)

> 政治和社会结构和规则，除了国家之外，可以被设计来纠正自然状态的缺陷。

- remedy: 纠正
- political and social structures: 政治和社会结构
- rules: 规则
- short of: 除了
- state: 国家
- can be devised: 可以被设计
- defects: 缺陷
- state of nature: 自然状态

4. Unlike Britain, the United States was a large country. The lack of a good system of internal transportation seemed to limit American economic development by making the transport of goods prohibitively expensive. This was gradually remedied, however. Thousands of miles of roads and canals were built linking east and west. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 与英国不同，美国是一个大国。缺乏良好的内部交通系统似乎通过使货物运输极其昂贵来限制美国经济发展。然而，这逐渐被纠正了。修建了数千英里的道路和运河，连接东西部。

- remedied: 纠正
- unlike Britain: 与英国不同
- United States: 美国
- large country: 大国
- lack of: 缺乏
- good system: 良好系统
- internal transportation: 内部交通
- seemed to limit: 似乎限制
- American economic development: 美国经济发展
- making: 使
- transport of goods: 货物运输
- prohibitively expensive: 极其昂贵
- gradually: 逐渐地
- however: 然而
- thousands of miles: 数千英里
- roads and canals: 道路和运河
- built: 修建
- linking: 连接
- east and west: 东西部
