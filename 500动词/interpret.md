- scholars: 学者们

- ethnographers: 民族志学者
- anthropologists: 人类学家
- study cultural behavior: 研究文化行为
- cave paintings: 洞穴绘画
- engravings: 雕刻

3. Some people refer to this activity as jihad, which they misleadingly interpret as "holy war." Jihad actually means "striving in the way of the Lord" to achieve personal betterment, which can include a fair, defensive fight to preserve one's life and one's faith. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 有些人将这种活动称为圣战，他们误导性地将其解释为"圣战"。圣战实际上意味着"在主的道路上努力"以实现个人改善，这可能包括公平的、防御性的战斗以保护自己的生命和信仰。

- activity: 活动
- jihad: 圣战
- misleadingly: 误导性地

- achieve: 实现

- defensive fight: 防御性的战斗

4. There are several basic theoretical dilemmas — matters of continuing or recurring controversy or dispute — which these clashes of viewpoint bring to our attention, some of which concern very general matters to do with how we should interpret human activities and social institutions. (Giddens, 2009)

> 有几个基本的理论困境——持续或反复的争议或争端问题——这些观点冲突引起了我们的注意，其中一些涉及非常一般的问题，关于我们应该如何解释人类活动和社会制度。

- several basic theoretical dilemmas: 几个基本的理论困境
- matters: 问题
- continuing or recurring: 持续或反复的

- clashes of viewpoint: 观点冲突

- very general matters: 非常一般的问题
