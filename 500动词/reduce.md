1. But the market can be improved by allowing governments to provide public goods, and to introduce legislation to reduce the supply of 'public bads' (goods with negative externalities). (Wolff, 2006)

> 但市场可以通过允许政府提供公共物品，并引入立法来减少"公共坏品"（具有负外部性的商品）的供应来改善。

- reduce: 减少
- market: 市场
- improved by: 通过...改善
- allowing governments: 允许政府
- provide public goods: 提供公共物品
- introduce legislation: 引入立法
- supply: 供应
- public bads: 公共坏品
- goods with negative externalities: 具有负外部性的商品

2. My colleagues in sociology have pointed out that minority groups who feel excluded show many of the same patterns that our laboratory manipulations elicit: high rates of aggression and antisocial behavior, decreased willingness to cooperate and obey rules, poorer intellectual performance, more self-destructive acts, short-term focus, and the like. If we could promote a more inclusive society, in which more people feel themselves accepted as valued members, some of these tragic patterns might be reduced. (Myers, 2012)

> 我在社会学的同事们指出，感到被排斥的少数群体表现出许多与我们实验室操作引发的相同模式：高攻击率和反社会行为、合作和遵守规则的意愿降低、智力表现较差、更多自毁行为、短期关注等等。如果我们能够促进一个更具包容性的社会，在这个社会中更多的人感到自己被接受为有价值的成员，这些悲剧模式中的一些可能会减少。

- reduced: 减少
- colleagues in sociology: 在社会学的同事们
- pointed out that: 指出
- minority groups: 少数群体
- feel excluded: 感到被排斥
- show: 表现出
- same patterns: 相同模式
- laboratory manipulations: 实验室操作
- elicit: 引发
- high rates: 高比率
- aggression: 攻击
- antisocial behavior: 反社会行为
- decreased willingness: 降低的意愿
- cooperate: 合作
- obey rules: 遵守规则
- poorer intellectual performance: 较差的智力表现
- self-destructive acts: 自毁行为
- short-term focus: 短期关注
- and the like: 等等
- promote: 促进
- more inclusive society: 更具包容性的社会
- accepted as valued members: 被接受为有价值的成员
- tragic patterns: 悲剧模式
- might be: 可能会

- design change: 设计变化
- reveals: 揭示
- continuing desire: 持续的愿望
- wall surface: 墙面
- increase: 增加
- amount of light: 光量
