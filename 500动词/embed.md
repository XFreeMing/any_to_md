1. In experiments, even single co-occurrences of an unusual act by someone in an atypical group—"Ben, a Jehovah's Witness, owns a pet sloth"—can embed illusory correlations in people's minds. (Myers, 2012)

> 在实验中，即使是某人在非典型群体中的不寻常行为的单一共同发生——"本，一个耶和华见证人，拥有一只宠物树懒"——可以在人们的心中嵌入虚幻的相关性。

- embed: 嵌入
- experiments: 实验
- even: 即使
- single co-occurrences: 单一共同发生
- unusual act: 不寻常行为
- atypical group: 非典型群体
- Ben: 本
- Jehovah's Witness: 耶和华见证人
- owns: 拥有
- pet sloth: 宠物树懒
- illusory correlations: 虚幻的相关性
- people's minds: 人们的心中

2. Turner's quest for the grandiose and his rich imagination led him to embed spectacular mythological and historical moments in his landscapes, creating historical landscape on an epic, sublime scale. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 特纳对宏伟的追求和他丰富的想象力使他在风景中嵌入壮观的神话和历史时刻，创造出史诗般、崇高的历史风景。

- embed: 嵌入
- Turner's quest: 特纳的追求
- grandiose: 宏伟的
- rich imagination: 丰富的想象力
- led him to: 使他
- spectacular: 壮观的
- mythological and historical moments: 神话和历史时刻
- landscapes: 风景
- creating: 创造
- historical landscape: 历史风景
- epic: 史诗般的
- sublime scale: 崇高的规模

- embedded in: 嵌入在
- chief characteristic: 主要特征
- communal groups: 社区群体
- social fabric: 社会结构
- in the sense that: 在这个意义上
- membership: 成员身份
- based on: 基于
- birth: 出生
- rather than: 而不是
- recruitment: 招募
