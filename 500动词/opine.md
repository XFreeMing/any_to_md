1. The subsequent conversation between Death and God, somewhat reminiscent of that between Satan and God in the Book of Job (see chapter 1), shows God to be an angry, petulant figure who finds human beings "drowned in sin." If left to their own devices, he opines, "they will become much worse than beasts." (Fiero, 2011)

> 死亡和上帝之间的后续对话，有点让人想起《约伯记》中撒旦和上帝之间的对话（见第1章），显示上帝是一个愤怒、任性的形象，他发现人类"淹没在罪恶中"。如果让他们自行其是，他发表意见说，"他们会变得比野兽更糟糕。"

- opines: 发表意见
- subsequent conversation: 后续对话
- between Death and God: 死亡和上帝之间
- somewhat reminiscent of: 有点让人想起
- Satan and God: 撒旦和上帝
- Book of Job: 《约伯记》
- shows God to be: 显示上帝是
- angry: 愤怒的
- petulant figure: 任性的形象
- finds human beings: 发现人类
- drowned in sin: 淹没在罪恶中
- left to their own devices: 让他们自行其是
- become much worse than beasts: 变得比野兽更糟糕

2. Darwinism shows us that "ethics as we understand it is an illusion fobbed off on us by our genes to get us to cooperate" (Ruse and Wilson 1985: 52); and yet elsewhere Wilson opines that a proper evolutionary understanding of ethics will "make possible the selection of a more deeply understood and enduring code of moral values" (Wilson 1978: 196). (Copp, 2005)

> 达尔文主义向我们表明，"我们理解的伦理学是我们的基因强加给我们的幻觉，以使我们合作"（Ruse and Wilson 1985: 52）；然而在其他地方，威尔逊发表意见说，对伦理学的适当进化理解将"使选择更深入理解和持久的道德价值准则成为可能"（Wilson 1978: 196）。

- opines: 发表意见
- Darwinism: 达尔文主义
- shows us that: 向我们表明
- ethics: 伦理学
- illusion: 幻觉
- fobbed off on us: 强加给我们的
- genes: 基因
- cooperate: 合作
- yet elsewhere: 然而在其他地方
- Wilson: 威尔逊
- proper evolutionary understanding: 适当的进化理解
- make possible: 使...成为可能
- selection: 选择
- deeply understood: 深入理解
- enduring code: 持久的准则
- moral values: 道德价值

3. Easterbrook opines that the behavior of judges will be "influenced, if not determined" by their understanding of the sources and functions of legislation. (Shapiro, 2013)

> 伊斯特布鲁克发表意见说，法官的行为将"受到（如果不是被决定）"他们对立法来源和功能的理解的影响。

- opines: 发表意见
- Easterbrook: 伊斯特布鲁克
- behavior of judges: 法官的行为
- influenced: 受到影响
- if not determined: 如果不是被决定
- understanding: 理解
- sources: 来源
- functions: 功能
- legislation: 立法

4. But this position is also too strong. There seems to be no reason why legal philosophers should not opine on the meaning of "law," as well as the metaphysics of law. (Shapiro, 2013)

> 但这个立场也太强了。似乎没有理由为什么法律哲学家不应该就"法律"的含义以及法律的形而上学发表意见。

- but: 但
