## 使困惑；使惊讶

1. Early in his career, Wren held the position of chair in the astronomy department at Gresham College, London, and then professor of mathematics at Oxford University. His interest in architecture did not surface until he was about 30 years old. His technical knowledge may have affected the shape of his buildings; certainly, no previous architect had gone to such lengths to conceal a building's structural supports. Only an architect thoroughly grounded in geometry and mathematics could have achieved such results, and the technical proficiency of Wren's structures has continually confounded his critics. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在他职业生涯的早期，雷恩在伦敦格雷沙姆学院担任天文学系主任，然后在牛津大学担任数学教授。他对建筑的兴趣直到他大约30岁时才出现。他的技术知识可能影响了他建筑的形状；当然，没有哪位先前的建筑师会如此费心地隐藏建筑的结构支撑。只有一位在几何学和数学方面有扎实基础的建筑师才能取得这样的成果，雷恩结构的技术熟练程度一直让他的批评者感到困惑。

- confound: 使困惑，使惊讶
- early in his career: 在他职业生涯的早期
- held the position: 担任职位
- chair: 系主任
- astronomy department: 天文学系
- Gresham College: 格雷沙姆学院
- professor of mathematics: 数学教授
- Oxford University: 牛津大学
- interest: 兴趣
- architecture: 建筑
- surface: 出现
- technical knowledge: 技术知识
- affected: 影响
- shape: 形状
- buildings: 建筑
- previous architect: 先前的建筑师
- gone to such lengths: 如此费心
- conceal: 隐藏
- structural supports: 结构支撑
- thoroughly grounded: 有扎实基础的
- geometry: 几何学
- mathematics: 数学
- achieved: 取得
- results: 成果
- technical proficiency: 技术熟练程度
- structures: 结构
- continually: 一直
- critics: 批评者

2. In arithmetic, for example, Leonardo da Vinci made such rapid progress during the short time he studied it that he often confounded his teacher by his questions.

> 例如，在算术方面，列奥纳多·达·芬奇在学习的短时间内取得了如此快速的进步，以至于他经常用他的问题让老师感到困惑。

- confound: 使困惑
- arithmetic: 算术
- for example: 例如
- Leonardo da Vinci: 列奥纳多·达·芬奇
- made such rapid progress: 取得了如此快速的进步
- during: 在...期间
- short time: 短时间
- studied: 学习
- often: 经常
- teacher: 老师
- questions: 问题

## 困惑；混淆

1. The State of Nature, and the State of War, which however some Men have confounded, are as far distant as a State of Peace, Good Will, Mutual Assistance, and Preservation, and a State of Enmity, Malice, Violence, and Mutual Destruction are from one another. (Wolff, 2006)

> 自然状态和战争状态，然而有些人已经混淆了它们，它们之间的距离就像和平、善意、相互援助和保存的状态与敌意、恶意、暴力和相互毁灭的状态之间的距离一样远。

- confounded: 混淆
- State of Nature: 自然状态
- State of War: 战争状态
- however: 然而
- some Men: 有些人
- as far distant as: 像...一样远
- State of Peace: 和平状态
- Good Will: 善意
- Mutual Assistance: 相互援助
- Preservation: 保存
- State of Enmity: 敌意状态
- Malice: 恶意
- Violence: 暴力
- Mutual Destruction: 相互毁灭
- from one another: 相互之间

2. Art should be independent of all clap-trap——should stand alone, and appeal to the artistic sense of eye or ear, without confounding this with emotions entirely foreign to it, as devotion, pity, love, patriotism, and the like. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 艺术应该独立于所有哗众取宠——应该独立存在，并诉诸眼睛或耳朵的艺术感，而不将其与完全外来的情感混淆，如奉献、怜悯、爱、爱国主义等等。

- confounding: 混淆
- art: 艺术
- independent of: 独立于
- clap-trap: 哗众取宠
- stand alone: 独立存在
- appeal to: 诉诸
- artistic sense: 艺术感
- eye or ear: 眼睛或耳朵
- without: 不
- emotions: 情感
- entirely foreign to it: 完全外来的
- devotion: 奉献
- pity: 怜悯
- love: 爱
- patriotism: 爱国主义
- and the like: 等等
