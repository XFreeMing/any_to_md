1. In some contexts, legitimacy norms proscribe the sale of some entitlements, such as those that explicitly involve health and safety, personal integrity, and environmental protections, although there often is not an equally strong normative injunction to buy such entitlements if they are unowned.

> 在某些情况下，合法性规范禁止出售某些权利，如那些明确涉及健康和安全、个人完整性和环境保护的权利，尽管如果这些权利是无主的，通常没有同样强烈的规范性要求购买这些权利。

- proscribe: 禁止
- in some contexts: 在某些情况下
- legitimacy norms: 合法性规范
- sale: 出售
- entitlements: 权利
- such as: 如
- explicitly involve: 明确涉及
- health and safety: 健康和安全
- personal integrity: 个人完整性
- environmental protections: 环境保护
- although: 尽管
- equally strong: 同样强烈的
- normative injunction: 规范性要求
- buy: 购买
- unowned: 无主的

2. In Bad Acts and Guilty Minds, Leo Katz collects some cases from the colonial period in Africa that, rendered schematically, look like this: The actor kills someone and claims that the victim was an evil spirit or a witch, not a human being. The law proscribed knowingly killing human beings, not evil spirits or witches. But the actor's mistake is not of the ordinary factual kind. (Alexander, Ferzan, & Morse, 2009)

> 在《坏行为和罪恶心灵》中，利奥·卡茨收集了非洲殖民时期的一些案例，这些案例以示意图的方式呈现，看起来像这样：行为者杀死某人并声称受害者是邪恶精神或女巫，而不是人类。法律禁止故意杀害人类，而不是邪恶精神或女巫。但行为者的错误不是普通的事实类型。

- proscribed: 禁止
- Bad Acts and Guilty Minds: 《坏行为和罪恶心灵》
- Leo Katz: 利奥·卡茨
- collects: 收集
- cases: 案例
- colonial period: 殖民时期
- Africa: 非洲
- rendered schematically: 以示意图的方式呈现
- actor: 行为者
- kills someone: 杀死某人
- claims that: 声称
- victim: 受害者
- evil spirit: 邪恶精神
- witch: 女巫
- human being: 人类
- law: 法律
- knowingly killing: 故意杀害
- mistake: 错误
- ordinary factual kind: 普通的事实类型

3. This gap – between what a legislator should proscribe and what a citizen should do – cannot be closed. There are different approaches to confronting this gap, but none of these approaches will eliminate it. (Alexander, Ferzan, & Morse, 2009)

- gap: 差距
- between: 在...之间
- legislator: 立法者
- should: 应该
- citizen: 公民
- cannot be closed: 无法被弥合
- different approaches: 不同的方法

- none of these approaches: 这些方法都不
- eliminate: 消除
