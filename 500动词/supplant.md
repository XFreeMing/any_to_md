1. If photography is allowed to deputize for art in some of art's activities, it will not be long before it has supplanted or corrupted art altogether, thanks to the stupidity of the masses, its natural ally. (Wolff, 2006)

> 如果允许摄影在艺术的某些活动中代表艺术，那么由于大众的愚蠢（它的天然盟友），用不了多久它就会完全取代或腐蚀艺术。

- supplanted: 取代
- if: 如果
- photography: 摄影
- allowed to: 允许
- deputize for: 代表
- art: 艺术
- some of art's activities: 艺术的某些活动
- will not be long before: 用不了多久
- corrupted: 腐蚀
- altogether: 完全
- thanks to: 由于
- stupidity: 愚蠢
- masses: 大众
- natural ally: 天然盟友

2. The new freeway was to supplant the former canal and its thirteenth lock in New Brunswick, abandoned in 1932.

> 新的高速公路将取代前运河及其在新不伦瑞克的第十三个船闸，该运河于 1932 年被废弃。

- supplant: 取代
- new freeway: 新的高速公路
- former canal: 前运河
- thirteenth lock: 第十三个船闸
- New Brunswick: 新不伦瑞克
- abandoned in 1932: 于 1932 年被废弃

3. The alien invasion is a common theme in science fiction stories and film, in which extraterrestrials invade Earth either to exterminate and supplant human life, enslave it under a colonial system, harvest humans for food, steal the planet's resources, or destroy the planet altogether.

> 外星人入侵是科幻故事和电影中的常见主题，其中外星人入侵地球要么是为了消灭和取代人类生命，要么是在殖民制度下奴役它，要么是为了食物而收割人类，要么是偷取地球资源，要么是完全摧毁地球。

- supplant: 取代
- alien invasion: 外星人入侵
- common theme: 常见主题
- science fiction stories: 科幻故事
- film: 电影
- extraterrestrials: 外星人
- invade Earth: 入侵地球
- either to: 要么是为了
- exterminate: 消灭
- human life: 人类生命
- enslave: 奴役
- colonial system: 殖民制度
- harvest humans for food: 为了食物而收割人类
- steal: 偷取
- planet's resources: 地球资源
- destroy: 摧毁
- planet altogether: 完全摧毁地球

4. Liberals believed that education was important to personal and social improvement and sought in Catholic countries to supplant Catholic education with moral and civic training based on secular values. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 自由主义者认为教育对个人和社会改善很重要，并在天主教国家寻求用基于世俗价值观的道德和公民训练来取代天主教教育。

- supplant: 取代
- liberals: 自由主义者
- believed that: 认为
- education: 教育
- important to: 对...重要
- personal and social improvement: 个人和社会改善
- sought: 寻求
- Catholic countries: 天主教国家
- Catholic education: 天主教教育
- moral and civic training: 道德和公民训练
- based on: 基于
- secular values: 世俗价值观
