1. Meanwhile, the bright light and shadows of the colonnades create a multitude of vertical lines that harmonize with the fissures of the cliff. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 同时，柱廊的明亮光线和阴影创造了大量的垂直线，与悬崖的裂缝相协调。

- harmonize: 协调
- meanwhile: 同时
- bright light: 明亮光线
- shadows: 阴影
- colonnades: 柱廊
- create: 创造
- multitude: 大量
- vertical lines: 垂直线
- fissures: 裂缝
- cliff: 悬崖

2. The overriding task of scholasticism in the thirteenth century was to harmonize Christian revelation with the work of Aristotle, leaving the Christian revelation more harmonious. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 13 世纪经院哲学的首要任务是调和基督教启示与亚里士多德的工作，使基督教启示更加和谐。

- harmonize: 调和
- overriding task: 首要任务
- scholasticism: 经院哲学
- thirteenth century: 13 世纪
- Christian revelation: 基督教启示
- work of Aristotle: 亚里士多德的工作
- leaving: 使
- more harmonious: 更加和谐

3. Baroque artists sought to harmonize the Classical traditions of Renaissance art with the intense religious feelings fostered by the revival of religion in the Reformation. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 巴洛克艺术家试图调和文艺复兴艺术的古典传统与宗教改革中宗教复兴所培养的强烈宗教情感。

- harmonize: 调和
- Baroque artists: 巴洛克艺术家
- sought to: 试图
- Classical traditions: 古典传统
- Renaissance art: 文艺复兴艺术
- intense religious feelings: 强烈宗教情感
- fostered by: 由...培养的
- revival of religion: 宗教复兴
- Reformation: 宗教改革

4. The Social Contract, published in 1762, Rousseau tried to harmonize individual liberty with governmental authority. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在 1762 年出版的《社会契约论》中，卢梭试图调和个人自由与政府权威。

- harmonize: 调和
- Social Contract: 《社会契约论》
- published in 1762: 在 1762 年出版
- Rousseau: 卢梭
- tried to: 试图
- individual liberty: 个人自由
- governmental authority: 政府权威
