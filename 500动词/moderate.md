1. With our material appetites continually swelling—as more people seek personal computers, refrigeration, airconditioning, jet travel—what can be done to moderate consumption by those who can afford to overconsume? (Myers, 2012)

> 随着我们的物质欲望不断膨胀——随着越来越多的人寻求个人电脑、制冷、空调、喷气式旅行——可以做什么来缓和那些能够过度消费的人的消费？

- moderate: 缓和
- with: 随着
- material appetites: 物质欲望
- continually swelling: 不断膨胀
- as more people seek: 随着越来越多的人寻求
- personal computers: 个人电脑
- refrigeration: 制冷
- airconditioning: 空调
- jet travel: 喷气式旅行
- what can be done: 可以做什么
- consumption: 消费
- those who: 那些...的人
- can afford to overconsume: 能够过度消费

2. Germany grew stronger and the German people more devoted to the fuehrer. Hitler did not moderate his ambitions, and the appeasers did not avert war. (Perry, 2010)

> 德国变得更强大，德国人民更加忠于元首。希特勒没有缓和他的野心，绥靖主义者也没有避免战争。

- moderate: 缓和
- Germany: 德国
- grew stronger: 变得更强大
- German people: 德国人民
- more devoted to: 更加忠于
- fuehrer: 元首
- Hitler: 希特勒
- did not: 没有
- ambitions: 野心
- appeasers: 绥靖主义者
- avert war: 避免战争

3. Talleyrand suggested that Britain, Austria, and France join in an alliance to oppose Prussia and Russia. This clever move restored France to the family of nations. Now France was no longer the hated enemy but a necessary counterweight to Russia and Prussia. Threatened with war, Russia and Prussia moderated their demands and the crisis ended. (Perry, 2010)

> 塔列朗建议英国、奥地利和法国结成联盟来反对普鲁士和俄罗斯。这一巧妙的举动使法国恢复了国家大家庭的地位。现在法国不再是可恨的敌人，而是对俄罗斯和普鲁士的必要制衡。受到战争威胁，俄罗斯和普鲁士缓和了他们的要求，危机结束了。

- moderated: 缓和
- Talleyrand: 塔列朗
- suggested that: 建议
- Britain: 英国
- Austria: 奥地利
- France: 法国
- join in an alliance: 结成联盟
- oppose: 反对
- Prussia: 普鲁士
- Russia: 俄罗斯
- clever move: 巧妙的举动
- restored: 恢复
- family of nations: 国家大家庭
- no longer: 不再
- hated enemy: 可恨的敌人
- necessary counterweight: 必要制衡
- threatened with war: 受到战争威胁
- their demands: 他们的要求
- crisis ended: 危机结束

- moderate: 缓和
- with this population size: 以这种人口规模
- humans: 人类
- already overshot: 已经超过
- Earth's carrying capacity: 地球的承载能力
