1. Indeed, the figures obviously interact, revealing the artist's interest in narrative, a theme that persists throughout the history of Greek art. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 确实，人物显然相互作用，揭示了艺术家对叙事的兴趣，这是一个贯穿希腊艺术史的主题。

- persists: 持续
- indeed: 确实
- figures: 人物
- obviously interact: 显然相互作用
- revealing: 揭示
- artist's interest: 艺术家的兴趣
- narrative: 叙事
- theme: 主题
- throughout the history: 贯穿历史
- Greek art: 希腊艺术

2. Unfortunately, old religious rivalries and economic crises persisted, and the reign of Charles II's successor, James II, was soon in jeopardy. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 不幸的是，旧的宗教竞争和经济危机持续存在，查理二世的继任者詹姆斯二世的统治很快陷入危险。

- persisted: 持续存在
- unfortunately: 不幸的是
- old religious rivalries: 旧的宗教竞争
- economic crises: 经济危机
- reign: 统治
- Charles II's successor: 查理二世的继任者
- James II: 詹姆斯二世
- soon in jeopardy: 很快陷入危险

3. Nevertheless, although these two revolutions initiated what historians like to call the modern European world, remnants of the old persisted in the midst of the new. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 然而，尽管这两次革命开启了历史学家喜欢称之为现代欧洲世界的东西，但旧的残余在新事物中持续存在。

- persisted: 持续存在
- nevertheless: 然而
- although: 尽管
- two revolutions: 两次革命
- initiated: 开启
- historians: 历史学家
- modern European world: 现代欧洲世界
- remnants: 残余
- old: 旧的
- midst of the new: 新事物中

4. Nevertheless, tensions between the throne and Parliament persisted, and in 1629 Charles I dissolved Parliament, which would not meet again. (Perry, 2010)

> 然而，王位和议会之间的紧张关系持续存在，1629年查理一世解散了议会，议会将不再召开。

- persisted: 持续存在
- nevertheless: 然而
- tensions: 紧张关系
- between: 在...之间
- throne: 王位
- Parliament: 议会
- 1629: 1629年
- Charles I: 查理一世
- dissolved: 解散
- would not meet again: 将不再召开
