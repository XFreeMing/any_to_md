1. Because the context for looking at art constantly changes, our interpretations and insights into art and entire periods evolve as well. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 因为看待艺术的背景不断变化，我们对艺术和整个时期的解释和洞察也在演变。

- evolve: 演变
- because: 因为
- context: 背景
- for looking at art: 看待艺术的
- constantly changes: 不断变化
- interpretations: 解释
- insights: 洞察
- into art: 对艺术
- entire periods: 整个时期
- as well: 也

2. Using Darwin's terminology, social Darwinists argued that societies were organisms that evolved through time from a struggle with their environment. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 使用达尔文的术语，社会达尔文主义者认为社会是通过时间从与环境的斗争中进化而来的有机体。

- evolved: 进化
- using: 使用
- Darwin's terminology: 达尔文的术语
- social Darwinists: 社会达尔文主义者
- argued that: 认为
- societies: 社会
- organisms: 有机体
- through time: 通过时间
- from a struggle with: 从与...的斗争
- their environment: 它们的环境

3. This means that we are constantly responding and adjusting to the changing environment around us; as individuals, we evolve with and within the larger context in which we live. (Giddens, 2009)

> 这意味着我们不断对我们周围不断变化的环境做出反应和调整；作为个人，我们在我们生活的更大背景中演变。

- evolve: 演变
- this means that: 这意味着
- constantly responding: 不断做出反应
- adjusting to: 调整以适应
- changing environment: 不断变化的环境
- around us: 我们周围的
- as individuals: 作为个人
- with and within: 在...中
- larger context: 更大背景
- in which we live: 我们生活的

4. In all this, there is a message of hope. If prejudice is not deeply ingrained in personality, then as fashions change and new norms evolve, prejudice can diminish. And so it has. (Myers, 2012)

> 在这一切中，有一个希望的信息。如果偏见没有深深根植于人格中，那么随着时尚的变化和新规范的演变，偏见可以减少。它确实如此。

- evolve: 演变
- in all this: 在这一切中
- message of hope: 希望的信息
- if: 如果
- prejudice: 偏见
- not deeply ingrained: 没有深深根植
- in personality: 在人格中
- then: 那么
- as: 随着
- fashions change: 时尚的变化
- new norms: 新规范
- can diminish: 可以减少
- and so it has: 它确实如此
