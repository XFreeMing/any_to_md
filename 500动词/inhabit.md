1. From earliest times, most African societies maintained the animistic belief that powerful spirits inhabit the natural world. They honored a single creator god, but they addressed his powers indirectly through spirits associated with nature and the souls of departed ancestors. (Fiero, 2011)

- natural world: 自然世界

- single creator god: 单一的创造神
- addressed his powers: 处理他的力量
- indirectly: 间接地
- spirits associated with nature: 与自然相关的精神
- souls of departed ancestors: 已故祖先的灵魂
