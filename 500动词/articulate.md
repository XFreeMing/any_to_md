1. They articulate the ancient Chinese conviction that human beings must heed a moral order that is fixed in nature, not in divine pronouncement. (Fiero, 2011)

> 他们阐述了古代中国人的信念，即人类必须遵循固定在自然中的道德秩序，而不是神圣的宣告。

- articulate: 阐述
- they: 他们
- ancient Chinese conviction: 古代中国人的信念
- human beings: 人类
- must heed: 必须遵循
- moral order: 道德秩序
- fixed in nature: 固定在自然中
- not in: 而不是在
- divine pronouncement: 神圣的宣告

2. Transformational leaders—many of whom are charismatic, energetic, self-confident extraverts—articulate high standards, inspire people to share their vision, and offer personal attention. (Myers, 2012)

> 变革型领导者——其中许多是有魅力、精力充沛、自信的外向者——阐述高标准，激励人们分享他们的愿景，并提供个人关注。

- articulate: 阐述
- transformational leaders: 变革型领导者
- many of whom: 其中许多
- charismatic: 有魅力的
- energetic: 精力充沛的
- self-confident extraverts: 自信的外向者
- high standards: 高标准
- inspire: 激励
- people: 人们
- share their vision: 分享他们的愿景
- offer: 提供
- personal attention: 个人关注

3. Even more than physical assaults or vulgar verbal abuse, interactional vandalism leaves victims unable to articulate what has happened. (Giddens, 2009)

> 甚至比身体攻击或粗俗的言语虐待更严重，互动破坏使受害者无法表达发生了什么。

- articulate: 表达
- even more than: 甚至比...更严重
- physical assaults: 身体攻击
- vulgar verbal abuse: 粗俗的言语虐待
- interactional vandalism: 互动破坏
- leaves: 使
- victims: 受害者
- unable to: 无法
- what has happened: 发生了什么

4. Matisse was among the first to articulate the Modernist scorn for representational art: "Exactitude is not truth," he insisted. (Fiero, 2011)

> 马蒂斯是第一批阐述现代主义对表现艺术的蔑视的人之一："精确不是真理，"他坚持说。

- articulate: 阐述
- Matisse: 马蒂斯
- among the first: 第一批之一
- Modernist scorn: 现代主义蔑视
- representational art: 表现艺术
- exactitude is not truth: 精确不是真理
- insisted: 坚持说
