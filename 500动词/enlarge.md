1. Japan hoped that a quick strike against the American fleet in the Pacific would give it time to enlarge and consolidate its empire. (Perry, 2010)

> 日本希望快速打击太平洋上的美国舰队能给它时间扩大和巩固其帝国。

- enlarge: 扩大
- Japan: 日本
- hoped that: 希望
- quick strike: 快速打击
- against: 针对
- American fleet: 美国舰队
- in the Pacific: 在太平洋上
- give it time: 给它时间
- consolidate: 巩固
- its empire: 其帝国

2. In the 1860s, King William I (1861--1888) attempted to enlarge and strengthen the Prussian army. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在19世纪60年代，国王威廉一世（1861-1888）试图扩大和加强普鲁士军队。

- enlarge: 扩大
- in the 1860s: 在19世纪60年代
- King William I: 国王威廉一世
- attempted to: 试图
- strengthen: 加强
- Prussian army: 普鲁士军队

3. The more ambitious tried to enlarge their domains through conquest. (Davies, et al., 2011)

> 更有野心的人试图通过征服来扩大他们的领地。

- enlarge: 扩大
- more ambitious: 更有野心的人
- tried to: 试图
- their domains: 他们的领地
- through conquest: 通过征服

4. In works such as the bronze doors of Bishop Bernward, Ottonian art had enlarged the small scale of this tradition but had not changed its spirit. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在诸如主教伯恩瓦德的青铜门等作品中，奥托艺术扩大了这一传统的小规模，但没有改变其精神。

- enlarged: 扩大
- works: 作品
- such as: 诸如
- bronze doors: 青铜门
- Bishop Bernward: 主教伯恩瓦德
- Ottonian art: 奥托艺术
- small scale: 小规模
- this tradition: 这一传统
- had not changed: 没有改变
- its spirit: 其精神
