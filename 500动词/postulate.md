1. The former (the Neoplatonic hierarchy of substances) postulated a hierarchy of substances, or great chain of being, from the lowest form of physical matter (plants) to the purest spirit (God), in which humans occupied a central or middle position. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 前者（新柏拉图主义的物质等级）假设了一个物质等级，或存在的伟大链条，从最低形式的物理物质（植物）到最纯粹的精神（上帝），其中人类占据中心或中间位置。

- postulated: 假设
- former: 前者
- Neoplatonic hierarchy of substances: 新柏拉图主义的物质等级
- hierarchy of substances: 物质等级
- great chain of being: 存在的伟大链条
- from: 从
- lowest form: 最低形式
- physical matter: 物理物质
- plants: 植物
- purest spirit: 最纯粹的精神
- God: 上帝
- humans: 人类
- occupied: 占据
- central or middle position: 中心或中间位置

2. Bergson postulated that we experience life not as a series of continuous rational moments, but as intuited random memories and perceptions that we then piece together to form ideas. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 柏格森假设我们体验生活不是作为一系列连续理性的时刻，而是作为直觉的随机记忆和感知，然后我们将它们拼凑在一起形成想法。

- postulated: 假设
- Bergson: 柏格森
- experience life: 体验生活
- not as: 不是作为
- series of continuous rational moments: 一系列连续理性的时刻
- but as: 而是作为
- intuited random memories: 直觉的随机记忆
- perceptions: 感知
- piece together: 拼凑在一起
- form ideas: 形成想法

3. Rejecting the medieval division of the universe into higher and lower realms and superior and inferior substances, the modern view postulates the uniformity of nature and of nature's laws: the cosmos knows no privilege of rank; heavenly bodies follow the same laws of nature as earthly objects. (Perry, 2010)

> 拒绝中世纪将宇宙分为高低领域和优劣物质的划分，现代观点假设了自然和自然法则的统一性：宇宙不知道等级特权；天体遵循与地球物体相同的自然法则。

- postulates: 假设
- rejecting: 拒绝
- medieval division: 中世纪的划分
- universe: 宇宙
- higher and lower realms: 高低领域
- superior and inferior substances: 优劣物质
- modern view: 现代观点
- uniformity: 统一性
- nature: 自然
- nature's laws: 自然法则
- cosmos: 宇宙
- knows no privilege of rank: 不知道等级特权
- heavenly bodies: 天体
- follow: 遵循
- same laws of nature: 相同的自然法则
- earthly objects: 地球物体

4. Freud postulated a harrowing conflict between the relentless strivings of our instinctual nature and the requirements of civilization. (Perry, 2010)

> 弗洛伊德假设了我们本能本性的无情努力与文明要求之间的痛苦冲突。

- postulated: 假设
- Freud: 弗洛伊德
- harrowing conflict: 痛苦冲突
- between: 在...之间
- relentless strivings: 无情努力
- instinctual nature: 本能本性
- requirements: 要求
- civilization: 文明
