1. This means that there is both more competition amongst firms, since there are more of them, and less opportunity for a dominant capitalist or managerial class to reproduce and pass on its own privilege to the next generation of capitalists. (Giddens, 2009)

> 这意味着公司之间有更多的竞争，因为它们数量更多，而占主导地位的资本家或管理阶层复制并将其特权传递给下一代资本家的机会更少。

- reproduce: 复制
- this means: 这意味着
- more competition: 更多竞争
- amongst firms: 公司之间
- since: 因为
- more of them: 它们数量更多
- less opportunity: 更少机会
- dominant capitalist: 占主导地位的资本家
- managerial class: 管理阶层
- pass on: 传递
- its own privilege: 其自己的特权
- next generation: 下一代
- capitalists: 资本家

- as: 正如
- evolutionary psychologists: 进化心理学家
- remind us: 提醒我们
- inherited human nature: 继承的人类本性
- predisposes us: 使我们倾向于
- behave in ways: 以...方式行事
- helped: 帮助
- ancestors: 祖先
- survive: 生存

- one argument: 一个论点

- system of male dominance: 男性主导制度

- despite: 尽管
- artistic advantages: 艺术优势
