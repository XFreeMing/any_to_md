1. But whereas biological approaches focus on physical features that predispose individuals to crime, psychological views concentrate on personality types. (Giddens, 2009)

> 但是，虽然生物学方法关注使个人倾向于犯罪的物理特征，但心理学观点集中在人格类型上。

- predispose: 使...倾向于
- whereas: 虽然
- biological approaches: 生物学方法
- focus on: 关注
- physical features: 物理特征
- individuals: 个人
- crime: 犯罪
- psychological views: 心理学观点
- concentrate on: 集中在
- personality types: 人格类型

2. Rousseau reflected the view of many male thinkers when he argued that there were natural biological differences between men and women that predisposed women to be mothers rather than intellectuals. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 卢梭反映了许多男性思想家的观点，当时他认为男女之间存在自然的生物学差异，这使妇女倾向于成为母亲而不是知识分子。

- predisposed: 使...倾向于
- Rousseau: 卢梭
- reflected: 反映
- view: 观点
- many male thinkers: 许多男性思想家
- argued that: 认为
- natural biological differences: 自然的生物学差异
- between men and women: 男女之间
- women: 妇女
- mothers: 母亲
- rather than: 而不是
- intellectuals: 知识分子

3. As evolutionary psychologists remind us, our inherited human nature predisposes us to behave in ways that helped our ancestors survive and reproduce. (Myers, 2012)

> 正如进化心理学家提醒我们的那样，我们继承的人类本性使我们倾向于以帮助我们的祖先生存和繁殖的方式行事。

- predisposes: 使...倾向于
- evolutionary psychologists: 进化心理学家
- remind us: 提醒我们
- inherited human nature: 继承的人类本性
- behave in ways: 以...方式行事
- helped: 帮助
- ancestors: 祖先
- survive: 生存
- reproduce: 繁殖

4. It almost seems as though the British were predisposed to embracing Neoclassicism, not only because the birth of Enlightenment occurred in Britain but also because the nation already had an intense involvement with antiquity in literature, which dated to the opening decades of the eighteenth century. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 几乎似乎英国人倾向于拥抱新古典主义，不仅因为启蒙运动的诞生发生在英国，而且因为这个国家在文学中已经与古代有密切的联系，这可以追溯到18世纪的开头几十年。

- predisposed to: 倾向于
- almost seems as though: 几乎似乎
- British: 英国人
- embracing Neoclassicism: 拥抱新古典主义
- not only because: 不仅因为
- birth of Enlightenment: 启蒙运动的诞生
- occurred in Britain: 发生在英国
- but also because: 而且因为
- nation: 国家
- already had: 已经有
- intense involvement: 密切的联系
- antiquity: 古代
- literature: 文学
- dated to: 追溯到
- opening decades: 开头几十年
- eighteenth century: 18世纪
