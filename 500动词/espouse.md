1. Both Alberti and Brunelleschi espoused the Hellenic theory that the human form mirrored the order inherent in the universe. (Fiero, 2011)

> 阿尔贝蒂和布鲁内莱斯基都拥护希腊理论，即人体形式反映了宇宙中固有的秩序。

- espoused: 拥护
- both Alberti and Brunelleschi: 阿尔贝蒂和布鲁内莱斯基
- Hellenic theory: 希腊理论
- human form: 人体形式
- mirrored: 反映
- order: 秩序
- inherent in: 固有的
- universe: 宇宙

2. Having attuned their behavior to the situation, they are more likely to espouse attitudes they don't really hold. (Giddens, 2009)

> 在使他们的行为适应情况后，他们更可能拥护他们并不真正持有的态度。

- espouse: 拥护
- having attuned: 在使...适应后
- their behavior: 他们的行为
- situation: 情况
- more likely to: 更可能
- attitudes: 态度
- don't really hold: 并不真正持有

3. Expected to recant the heretical doctrines he had espoused, Luther refused and made the famous reply that became the battle cry of the Reformation. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 被期望放弃他所拥护的异端教义，路德拒绝了，并做出了成为宗教改革战斗口号的著名回答。

- espoused: 拥护
- expected to recant: 被期望放弃
- heretical doctrines: 异端教义
- Luther: 路德
- refused: 拒绝
- made: 做出
- famous reply: 著名回答
- became: 成为
- battle cry: 战斗口号
- Reformation: 宗教改革

4. James espoused the divine right of kings, a viewpoint that alienated Parliament, which had grown accustomed under the Tudors to act on the premise that monarch and Parliament together ruled England as a ''balanced polity.'' (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 詹姆斯拥护君权神授，这一观点疏远了议会，议会在都铎王朝下已经习惯于在君主和议会共同统治英格兰作为"平衡政体"的前提下行事。

- espoused: 拥护
- James: 詹姆斯
- divine right of kings: 君权神授
- viewpoint: 观点
- alienated: 疏远
- Parliament: 议会
- grown accustomed: 已经习惯于
- under the Tudors: 在都铎王朝下
- act on the premise: 在...前提下行事
- monarch: 君主
- together ruled: 共同统治
- England: 英格兰
- balanced polity: 平衡政体
