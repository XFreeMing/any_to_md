2. I begin by discussing the thesis of the fundamental standing of animals. I then consider some theories that might seem to account for it, and point out difficulties they face. Next, I briefly explicate the society-centered theory in order to show that it underwrites a version of the thesis of the fundamental concern of morality. Finally, I show how the society-centered theory can underwrite the thesis of the fundamental standing of animals. (Copp, 2011)

3. Welfarism has been ably explicated and defended by Wayne Sumner. According to the welfarist, Sumner explains, "the ultimate point of ethics is to bring about intrinsically valuable states of affairs," and, because of this, "everyone has a reason to promote" such states of affairs, or to want them to come about. (Copp, 2011)

> 福利主义已经被韦恩·萨姆纳有力地阐述和辩护。根据福利主义者的观点，萨姆纳解释说，"伦理学的最终目的是带来内在有价值的事态，"因此，"每个人都有理由促进"这样的事态，或希望它们发生。

4. One way to explicate this generic idea is in terms of the conjunction of the following two claims: first, that moral obligations entail normative reasons to act, in that someone who has a moral obligation has a reason to do what it demands; second, that the person has this reason irrespective of whether doing as the obligation demands either serves or conflicts with the person's motives. (Skorupski, 2012)

> 阐述这个一般概念的一种方法是根据以下两个主张的结合：首先，道德义务包含行动的规范性理由，即具有道德义务的人有理由做它所要求的事情；其次，这个人有这个理由，无论按照义务要求做是否服务于或与这个人的动机冲突。

- person's motives: 这个人的动机
