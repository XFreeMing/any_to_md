1. Although popular wisdom stresses the impact of attitudes on behavior, in fact, attitudes are often poor predictors of behaviors. (Myers, 2012)

> 虽然流行智慧强调态度对行为的影响，但事实上，态度往往是行为的糟糕预测因子。

- stresses: 强调
- although: 虽然
- popular wisdom: 流行智慧
- impact: 影响
- attitudes: 态度
- behavior: 行为
- in fact: 事实上
- often: 往往
- poor predictors: 糟糕的预测因子
- behaviors: 行为

2. Durkheim stressed the importance of abandoning prejudices and ideology. (Giddens, 2009)

> 涂尔干强调了放弃偏见和意识形态的重要性。

- stressed: 强调
- Durkheim: 涂尔干
- importance: 重要性
- abandoning: 放弃
- prejudices: 偏见
- ideology: 意识形态
