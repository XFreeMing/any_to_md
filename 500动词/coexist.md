1. In studies of American adults, Theodor Adorno and his colleagues (1950) discovered that hostility toward Jews often coexisted with hostility toward other minorities. (Myers, 2012)

> 在对美国成年人的研究中，西奥多·阿多诺和他的同事（1950）发现，对犹太人的敌意往往与对其他少数群体的敌意共存。

- coexisted with: 与...共存
- studies: 研究
- American adults: 美国成年人
- Theodor Adorno: 西奥多·阿多诺
- colleagues: 同事
- discovered that: 发现
- hostility toward Jews: 对犹太人的敌意
- often: 往往
- hostility toward other minorities: 对其他少数群体的敌意

2. Just as the Gothic revival thrived alongside the Classical revival in Britain, Romantic painting coexisted with Neoclassical. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 正如哥特式复兴在英国与古典复兴一起蓬勃发展一样，浪漫主义绘画与新古典主义共存。

- coexisted with: 与...共存
- just as: 正如
- Gothic revival: 哥特式复兴
- thrived alongside: 与...一起蓬勃发展
- Classical revival: 古典复兴
- Britain: 英国
- Romantic painting: 浪漫主义绘画
- Neoclassical: 新古典主义

3. Great affluence and abject poverty coexist side by side, but contact between the two worlds can be minimal. (Giddens, 2009)

> 巨大的富裕和极度的贫困并存，但两个世界之间的联系可能是最小的。

- coexist: 共存
- great affluence: 巨大的富裕
- abject poverty: 极度的贫困
- side by side: 并存
- but: 但
- contact: 联系
- between the two worlds: 两个世界之间
- minimal: 最小的

4. God's house, which we think of as one, is thus divided into three; some pray, others fight, and yet others work. The three groups, which coexist, cannot bear to be separated; the services rendered by one are a precondition for the labours of the two others; each in his turn takes it upon himself to relieve the whole. (Oxford English Dictionary, 1989)

> 上帝的房屋，我们认为是统一的，因此被分为三部分；一些人祈祷，其他人战斗，还有一些人工作。这三个群体共存，不能忍受被分离；一个群体提供的服务是其他两个群体劳动的前提；每个人轮流承担起缓解整体的责任。

- coexist: 共存
- God's house: 上帝的房屋
- think of as one: 认为是统一的
- divided into three: 被分为三部分
- some pray: 一些人祈祷
- others fight: 其他人战斗
- yet others work: 还有一些人工作
- three groups: 三个群体
- cannot bear to be separated: 不能忍受被分离
- services rendered by one: 一个群体提供的服务
- precondition: 前提
- labours of the two others: 其他两个群体的劳动
- each in his turn: 每个人轮流
- takes it upon himself: 承担起
- relieve the whole: 缓解整体
