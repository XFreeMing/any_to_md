1. He emphasized the importance of the rule of law. (Perry, 2010)

> 他强调了法治的重要性。

- emphasized: 强调
- importance: 重要性
- rule of law: 法治

2. With one group, the benevolent caregivers emphasized "our responsibility to make this a home you can be proud of and happy in." (Myers, 2012)

> 在一个群体中，仁慈的照顾者强调"我们有责任使这成为一个你可以为之骄傲和快乐的家。"

- emphasized: 强调
- one group: 一个群体
- benevolent caregivers: 仁慈的照顾者
- responsibility: 责任
- make this a home: 使这成为一个家
- proud of: 为之骄傲
- happy in: 快乐

3. Social scientists have emphasized the importance of social practices and norms, which have a significant influence on individual decisions. (Zamir & Teichman, 2014)

> 社会科学家强调了社会实践和规范的重要性，它们对个人决定有重大影响。

- emphasized: 强调
- social scientists: 社会科学家
- importance: 重要性
- social practices: 社会实践
- norms: 规范
- significant influence: 重大影响
- individual decisions: 个人决定

4. While, as we saw, Hobbes identified the state of nature with a state of war, Locke is keen to emphasize that this is a mistake. (Wolff, 2006)

> 虽然，正如我们所看到的，霍布斯将自然状态等同于战争状态，但洛克热衷于强调这是一个错误。

- emphasize: 强调
- while: 虽然
- as we saw: 正如我们所看到的
- Hobbes: 霍布斯
- identified: 将...等同于
- state of nature: 自然状态
- state of war: 战争状态
- Locke: 洛克
- keen to: 热衷于
- mistake: 错误
