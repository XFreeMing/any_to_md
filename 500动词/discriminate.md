1. To choose a different principle to regulate liberty would, in effect, be to discriminate against a certain group, or to accept diminished liberty for all. (Wolff, 2006)

> 选择不同的原则来规范自由，实际上就是歧视某个群体，或者接受所有人自由减少。

- discriminate: 歧视
- choose: 选择
- different principle: 不同的原则
- regulate: 规范
- liberty: 自由
- in effect: 实际上
- against: 对
- certain group: 某个群体
- accept: 接受
- diminished liberty: 减少的自由
- for all: 所有人

2. Any policy must discriminate on some grounds. The university admissions office ought to discriminate between the clever and the not-so-clever, for example. (Wolff, 2006)

> 任何政策都必须基于某些理由进行区分。例如，大学招生办公室应该在聪明和不那么聪明的人之间进行区分。

- discriminate: 区分
- any policy: 任何政策
- must: 必须
- on some grounds: 基于某些理由
- university admissions office: 大学招生办公室
- ought to: 应该
- between: 在...之间
- clever: 聪明的
- not-so-clever: 不那么聪明的
- for example: 例如

3. After all, affirmative action programmes are supposed to be a remedy for discrimination, but all they seem to do is discriminate on different grounds. (Wolff, 2006)

> 毕竟，平权行动计划应该是歧视的补救措施，但它们似乎所做的只是基于不同的理由进行区分。

- discriminate: 区分
- after all: 毕竟
- affirmative action programmes: 平权行动计划
- supposed to be: 应该是
- remedy: 补救措施
- discrimination: 歧视
- but: 但
- all they seem to do: 它们似乎所做的
- on different grounds: 基于不同的理由

4. Furthermore, while it is illegal to discriminate in employment practices, the state hardly has the resources to supervise every employment panel. (Wolff, 2006)

> 此外，虽然在就业实践中歧视是非法的，但国家几乎没有资源来监督每个就业小组。

- discriminate: 歧视
- furthermore: 此外
- while: 虽然
- illegal: 非法的
- employment practices: 就业实践
- state: 国家
- hardly: 几乎没有
- resources: 资源
- supervise: 监督
- every employment panel: 每个就业小组
