1. The bishops of four great cities, Rome, Jerusalem, Alexandria, and Antioch, held positions of special power in church affairs because the churches in these cities all asserted that they had been founded by the original apostles sent out by Jesus. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 四个大城市的主教，罗马、耶路撒冷、亚历山大和安提阿，在教会事务中拥有特殊权力，因为这些城市的教会都声称它们是由耶稣派出的原始使徒建立的。

- asserted: 声称
- bishops: 主教
- four great cities: 四个大城市
- Rome: 罗马
- Jerusalem: 耶路撒冷
- Alexandria: 亚历山大
- Antioch: 安提阿
- held positions: 拥有地位
- special power: 特殊权力
- church affairs: 教会事务
- churches: 教会
- founded by: 由...建立
- original apostles: 原始使徒
- sent out by Jesus: 由耶稣派出的

2. In the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, kings successfully asserted their authority over competing powers, continuing a trend that had begun in the Late Middle Ages. (Perry, 2010)

> 在16和17世纪，国王成功地维护了他们对竞争势力的权威，延续了始于中世纪末期的趋势。

- asserted: 维护
- sixteenth and seventeenth centuries: 16和17世纪
- kings: 国王
- successfully: 成功地
- authority: 权威
- over: 对
- competing powers: 竞争势力
- continuing: 延续
- trend: 趋势
- begun in: 始于
- Late Middle Ages: 中世纪末期

3. Kandinsky's Russian contemporary Kasimir Malevich arrived at nonrepresentational art not by way of Fauvism but through the influence of Analytic Cubism, which asserted the value of line over color. (Fiero, 2011)

> 康定斯基的俄罗斯同时代人卡西米尔·马列维奇通过分析立体主义的影响而不是野兽主义到达了非表现艺术，分析立体主义主张线条的价值高于色彩。

- asserted: 主张
- Kandinsky's Russian contemporary: 康定斯基的俄罗斯同时代人
- Kasimir Malevich: 卡西米尔·马列维奇
- arrived at: 到达
- nonrepresentational art: 非表现艺术
- not by way of: 不是通过
- Fauvism: 野兽主义
- through the influence of: 通过...的影响
- Analytic Cubism: 分析立体主义
- value of line: 线条的价值
- over color: 高于色彩

4. After his father's assassination, Alexander moved quickly to assert his authority, securing the Macedonian frontiers and smothering a rebellion in Greece. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在他父亲被暗杀后，亚历山大迅速维护他的权威，确保马其顿边界并镇压了希腊的叛乱。

- assert: 维护
- after: 在...后
- father's assassination: 父亲被暗杀
- Alexander: 亚历山大
- moved quickly: 迅速行动
- authority: 权威
- securing: 确保
- Macedonian frontiers: 马其顿边界
- smothering: 镇压
- rebellion: 叛乱
- Greece: 希腊
