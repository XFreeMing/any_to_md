1. First, many critics have argued that functionalism is good at explaining consensus why societies hold together and share a common morality - but it is much less able to explain conflict and radical social change. Others argue that Durkheimian functionalism seems to prioritize societies' constraints over people and does not allow enough room for the creative actions of individuals. Finally, functional analysis tends to impute purposes and needs to society itself. (Giddens, 2009)

> 首先，许多批评者认为，功能主义善于解释共识——为什么社会团结在一起并分享共同的道德——但它解释冲突和激进社会变革的能力要差得多。其他人认为，涂尔干功能主义似乎优先考虑社会对人们的约束，没有为个人的创造性行动留出足够的空间。最后，功能分析倾向于将目的和需要归因于社会本身。

3. The economist may generate a "law" of behavior: Individuals will choose courses of action so as to satisfy their wants of greater subjective significance before those of lesser significance. Individuals will impute value to a commodity on the basis of the particular want satisfied by the added or lost unit of that commodity (the marginal unit). (Beauchamp & Frey, 2014)

> 经济学家可能产生一个行为的"法则"：个人将选择行动路线，以便在那些重要性较小的需求之前满足那些主观重要性更大的需求。个人将根据该商品的增加或损失单位（边际单位）所满足的特定需求，将价值归因于商品。

- impute: 归因
- economist: 经济学家

- law of behavior: 行为的法则
- individuals: 个人
- choose: 选择
- courses of action: 行动路线
- satisfy: 满足
- wants: 需求
- greater subjective significance: 更大的主观重要性
- lesser significance: 较小的重要性
- value: 价值
- commodity: 商品
- basis: 基础
- particular want: 特定需求
- satisfied by: 由...满足
- added or lost unit: 增加或损失单位
- marginal unit: 边际单位

4. In another line of interpretation on the division of labour issue, it has been suggested that in fact Smith imputed too much importance to labour specialization in economic development, at the expense of missing the significance of 'mechanization'. (Berry, Paganelli, & Smith, 2016)
