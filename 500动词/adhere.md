1. That said, a major trend emerged in the 1980s that challenged the idea that architecture had to adhere to any single concept or ideal. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 话虽如此，20世纪80年代出现了一个主要趋势，挑战了建筑必须坚持任何单一概念或理想的观念。

- adhere to: 坚持
- that said: 话虽如此
- major trend: 主要趋势
- emerged: 出现
- 1980s: 20世纪80年代
- challenged: 挑战
- idea: 观念
- architecture: 建筑
- single concept or ideal: 单一概念或理想

2. Modern democracies adhere to the representative model, in which elections are used to determine who will form the government, rather than to decide the particular issues of the day. (Wolff, 2006)

> 现代民主坚持代表制模式，其中选举用于确定谁将组成政府，而不是决定当天的特定问题。

- adhere to: 坚持
- modern democracies: 现代民主
- representative model: 代表制模式
- elections: 选举
- used to: 用于
- determine: 确定
- form the government: 组成政府
- rather than: 而不是
- decide: 决定
- particular issues: 特定问题
- of the day: 当天的

3. The aim of the perfect noble, then, was to serve his prince in an effective and honest way. Nobles would adhere to these principles for hundreds of years while they continued to dominate European life socially and politically. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 那么，完美贵族的目的是以有效和诚实的方式为他的君主服务。贵族会坚持这些原则数百年，同时他们继续在社会和政治上主导欧洲生活。

- adhere to: 坚持
- aim: 目的
- perfect noble: 完美贵族
- serve: 服务
- prince: 君主
- effective and honest way: 有效和诚实的方式
- nobles: 贵族
- principles: 原则
- hundreds of years: 数百年
- while: 同时
- continued to dominate: 继续主导
- European life: 欧洲生活
- socially and politically: 在社会和政治上

4. Most people do adhere to norms of social responsibility, reciprocity, equity, and keeping one's commitments. (Myers, 2012)

> 大多数人确实坚持社会责任、互惠、公平和遵守承诺的规范。

- adhere to: 坚持
- most people: 大多数人
- norms: 规范
- social responsibility: 社会责任
- reciprocity: 互惠
- equity: 公平
- keeping one's commitments: 遵守承诺
