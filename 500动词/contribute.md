1. A variety of historical factors contributed to the slow but growing receptivity to Christianity within the Roman Empire. (Fiero, 2011)

> 各种历史因素促成了罗马帝国内对基督教缓慢但不断增长的接受度。

- contributed to: 促成
- variety of historical factors: 各种历史因素
- slow but growing: 缓慢但不断增长的
- receptivity: 接受度
- Christianity: 基督教
- within: 在...内
- Roman Empire: 罗马帝国

2. If two people receive equal outcomes, they should contribute equally; otherwise one or the other will feel it is unfair. (Fiero, 2011)

> 如果两个人得到相等的结果，他们应该平等地贡献；否则一方或另一方会感到不公平。

- contribute: 贡献
- if: 如果
- two people: 两个人
- receive: 得到
- equal outcomes: 相等的结果
- should: 应该
- equally: 平等地
- otherwise: 否则
- one or the other: 一方或另一方
- feel: 感到
- unfair: 不公平

3. By the teaching of great literature, philosophy, and science, liberal arts education seeks to expand our awareness and to liberate us from the confines of the present. Social psychology contributes to these goals. (Myers, 2012)

> 通过教授伟大的文学、哲学和科学，文科教育寻求扩展我们的意识，并将我们从现在的限制中解放出来。社会心理学有助于实现这些目标。

- contributes to: 有助于
- teaching: 教授
- great literature: 伟大的文学
- philosophy: 哲学
- science: 科学
- liberal arts education: 文科教育
- seeks to: 寻求
- expand: 扩展
- awareness: 意识
- liberate: 解放
- confines: 限制
- present: 现在
- social psychology: 社会心理学
- goals: 目标

4. Nineteenth-century steel and cast-iron technology also contributed to the construction of bridges. In 1870, work began on the first steel-wire suspension bridge in the United States: the Brooklyn Bridge. (Fiero, 2011)

> 19世纪的钢铁和铸铁技术也有助于桥梁的建设。1870年，美国第一座钢丝悬索桥开始建设：布鲁克林大桥。

- contributed to: 有助于
- nineteenth-century: 19世纪的
- steel: 钢铁
- cast-iron technology: 铸铁技术
- also: 也
- construction: 建设
- bridges: 桥梁
- 1870: 1870年
- work began: 开始建设
- first steel-wire suspension bridge: 第一座钢丝悬索桥
- United States: 美国
- Brooklyn Bridge: 布鲁克林大桥
