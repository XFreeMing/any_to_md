- Jesus' death: 耶稣的死
- devotees: 虔诚信徒
- seeking to: 寻求
- convey: 传达
- religious truth: 宗教真理
- faith: 信仰

4. In a sense, art is a form of propaganda, for it represents an individual's or group's point of view, and this view is often presented as truth or fact. For centuries, art was used by church and state to propagate their importance, superiority, and greatness. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在某种意义上，艺术是一种宣传形式，因为它代表个人或群体的观点，这种观点经常被呈现为真理或事实。几个世纪以来，艺术被教会和国家用来传播它们的重要性、优越性和伟大。
