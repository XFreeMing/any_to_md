1. Hammurabi's Code covers a broad spectrum of moral, social, and commercial obligations. Its civil and criminal statutes specify penalties for murder, theft, incest, adultery, kidnapping, assault and battery, and many other crimes. (Fiero, 2011)

> 《汉谟拉比法典》涵盖了广泛的道德、社会和商业义务。其民法和刑法法规规定了谋杀、盗窃、乱伦、通奸、绑架、攻击和殴打以及许多其他犯罪的惩罚。

- specify: 规定
- Hammurabi's Code: 《汉谟拉比法典》
- covers: 涵盖
- broad spectrum: 广泛范围
- moral, social, and commercial obligations: 道德、社会和商业义务
- civil and criminal statutes: 民法和刑法法规
- penalties: 惩罚
- murder: 谋杀
- theft: 盗窃
- incest: 乱伦
- adultery: 通奸
- kidnapping: 绑架
- assault and battery: 攻击和殴打
- many other crimes: 许多其他犯罪

2. The American delegation wanted the treaty to fix a reasonable sum that Germany would have to pay and specify the period of years allotted for payment. (Perry, 2010)

- specify: 规定
- American delegation: 美国代表团
- wanted: 希望
- treaty: 条约
- fix: 确定
- reasonable sum: 合理金额
- Germany: 德国
- would have to pay: 必须支付
- period of years: 年限
- allotted for payment: 分配的付款

3. Several critics have argued that although hegemonic masculinity appears to be fairly obvious, Cormell does not really present a satisfactory account of it. This is because she does not specify what would count as 'counterhegemonic'. (Giddens, 2009)

> 几位批评者认为，尽管霸权男性气质似乎相当明显，但科内尔并没有真正提出令人满意的解释。这是因为她没有具体说明什么算作"反霸权"。

- specify: 具体说明
- several critics: 几位批评者
- argued that: 认为
- although: 尽管
- hegemonic masculinity: 霸权男性气质
- appears to be: 似乎
- fairly obvious: 相当明显
- Cormell: 科内尔
- does not really present: 并没有真正提出
- satisfactory account: 令人满意的解释
- this is because: 这是因为
- does not: 没有
- what would count as: 什么算作
- counterhegemonic: 反霸权

> 知道两个变量一起变化（相关）使我们能够在知道另一个时预测一个，但相关性并不具体说明因果关系。

- specify: 具体说明
- knowing that: 知道
- two variables: 两个变量
- change together: 一起变化
- correlate: 相关
- enables us to: 使我们能够
- predict: 预测
- one: 一个
- when we know: 当我们知道
- other: 另一个
- but: 但
- correlation: 相关性
- does not: 并不
- cause and effect: 因果关系
