1. In the visual arts, the influence of late nineteenth-century Western printmakers such as Käthe Kollwitz helped to shape the powerful realism of many Chinese artists, including Li Hua (1907–1994). Li's stark and searing woodcut of a bound man—a metaphor for modern China— reiterated the silent scream of Munch and Eisenstein. (Fiero, 2011)

> 在视觉艺术中，19世纪晚期西方版画家如凯绥·珂勒惠支的影响帮助塑造了许多中国艺术家（包括李桦（1907-1994））的强大现实主义。李桦对一个被绑男人的严峻和灼热的木刻——现代中国的隐喻——重申了蒙克和爱森斯坦的无声呐喊。
