1. If the goal of constructing a voluntaristic account of the state is to be achieved, then it is important that all those presently said to be bound by the state should have been able to consent to it. (Wolff, 2006)

> 如果要实现构建国家自愿主义说明的目标，那么重要的是，所有目前被认为受国家约束的人都应该能够同意它。

- consent to: 同意
- if: 如果
- goal: 目标
- constructing: 构建
- voluntaristic account: 自愿主义说明
- state: 国家
- achieved: 实现
- important that: 重要的是
- all those: 所有那些
- presently said to be bound by: 目前被认为受...约束
- should have been able to: 应该能够

2. Consider the argument that hypothetical consent somehow indicates the presence of real consent. We should start from the thought that although almost no one ever formally expresses their consent to the state, there is nevertheless a sense in which all or most of us can be said to consent. (Wolff, 2006)

> 考虑假设同意在某种程度上表明真实同意存在的论点。我们应该从这样的想法开始：虽然几乎没有人正式表达他们对国家的同意，但在某种意义上，我们所有人或大多数人都可以说同意。

- consent: 同意
- consider: 考虑
- argument: 论点
- hypothetical consent: 假设同意
- somehow: 在某种程度上
- indicates: 表明
- presence: 存在
- real consent: 真实同意
- start from: 从...开始
- thought: 想法
- although: 虽然
- almost no one: 几乎没有人
- formally expresses: 正式表达
- state: 国家
- nevertheless: 然而
- sense: 意义
- all or most of us: 我们所有人或大多数人
- can be said to: 可以说

3. The social contract thus preserves the natural rights of the governed. While individuals may willingly consent to give up some of their liberty in return for the ruler's protection, they may never relinquish their ultimate authority. (Fiero, 2011)

> 因此，社会契约保护被统治者的自然权利。虽然个人可能愿意同意放弃一些自由以换取统治者的保护，但他们可能永远不会放弃他们的最终权威。

- consent to: 同意
- social contract: 社会契约
- preserves: 保护
- natural rights: 自然权利
- governed: 被统治者
- while: 虽然
- individuals: 个人
- willingly: 愿意
- give up: 放弃
- liberty: 自由
- in return for: 以换取
- ruler's protection: 统治者的保护
- never relinquish: 永远不会放弃
- ultimate authority: 最终权威

4. We would have shown how the state comes to have universal authority—authority over each one of us—by showing that everyone has consented to that authority. (Wolff, 2006)

> 我们将通过表明每个人都同意该权威来展示国家如何获得普遍权威——对我们每个人的权威。

- consented to: 同意
- shown: 展示
- state: 国家
- comes to have: 获得
- universal authority: 普遍权威
- authority over: 对...的权威
- each one of us: 我们每个人
- by showing that: 通过表明
- everyone: 每个人
- that authority: 该权威
