1. Romanticism began to dissipate in Europe as an intellectual attitude and stylistic trend after 1848 and was gradually superseded by Realism. Increasingly, people came to rely on the physical, physiological, empirical, and scientific as a way to understand nature, society, and human behavior. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 浪漫主义在1848年后作为知识态度和风格趋势在欧洲开始消散，并逐渐被现实主义取代。越来越多的人开始依赖物理、生理、经验和科学作为理解自然、社会和人类行为的方式。

- came to rely on: 开始依赖
- physical: 物理的

- empirical: 经验的
- scientific: 科学的

- nature: 自然
- society: 社会
- human behavior: 人类行为

2. Economic interdependence: The outcome of specialization and the division of labour, when self-sufficiency is superseded and individuals depend on others to produce many or most of the goods they need to sustain their lives. (Giddens, 2009)

> 经济相互依存：专业化和劳动分工的结果，当自给自足被取代，个人依赖他人生产他们需要维持生活的许多或大部分商品时。

- superseded: 被取代
- economic interdependence: 经济相互依存
- outcome: 结果

- self-sufficiency: 自给自足

3. Before the twelfth century, intellectuals (as well as ordinary men and women) considered Scripture and the writings of the church fathers the major repositories of knowledge. Faith in these established sources superseded rational inquiry and preempted the empirical examination of the physical world. Indeed, most intellectuals upheld the Augustinian credo that faith preceded reason. (Fiero, 2011)

> 在12世纪之前，知识分子（以及普通男女）认为《圣经》和教会教父的著作是知识的主要宝库。对这些既定来源的信仰取代了理性探究，并先于对物理世界的经验检验。确实，大多数知识分子坚持奥古斯丁的信条，即信仰先于理性。
