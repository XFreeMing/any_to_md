1. French aristocrats in the service of Louis XIV brought back to France enthusiastic reports of the Imperial Mosque—a fact that has led scholars to detect the influence of Persian art on some of Louis' more lavish enterprises at Versailles. (Fiero, 2011)

> 为路易十四服务的法国贵族带回了对帝国清真寺的热情报告——这一事实使学者们发现了波斯艺术对路易在凡尔赛的一些更奢华企业的影响。

- detect: 发现
- French aristocrats: 法国贵族
- service of Louis XIV: 为路易十四服务
- brought back: 带回
- France: 法国
- enthusiastic reports: 热情报告
- Imperial Mosque: 帝国清真寺
- fact: 事实
- led scholars to: 使学者们
- influence: 影响
- Persian art: 波斯艺术
- more lavish enterprises: 更奢华的企业
- Versailles: 凡尔赛

2. Some archaeologists have demonstrated that the stone circles were used as observatories to detect not only such simple astronomical phenomena as the midwinter and midsummer sunrises but also such sophisticated observations as the major and minor standstills of the moon. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 一些考古学家已经证明，石圈被用作天文台，不仅检测冬至和夏至日出等简单的天文现象，还检测月亮的主要和次要停滞等复杂观测。

- detect: 检测
- some archaeologists: 一些考古学家
- demonstrated that: 证明
- stone circles: 石圈
- used as observatories: 被用作天文台
- not only: 不仅
- simple astronomical phenomena: 简单的天文现象
- midwinter and midsummer sunrises: 冬至和夏至日出
- but also: 还
- sophisticated observations: 复杂观测
- major and minor standstills: 主要和次要停滞
- moon: 月亮

3. The existence of this inclination to aggression, which we can detect in ourselves and justly assume to be present in others, is the factor which disturbs our relations with our neighbor and which forces civilization into such a high expenditure [of energy]. (Fiero, 2011)

> 这种攻击倾向的存在，我们可以在自己身上发现并合理地假设也存在于他人身上，是扰乱我们与邻居关系的因素，也是迫使文明付出如此高代价[能量]的因素。

- detect: 发现
- existence: 存在
- inclination to aggression: 攻击倾向
- ourselves: 我们自己
- justly assume: 合理地假设
- present in others: 存在于他人身上
- factor: 因素
- disturbs: 扰乱
- relations with: 与...的关系
- neighbor: 邻居
- forces: 迫使
- civilization: 文明
- high expenditure: 高代价
- energy: 能量
