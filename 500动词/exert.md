1. Despite this male domination, however, both French and foreign observers complained that females exerted undue influence in French political affairs. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 尽管有这种男性统治，但法国和外国观察家都抱怨女性在法国政治事务中施加了不当影响。

- exerted: 施加
- despite: 尽管
- male domination: 男性统治
- however: 但
- French and foreign observers: 法国和外国观察家
- complained that: 抱怨
- females: 女性
- undue influence: 不当影响
- French political affairs: 法国政治事务

2. Waging war was an expensive undertaking and sovereigns throughout Europe realized the pressing need to consolidate the state's power and exert economic control. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 发动战争是一项昂贵的事业，整个欧洲的君主们意识到迫切需要巩固国家权力并施加经济控制。

- exert: 施加
- waging war: 发动战争
- expensive undertaking: 昂贵的事业
- sovereigns: 君主们
- throughout Europe: 整个欧洲
- realized: 意识到
- pressing need: 迫切需要
- consolidate: 巩固
- state's power: 国家权力
- economic control: 经济控制

3. Despite the pressures exerted by reactionary nobles and clergy on the one hand and discontented sansculottes on the other, the Revolution might not have taken a radical turn if France had remained at peace. (Perry, 2010)

> 尽管一方面反动贵族和神职人员施加压力，另一方面不满的无套裤汉施加压力，但如果法国保持和平，革命可能不会采取激进的转向。

- exerted: 被施加
- despite: 尽管
- pressures: 压力
- reactionary nobles: 反动贵族
- clergy: 神职人员
- on the one hand: 一方面
- discontented sansculottes: 不满的无套裤汉
- on the other: 另一方面
- Revolution: 革命
- might not have taken: 可能不会采取
- radical turn: 激进的转向
- France: 法国
- remained at peace: 保持和平

4. Social pressure, public opinion, fear of a poor reputation, even gossip, can all exert their effects on individual behaviour. (Wolff, 2006)

- exert: 产生
- social pressure: 社会压力
- public opinion: 公众舆论
- fear of a poor reputation: 对坏名声的恐惧
- gossip: 流言蜚语
- effects: 影响
- individual behaviour: 个人行为
