1. Classical rules of style dictate that every poem must exhibit a single characteristic sentiment, such as anger, courage, wonder, or passion. Grief, however—the emotion humans seek to avoid—may not dominate any poem or play. (Fiero, 2011)

> 古典风格规则规定，每首诗必须表现出单一的特征情感，如愤怒、勇气、惊奇或激情。然而，悲伤——人类寻求避免的情感——可能不会主导任何诗歌或戏剧。

- exhibit: 表现
- classical rules of style: 古典风格规则
- dictate that: 规定
- every poem: 每首诗
- must: 必须
- single characteristic sentiment: 单一的特征情感
- such as: 如
- anger: 愤怒
- courage: 勇气
- wonder: 惊奇
- passion: 激情
- grief: 悲伤
- however: 然而
- emotion: 情感
- humans seek to avoid: 人类寻求避免
- may not dominate: 可能不会主导
- poem or play: 诗歌或戏剧

2. In traditional Flemish fashion, the forms exhibit detailed renderings of textures, layers of colors that create brilliant effects, and symbolic forms to enhance the meaning, such as the grapes held by the Christ Child and the attributes of the saints. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在传统的佛兰芒风格中，形式表现出详细的纹理渲染、创造辉煌效果的色彩层次，以及增强意义的象征形式，如基督儿童持有的葡萄和圣徒的属性。

- exhibit: 表现
- traditional Flemish fashion: 传统的佛兰芒风格
- forms: 形式
- detailed renderings: 详细的渲染
- textures: 纹理
- layers of colors: 色彩层次
- create brilliant effects: 创造辉煌效果
- symbolic forms: 象征形式
- enhance the meaning: 增强意义
- grapes: 葡萄
- held by: 由...持有
- Christ Child: 基督儿童
- attributes: 属性
- saints: 圣徒

3. The works of Dürer and Grünewald exhibit the Northern Renaissance passion for realistic detail and graphic expression, while the fantastic imagery of Hieronymus Bosch suggests a pessimistic and typically Northern concern with sin and death. (Fiero, 2011)

> 丢勒和格吕内瓦尔德的作品表现出北方文艺复兴对现实主义细节和图形表达的热情，而希罗尼穆斯·博斯的奇幻意象暗示了对罪恶和死亡的悲观和典型的北方关注。

- exhibit: 表现
- works: 作品
- Dürer: 丢勒
- Grünewald: 格吕内瓦尔德
- Northern Renaissance: 北方文艺复兴
- passion: 热情
- realistic detail: 现实主义细节
- graphic expression: 图形表达
- while: 而
- fantastic imagery: 奇幻意象
- Hieronymus Bosch: 希罗尼穆斯·博斯
- suggests: 暗示
- pessimistic: 悲观的
- typically Northern concern: 典型的北方关注
- sin and death: 罪恶和死亡

4. Contrary to the presumption that most people suffer from low self-esteem or feelings of inferiority, researchers consistently find that most people exhibit a self-serving bias. In experiments and everyday life, we often take credit for our successes while blaming failures on the situation. (Myers, 2012)

> 与大多数人遭受低自尊或自卑感的假设相反，研究人员一致发现大多数人表现出自我服务偏见。在实验和日常生活中，我们经常为成功邀功，同时将失败归咎于情况。

- exhibit: 表现
- contrary to: 与...相反
- presumption: 假设
- most people: 大多数人
- suffer from: 遭受
- low self-esteem: 低自尊
- feelings of inferiority: 自卑感
- researchers: 研究人员
- consistently find: 一致发现
- self-serving bias: 自我服务偏见
- experiments: 实验
- everyday life: 日常生活
- take credit for: 为...邀功
- successes: 成功
- while: 同时
- blaming failures on: 将失败归咎于
- situation: 情况
