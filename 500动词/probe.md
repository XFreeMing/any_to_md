1. The Sophists and the humanist philosophers Socrates, Plato, and Aristotle moved beyond the naturalists to probe the nature of human knowledge, the question of right conduct, and the advance of wisdom. (Fiero, 2011)

> 诡辩家和人文主义哲学家苏格拉底、柏拉图和亚里士多德超越了自然主义者，探索人类知识的本质、正确行为的问题和智慧的进步。

- probe: 探索
- Sophists: 诡辩家
- humanist philosophers: 人文主义哲学家
- Socrates: 苏格拉底
- Plato: 柏拉图
- Aristotle: 亚里士多德
- moved beyond: 超越
- naturalists: 自然主义者
- nature of human knowledge: 人类知识的本质
- question of right conduct: 正确行为的问题
- advance of wisdom: 智慧的进步

2. To the Renaissance passion for realistic representation, Jan introduced the phenomenon of the psychological portrait—the portrait that probed the temperament, character, or unique personality of the subject. (Fiero, 2011)

> 对于文艺复兴对现实主义表现的热情，扬引入了心理肖像的现象——探索主体气质、性格或独特个性的肖像。

- probed: 探索
- Renaissance passion: 文艺复兴的热情
- realistic representation: 现实主义表现
- Jan: 扬
- introduced: 引入
- phenomenon: 现象
- psychological portrait: 心理肖像
- temperament: 气质
- character: 性格
- unique personality: 独特个性
- subject: 主体

3. Greek tragedies dealt with universal themes still relevant in our day. They probed such problems as the nature of good and evil, the conflict between spiritual values and the demands of the state or family, the rights of the individual, the nature of divine forces, and human nature. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 希腊悲剧处理了在我们今天仍然相关的普遍主题。它们探索了诸如善恶的本质、精神价值与国家或家庭要求之间的冲突、个人权利、神圣力量的本质和人性等问题。

- probed: 探索
- Greek tragedies: 希腊悲剧
- dealt with: 处理
- universal themes: 普遍主题
- still relevant: 仍然相关
- our day: 我们今天
- nature of good and evil: 善恶的本质
- conflict between: 在...之间的冲突
- spiritual values: 精神价值
- demands of the state or family: 国家或家庭的要求
- rights of the individual: 个人权利
- nature of divine forces: 神圣力量的本质
- human nature: 人性

4. To many Romantics, music was the most Romantic of the arts because it enabled the composer to probe deeply into human emotions. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 对许多浪漫主义者来说，音乐是最浪漫的艺术，因为它使作曲家能够深入探索人类情感。

- probe: 探索
- many Romantics: 许多浪漫主义者
- music: 音乐
- most Romantic of the arts: 最浪漫的艺术
- because: 因为
- enabled: 使...能够
- composer: 作曲家
- deeply into: 深入
- human emotions: 人类情感
