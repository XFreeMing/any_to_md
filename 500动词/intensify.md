1. In the arid climates of North Africa and the Middle East, for example, the pressure on water supply is acute and shortages have become commonplace. This trend is almost sure to intensify in the years to come. (Giddens, 2009)

> 例如，在北非和中东的干旱气候中，对供水的压力是严重的，短缺已经变得普遍。这种趋势几乎肯定会在未来几年加剧。

- intensify: 加剧
- arid climates: 干旱气候
- North Africa: 北非
- Middle East: 中东
- for example: 例如
- pressure on water supply: 对供水的压力
- acute: 严重的
- shortages: 短缺
- become commonplace: 变得普遍
- trend: 趋势
- almost sure to: 几乎肯定
- in the years to come: 在未来几年

2. Rising worldwide consumption patterns are linked to industrial production in a 'treadmill of production and consumption', but they also intensify environmental damage and tend to exacerbate global inequality. (Giddens, 2009)

> 不断上升的全球消费模式与"生产和消费的跑步机"中的工业生产相关，但它们也加剧了环境损害，并倾向于加剧全球不平等。

- intensify: 加剧
- rising worldwide consumption patterns: 不断上升的全球消费模式
- linked to: 与...相关
- industrial production: 工业生产
- treadmill of production and consumption: 生产和消费的跑步机
- also: 也
- environmental damage: 环境损害
- tend to: 倾向于
- exacerbate: 加剧
- global inequality: 全球不平等

3. Marx predicted how capitalism would be destroyed. Periodic unemployment would increase the misery of the workers and intensify their hatred of capitalists. (Perry, 2010)

> 马克思预测了资本主义将如何被摧毁。周期性失业将增加工人的痛苦并加剧他们对资本家的仇恨。

- intensify: 加剧
- Marx: 马克思
- predicted: 预测
- how capitalism would be destroyed: 资本主义将如何被摧毁
- periodic unemployment: 周期性失业
- increase: 增加
- misery of the workers: 工人的痛苦
- their hatred: 他们的仇恨
- capitalists: 资本家

4. In everyday situations, too, group interaction tends to intensify opinions. This group polarization phenomenon provided a window through which researchers could observe group influence. (Myers, 2012)

> 在日常情况下，群体互动也倾向于加剧意见。这种群体极化现象提供了一个窗口，研究人员可以通过它观察群体影响。

- intensify: 加剧
- everyday situations: 日常情况
- too: 也
- group interaction: 群体互动
- tends to: 倾向于
- opinions: 意见
- group polarization phenomenon: 群体极化现象
- provided: 提供
- window: 窗口
- through which: 通过它
- researchers: 研究人员
- could observe: 可以观察
- group influence: 群体影响
