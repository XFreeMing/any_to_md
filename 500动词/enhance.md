1. Heavy walls created solid and durable structures that conveyed a sense of enclosure and security, and the stone vaults covering these buildings enhanced their stability. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 厚重的墙壁创造了坚固耐用的结构，传达了封闭和安全感，覆盖这些建筑物的石拱顶增强了它们的稳定性。

- enhanced: 增强
- heavy walls: 厚重的墙壁
- created: 创造
- solid and durable structures: 坚固耐用的结构
- conveyed: 传达
- sense of enclosure: 封闭感
- security: 安全感
- stone vaults: 石拱顶
- covering: 覆盖
- these buildings: 这些建筑物
- their stability: 它们的稳定性

2. Cool, bright colors and even lighting enhance the elegiac mood, while sharp contours and the sure use of line provide absolute clarity of design. (Fiero, 2011)

> 凉爽、明亮的色彩和均匀的照明增强了挽歌的情绪，而清晰的轮廓和线条的确定使用提供了设计的绝对清晰度。

- enhance: 增强
- cool, bright colors: 凉爽、明亮的色彩
- even lighting: 均匀的照明
- elegiac mood: 挽歌的情绪
- while: 而
- sharp contours: 清晰的轮廓
- sure use of line: 线条的确定使用
- provide: 提供
- absolute clarity: 绝对清晰度
- design: 设计

3. A dynamic corporate culture - involving company events, rituals and traditions - is thought to enhance employee loyalty and promote group solidarity. (Giddens, 2009)

> 动态的企业文化——涉及公司活动、仪式和传统——被认为可以增强员工忠诚度并促进群体团结。

- enhance: 增强
- dynamic corporate culture: 动态的企业文化
- involving: 涉及
- company events: 公司活动
- rituals: 仪式
- traditions: 传统
- is thought to: 被认为可以
- employee loyalty: 员工忠诚度
- promote: 促进
- group solidarity: 群体团结

4. Good feelings often enhance persuasion, partly by enhancing positive thinking and partly by linking good feelings with the message. (Myers, 2012)

> 良好的感觉经常增强说服力，部分通过增强积极思维，部分通过将良好感觉与信息联系起来。

- enhance: 增强
- good feelings: 良好的感觉
- often: 经常
- persuasion: 说服力
- partly by: 部分通过
- enhancing: 增强
- positive thinking: 积极思维
- linking: 联系
- with the message: 与信息
