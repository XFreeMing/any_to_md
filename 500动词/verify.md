1. We work at managing the impressions we create. We excuse, justify, or apologize as necessary to shore up our self-esteem and verify our self-images. (Myers, 2012)

> 我们努力管理我们创造的印象。我们在必要时找借口、证明或道歉，以支撑我们的自尊并验证我们的自我形象。

- verify: 验证
- work at: 努力
- managing: 管理
- impressions: 印象
- create: 创造
- excuse: 找借口
- justify: 证明
- apologize: 道歉
- as necessary: 在必要时
- shore up: 支撑
- self-esteem: 自尊
- self-images: 自我形象

2. This scientific method began with systematic observations and experiments, which were used to arrive at general concepts. New deductions derived from these general concepts could then be tested and verified by precise experiments. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 这种科学方法从系统的观察和实验开始，这些被用来得出一般概念。从这些一般概念推导出的新推论然后可以通过精确的实验进行测试和验证。

- verified: 被验证
- scientific method: 科学方法
- began with: 从...开始
- systematic observations: 系统的观察
- experiments: 实验
- used to: 被用来
- arrive at: 得出
- general concepts: 一般概念
- new deductions: 新推论
- derived from: 从...推导出
- could then be tested: 然后可以被测试
- precise experiments: 精确的实验

3. Rather, experiments enable us to formulate and verify powerful theoretical principles, such as the reciprocity norm and the self-serving bias. (Myers, 2012)

> 相反，实验使我们能够制定和验证强大的理论原则，如互惠规范和自我服务偏见。

- verify: 验证
- rather: 相反
- experiments: 实验
- enable us to: 使我们能够
- formulate: 制定
- powerful theoretical principles: 强大的理论原则
- reciprocity norm: 互惠规范
- self-serving bias: 自我服务偏见

4. Still, even with these advanced detection methods, not all experts agree about the authenticity of a given work since these technologies can often only verify the time or place a work was produced and not the artists hand. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 尽管如此，即使有了这些先进的检测方法，并非所有专家都同意给定作品的真实性，因为这些技术通常只能验证作品产生的时间或地点，而不能验证艺术家的手。

- verify: 验证
- still: 尽管如此
- even with: 即使有了
- advanced detection methods: 先进的检测方法
- not all experts: 并非所有专家
- agree about: 同意
- authenticity: 真实性
- given work: 给定作品
- since: 因为
- technologies: 技术
- often only: 通常只能
- time or place: 时间或地点
- work was produced: 作品产生
- artists hand: 艺术家的手
