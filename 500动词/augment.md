1. The original Arab adherents of Islam were eventually augmented by Berbers, Visigoths, Turks, and Persians. The cultural complexity of today's Islamic world existed almost from the beginning. (Davies, et al., 2011)

> 伊斯兰教最初的阿拉伯信徒最终被柏柏尔人、西哥特人、土耳其人和波斯人增补。今天伊斯兰世界的文化复杂性几乎从一开始就存在。

- augmented: 增补
- original Arab adherents: 最初的阿拉伯信徒
- of Islam: 伊斯兰教的
- eventually: 最终
- Berbers: 柏柏尔人
- Visigoths: 西哥特人
- Turks: 土耳其人
- Persians: 波斯人
- cultural complexity: 文化复杂性
- today's Islamic world: 今天伊斯兰世界
- existed: 存在
- almost from the beginning: 几乎从一开始

2. When budget allowed, he augmented this refinement by using luxurious materials, such as expensive marble and travertine, bronze, chrome, and tinted glass. (Davies, et al., 2011)

> 当预算允许时，他通过使用豪华材料来增强这种精致，如昂贵的大理石和石灰华、青铜、铬和有色玻璃。

- augmented: 增强
- when budget allowed: 当预算允许时
- refinement: 精致
- by using: 通过使用
- luxurious materials: 豪华材料
- such as: 如
- expensive marble: 昂贵的大理石
- travertine: 石灰华
- bronze: 青铜
- chrome: 铬
- tinted glass: 有色玻璃

- augment: 增强
- if he were born: 如果他生来
- courageous: 勇敢的
- would he not seek: 他难道不会寻求
- natural courage: 天生的勇气
- with the art of defense: 用防御艺术
