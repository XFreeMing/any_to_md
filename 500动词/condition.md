1. The German physiologist Wilhelm Wundt (1832 - 1920) transformed psychology from a philosophy to a natural science by basing it on scientific method, which he detailed in his 1874 Principles of Physiological Psychology. Meanwhile, the research of the Russian Ivan Pavlov (1849 - 1936) sparked an intense interest in how human behavior is conditioned by experience and environment. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 德国生理学家威廉·冯特（1832-1920）通过将心理学建立在科学方法的基础上，将心理学从哲学转变为自然科学，他在1874年的《生理心理学原理》中详细阐述了这一点。与此同时，俄罗斯人伊万·巴甫洛夫（1849-1936）的研究引发了对人类行为如何受经验和环境制约的强烈兴趣。

2. The mother everywhere is normally the most important individual in the child's early life, but the nature of the relationships established between mothers and their children is influenced by the form and regularity of their contact. This is, in turn, conditioned by the character of family institutions and their relation to other groups in society. (Giddens, 2009)

3. Both the United States and the Soviet Union took steps at the end of World War II that were unwise or might have been avoided. Both nations, however, were working within a framework conditioned by the past. Ultimately, the rivalry between the two superpowers stemmed from their different historical perspectives and their irreconcilable political ambitions. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 美国和苏联都在第二次世界大战结束时采取了不明智或本可以避免的步骤。然而，两个国家都在受过去制约的框架内工作。最终，两个超级大国之间的竞争源于他们不同的历史观点和不可调和的政治野心。
