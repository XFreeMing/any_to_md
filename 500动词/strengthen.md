1. This first emperor of China could command the creation of a Terracotta Army to guard his tomb in the city of Xi'an (西安), as well as a realignment of the Great Wall of China to strengthen his empire against insurrection, invasion, and incursion. ("Army," 2018)

> 中国的第一位皇帝可以命令创建兵马俑来守卫他在西安市的陵墓，以及重新调整中国长城以加强他的帝国对抗叛乱、入侵和侵犯。

- strengthen: 加强
- first emperor of China: 中国的第一位皇帝
- command: 命令
- creation: 创建
- Terracotta Army: 兵马俑
- guard: 守卫
- tomb: 陵墓
- city of Xi'an: 西安市
- realignment: 重新调整
- Great Wall of China: 中国长城
- his empire: 他的帝国
- against: 对抗
- insurrection: 叛乱
- invasion: 入侵
- incursion: 侵犯

2. The rebuilding of the Temple was encouraged by the leaders of the empire in hopes that it would strengthen the authorities in local contexts. ("Book of Zechariah," 2018)

> 圣殿的重建受到帝国领导人的鼓励，希望它能加强地方背景下的权威。

- strengthen: 加强
- rebuilding: 重建
- Temple: 圣殿
- encouraged by: 受到...鼓励
- leaders of the empire: 帝国领导人
- in hopes that: 希望
- authorities: 权威
- local contexts: 地方背景

3. It was important not to suffer a defeat, for that would confirm and strengthen this instinctive fear. ("Abu Bakr," 2018)

> 重要的是不要遭受失败，因为那会确认并加强这种本能的恐惧。

- strengthen: 加强
- important: 重要的
- not to suffer: 不要遭受
- defeat: 失败
- for: 因为
- would confirm: 会确认
- instinctive fear: 本能的恐惧

- strengthen: 加强
