1. The Reformation's stress on individual conscience may have contributed to the development of the capitalist spirit, which underlies modern economic life. (Perry, 2010)

> 宗教改革对个人良心的强调可能促进了资本主义精神的发展，这是现代经济生活的基础。

- underlies: 是...的基础
- Reformation's stress: 宗教改革的强调
- individual conscience: 个人良心
- contributed to: 促进
- development: 发展
- capitalist spirit: 资本主义精神
- modern economic life: 现代经济生活

2. Try to figure out what feeling underlies your anger, find out what's based upon it and coordinate your emotion.

> 试着找出什么感觉是你愤怒的基础，找出它基于什么，并协调你的情绪。

- underlies: 是...的基础
- figure out: 找出
- feeling: 感觉
- anger: 愤怒
- find out: 找出
- based upon: 基于
- coordinate: 协调
- emotion: 情绪

3. According to de Gobineau, the white race possesses superior intelligence, morality and will-power, and it is these inherited qualities that underlie the spread of Western influence across the world. (Giddens, 2009)

> 根据德·戈比诺的说法，白种人拥有优越的智力、道德和意志力，正是这些遗传品质构成了西方影响在世界范围内传播的基础。

- underlie: 构成...的基础
- according to: 根据
- de Gobineau: 德·戈比诺
- white race: 白种人
- possesses: 拥有
- superior intelligence: 优越的智力
- morality: 道德
- will-power: 意志力
- inherited qualities: 遗传品质
- spread: 传播
- Western influence: 西方影响
- across the world: 在世界范围内

4. Recent research shows how the stereotyping that underlies prejudice is a by-product of our thinking——our ways of simplifying the world. (Giddens, 2009)

> 最近的研究表明，构成偏见基础的刻板印象是我们思维的副产品——我们简化世界的方式。

- underlies: 构成...的基础
- recent research: 最近的研究
- shows: 表明
- stereotyping: 刻板印象
- prejudice: 偏见
- by-product: 副产品
- thinking: 思维
- ways: 方式
- simplifying: 简化
- world: 世界
