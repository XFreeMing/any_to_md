1. One medieval bishop argued that seeing animals sculpted in churches would so terrify parishioners that they would be encouraged to refrain from sinful deeds. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 一位中世纪主教认为，看到教堂中雕刻的动物会如此恐吓教区居民，以至于他们会被鼓励避免罪恶行为。

- refrain from: 避免
- medieval bishop: 中世纪主教
- argued that: 认为
- seeing animals sculpted: 看到雕刻的动物
- churches: 教堂
- terrify: 恐吓
- parishioners: 教区居民
- encouraged to: 被鼓励
- sinful deeds: 罪恶行为

2. Most religions, in fact, encourage us to take responsibility for ourselves but to refrain from judging others. (Myers, 2012)

> 事实上，大多数宗教鼓励我们对自己负责，但要避免评判他人。

- refrain from: 避免
- most religions: 大多数宗教
- in fact: 事实上
- encourage us to: 鼓励我们
- take responsibility: 负责
- ourselves: 自己
- judging others: 评判他人

3. Most of us obey the laws against murder without a second thought. If we were asked why we refrain from killing people most of us would surely answer that the idea of doing so has never entered our heads as a serious option. (Wolff, 2006)

> 我们大多数人毫不犹豫地遵守反对谋杀的法律。如果我们被问为什么我们避免杀人，我们大多数人肯定会回答说，这样做的想法从未作为严肃的选择进入我们的头脑。

- refrain from: 避免
- most of us: 我们大多数人
- obey the laws: 遵守法律
- against murder: 反对谋杀
- without a second thought: 毫不犹豫
- if we were asked: 如果我们被问
- why: 为什么
- killing people: 杀人
- surely answer: 肯定会回答
- idea: 想法
- never entered our heads: 从未进入我们的头脑
- serious option: 严肃的选择

4. So it is wise to refrain from planting false, unreachable expectations in people's minds.

> 因此，避免在人们心中植入虚假、无法达到的期望是明智的。

- refrain from: 避免
- so: 因此
- wise to: 明智的
- planting: 植入
- false: 虚假的
- unreachable expectations: 无法达到的期望
- people's minds: 人们的心中
