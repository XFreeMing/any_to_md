1. In The Age of Reason (1794–1795), Paine declared: "I believe in the equality of man; and I believe that religious duties consist in doing justice, loving mercy, and endeavoring to make our fellow- creatures happy." (Perry, 2010)

> 在《理性时代》（1794-1795）中，潘恩宣称："我相信人的平等；我相信宗教职责在于行正义、爱仁慈，并努力使我们的同胞幸福。"

- endeavoring: 努力
- The Age of Reason: 《理性时代》
- Paine: 潘恩
- declared: 宣称
- believe in: 相信
- equality of man: 人的平等
- religious duties: 宗教职责
- consist in: 在于
- doing justice: 行正义
- loving mercy: 爱仁慈
- make our fellow-creatures happy: 使我们的同胞幸福

2. "I have endeavored," he wrote, "to treat my subjects as a dramatic writer: my picture is my stage, and men and women my actors." (Fiero, 2011)

> "我努力，"他写道，"像戏剧作家一样对待我的主题：我的画是我的舞台，男人和女人是我的演员。"

- endeavored: 努力
- wrote: 写道
- treat: 对待
- subjects: 主题
- dramatic writer: 戏剧作家
- picture: 画
- stage: 舞台
- men and women: 男人和女人
- actors: 演员

3. I hope it is not a partisan treatment; as usual I endeavored to cover the major perspectives in sociology and the major findings of contemporary research in an even-handed, though not indiscriminate, way. (Giddens, 2009)

> 我希望这不是一种党派性的处理；像往常一样，我努力以公正但不加区别的方式涵盖社会学的主要观点和当代研究的主要发现。

- endeavored: 努力
- hope: 希望
- partisan treatment: 党派性的处理
- as usual: 像往常一样
- cover: 涵盖
- major perspectives: 主要观点
- sociology: 社会学
- major findings: 主要发现
- contemporary research: 当代研究
- even-handed: 公正的
- though not indiscriminate: 虽然不加区别
- way: 方式

4. The warrior pope Julius II endeavored to add to the splendor of his pontificate by tearing down an old church and beginning construction of what was to be the greatest building in Christendom, Saint Peter's Basilica. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 战士教皇尤利乌斯二世努力通过拆除一座旧教堂并开始建造将成为基督教世界最伟大建筑的圣彼得大教堂来增加他教皇任期的辉煌。

- endeavored: 努力
- warrior pope: 战士教皇
- Julius II: 尤利乌斯二世
- add to: 增加
- splendor: 辉煌
- pontificate: 教皇任期
- tearing down: 拆除
- old church: 旧教堂
- beginning construction: 开始建造
- greatest building: 最伟大的建筑
- Christendom: 基督教世界
- Saint Peter's Basilica: 圣彼得大教堂
