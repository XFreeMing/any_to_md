1. After the customer agrees to buy a new car because of its bargain price and begins completing the sales forms, the salesperson removes the price advantage by charging for options or by checking with a boss who disallows the deal because "we'd be losing money." (Giddens, 2009)

> 在客户因为便宜的价格同意购买新车并开始填写销售表格后，销售人员通过收取选项费用或与老板核对来消除价格优势，老板不允许这笔交易，因为"我们会亏钱"。

- disallows: 不允许
- after: 在...后
- customer: 客户
- agrees to buy: 同意购买
- new car: 新车
- because of: 因为
- bargain price: 便宜的价格
- begins completing: 开始填写
- sales forms: 销售表格
- salesperson: 销售人员
- removes: 消除
- price advantage: 价格优势
- by charging for: 通过收取...费用
- options: 选项
- checking with: 与...核对
- boss: 老板
- deal: 交易
- losing money: 亏钱

2. This justification drew on a basic moral intuition (as well as on scripture) that there is something deeply wrong with harming the weak (people), which the Christian duty of mercy disallowed in a novel and compelling way. (Beauchamp & Frey, 2014)

> 这种理由基于一种基本的道德直觉（以及经文），即伤害弱者（人）有某种深刻的错误，基督教的仁慈义务以一种新颖而令人信服的方式不允许这样做。

- disallowed: 不允许
- justification: 理由
- drew on: 基于
- basic moral intuition: 基本的道德直觉
- as well as on scripture: 以及经文
- something deeply wrong: 某种深刻的错误
- harming the weak: 伤害弱者
- people: 人
- Christian duty of mercy: 基督教的仁慈义务
- novel and compelling way: 新颖而令人信服的方式

3. As we have seen, the Court considered pertinent social science evidence when disallowing five-member juries and ending school desegregation. (Myers, 2012)

> 正如我们所看到的，法院在不允许五人陪审团和结束学校种族隔离时考虑了相关的社会科学证据。

- disallowing: 不允许
- as we have seen: 正如我们所看到的
- Court: 法院
- considered: 考虑
- pertinent social science evidence: 相关的社会科学证据
- five-member juries: 五人陪审团
- ending: 结束
- school desegregation: 学校种族隔离
