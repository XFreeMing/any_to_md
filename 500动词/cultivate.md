1. Although, according to Castiglione, the goal of the ideal gentleman was to cultivate his full potential as a human being, such was not the case with the Renaissance gentlewoman. (Fiero, 2011)

> 虽然，根据卡斯蒂廖内，理想绅士的目标是培养他作为人的全部潜力，但文艺复兴时期的淑女并非如此。

- cultivate: 培养
- although: 虽然
- according to: 根据
- Castiglione: 卡斯蒂廖内
- goal: 目标
- ideal gentleman: 理想绅士
- full potential: 全部潜力
- as a human being: 作为人
- such was not the case: 并非如此
- Renaissance gentlewoman: 文艺复兴时期的淑女

2. The city-states of northern Italy that spawned the Renaissance were developed urban centers, where people had the wealth, freedom, and inclination to cultivate the arts and to enjoy the fruits of worldly life. (Perry, 2010)

> 催生文艺复兴的意大利北部城邦是发达的都市中心，那里的人们拥有财富、自由和培养艺术并享受世俗生活成果的倾向。

- cultivate: 培养
- city-states: 城邦
- northern Italy: 意大利北部
- spawned: 催生
- Renaissance: 文艺复兴
- developed urban centers: 发达的都市中心
- people: 人们
- wealth: 财富
- freedom: 自由
- inclination: 倾向
- the arts: 艺术
- enjoy: 享受
- fruits: 成果
- worldly life: 世俗生活

3. Most people, presumably, would rather cultivate their own plot than invade their neighbour's, and so we can expect a relatively peaceful climate and few sources of quarrel. (Wolff, 2006)

> 大多数人，大概，宁愿培养自己的地块而不是入侵邻居的，因此我们可以期待相对和平的气候和很少的争吵来源。

- cultivate: 培养
- most people: 大多数人
- presumably: 大概
- would rather: 宁愿
- their own plot: 自己的地块
- than: 而不是
- invade: 入侵
- neighbour's: 邻居的
- so: 因此
- expect: 期待
- relatively peaceful climate: 相对和平的气候
- few sources of quarrel: 很少的争吵来源

4. The perfect courtier must also cultivate certain achievements. Primarily, he should participate in military and bodily exercises because the principal profession of a courtier was arms. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 完美的朝臣还必须培养某些成就。首先，他应该参加军事和身体锻炼，因为朝臣的主要职业是武器。

- cultivate: 培养
- perfect courtier: 完美的朝臣
- must also: 还必须
- certain achievements: 某些成就
- primarily: 首先
- participate in: 参加
- military and bodily exercises: 军事和身体锻炼
- because: 因为
- principal profession: 主要职业
- courtier: 朝臣
- arms: 武器
