1. Familiarize yourself again with table and reflect on what questions might follow from this. (Giddens, 2009)

> 再次熟悉表格，并思考可能由此产生的问题。

- familiarize: 熟悉
- yourself: 你自己
- again: 再次
- table: 表格
- reflect on: 思考
- questions: 问题
- might follow from this: 可能由此产生

2. Use the time to familiarize yourself with the environment and calm down.

> 利用这段时间熟悉环境并冷静下来。

- familiarize: 熟悉
- yourself: 你自己
- use the time: 利用这段时间
- environment: 环境
- calm down: 冷静下来

3. Employees must familiarize themselves with the health and safety manual. (Giddens, 2009)

> 员工必须熟悉健康和安全手册。

- familiarize: 熟悉
- employees: 员工
- must: 必须
- themselves: 他们自己
- health and safety manual: 健康和安全手册

4. The goal of the experiment was to familiarize the people with the new laws.  (Goodwin, 1976)

> 实验的目标是让人民熟悉新法律。

- familiarize: 使熟悉
- goal: 目标
- experiment: 实验
- people: 人民
- new laws: 新法律
