1. Seers, purported to have supernatural skills, might offer advice but could not override the rulings of the Assembly. (Perry, 2010)

> 据称拥有超自然技能的预言家可能会提供建议，但不能推翻议会的裁决。

- purported to: 据称
- seers: 预言家
- supernatural skills: 超自然技能
- might offer advice: 可能会提供建议
- could not override: 不能推翻
- rulings: 裁决
- Assembly: 议会

2. Accordingly, a state which purports to exercise political power over me, but which does not have my consent, has no right to govern and hence is illegitimate. (Wolff, 2006)

> 因此，一个声称对我行使政治权力但没有我同意的国家没有治理权，因此是不合法的。

- purports to: 声称
- accordingly: 因此
- state: 国家
- exercise political power: 行使政治权力
- over me: 对我
- does not have my consent: 没有我的同意
- no right to govern: 没有治理权
- hence: 因此
- illegitimate: 不合法的
