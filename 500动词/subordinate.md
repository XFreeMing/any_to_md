1. In fact rulers and their dynasties now came under pressure to demonstrate that they were patriotic and prepared to serve the state they ruled, and even to subordinate merely dynastic concerns to the wellbeing of the state more clearly than in the past. (Scott, 2018)

> 事实上，统治者及其王朝现在面临压力，要证明他们是爱国的，准备为他们统治的国家服务，甚至比过去更清楚地将仅仅王朝的关注从属于国家的福祉。

- subordinate: 从属于

- rulers: 统治者
- dynasties: 王朝
- came under pressure: 面临压力
- demonstrate: 证明
- patriotic: 爱国的
- prepared to serve: 准备服务
- state they ruled: 他们统治的国家
- even: 甚至
- merely dynastic concerns: 仅仅王朝的关注
- wellbeing of the state: 国家的福祉
- more clearly than: 比...更清楚
- in the past: 过去

2. Not all religions anthropomorphize the divine, but many do. Symbolically constructing a divine order that mirrors a society's gender structure, many religions recognize male and female deities. Gods and goddesses, or divinities, are the great and more remote supernatural beings. Generally speaking, cultures that subordinate women to men attribute masculine gender to the more powerful gods or supreme deity. (Haviland, Prins, McBride, & Walrath, 2013)

> 并非所有宗教都将神圣人格化，但许多宗教确实如此。象征性地构建反映社会性别结构的神圣秩序，许多宗教承认男性和女性神祇。神和女神，或神性，是伟大和更遥远的超自然存在。一般来说，将妇女从属于男人的文化将男性性别归因于更强大的神或至高神。

- subordinate: 从属于
- not all religions: 并非所有宗教
- anthropomorphize: 人格化
- divine: 神圣的
- symbolically constructing: 象征性地构建
- divine order: 神圣秩序
- mirrors: 反映
- society's gender structure: 社会的性别结构
- recognize: 承认
- male and female deities: 男性和女性神祇
- gods and goddesses: 神和女神
- divinities: 神性
- great and more remote: 伟大和更遥远的
- supernatural beings: 超自然存在
- generally speaking: 一般来说
- cultures: 文化
- women to men: 妇女到男人
- attribute: 归因
- masculine gender: 男性性别
- more powerful gods: 更强大的神
- supreme deity: 至高神

- subordinating: 从属于
- idea: 观念
- importance of the group: 群体的重要性
- ego: 自我
- group: 群体
- accepting: 接受
- one's fate: 自己的命运
- part of the group: 群体的一部分
- so-called: 所谓的
- collectivism: 集体主义
