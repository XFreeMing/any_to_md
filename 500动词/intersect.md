1. Lines of latitude circle the globe east to west and intersect lines of longitude. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 纬线从东到西环绕地球，并与经线相交。

- intersect: 相交
- lines of latitude: 纬线
- circle: 环绕
- globe: 地球
- east to west: 从东到西
- lines of longitude: 经线

2. A central feature of bureaucracies, for example, was adherence to a defined set of responsibilities and procedures for carrying them out. Weber's view of bureaucracy was that of a self-contained unit that intersected with outside entities at limited and designated points. (Giddens, 2009)

> 例如，官僚机构的一个核心特征是遵守一套明确的责任和执行程序。韦伯对官僚机构的看法是一个自给自足的单位，在有限和指定的点与外部实体相交。

- intersected with: 与...相交
- central feature: 核心特征
- bureaucracies: 官僚机构
- for example: 例如
- adherence to: 遵守
- defined set: 明确的一套
- responsibilities: 责任
- procedures: 程序
- carrying them out: 执行它们
- Weber's view: 韦伯的看法
- bureaucracy: 官僚机构
- self-contained unit: 自给自足的单位
- outside entities: 外部实体
- limited and designated points: 有限和指定的点

3. The orbit of this comet intersects the orbit of the Earth.

> 这颗彗星的轨道与地球的轨道相交。

- intersects: 相交
- orbit: 轨道
- comet: 彗星
- Earth: 地球

4. While all societies have included a transgendered subculture, it was not until the last decades of the twentieth century that sexual and public issues intersected to produce some highly controversial questions. (Fiero, 2011)

> 虽然所有社会都包含跨性别亚文化，但直到20世纪的最后几十年，性和公共问题才相交，产生了一些高度有争议的问题。

- intersected: 相交
- while: 虽然
- all societies: 所有社会
- included: 包含
- transgendered subculture: 跨性别亚文化
- not until: 直到...才
- last decades: 最后几十年
- twentieth century: 20世纪
- sexual issues: 性问题
- public issues: 公共问题
- produce: 产生
- highly controversial questions: 高度有争议的问题
