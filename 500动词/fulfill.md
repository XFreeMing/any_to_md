1. However, today's computerized forms of communication, such as Internet websites and web-logs, empower individuals to respond to mass advertising. (Fiero, 2011)

> 然而，今天的计算机化通信形式，如互联网网站和网络日志，使个人能够回应大众广告。

- fulfill: 实现（注意：这个例句似乎更适合empower，但根据文件内容，我按fulfill处理）
- however: 然而
- today's computerized forms: 今天的计算机化形式
- of communication: 通信的
- such as: 如
- Internet websites: 互联网网站
- web-logs: 网络日志
- individuals: 个人
- respond to: 回应
- mass advertising: 大众广告

2. They were expected to carry and bear their children and at the same time fulfill their obligation to labor in the fields. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 她们被期望携带和生育孩子，同时履行在田间劳动的义务。

- fulfill: 履行
- they: 她们
- expected to: 被期望
- carry and bear: 携带和生育
- their children: 她们的孩子
- at the same time: 同时
- obligation: 义务
- labor in the fields: 在田间劳动

3. The antihero was an important new kind of hero, one who established personal moral codes and followed personal passions to attain freedom and fulfill individual needs. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 反英雄是一种重要的新型英雄，他建立个人道德准则并遵循个人激情来获得自由并满足个人需求。

- fulfill: 满足
- antihero: 反英雄
- important new kind: 重要的新型
- hero: 英雄
- established: 建立
- personal moral codes: 个人道德准则
- followed: 遵循
- personal passions: 个人激情
- attain freedom: 获得自由
- individual needs: 个人需求

4. He believed that color, like music, could fulfill a spiritual goal of appealing directly to the human senses. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 他相信颜色，像音乐一样，可以实现直接吸引人类感官的精神目标。

- fulfill: 实现
- he believed that: 他相信
- color: 颜色
- like music: 像音乐一样
- could: 可以
- spiritual goal: 精神目标
- appealing directly to: 直接吸引
- human senses: 人类感官
