1. Classical Greek art did not aim at experimentation for experiment's sake but was concerned with expressing eternally true ideals. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 古典希腊艺术不是为了实验而实验，而是关注表达永恒真实的理想。

- concerned with: 关注
- Classical Greek art: 古典希腊艺术
- did not aim at: 不是为了
- experimentation: 实验
- for experiment's sake: 为了实验而实验
- expressing: 表达
- eternally true ideals: 永恒真实的理想

2. During their Classical period, the Greeks raised and debated fundamental questions about the purpose of human existence, the structure of human society, and the nature of the universe that have concerned Western thinkers ever since. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在他们的古典时期，希腊人提出并辩论了关于人类存在的目的、人类社会的结构和宇宙本质的基本问题，这些问题自那时起一直困扰着西方思想家。

- concerned: 困扰
- during: 在...期间
- Classical period: 古典时期
- Greeks: 希腊人
- raised: 提出
- debated: 辩论
- fundamental questions: 基本问题
- purpose: 目的
- human existence: 人类存在
- structure: 结构
- human society: 人类社会
- nature: 本质
- universe: 宇宙
- Western thinkers: 西方思想家
- ever since: 自那时起

3. Early Greek philosophers were concerned with the development of critical or rational thought about the nature of the universe and the place of divine forces in it. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 早期希腊哲学家关注关于宇宙本质和神圣力量在其中位置的关键或理性思想的发展。

- concerned with: 关注
- early Greek philosophers: 早期希腊哲学家
- development: 发展
- critical or rational thought: 关键或理性思想
- nature of the universe: 宇宙本质
- place: 位置
- divine forces: 神圣力量
- in it: 在其中

4. Like Epicureanism, Stoicism was concerned with how individuals find happiness. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 像伊壁鸠鲁主义一样，斯多葛主义关注个人如何找到幸福。

- concerned with: 关注
- like: 像
- Epicureanism: 伊壁鸠鲁主义
- Stoicism: 斯多葛主义
- how: 如何
- individuals: 个人
- find: 找到
- happiness: 幸福
