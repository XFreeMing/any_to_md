1. It (logical discussion) forced people out of their apathy and smugness and compelled them to examine their thoughts critically; to confront illogical, inconsistent, dogmatic, and imprecise assertions; and to express their ideas in clearly defined terms. (Perry, 2010)

> 它（逻辑讨论）迫使人们摆脱冷漠和自满，迫使他们批判性地审视他们的思想；面对不合逻辑、不一致、教条和不精确的断言；并用明确定义的术语表达他们的想法。

- examine: 审视
- logical discussion: 逻辑讨论
- forced people out of: 迫使人们摆脱
- apathy: 冷漠
- smugness: 自满
- compelled them to: 迫使他们
- thoughts: 思想
- critically: 批判性地
- confront: 面对
- illogical: 不合逻辑的
- inconsistent: 不一致的
- dogmatic: 教条的
- imprecise assertions: 不精确的断言
- express: 表达
- ideas: 想法
- clearly defined terms: 明确定义的术语

2. For Durkheim, the main intellectual concern of sociology is the study of social facts. Rather than applying sociological methods to the study of individuals, sociologists should instead examine social facts —— aspects of social life that shape our actions as individuals, such as the state of the economy or the influence of religion. (Giddens, 2009)

> 对涂尔干来说，社会学的主要知识关注是对社会事实的研究。与其将社会学方法应用于个人研究，社会学家应该审视社会事实——塑造我们作为个人行动的社会生活方面，如经济状况或宗教的影响。

- examine: 审视
- Durkheim: 涂尔干
- main intellectual concern: 主要知识关注
- sociology: 社会学
- study of social facts: 对社会事实的研究
- rather than: 与其
- applying sociological methods: 应用社会学方法
- study of individuals: 个人研究
- sociologists: 社会学家
- should instead: 应该
- aspects of social life: 社会生活方面
- shape our actions: 塑造我们的行动
- individuals: 个人
- state of the economy: 经济状况
- influence of religion: 宗教的影响

> 从研究疾病如何影响个人，我们现在转向审视社会内的疾病和健康模式，并讨论健康结果在社会群体之间的差异。

- examine: 审视
- from studying: 从研究
- illness affects: 疾病影响
- individual: 个人
- turn to: 转向
- patterns: 模式
- illness and health: 疾病和健康
- within society: 社会内
- discuss: 讨论
- health outcomes: 健康结果
- differ between: 在...之间差异
- social groups: 社会群体
