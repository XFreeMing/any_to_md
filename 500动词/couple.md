1. The Athenian pursuit of democracy at home was coupled with increasing imperialism abroad. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 雅典在国内追求民主与在国外日益增长的帝国主义相结合。

- coupled with: 与...相结合
- Athenian pursuit: 雅典的追求
- democracy: 民主
- at home: 在国内
- increasing imperialism: 日益增长的帝国主义
- abroad: 在国外

2. Freedom of speech and the press were coupled with the outlawing of arbitrary arrests. It's just liked a coin with two sides. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 言论和新闻自由与禁止任意逮捕相结合。就像一枚硬币有两面。

- coupled with: 与...相结合
- freedom of speech: 言论自由
- press: 新闻
- outlawing: 禁止
- arbitrary arrests: 任意逮捕
- coin with two sides: 一枚硬币有两面

3. The increased demand for white-collar workers at relatively low wages coupled with a shortage of male workers led employers to hire women. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 对相对低工资白领工人需求的增加与男性工人的短缺相结合，导致雇主雇用妇女。

- coupled with: 与...相结合
- increased demand: 增加的需求
- white-collar workers: 白领工人
- relatively low wages: 相对低工资
- shortage: 短缺
- male workers: 男性工人
- led: 导致
- employers: 雇主
- hire women: 雇用妇女

4. All citizens of a polis possessed basic rights, but these were coupled with responsibilities. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 城邦的所有公民都拥有基本权利，但这些权利与责任相结合。

- coupled with: 与...相结合
- all citizens: 所有公民
- polis: 城邦
- possessed: 拥有
- basic rights: 基本权利
- responsibilities: 责任
