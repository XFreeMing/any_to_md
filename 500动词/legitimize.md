1. To Bytheway, the categories we use to describe ageing such as 'the elderly' and 'the old' - are themselves ageist. They are socially constructed in order to legitimize the separation and management of people on the basis of their chronological age by dominant groups with something to gain from the inequalities associated with ageism. (Giddens, 2009)

> 对拜特韦来说，我们用来描述老龄化的类别，如"老年人"和"老人"——本身就是年龄歧视的。它们是为了使占主导地位的群体基于人们的实际年龄对人们进行分离和管理合法化而社会建构的，这些群体从与年龄歧视相关的不平等中获得某些东西。

- legitimize: 使...合法化
- Bytheway: 拜特韦
- categories: 类别
- describe ageing: 描述老龄化
- elderly: 老年人
- old: 老人
- ageist: 年龄歧视的
- socially constructed: 社会建构的
- separation: 分离
- management: 管理
- people: 人们
- basis: 基础
- chronological age: 实际年龄
- dominant groups: 占主导地位的群体
- something to gain: 某些东西获得
- inequalities: 不平等
- associated with: 与...相关
- ageism: 年龄歧视

2. What critics suggest therefore, is that the 'bourgeois concept' of the public sphere was a male-dominated one that helped to legitimize systematic social inequalities. (Giddens, 2009)

> 因此，批评者建议的是，"资产阶级概念"的公共领域是一个男性主导的领域，它有助于使系统性的社会不平等合法化。

- legitimize: 使...合法化
- critics: 批评者
- suggest: 建议
- therefore: 因此
- bourgeois concept: 资产阶级概念
- public sphere: 公共领域
- male-dominated: 男性主导的
- helped to: 有助于
- systematic social inequalities: 系统性的社会不平等
