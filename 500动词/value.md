1. Wilson, an early advocate for the preservation of the environment, has advanced "scientific humanism," an inter-disciplinary discipline that values biodiversity and a sound environmental ethic. (Fiero, 2011)

> 威尔逊，环境保护的早期倡导者，提出了"科学人文主义"，一个重视生物多样性和健全环境伦理的跨学科学科。

- values: 重视
- Wilson: 威尔逊
- early advocate: 早期倡导者
- preservation of the environment: 环境保护
- advanced: 提出
- scientific humanism: 科学人文主义
- inter-disciplinary discipline: 跨学科学科
- biodiversity: 生物多样性
- sound environmental ethic: 健全环境伦理

2. The Athenians also were determined to protect their city from enemies, but, unlike the Spartans, they valued political freedom and sought the full development and enrichment of the human personality. (Perry, 2010)

> 雅典人也决心保护他们的城市免受敌人侵害，但与斯巴达人不同，他们重视政治自由并寻求人类个性的充分发展和丰富。

- valued: 重视
- Athenians: 雅典人
- also: 也
- determined to protect: 决心保护
- city: 城市
- enemies: 敌人
- unlike: 与...不同
- Spartans: 斯巴达人
- political freedom: 政治自由
- sought: 寻求
- full development: 充分发展
- enrichment: 丰富
- human personality: 人类个性

3. Moreover, Greek democracy, which valued freedom, was unable to overcome a weakness that has afflicted despotic governments: an incautious attitude toward power that causes the state to overreach itself. (Perry, 2010)

> 此外，重视自由的希腊民主无法克服困扰专制政府的弱点：对权力的不谨慎态度，导致国家超越自身。

- valued: 重视
- moreover: 此外
- Greek democracy: 希腊民主
- freedom: 自由
- unable to overcome: 无法克服
- weakness: 弱点
- afflicted: 困扰
- despotic governments: 专制政府
- incautious attitude: 不谨慎态度
- power: 权力
- causes: 导致
- state: 国家
- overreach itself: 超越自身

4. Humanists valued the works of the ancients, both in the literary and the visual arts, and they looked to the classical past for solutions to modern problems. (Fiero, 2011)

> 人文主义者重视古人的作品，无论是在文学还是视觉艺术方面，他们都在古典过去中寻找现代问题的解决方案。

- valued: 重视
- humanists: 人文主义者
- works of the ancients: 古人的作品
- both in: 无论是在...方面
- literary: 文学的
- visual arts: 视觉艺术
- looked to: 在...中寻找
- classical past: 古典过去
- solutions: 解决方案
- modern problems: 现代问题
