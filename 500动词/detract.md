1. However, given the subject upon which the painter is engaged is great, he must first of all make every effort to avoid getting lost in minute detail, so as not to detract from the dignity of the story. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 然而，鉴于画家所从事的主题是伟大的，他必须首先尽一切努力避免迷失在微小的细节中，以免损害故事的尊严。

- detract: 损害
- however: 然而
- given: 鉴于
- subject: 主题
- painter: 画家
- engaged: 从事
- great: 伟大的
- must first of all: 必须首先
- make every effort: 尽一切努力
- avoid: 避免
- getting lost in: 迷失在
- minute detail: 微小的细节
- so as not to: 以免
- dignity: 尊严
- story: 故事

> 这个案例和其他类似的案例也提出了一个问题：允许大量平民——在佛罗里达州接近150万——携带隐蔽武器的枪支法律是有助于还是损害公共安全。

- detracts: 损害
- this case: 这个案例
- others like it: 其他类似的
- raised: 提出
- issue: 问题
- whether: 是否
- gun laws: 枪支法律
- allow: 允许
- large number of civilians: 大量平民
- close to 1.5 million: 接近150万
- in Florida: 在佛罗里达州
- carry concealed weapons: 携带隐蔽武器
- contributes to: 有助于
- public safety: 公共安全

3. The structure of a relationship will add to or detract from commitment. Depending on the type of relationship—whether it is dating, living together, or marriage—different roles and expectations are structured. (Strong, DeVault, & Cohen, 2010)

- detract: 减少
- structure of a relationship: 关系的结构
- add to: 增加
- commitment: 承诺
- depending on: 根据
- type of relationship: 关系的类型
- whether: 是否
- dating: 约会
- living together: 同居
- marriage: 婚姻
- different roles: 不同的角色
- expectations: 期望
- structured: 被结构化
