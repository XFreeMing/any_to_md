1. In other words, utilitarianism would seem to encourage exactly the type of paternalistic intervention that the Liberty Principle expressly rules out: remember that the Liberty Principle does not permit anyone to interfere with another even for their own good. (Wolff, 2006)

> 换句话说，功利主义似乎会鼓励自由原则明确排除的那种家长式干预：记住，自由原则不允许任何人干涉他人，即使是为了他们自己的利益。

- permit: 允许
- in other words: 换句话说
- utilitarianism: 功利主义
- would seem to encourage: 似乎会鼓励
- type of paternalistic intervention: 家长式干预的类型
- Liberty Principle: 自由原则
- expressly rules out: 明确排除
- remember that: 记住
- does not permit: 不允许
- anyone: 任何人
- interfere with another: 干涉他人
- even for their own good: 即使是为了他们自己的利益

2. In keeping with the "attitude follows behavior" principle, experiments suggest that if you want people to do a big favor for you, an effective strategy is to get them to do a small favor first. In the best-known demonstration of this foot-in-the-door phenomenon, researchers posing as drivesafely volunteers asked Californians to permit the installation of huge, poorly lettered "Drive Carefully" signs in their front yards. (Myers, 2012)

> 根据"态度跟随行为"原则，实验表明，如果你想让人们为你做一件大好事，一个有效的策略是让他们先做一件小好事。在这个最著名的登门槛现象演示中，伪装成安全驾驶志愿者的研究人员要求加利福尼亚人允许在他们前院安装巨大的、字迹不佳的"小心驾驶"标志。

- permit: 允许
- in keeping with: 根据
- attitude follows behavior principle: 态度跟随行为原则
- experiments: 实验
- suggest that: 表明
- want people to do: 想让人们做
- big favor: 大好事
- effective strategy: 有效策略
- small favor first: 先做小好事
- best-known demonstration: 最著名的演示
- foot-in-the-door phenomenon: 登门槛现象
- researchers: 研究人员
- posing as: 伪装成
- drivesafely volunteers: 安全驾驶志愿者
- asked: 要求
- Californians: 加利福尼亚人
- installation: 安装
- huge: 巨大的
- poorly lettered: 字迹不佳的
- Drive Carefully signs: 小心驾驶标志
- front yards: 前院
