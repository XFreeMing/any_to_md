1. In the Virgin of Jeanne d Évreux, the graceful sway of the Virgin's body is counterbalanced by the harmonious way in which the drapery's vertical folds and soft curves play off each other. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在让娜·德·埃夫勒的圣母像中，圣母身体的优雅摇摆被帷幔的垂直褶皱和柔和曲线相互作用的和谐方式所平衡。

- counterbalanced: 平衡
- Virgin of Jeanne d Évreux: 让娜·德·埃夫勒的圣母像
- graceful sway: 优雅摇摆
- Virgin's body: 圣母身体
- harmonious way: 和谐方式
- drapery's vertical folds: 帷幔的垂直褶皱
- soft curves: 柔和曲线
- play off each other: 相互作用

2. The galleries built over the inner aisles counterbalance the lateral pressure of the nave vault. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 建在内侧过道上方的画廊平衡了中殿拱顶的侧向压力。

- counterbalance: 平衡
- galleries: 画廊
- built over: 建在...上方
- inner aisles: 内侧过道
- lateral pressure: 侧向压力
- nave vault: 中殿拱顶

3. To acknowledge the functionalism of the grid, Mies used thin I-beams for the mullions between windows. They provide the vertical accent that the proportions of the horizontal spandrels so perfectly counterbalance with their thin ridges on top and bottom. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 为了承认网格的功能主义，密斯在窗户之间的竖框上使用了薄工字梁。它们提供了垂直重音，水平拱肩的比例与它们顶部和底部的薄脊完美平衡。

- counterbalance: 平衡
- acknowledge: 承认
- functionalism: 功能主义
- grid: 网格
- Mies: 密斯
- thin I-beams: 薄工字梁
- mullions: 竖框
- between windows: 窗户之间
- provide: 提供
- vertical accent: 垂直重音
- proportions: 比例
- horizontal spandrels: 水平拱肩
- perfectly: 完美地
- thin ridges: 薄脊
- on top and bottom: 顶部和底部

4. Riskier investments tend to be counterbalanced by high rewards. (McConnell, Brue, & Flynn, 2014)

> 风险更高的投资往往被高回报所平衡。

- counterbalanced: 平衡
- riskier investments: 风险更高的投资
- tend to be: 往往
- high rewards: 高回报
