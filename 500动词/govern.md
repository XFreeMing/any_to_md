1. Using mind or human reason, the path to certain knowledge, and its best instrument, mathematics, humans can understand the material world because it is pure mechanism, a machine that is governed by its own physical laws because it was created by God---the great geometrician. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 使用心灵或人类理性，通往确定知识的道路，及其最佳工具数学，人类可以理解物质世界，因为它是纯粹的机制，一台由自己的物理规律支配的机器，因为它是由上帝——伟大的几何学家——创造的。

- governed: 支配
- using: 使用
- mind or human reason: 心灵或人类理性
- path to certain knowledge: 通往确定知识的道路
- best instrument: 最佳工具
- mathematics: 数学
- humans: 人类
- understand: 理解
- material world: 物质世界
- because: 因为
- pure mechanism: 纯粹的机制
- machine: 机器
- its own physical laws: 自己的物理规律
- created by God: 由上帝创造的
- great geometrician: 伟大的几何学家

2. The six forest cantons were democratic republics, while the seven urban cantons, which included Zürich, Bern, and Basel, were governed primarily by city councils controlled by narrow oligarchies of wealthy citizens. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 六个森林州是民主共和国，而七个城市州，包括苏黎世、伯尔尼和巴塞尔，主要由由富裕公民的狭隘寡头控制的市议会管理。

- governed: 管理
- six forest cantons: 六个森林州
- democratic republics: 民主共和国
- while: 而
- seven urban cantons: 七个城市州
- included: 包括
- Zürich: 苏黎世
- Bern: 伯尔尼
- Basel: 巴塞尔
- primarily by: 主要由
- city councils: 市议会
- controlled by: 由...控制的
- narrow oligarchies: 狭隘寡头
- wealthy citizens: 富裕公民

- new kingdom: 新王国
- not a centralized state: 不是一个中央集权国家
- but: 而是
- collection: 集合
- semi-independent territories: 半独立领土
- local lords: 地方领主
