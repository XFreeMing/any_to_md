1. Sigmund Freud (1856–1939), the father of psychoanalysis, penetrated the world of the unconscious in a scientific way. He concluded that powerful drives govern human behavior more than reason does. (Perry, 2010)

> 西格蒙德·弗洛伊德（1856-1939），精神分析之父，以科学的方式深入了无意识的世界。他得出结论，强大的驱动力比理性更能支配人类行为。

- concluded: 得出结论
- Sigmund Freud: 西格蒙德·弗洛伊德
- father of psychoanalysis: 精神分析之父
- penetrated: 深入
- world of the unconscious: 无意识的世界
- scientific way: 科学的方式
- powerful drives: 强大的驱动力
- govern: 支配
- human behavior: 人类行为
- more than: 比...更
- reason: 理性

2. Yet it may be premature to conclude that the Internet will sweep aside traditional cultures, replacing them with radically new cultural values. (Giddens, 2009)

- conclude: 得出结论
- yet: 然而
- may be premature: 可能还为时过早
- Internet: 互联网
- sweep aside: 扫除
- traditional cultures: 传统文化
- replacing: 取代
- radically new cultural values: 全新的文化价值观

- Palace at Knossus: 克诺索斯宫
- decorated with: 装饰着

- bright colors: 色彩鲜艳的

- sporting events: 体育赛事

- led some observers to: 使一些观察者

- concluded: 得出结论
- despite: 尽管

- beginning in: 从...开始

- reached its highest point: 达到最高点
