1. Factual studies of the paths of evolution of modern societies and less developed countries help us assess how far patterns of change conform to one side or the other. (Giddens, 2009)

> 对现代社会和欠发达国家进化路径的事实研究帮助我们评估变化模式在多大程度上符合一方或另一方。

- assess: 评估
- factual studies: 事实研究
- paths of evolution: 进化路径
- modern societies: 现代社会
- less developed countries: 欠发达国家
- help us: 帮助我们
- how far: 多大程度
- patterns of change: 变化模式
- conform to: 符合
- one side or the other: 一方或另一方

2. Self-serving bias additionally helps buffer stress. George Bonanno and colleagues (2005) assessed the emotional resiliency of workers who escaped from the World Trade Center or its environs on September 11, 2001. They found that those who displayed self-enhancing tendencies were the most resilient. (Myers, 2012)

> 自我服务偏见还有助于缓冲压力。乔治·博南诺和同事（2005）评估了2001年9月11日从世界贸易中心或其周围地区逃生的工人的情绪韧性。他们发现那些表现出自我增强倾向的人最有韧性。

- assessed: 评估
- self-serving bias: 自我服务偏见
- additionally: 还
- helps buffer stress: 有助于缓冲压力
- George Bonanno: 乔治·博南诺
- colleagues: 同事
- emotional resiliency: 情绪韧性
- workers: 工人
- escaped from: 从...逃生
- World Trade Center: 世界贸易中心
- environs: 周围地区
- September 11, 2001: 2001年9月11日
- found that: 发现
- displayed: 表现出
- self-enhancing tendencies: 自我增强倾向
- most resilient: 最有韧性

3. Researchers have also found evidence of social loafing in varied cultures, particularly by assessing agricultural output in formerly communist countries. (Myers, 2012)

> 研究人员还在各种文化中发现了社会惰化的证据，特别是通过评估前共产主义国家的农业产出。

- assessing: 评估
- researchers: 研究人员
- also found: 还发现
- evidence: 证据
- social loafing: 社会惰化
- varied cultures: 各种文化
- particularly: 特别是
- agricultural output: 农业产出
- formerly communist countries: 前共产主义国家

- assess: 评估
