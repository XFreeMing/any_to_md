1. The M/F scale was also bipolar, which meant that masculinity and femininity were viewed as opposite ends of a single continuum. The sum of the feminine items was subtracted from the sum of the masculine items to yield a total M/F score. (Helgeson, 2011)

> M/F量表也是双极的，这意味着男性和女性被视为单一连续体的相反两端。从男性项目的总和中减去女性项目的总和，得出总M/F分数。

- subtracted: 减去
- M/F scale: M/F量表
- bipolar: 双极的
- meant that: 意味着
- masculinity: 男性
- femininity: 女性
- viewed as: 被视为
- opposite ends: 相反两端
- single continuum: 单一连续体
- sum: 总和
- feminine items: 女性项目
- masculine items: 男性项目
- yield: 得出
- total M/F score: 总M/F分数

2. To avoid overstating the value of domestic production, we must subtract the amount spent on imported goods because such spending generates production and income abroad rather than at home. (McConnell, Brue, & Flynn, 2014)

> 为了避免高估国内生产的价值，我们必须减去花在进口商品上的金额，因为这种支出在国外而不是在国内产生生产和收入。

- subtract: 减去
- to avoid: 为了避免
- overstating: 高估
- value: 价值
- domestic production: 国内生产
- must: 必须
- amount spent on: 花在...上的金额
- imported goods: 进口商品
- because: 因为
- such spending: 这种支出
- generates: 产生
- production and income: 生产和收入
- abroad: 在国外
- rather than: 而不是
- at home: 在国内

3. In moving from national income to personal income, we must subtract the income that is earned but not received and add the income that is received but not earned. (McConnell, Brue, & Flynn, 2014)

> 在从国民收入转向个人收入时，我们必须减去已赚取但未收到的收入，并加上已收到但未赚取的收入。

- subtract: 减去
- moving from: 从...转向
- national income: 国民收入
- personal income: 个人收入
- must: 必须
- income: 收入
- earned but not received: 已赚取但未收到的
- add: 加上
- received but not earned: 已收到但未赚取的

4. If we define meaning as helping people in faraway places, we implicitly subtract meaning from the actual work we are doing. (Oxford English Dictionary, 1989)

> 如果我们将意义定义为帮助远方的人们，我们实际上从我们正在做的实际工作中减去了意义。

- subtract: 减去
- if: 如果
- define meaning as: 将意义定义为
- helping people: 帮助人们
- faraway places: 远方的地方
- implicitly: 实际上
- actual work: 实际工作
- we are doing: 我们正在做的
