1. In addition, the power of rulers, who were regarded either as gods or as agents of the gods, derived from religion. (Perry, 2010)

> 此外，统治者的权力，他们被视为神或神的代理人，来源于宗教。

- derived from: 来源于
- in addition: 此外
- power of rulers: 统治者的权力
- regarded either as: 被视为...或
- gods: 神
- agents of the gods: 神的代理人
- religion: 宗教

2. Natural barriers—mountains, deserts, cataracts (rapids) in the Nile, and the Mediterranean—protected Egypt from attack, allowing the inhabitants to enjoy long periods of peace and prosperity. Thus, unlike Mesopotamians, Egyptians derived a sense of security from their environment. (Perry, 2010)

> 自然屏障——山脉、沙漠、尼罗河的急流和地中海——保护埃及免受攻击，使居民能够享受长期的和平与繁荣。因此，与美索不达米亚人不同，埃及人从他们的环境中获得了安全感。

- derived: 获得
- natural barriers: 自然屏障
- mountains: 山脉
- deserts: 沙漠
- cataracts: 急流
- rapids: 急流
- Nile: 尼罗河
- Mediterranean: 地中海
- protected: 保护
- Egypt: 埃及
- attack: 攻击
- allowing: 使
- inhabitants: 居民
- enjoy: 享受
- long periods: 长期
- peace and prosperity: 和平与繁荣
- thus: 因此
- unlike: 与...不同
- Mesopotamians: 美索不达米亚人
- sense of security: 安全感
- environment: 环境

3. Derived from carefully observed facts, Kepler's laws of planetary motion buttressed Copernicanism, for they made sense only in a heliocentric universe. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 从仔细观察到的事实中得出，开普勒的行星运动定律支持了哥白尼主义，因为它们只有在日心宇宙中才有意义。

- derived from: 从...得出
- carefully observed facts: 仔细观察到的事实
- Kepler's laws: 开普勒的定律
- planetary motion: 行星运动
- buttressed: 支持
- Copernicanism: 哥白尼主义
- made sense: 有意义
- only in: 只有在
- heliocentric universe: 日心宇宙

4. The Declaration of Independence clearly articulated Locke's basic principles: that government derives its authority from the governed; that human beings are born with natural rights, which government has a responsibility to protect; and that citizens have the right to resist a government that deprives them of these rights. (Perry, 2010)

> 《独立宣言》清楚地阐述了洛克的基本原则：政府从被统治者那里获得其权威；人类生来就有自然权利，政府有责任保护这些权利；公民有权抵抗剥夺他们这些权利的政府。

- derives: 获得
- Declaration of Independence: 《独立宣言》
- clearly articulated: 清楚地阐述
- Locke's basic principles: 洛克的基本原则
- government: 政府
- authority: 权威
- governed: 被统治者
- human beings: 人类
- born with: 生来就有
- natural rights: 自然权利
- responsibility: 责任
- protect: 保护
- citizens: 公民
- right to resist: 抵抗的权利
- deprives them of: 剥夺他们的
