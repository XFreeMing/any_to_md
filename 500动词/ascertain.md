1. Versailles was called by a contemporary pure harmony. Everything there is part of the unity of a perfect work of art. But the elaborate and expansive gardens had their detractors as well. From these critics we are able to ascertain what life was like at Versailles. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 凡尔赛被同时代人称为纯粹的和谐。那里的一切都是完美艺术作品统一的一部分。但精心设计和广阔的园林也有它们的批评者。从这些批评者那里，我们能够确定凡尔赛的生活是什么样的。

2. His [Montesquieu's] most famous work, The Spirit of the Laws, was published in 1748. (Spielvogel, 2010)

- social and political arena: 社会和政治领域
- natural laws: 自然法则
- governing: 支配
- social relationships: 社会关系
- human beings: 人类
