1. Sometimes tensions run so high that genuine communication is impossible. In such cases, small conciliatory gestures by one party may elicit reciprocal conciliatory acts by the other party. One such conciliatory strategy, graduated and reciprocated initiatives in tension reduction (GRIT), aims to alleviate tense international situations. (Myers, 2012)

> 有时紧张局势如此高涨，以至于真正的沟通是不可能的。在这种情况下，一方的和解姿态可能引发另一方的互惠和解行为。一种这样的和解策略，逐步和互惠的紧张局势减少倡议（GRIT），旨在缓解紧张的国际局势。

2. Convinced that individuals were responsible for their own misfortunes, liberals were often unmoved by the misery of the poor. 4. What feminism means to people differs across the world. In parts of the developing world, feminism means working to alleviate absolute poverty and to change traditional male attitudes, which favour large families and dislike contraception, while in the developed countries, feminism means continuing campaigns for equality in employment, adequate childcare provision and the ending of male violence towards women. (Giddens, 2009)

> 女权主义对人们的意义在世界各地不同。在发展中世界的部分地区，女权主义意味着努力缓解绝对贫困并改变传统男性态度，这些态度偏爱大家庭并不喜欢避孕，而在发达国家，女权主义意味着继续为就业平等、充分的儿童保育提供和结束对妇女的男性暴力而进行运动。
