1. In general, the dominant male-generated stereotype of the Romantic hero influenced female literary characterization well into the mid nineteenth century. (Fiero, 2011)

> 总的来说，占主导地位的男性产生的浪漫英雄刻板印象影响了女性文学特征，一直延续到19世纪中期。

- in general: 总的来说
- dominant: 占主导地位的
- male-generated stereotype: 男性产生的刻板印象
- Romantic hero: 浪漫英雄
- female literary characterization: 女性文学特征
- well into: 一直延续到
- mid nineteenth century: 19世纪中期

2. Independence of judgement, Mill claims, will surely lead to superior consequences. But he does not mean that no one should ever try to influence other people's behavior. On the contrary, he is keen to emphasize that each of us has a duty to try to convince others of their mistakes, if we feel that they are embarking on foolish or damaging courses of action. (Wolff, 2006)

> 密尔声称，判断的独立性肯定会带来更好的后果。但他的意思不是没有人应该尝试影响他人的行为。相反，他热衷于强调，如果我们觉得他们正在从事愚蠢或有害的行动路线，我们每个人都有责任尝试说服他人他们的错误。

- influence: 影响
- independence of judgement: 判断的独立性
- Mill: 密尔
- claims: 声称
- surely lead to: 肯定会带来
- superior consequences: 更好的后果
- does not mean: 不是...的意思
- no one: 没有人
- should ever try: 应该尝试
- other people's behavior: 他人的行为
- on the contrary: 相反
- keen to emphasize: 热衷于强调
- each of us: 我们每个人
- duty: 责任
- convince others: 说服他人
- mistakes: 错误
- feel that: 觉得
- embarking on: 从事
- foolish or damaging courses of action: 愚蠢或有害的行动路线

3. The first black dance company was founded by Katharine Dunham who was strongly influenced by popular American dance styles and by the dance traditions of Haiti and Trinidad. (Fiero, 2011)

> 第一个黑人舞蹈公司由凯瑟琳·邓纳姆创立，她深受流行美国舞蹈风格以及海地和特立尼达的舞蹈传统的影响。

- influenced by: 受...影响
- first black dance company: 第一个黑人舞蹈公司
- founded by: 由...创立
- Katharine Dunham: 凯瑟琳·邓纳姆
- strongly: 深受
- popular American dance styles: 流行美国舞蹈风格
- dance traditions: 舞蹈传统
- Haiti: 海地
- Trinidad: 特立尼达

4. The prime exemplar of the transcendentalists was Ralph Waldo Emerson (1803–1882), whose essays powerfully influenced nineteenth-century American thought. (Fiero, 2011)

> 超验主义者的主要典范是拉尔夫·沃尔多·爱默生（1803-1882），他的散文有力地影响了19世纪的美国思想。

- influenced: 影响
- prime exemplar: 主要典范
- transcendentalists: 超验主义者
- Ralph Waldo Emerson: 拉尔夫·沃尔多·爱默生
- essays: 散文
- powerfully: 有力地
- nineteenth-century American thought: 19世纪的美国思想
