1. In his first major work, The Division of Labour in Society, Durkheim presented an analysis of social change that argued that the advent of the industrial era meant the emergence of a new type of solidarity. (Giddens, 2009)

> 在他的第一部主要作品《社会分工论》中，涂尔干提出了对社会变革的分析，认为工业时代的到来意味着一种新型团结的出现。

- presented: 提出
- first major work: 第一部主要作品
- The Division of Labour in Society: 《社会分工论》
- Durkheim: 涂尔干
- analysis: 分析
- social change: 社会变革
- argued that: 认为
- advent: 到来
- industrial era: 工业时代
- meant: 意味着
- emergence: 出现
- new type of solidarity: 新型团结

2. He presents an example in which the other members of your neighbourhood have discovered a public address system, and decide to institute a scheme for public entertainment. (Wolff, 2006)

> 他提出了一个例子，在这个例子中，你社区的其他成员发现了一个公共广播系统，并决定建立一个公共娱乐计划。

- presents: 提出
- example: 例子
- other members: 其他成员
- neighbourhood: 社区
- discovered: 发现
- public address system: 公共广播系统
- decide to institute: 决定建立
- scheme: 计划
- public entertainment: 公共娱乐

3. Self-presentation refers to our wanting to present a desired image both to an external audience (other people) and to an internal audience (ourselves). (Myers, 2012)

> 自我呈现指的是我们想要向外部观众（其他人）和内部观众（我们自己）呈现期望的形象。

- present: 呈现
- self-presentation: 自我呈现
- refers to: 指的是
- wanting to: 想要
- desired image: 期望的形象
- external audience: 外部观众
- other people: 其他人
- internal audience: 内部观众
- ourselves: 我们自己

4. In Sic et Non (Yes and No), he took 150 theological issues and, by presenting passages from the Bible and the church fathers, showed that there were conﬂicting opinions. (Perry, 2010)

> 在《是与否》中，他选取了150个神学问题，并通过呈现《圣经》和教会教父的段落，表明存在冲突的意见。

- presenting: 呈现
- Sic et Non: 《是与否》
- Yes and No: 是与否
- took: 选取
- 150 theological issues: 150个神学问题
- passages: 段落
- Bible: 《圣经》
- church fathers: 教会教父
- showed that: 表明
- conflicting opinions: 冲突的意见
