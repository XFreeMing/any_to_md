1. Using the legend, find the shade of color that corresponds to the first outbreak of plague, in December 1347, and follow the spread of disease, shown here in six-month intervals, as you follow the colors northward. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 使用图例，找到与1347年12月第一次瘟疫爆发相对应的颜色阴影，并跟随疾病的传播，这里以六个月为间隔显示，当你跟随颜色向北时。

- using the legend: 使用图例
- shade of color: 颜色阴影
- first outbreak: 第一次爆发
- plague: 瘟疫

- follow: 跟随
- spread of disease: 疾病的传播
- shown here: 这里显示
- six-month intervals: 六个月为间隔
- northward: 向北

> 在尤卡坦半岛奇琴伊察的主要金字塔上，四个侧面各有的九十一步台阶，加上寺庙所在的平台，对应于太阳历中的365天。

- ninety-one steps: 九十一步台阶

- plus: 加上
- platform: 平台
- temple stands: 寺庙所在
- 365 days: 365天
- solar calendar: 太阳历

3. Throughout most of the Middle Ages, time was determined by natural rhythms (daybreak and nightfall) or church bells that were rung at more or less regular three-hour intervals, corresponding to the ecclesiastical offices of the church. (Spielvogel, 2010)

- corresponding to: 对应于
- throughout: 在...期间
- most of the Middle Ages: 中世纪的大部分时间
- time: 时间
- determined by: 由...确定
- natural rhythms: 自然节律
- daybreak: 黎明
- nightfall: 黄昏
- church bells: 教堂钟声
- rung: 敲响
- more or less: 或多或少
- regular three-hour intervals: 规律的三小时间隔
- ecclesiastical offices: 教会办公室
- church: 教会
