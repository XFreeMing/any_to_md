1. Sometimes caring means simply being attuned to someone else's psychological well-being——several feminist writers have been interested in 'emotion work' within relationships. (Giddens, 2009)

> 有时关怀只是意味着适应他人的心理健康——几位女权主义作家对关系中的"情感工作"感兴趣。

- attuned to: 适应
- sometimes: 有时
- caring: 关怀
- means: 意味着
- simply: 只是
- someone else's: 他人的
- psychological well-being: 心理健康
- several feminist writers: 几位女权主义作家
- interested in: 对...感兴趣
- emotion work: 情感工作
- within relationships: 在关系中

2. According to Bem's (1965, 1972) self-perception theory, when people are not attuned to their internal states, they can infer their own attitudes from their behaviors just as they might do when inferring the attitudes of others.

> 根据贝姆（1965, 1972）的自我感知理论，当人们不适应他们的内部状态时，他们可以从自己的行为推断自己的态度，就像他们在推断他人的态度时可能做的那样。

- attuned to: 适应
- according to: 根据
- Bem's self-perception theory: 贝姆的自我感知理论
- when: 当
- people: 人们
- not: 不
- internal states: 内部状态
- infer: 推断
- their own attitudes: 自己的态度
- from their behaviors: 从自己的行为
- just as: 就像
- inferring the attitudes of others: 推断他人的态度

3. Only where the state monopolization of physical force is relatively stable and secure can individuals, from infancy onwards, become attuned to a new higher level of self-control, which then becomes 'second nature' to them. (Giddens, 2009)

> 只有在国家对物理力量的垄断相对稳定和安全的地方，个人才能从婴儿期开始适应新的更高水平的自我控制，这然后成为他们的"第二天性"。

- attuned to: 适应
- only where: 只有在...的地方
- state monopolization: 国家垄断
- physical force: 物理力量
- relatively stable and secure: 相对稳定和安全
- individuals: 个人
- from infancy onwards: 从婴儿期开始
- become: 成为
- new higher level: 新的更高水平
- self-control: 自我控制
- second nature: 第二天性
- to them: 对他们

4. As adults, women in individualist cultures describe themselves in more relational terms, welcome more help, experience more relationship-linked emotions, and are more attuned to others' relationships. (Miller & Perlman, 2011)

> 作为成年人，个人主义文化中的女性用更多关系术语描述自己，欢迎更多帮助，体验更多与关系相关的情感，并且更适应他人的关系。

- attuned to: 适应
- as adults: 作为成年人
- women: 女性
- individualist cultures: 个人主义文化
- describe themselves: 描述自己
- more relational terms: 更多关系术语
- welcome more help: 欢迎更多帮助
- experience: 体验
- relationship-linked emotions: 与关系相关的情感
- others' relationships: 他人的关系
