1. Negotiating the distinction between art and popular culture was essential because many people equated merit with sales or economic value. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 协商艺术和流行文化之间的区别是必要的，因为许多人将价值等同于销售或经济价值。

- equated: 等同于
- negotiating: 协商
- distinction: 区别
- between: 在...之间
- art: 艺术
- popular culture: 流行文化
- essential: 必要的
- because: 因为
- many people: 许多人
- merit: 价值
- sales: 销售
- economic value: 经济价值

2. Although it has become customary to equate the more radical sans-culottes with working people or the poor, many were merchants and better-off artisans who were often the elite of their neighborhoods. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 尽管将更激进的无套裤汉等同于工人或穷人已成为习惯，但许多人是商人和较富裕的工匠，他们往往是他们社区的精英。

- equate: 等同于
- although: 尽管
- become customary: 成为习惯
- more radical: 更激进的
- sans-culottes: 无套裤汉
- working people: 工人
- poor: 穷人
- many were: 许多人是
- merchants: 商人
- better-off artisans: 较富裕的工匠
- often: 往往
- elite: 精英
- their neighborhoods: 他们的社区

3. The split between mind and body led Westerners to equate their identity with mind and reason rather than with the whole organism. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 心灵和身体的分裂导致西方人将他们的身份等同于心灵和理性，而不是整个有机体。

- equate: 等同于
- split: 分裂
- between mind and body: 心灵和身体之间
- led Westerners to: 导致西方人
- identity: 身份
- mind and reason: 心灵和理性
- rather than: 而不是
- whole organism: 整个有机体

4. Pantheism is a doctrine that equates God with the universe and all that is in it. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 泛神论是一种将上帝等同于宇宙及其中的一切的教义。

- equates: 等同于
- pantheism: 泛神论
- doctrine: 教义
- God: 上帝
- universe: 宇宙
- all that is in it: 其中的一切
