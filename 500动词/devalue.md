1. Aversion to a techno-scientific culture and to its methodology leads postmodernists to devalue the principle of objectivity in the social sciences and to give greater weight to the subjective, to feelings, intuition, and fantasy, to the poetry of life. (Perry, 2010)

> 对技术科学文化及其方法的厌恶导致后现代主义者贬低社会科学中的客观性原则，并给予主观、感觉、直觉和幻想、生活的诗意更大的权重。

- techno-scientific culture: 技术科学文化

- principle of objectivity: 客观性原则
- social sciences: 社会科学
- give greater weight to: 给予更大的权重
- subjective: 主观
- feelings: 感觉
- intuition: 直觉
- fantasy: 幻想
- poetry of life: 生活的诗意
