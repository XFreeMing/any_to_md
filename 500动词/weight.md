1. Google has become a dominant search engine by harnessing what James Surowiecki (2004) calls The Wisdom of Crowds. Google interprets a link to Page X as a vote for Page X, and weights most heavily links from pages that are themselves highly ranked.

> 谷歌通过利用詹姆斯·索罗维基（2004）所称的"群体智慧"而成为主导搜索引擎。谷歌将指向页面X的链接解释为对页面X的投票，并最重地加权来自本身排名很高的页面的链接。

- Google: 谷歌
- dominant search engine: 主导搜索引擎
- by harnessing: 通过利用
- The Wisdom of Crowds: "群体智慧"
- interprets: 解释
- link to Page X: 指向页面X的链接
- as a vote for: 作为对...的投票
- most heavily: 最重地
- links from pages: 来自页面的链接
- themselves highly ranked: 本身排名很高的
