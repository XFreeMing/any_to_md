1. Leading politicians have, as a result, come to live in a kind of ongoing reality-television programme, the sole purpose of which appears to be to embarrass and denigrate them at every possible turn. (Heywood, 2013)

- sole purpose: 唯一目的

- at every possible turn: 在每一个可能的转折点

4. Prisoner-of-war camp guards would sometimes display good manners to captives in their first days on the job, but not for long. Soldiers ordered to kill may initially react with revulsion to the point of sickness over their act. But not for long (Waller, 2002). Before long, they will denigrate their enemies with nicknames. People tend to dehumanize their enemies and humanize their pets. (Giddens, 2009)

> 战俘营守卫有时会在工作的最初几天对俘虏表现出良好的举止，但不会持续很久。被命令杀人的士兵最初可能会对他们的行为感到厌恶到恶心的程度。但不会持续很久（Waller, 2002）。不久之后，他们会用绰号诋毁他们的敌人。人们倾向于非人化他们的敌人并人化他们的宠物。

- prisoner-of-war camp guards: 战俘营守卫
- sometimes: 有时
- display good manners: 表现出良好的举止
- captives: 俘虏
- first days on the job: 工作的最初几天
- not for long: 不会持续很久
- soldiers: 士兵
- ordered to kill: 被命令杀人
- initially react: 最初反应
- revulsion: 厌恶
- to the point of sickness: 到恶心的程度
- their act: 他们的行为
- before long: 不久之后
- enemies: 敌人
- nicknames: 绰号
- people tend to: 人们倾向于
- dehumanize: 非人化
- humanize: 人化
- pets: 宠物
