1. Both the Hebrews and the Greeks borrowed Mesopotamian literary themes. For instance, some biblical stories—the Flood, the quarrel between Cain and Abel, and the Tower of Babel—stem from Mesopotamian antecedents. (Perry, 2010)

> 希伯来人和希腊人都借用了美索不达米亚的文学主题。例如，一些圣经故事——洪水、该隐和亚伯之间的争吵，以及巴别塔——源于美索不达米亚的前身。

- both: 两者
- Hebrews: 希伯来人
- Greeks: 希腊人
- borrowed: 借用
- Mesopotamian literary themes: 美索不达米亚的文学主题
- for instance: 例如
- biblical stories: 圣经故事
- Flood: 洪水
- quarrel: 争吵
- Cain and Abel: 该隐和亚伯
- Tower of Babel: 巴别塔
- Mesopotamian antecedents: 美索不达米亚的前身

4. Four major types of stratification system can be identified: slavery, caste, estates and class. Whereas the first three depend on legal or religiously sanctioned inequalities, class divisions are not 'officially' recognized, but stem from economic factors affecting the material circumstances of people's lives. (Giddens, 2009)

> 可以识别四种主要的分层系统类型：奴隶制、种姓、等级和阶级。虽然前三种依赖于法律或宗教认可的不平等，但阶级划分不是"官方"认可的，而是源于影响人们生活物质环境的经济因素。

- four major types: 四种主要类型
- stratification system: 分层系统
- identified: 识别
- slavery: 奴隶制
- caste: 种姓
