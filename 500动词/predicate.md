## 断言；宣称

1. While the Vedas teach worship through prayer and sacrifice, the Upanishads teach enlightenment through meditation. They predicate the concept of the single, all-pervading cosmic force called Brahman. (Fiero, 2011)

> 虽然《吠陀经》通过祈祷和牺牲来教导崇拜，但《奥义书》通过冥想来教导觉悟。它们断言了被称为梵天的单一、遍及一切的宇宙力量的概念。

- predicate: 断言，宣称
- while: 虽然
- Vedas: 《吠陀经》
- teach: 教导
- worship: 崇拜
- prayer: 祈祷
- sacrifice: 牺牲
- Upanishads: 《奥义书》
- enlightenment: 觉悟
- meditation: 冥想
- concept: 概念
- single: 单一的
- all-pervading: 遍及一切的
- cosmic force: 宇宙力量
- Brahman: 梵天

## 使某事基于某事

1. Government regulations to ensure the superiority of export goods, the construction of roads and canals, and the granting of subsidies to create trade companies were all predicated on government involvement in economic affairs. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 确保出口商品优越性的政府法规、道路和运河的建设，以及为创建贸易公司而给予的补贴，都基于政府对经济事务的参与。

- predicated on: 基于
- government regulations: 政府法规
- ensure: 确保
- superiority: 优越性
- export goods: 出口商品
- construction: 建设
- roads and canals: 道路和运河
- granting of subsidies: 给予补贴
- create trade companies: 创建贸易公司
- government involvement: 政府参与
- economic affairs: 经济事务

2. The Western tradition of marrying 'for love' frequently comes into conflict with the practice of arranged marriages within Asian communities. Such unions, arranged by parents and family members, are predicated on the belief that love comes from within marriage. (Giddens, 2009)

> 西方"为爱而婚"的传统经常与亚洲社区内包办婚姻的做法发生冲突。这种由父母和家庭成员安排的结合基于爱情来自婚姻内部的信念。

- predicated on: 基于
- Western tradition: 西方传统
- marrying for love: 为爱而婚
- frequently: 经常
- comes into conflict with: 与...发生冲突
- practice: 做法
- arranged marriages: 包办婚姻
- Asian communities: 亚洲社区
- unions: 结合
- arranged by: 由...安排
- parents and family members: 父母和家庭成员
- belief: 信念
- love comes from within marriage: 爱情来自婚姻内部

3. According to Goldthorpe, whose own class scheme was originally predicated on this argument, this is not a view based on an ideology of sexism. (Giddens, 2009)

> 根据戈德索普的说法，他自己的阶级方案最初基于这个论点，这不是基于性别歧视意识形态的观点。

- predicated on: 基于
- according to: 根据
- Goldthorpe: 戈德索普
- own class scheme: 自己的阶级方案
- originally: 最初
- argument: 论点
- view: 观点
- based on: 基于
- ideology of sexism: 性别歧视意识形态

4. There are three main assumptions on which the biomedical model of health is predicated. First, disease is viewed as a breakdown within the human body that diverts it from its 'normal' state of being. Second, the mind and body can be treated separately Third, trained medical specialists are considered the only experts in the treatment of disease. (Giddens, 2009)

> 有三个主要假设，生物医学健康模型基于这些假设。首先，疾病被视为人体内的故障，使其偏离其"正常"的存在状态。其次，心灵和身体可以分开治疗。第三，训练有素的医学专家被认为是治疗疾病的唯一专家。

- predicated: 基于
- three main assumptions: 三个主要假设
- biomedical model of health: 生物医学健康模型
- first: 首先
- disease: 疾病
- viewed as: 被视为
- breakdown: 故障
- within the human body: 人体内
- diverts it from: 使其偏离
- normal state of being: 正常的存在状态
- second: 其次
- mind and body: 心灵和身体
- treated separately: 分开治疗
- third: 第三
- trained medical specialists: 训练有素的医学专家
- considered: 被认为是
- only experts: 唯一专家
- treatment of disease: 治疗疾病
