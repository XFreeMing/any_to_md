1. Persuasion is neither inherently good nor bad. It is a message's purpose and content that elicit judgments of good or bad. The bad we call "propaganda." The good we call "education." (Myers, 2012)

> 说服本身既不好也不坏。是信息的目的和内容引起好或坏的判断。坏的我们称为"宣传"。好的我们称为"教育"。

- elicit: 引起
- persuasion: 说服
- neither inherently good nor bad: 本身既不好也不坏
- message's purpose: 信息的目的
- content: 内容
- judgments: 判断
- good or bad: 好或坏
- propaganda: 宣传
- education: 教育

2. Dissonance theory insists that encouragement and inducement should be enough to elicit the desired action (so that attitudes may follow the behavior). (Myers, 2012)

> 失调理论坚持认为鼓励和诱导应该足以引起期望的行为（以便态度可能跟随行为）。

- elicit: 引起
- dissonance theory: 失调理论
- insists that: 坚持认为
- encouragement: 鼓励
- inducement: 诱导
- should be enough: 应该足以
- desired action: 期望的行为
- so that: 以便
- attitudes: 态度
- may follow: 可能跟随
- behavior: 行为

3. But tension can also elicit yawning, which is commonly observed among paratroopers before their first jump, Olympic athletes before their event, and violinists waiting to go onstage. (Myers, 2012)

> 但紧张也会引起打哈欠，这在伞兵第一次跳伞前、奥运会运动员比赛前和等待上台的小提琴手中很常见。

- elicit: 引起
- but: 但
- tension: 紧张
- can also: 也会
- yawning: 打哈欠
- commonly observed: 很常见
- among: 在...中
- paratroopers: 伞兵
- before their first jump: 第一次跳伞前
- Olympic athletes: 奥运会运动员
- before their event: 比赛前
- violinists: 小提琴手
- waiting to go onstage: 等待上台

4. We have already seen, for example, how British architects evoked republican Rome in their buildings in order to elicit a sense of democracy. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 例如，我们已经看到，英国建筑师如何在他们的建筑中唤起共和罗马，以引起民主感。

- elicit: 引起
- we have already seen: 我们已经看到
- for example: 例如
- British architects: 英国建筑师
- evoked: 唤起
- republican Rome: 共和罗马
- buildings: 建筑
- in order to: 以便
- sense of democracy: 民主感
