1. Between 4000 and 3000 B.C., significant technical developments began to transform the Neolithic towns. The invention of writing enabled records to be kept, and the use of metals marked a new level of human control over the environment and its resources. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在公元前 4000 年到 3000 年之间，重大的技术发展开始转变新石器时代的城镇。文字的发明使记录得以保存，金属的使用标志着人类对环境及其资源的控制达到了新的水平。

- transform: 转变
- between 4000 and 3000 B.C.: 在公元前 4000 年到 3000 年之间
- significant technical developments: 重大的技术发展
- began to: 开始
- Neolithic towns: 新石器时代的城镇
- invention of writing: 文字的发明
- enabled records to be kept: 使记录得以保存
- use of metals: 金属的使用
- marked: 标志着
- new level: 新水平
- human control: 人类控制
- environment: 环境
- its resources: 其资源

2. Homer gave the Greeks a model of heroism, honor, and nobility. But in time, as a new world of city-states emerged in Greece, new values of cooperation and community also transformed what the Greeks learned from Homer. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 荷马给希腊人提供了一个英雄主义、荣誉和高贵的典范。但随着时间的推移，随着城邦的新世界在希腊出现，合作和社区的新价值观也转变了希腊人从荷马那里学到的东西。

- transformed: 转变
- Homer: 荷马
- gave: 给
- Greeks: 希腊人
- model: 典范
- heroism: 英雄主义
- honor: 荣誉
- nobility: 高贵
- in time: 随着时间的推移
- new world: 新世界
- city-states: 城邦
- emerged: 出现
- in Greece: 在希腊
- new values: 新价值观
- cooperation: 合作
- community: 社区
- also: 也
- what the Greeks learned: 希腊人学到的
- from Homer: 从荷马

3. To ensure control over their conquered Laconian and Messenian helots, the Spartans decided to create a military state. By the early sixth century B.C., they had transformed Sparta into a military camp. The lives of all Spartans were now rigidly organized. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 为了确保对他们征服的拉科尼亚和麦西尼亚黑劳士的控制，斯巴达人决定创建一个军事国家。到公元前六世纪初，他们已经将斯巴达转变为一个军营。所有斯巴达人的生活现在都被严格组织起来。

- transformed: 转变
- to ensure: 为了确保
- control over: 对...的控制
- conquered: 征服的
- Laconian and Messenian helots: 拉科尼亚和麦西尼亚黑劳士
- Spartans: 斯巴达人
- decided to create: 决定创建
- military state: 军事国家
- by the early sixth century B.C.: 到公元前六世纪初
- Sparta: 斯巴达
- into a military camp: 为一个军营
- lives of all Spartans: 所有斯巴达人的生活
- rigidly organized: 严格组织

4. Philip instituted military reforms that transformed Macedonia into a major military power. He created a new phalanx of infantrymen who were more lightly armed than Greek hoplites; each carried a smaller shield and a shorter sword. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 菲利普建立了军事改革，将马其顿转变为一个主要军事强国。他创建了一个新的步兵方阵，这些步兵比希腊重装步兵装备更轻；每个人都携带较小的盾牌和较短的剑。

- transformed: 转变
- Philip: 菲利普
- instituted: 建立
- military reforms: 军事改革
- Macedonia: 马其顿
- into a major military power: 为一个主要军事强国
- created: 创建
- new phalanx: 新方阵
- infantrymen: 步兵
- more lightly armed: 装备更轻
- Greek hoplites: 希腊重装步兵
- each carried: 每个人都携带
- smaller shield: 较小的盾牌
- shorter sword: 较短的剑
