1. Factors like time of day or an activity that 'explains' a person's presence can also affect in what way and how quickly the image of 'stranger' is neutralized (Giddens, 2009)

> 像一天中的时间或"解释"一个人存在的活动这样的因素也会影响"陌生人"形象被中和的方式和速度。

- neutralized: 被中和
- factors: 因素
- like: 像
- time of day: 一天中的时间
- activity: 活动
- explains: 解释
- person's presence: 一个人的存在
- affect: 影响
- in what way: 以什么方式
- how quickly: 多快
- image of 'stranger': "陌生人"形象

2. He used threats of harm to those who fled the community, beatings for noncompliance, and drugs to neutralize disagreeable members. By the end, he was as much an arm twister as a mind bender. (Myers, 2012)

> 他对逃离社区的人使用伤害威胁，对不服从的人进行殴打，并使用药物来中和令人不快的成员。到最后，他既是手臂扭曲者，也是思想扭曲者。

- neutralize: 中和
- used: 使用
- threats of harm: 伤害威胁
- those who fled: 逃离的人
- community: 社区
- beatings: 殴打
- noncompliance: 不服从
- drugs: 药物
- disagreeable members: 令人不快的成员
- by the end: 到最后
- arm twister: 手臂扭曲者
- mind bender: 思想扭曲者

3. If we learned that al Qaeda was secretly developing a new terrorist technique that could disrupt water supplies around the globe, force tens of millions from their homes and potentially endanger our entire planet, we would be aroused into a frenzy and deploy every possible asset to neutralize the threat," observed essayist Nicholas Kristof. (Myers, 2012)

> 如果我们得知基地组织正在秘密开发一种新的恐怖主义技术，这种技术可能破坏全球的供水，迫使数千万人离开家园，并可能危及我们的整个星球，我们会被激起狂热，并部署所有可能的资产来中和威胁，"散文家尼古拉斯·克里斯托夫观察到。

- neutralize: 中和
- if we learned that: 如果我们得知
- al Qaeda: 基地组织
- secretly developing: 秘密开发
- new terrorist technique: 新的恐怖主义技术
- disrupt: 破坏
- water supplies: 供水
- around the globe: 全球
- force: 迫使
- tens of millions: 数千万人
- from their homes: 离开家园
- potentially endanger: 可能危及
- entire planet: 整个星球
- aroused into a frenzy: 被激起狂热
- deploy: 部署
- every possible asset: 所有可能的资产
- threat: 威胁
- essayist: 散文家
- Nicholas Kristof: 尼古拉斯·克里斯托夫

- Venetian leaders: 威尼斯领导人
- Fourth Crusade: 第四次十字军东征
- saw an opportunity: 看到了一个机会
