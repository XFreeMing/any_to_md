1. Indeed, talking with another person not only forces you to be clear and concrete in your articulation of your beliefs; it allows you—or forces you—to engage in a second essential feature of doing philosophy: arguing for your views.

> 确实，与另一个人交谈不仅迫使你在表达你的信念时清晰和具体；它允许你——或迫使你——参与做哲学的第二个基本特征：为你的观点辩护。

- indeed: 确实

- clear and concrete: 清晰和具体

- beliefs: 信念
- allows you: 允许你
- second essential feature: 第二个基本特征
- doing philosophy: 做哲学
- arguing for: 为...辩护
- views: 观点
