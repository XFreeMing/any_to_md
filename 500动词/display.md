1. Among the many kinds of grave goods Woolley found in the Royal Cemetery were weapons, jewelry, and vessels. Many of the objects display the great skill of Sumerian artists in representing nature. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在伍利在皇家墓地发现的许多种类的随葬品中有武器、珠宝和器皿。许多物品展示了苏美尔艺术家在表现自然方面的伟大技能。

- display: 展示
- among: 在...中
- many kinds: 许多种类
- grave goods: 随葬品
- Woolley: 伍利
- found in: 在...发现
- Royal Cemetery: 皇家墓地
- weapons: 武器
- jewelry: 珠宝
- vessels: 器皿
- objects: 物品
- great skill: 伟大技能
- Sumerian artists: 苏美尔艺术家
- representing nature: 表现自然

2. Africa's Muslim-dominated cities display some of the most visually striking structures in the history of world architecture. (Fiero, 2011)

> 非洲的穆斯林主导城市展示了世界建筑史上一些最视觉上引人注目的结构。

- display: 展示
- Africa's Muslim-dominated cities: 非洲的穆斯林主导城市
- most visually striking structures: 最视觉上引人注目的结构
- history: 历史
- world architecture: 世界建筑

3. Even much of our morality is common across cultures and eras. Before they can walk, babies will display a moral sense by disapproving what's wrong or naughty. (Myers, 2012)

> 甚至我们的许多道德在文化和时代之间是共同的。在他们能走路之前，婴儿会通过不赞成错误或顽皮的事情来展示道德感。

- display: 展示
- even: 甚至
- much of our morality: 我们的许多道德
- common: 共同的
- across cultures and eras: 在文化和时代之间
- before they can walk: 在他们能走路之前
- babies: 婴儿
- moral sense: 道德感
- disapproving: 不赞成
- wrong or naughty: 错误或顽皮的

4. To some, this suggests that humans are hardwired for empathy. Primates, elephants, dogs, and even mice also display empathy, indicating that the building blocks of altruism predate humanity. (Myers, 2012)

> 对一些人来说，这表明人类天生具有同理心。灵长类动物、大象、狗，甚至老鼠也表现出同理心，表明利他主义的构建块早于人类。

- display: 表现
- to some: 对一些人来说
- suggests that: 表明
- humans: 人类
- hardwired for: 天生具有
- empathy: 同理心
- primates: 灵长类动物
- elephants: 大象
- dogs: 狗
- even mice: 甚至老鼠
- also: 也
- indicating that: 表明
- building blocks: 构建块
- altruism: 利他主义
- predate: 早于
- humanity: 人类
