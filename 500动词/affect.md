1. Invasions, civil wars, and plague came close to causing an economic collapse of the Roman Empire in the third century. The population declined drastically, possibly by as much as one-third. There was a noticeable decline in trade and small industry. The labor shortage created by plague affected both military recruiting and the economy. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 入侵、内战和瘟疫在3世纪几乎导致罗马帝国的经济崩溃。人口急剧下降，可能多达三分之一。贸易和小型工业明显下降。瘟疫造成的劳动力短缺影响了军事招募和经济。

- affected: 影响
- invasions: 入侵
- civil wars: 内战
- plague: 瘟疫
- came close to: 几乎
- causing: 导致
- economic collapse: 经济崩溃
- Roman Empire: 罗马帝国
- third century: 3世纪
- population: 人口
- declined drastically: 急剧下降
- possibly by as much as: 可能多达
- one-third: 三分之一
- noticeable decline: 明显下降
- trade: 贸易
- small industry: 小型工业
- labor shortage: 劳动力短缺
- created by: 由...造成的
- both: 两者
- military recruiting: 军事招募
- economy: 经济

2. A spiritual worldview was of fundamental importance to Mesopotamian culture. To the peoples of Mesopotamia, the gods were living realities who affected all aspects of life. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 精神世界观对美索不达米亚文化具有根本重要性。对美索不达米亚人民来说，神是影响生活各个方面的活生生的现实。

- affected: 影响
- spiritual worldview: 精神世界观
- fundamental importance: 根本重要性
- Mesopotamian culture: 美索不达米亚文化
- peoples of Mesopotamia: 美索不达米亚人民
- gods: 神
- living realities: 活生生的现实
- all aspects of life: 生活的各个方面

3. The differing political situations in Florence and Venice affected palace design. Whereas the Florentine palace was solid and impenetrable, the Venetian equivalent is airy and open, full of windows and arcades that are anything but defensible. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 佛罗伦萨和威尼斯不同的政治情况影响了宫殿设计。虽然佛罗伦萨宫殿是坚固和不可穿透的，但威尼斯的对应物是通风和开放的，充满了窗户和拱廊，这些绝不是可防御的。

- affected: 影响
- differing political situations: 不同的政治情况
- Florence: 佛罗伦萨
- Venice: 威尼斯
- palace design: 宫殿设计
- whereas: 虽然
- Florentine palace: 佛罗伦萨宫殿
- solid: 坚固的
- impenetrable: 不可穿透的
- Venetian equivalent: 威尼斯的对应物
- airy: 通风的
- open: 开放的
- full of: 充满
- windows: 窗户
- arcades: 拱廊
- anything but: 绝不是
- defensible: 可防御的
