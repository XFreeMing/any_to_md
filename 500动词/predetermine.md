1. Apocalypses represent the world as governed from heaven, and either imply or insist that everything is predetermined.

> 启示录将世界表现为由天堂统治，并且要么暗示要么坚持认为一切都是预先确定的。

- predetermined: 预先确定的
- apocalypses: 启示录
- represent: 表现
- world: 世界
- governed from heaven: 由天堂统治
- either...or: 要么...要么
- imply: 暗示
- insist that: 坚持认为
- everything: 一切

2. Predestination is the belief, associated with Calvinism, that God, as a consequence of his foreknowledge of all events, has predetermined those who will be saved (the elect) and those who will be damned. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 预定论是与加尔文主义相关的信仰，即上帝作为他对所有事件的预知的结果，已经预先确定了那些将被拯救的人（选民）和那些将被诅咒的人。

- predetermined: 预先确定
- predestination: 预定论
- belief: 信仰
- associated with: 与...相关
- Calvinism: 加尔文主义
- God: 上帝
- consequence: 结果
- foreknowledge: 预知
- all events: 所有事件
- saved: 被拯救的
- elect: 选民
- damned: 被诅咒的

3. Instead of merely measuring people's preferences, CBA may actually mold their preferences by predetermining the standard of evaluation, which in turn helps to predetermine the outcome of the evaluation. (Zamir & Teichman, 2014)

> 与其仅仅衡量人们的偏好，CBA实际上可能通过预先确定评估标准来塑造他们的偏好，这反过来有助于预先确定评估的结果。

- predetermining: 预先确定
- instead of: 与其
- merely measuring: 仅仅衡量
- people's preferences: 人们的偏好
- CBA: 成本效益分析
- actually: 实际上
- mold: 塑造
- standard of evaluation: 评估标准
- which in turn: 这反过来
- helps to: 有助于
- outcome: 结果
- evaluation: 评估

4. Tragedy, in the sense that it was defined by Aristotle ("the imitation of an action" involving "some great error" made by an extraordinary man), was philosophically incompatible with the medieval world-view, which held that all events were predetermined by God. (Fiero, 2011)

> 悲剧，在亚里士多德定义的意义上（"对行动的模仿"，涉及非凡的人犯下的"某种重大错误"），在哲学上与中世纪的世界观不相容，后者认为所有事件都是由上帝预先确定的。

- predetermined: 预先确定
- tragedy: 悲剧
- sense: 意义
- defined by: 由...定义
- Aristotle: 亚里士多德
- imitation of an action: 对行动的模仿
- involving: 涉及
- great error: 重大错误
- extraordinary man: 非凡的人
- philosophically incompatible: 哲学上不相容
- medieval world-view: 中世纪的世界观
- held that: 认为
- all events: 所有事件
- God: 上帝
