1. During the Middle Ages, except for members of religious orders, women who sought a life of learning were severely hampered by the traditional attitude that a woman's proper role was as a daughter, wife, and mother. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在中世纪，除了宗教团体的成员外，寻求学习生活的妇女受到传统态度的严重阻碍，这种态度认为妇女的适当角色是女儿、妻子和母亲。

- hampered: 阻碍
- during the Middle Ages: 在中世纪
- except for: 除了
- members of religious orders: 宗教团体的成员
- women: 妇女
- sought: 寻求
- life of learning: 学习生活
- severely: 严重地
- traditional attitude: 传统态度
- woman's proper role: 妇女的适当角色
- daughter: 女儿
- wife: 妻子
- mother: 母亲

2. Fifteenth-century popes refused to acknowledge limits to papal power, thus hampering the efforts of church councils to exercise authority over the papacy. (Fiero, 2011)

> 15世纪的教皇拒绝承认教皇权力的限制，从而阻碍了教会会议对教皇行使权威的努力。

- hampering: 阻碍
- fifteenth-century popes: 15世纪的教皇
- refused to acknowledge: 拒绝承认
- limits: 限制
- papal power: 教皇权力
- thus: 从而
- efforts: 努力
- church councils: 教会会议
- exercise authority: 行使权威
- over the papacy: 对教皇

3. However, the attempt—through choice or through economic necessity—to combine a career and a family has led many women into an exhausting 'double day' of work and housework, which in turn has often hampered their career prospects.

> 然而，通过选择或经济必要性——将职业和家庭结合起来的尝试使许多妇女陷入疲惫的"双重日"工作和家务，这反过来往往阻碍了她们的职业前景。

- hampered: 阻碍
- however: 然而
- attempt: 尝试
- through choice: 通过选择
- through economic necessity: 通过经济必要性
- combine: 结合
- career: 职业
- family: 家庭
- led many women into: 使许多妇女陷入
- exhausting: 疲惫的
- double day: 双重日
- work and housework: 工作和家务
- in turn: 反过来
- often: 往往
- career prospects: 职业前景

4. British development was hampered by frequent labor strikes, many of them caused by conflicts between rival labor unions. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 英国的发展受到频繁的劳工罢工的阻碍，其中许多是由敌对工会之间的冲突引起的。

- hampered: 阻碍
- British development: 英国的发展
- frequent labor strikes: 频繁的劳工罢工
- many of them: 其中许多
- caused by: 由...引起的
- conflicts: 冲突
- between: 在...之间
- rival labor unions: 敌对工会
