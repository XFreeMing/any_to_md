1. Piliavin (2003) and Susan Andersen (1998) reviewed studies that showed that youth who engaged in community service projects, school-based "service learning," or tutoring children develop social skills and positive social values. (Myers, 2012)

> 皮利亚文（2003）和苏珊·安徒生（1998）审查了研究，这些研究显示参与社区服务项目、基于学校的"服务学习"或辅导儿童的年轻人发展社交技能和积极的社会价值观。

- reviewed: 审查
- Piliavin: 皮利亚文
- Susan Andersen: 苏珊·安徒生
- studies: 研究
- showed that: 显示
- youth: 年轻人
- engaged in: 参与
- community service projects: 社区服务项目
- school-based service learning: 基于学校的服务学习
- tutoring children: 辅导儿童
- develop: 发展
- social skills: 社交技能
- positive social values: 积极的社会价值观

2. University ethics committees review social psychological research to ensure that it will treat people humanely and that the scientific merit justifies any temporary deception or distress. (Myers, 2012)

> 大学伦理委员会审查社会心理学研究，以确保它将人道地对待人们，并且科学价值证明任何临时欺骗或痛苦是合理的。

- review: 审查
- university ethics committees: 大学伦理委员会
- social psychological research: 社会心理学研究
- ensure that: 确保
- treat people humanely: 人道地对待人们
- scientific merit: 科学价值
- justifies: 证明...是合理的
- temporary deception: 临时欺骗
- distress: 痛苦

3. When faced with a problem, children at this stage (formal operational stage) are able to review all the possible ways of solving it and go through them theoretically in order to reach a solution. (Giddens, 2009)

> 当面临问题时，这个阶段（形式运算阶段）的儿童能够审查所有可能的解决方法，并在理论上通过它们以找到解决方案。

- review: 审查
- when faced with: 当面临
- problem: 问题
- children: 儿童
- this stage: 这个阶段
- formal operational stage: 形式运算阶段
- able to: 能够
- all the possible ways: 所有可能的方法
- solving it: 解决它
- go through them: 通过它们
- theoretically: 理论上
- in order to: 以便
- reach a solution: 找到解决方案

4. Once the problem is identified, the next step taken in the research process is usually to review the available evidence in the field; it might be that previous research has already satisfactorily clarified the problem. (Giddens, 2009)

> 一旦问题被确定，研究过程中采取的下一步通常是审查该领域的现有证据；可能是先前的研究已经令人满意地澄清了问题。

- review: 审查
- once: 一旦
- problem is identified: 问题被确定
- next step: 下一步
- taken in: 在...中采取
- research process: 研究过程
- usually: 通常
- available evidence: 现有证据
- field: 领域
- might be that: 可能是
- previous research: 先前的研究
- already satisfactorily clarified: 已经令人满意地澄清
