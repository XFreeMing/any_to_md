1. So Hobbes would probably argue that even if people did have a natural right to punish offenders, this would rarely be used with any effect unless a single, stable, authority existed: for example, within a tribe or group an acknowledged leader to adjudicate disputes and enforce judgements. (Wolff, 2006)

> 所以霍布斯可能会认为，即使人们确实有惩罚罪犯的自然权利，这也很少会被有效使用，除非存在一个单一的、稳定的权威：例如，在一个部落或群体内有一个公认的领导者来裁决争端并执行判决。

2. There are major inconsistencies between the accounts given by different political philosophers. This raises not only the question of how to adjudicate between different accounts, but also leads to the troubling thought that a statement of what natural rights we have often seems little more than one person's opinion. (Wolff, 2006)

> 不同政治哲学家给出的说明之间存在重大不一致。这不仅提出了如何在不同说明之间裁决的问题，而且导致了一个令人不安的想法，即关于我们拥有什么自然权利的陈述往往似乎只不过是一个人的意见。
