1. In theory, all of China's emperors were absolute rulers. Nevertheless, over the centuries, their power was frequently contested by feudal lords, military generals, and government officials. (Fiero, 2011b)

> 理论上，中国所有的皇帝都是绝对统治者。然而，几个世纪以来，他们的权力经常受到封建领主、军事将领和政府官员的挑战。

- contested: 挑战
- in theory: 理论上
- all of China's emperors: 中国所有的皇帝
- absolute rulers: 绝对统治者
- nevertheless: 然而
- over the centuries: 几个世纪以来
- power: 权力
- frequently: 经常
- feudal lords: 封建领主
- military generals: 军事将领
- government officials: 政府官员

2. In recent years, globalization has become a hotly debated topic. Most people accept that there are important transformations occurring around us, but the extent to which it is valid to explain these as 'globalization' is contested. (Giddens, 2009)

> 近年来，全球化已成为一个激烈辩论的话题。大多数人接受我们周围正在发生重要的转变，但将这些解释为"全球化"的有效程度是有争议的。

- contested: 有争议的
- in recent years: 近年来
- globalization: 全球化
- hotly debated topic: 激烈辩论的话题
- most people: 大多数人
- accept that: 接受
- important transformations: 重要的转变
- occurring around us: 我们周围正在发生的
- extent: 程度
- valid to explain: 有效解释
- as 'globalization': 作为"全球化"

3. Some research problems in sociology are theoretical rather than empirical. Often, sociologists need systematically to think through their key concepts in order to clarify them and expand their reach. The concept of power, for example, has been contested, provoking much disagreement. Is power something people can hold, like other material possessions? Is it something that can be shared? (Guinn, 2006)

> 社会学中的一些研究问题是理论性的而不是经验性的。通常，社会学家需要系统地思考他们的关键概念，以便澄清它们并扩大它们的范围。例如，权力的概念一直有争议，引起了很多分歧。权力是人们可以持有的东西，就像其他物质财产吗？它是可以分享的东西吗？

- contested: 有争议的
- research problems: 研究问题
- sociology: 社会学
- theoretical rather than empirical: 理论性的而不是经验性的
- often: 通常
- sociologists: 社会学家
- systematically: 系统地
- think through: 思考
- key concepts: 关键概念
- clarify: 澄清
- expand their reach: 扩大它们的范围
- concept of power: 权力的概念
- for example: 例如
- provoking: 引起
- much disagreement: 很多分歧
- power: 权力
- people can hold: 人们可以持有
- material possessions: 物质财产
- shared: 分享

4. The claims of sociobiologists, such as Barash are fiercely contested, especially as regards any implications for human sexual behavior. (Guinn, 2006)

> 社会生物学家的主张，如巴拉什的主张，受到激烈争议，特别是关于对人类性行为的任何影响。

- contested: 争议
- claims: 主张
- sociobiologists: 社会生物学家
- such as: 如
- Barash: 巴拉什
- fiercely: 激烈地
- especially: 特别是
- as regards: 关于
- implications: 影响
- human sexual behavior: 人类性行为
