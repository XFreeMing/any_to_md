1. Social psychology is criticized for being trivial because it documents things that seem obvious. (Myers, 2012)

> 社会心理学因琐碎而受到批评，因为它记录了似乎显而易见的事情。

- criticized for: 因...而受到批评
- social psychology: 社会心理学
- trivial: 琐碎的
- documents: 记录
- things: 事情
- seem obvious: 似乎显而易见

2. No one, it would seem, could reasonably be criticized for any action they take to defend themselves. (Wolff, 2006)

> 似乎没有人可以因为为保护自己而采取的任何行动而受到合理的批评。

- criticized for: 因...而受到批评
- no one: 没有人
- it would seem: 似乎
- reasonably: 合理地
- action: 行动
- defend themselves: 保护自己

3. After Bernini problems failed, he proposed making the towers free-standing, but he was forced to abandon the plan when it was severely criticized. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在贝尔尼尼的问题失败后，他提议使塔楼独立，但当它受到严厉批评时，他被迫放弃了这个计划。

- criticized: 被批评
- after: 在...后
- Bernini problems: 贝尔尼尼的问题
- failed: 失败
- proposed: 提议
- making the towers free-standing: 使塔楼独立
- forced to abandon: 被迫放弃
- plan: 计划
- severely: 严厉地

4. Inspired by the Gospels, reformers criticized the church for its wealth and involvement in worldly affairs. (Perry, 2010)

> 受福音书的启发，改革者批评教会因其财富和对世俗事务的参与。

- criticized: 批评
- inspired by: 受...启发
- Gospels: 福音书
- reformers: 改革者
- church: 教会
- wealth: 财富
- involvement: 参与
- worldly affairs: 世俗事务
