1. This balance is disturbed if new species 'invade' - try to make the lake their home. Some of the organisms, which used to proliferate in the central area of the lake, are driven out to suffer a more precarious existence around its fringes. The invading species are their successors in the central sections. (Giddens, 2009)

> 如果新物种"入侵"——试图将湖泊作为它们的家园，这种平衡就会被扰乱。一些曾经在湖泊中央区域大量繁殖的生物被驱赶到边缘，在那里遭受更不稳定的生存。入侵物种是它们在中央区域的继承者。

- disturbed: 被扰乱
- new species: 新物种
- invade: 入侵
- try to make: 试图将...作为
- lake their home: 湖泊作为它们的家园
- organisms: 生物
- used to: 曾经
- central area: 中央区域
- of the lake: 湖泊的
- driven out: 被驱赶
- suffer: 遭受
- more precarious existence: 更不稳定的生存
- around its fringes: 在边缘
- invading species: 入侵物种
- successors: 继承者
- central sections: 中央区域
