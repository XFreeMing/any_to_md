1. Wyclif alleged that there was no basis in Scripture for papal claims of temporal authority and advocated that the popes be stripped of their authority and their property. (Spielvogel, 2010)

- advocated: 主张
- Wyclif: 威克里夫
- alleged that: 声称
- no basis: 没有基础

- papal claims: 教皇声称
- temporal authority: 世俗权威
- popes: 教皇
- stripped of: 被剥夺
- authority: 权威
- property: 财产

2. Here, Jean-Jacques Rousseau advocated a return to nature, arguing, like Locke, that humans are born good, not in sin, and that they use their innate sense or instincts to distinguish between good and bad, that is, between what makes them happy and sad. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在这里，让-雅克·卢梭主张回归自然，像洛克一样认为，人类生来是善良的，不是在罪恶中，他们使用他们天生的感觉或本能来区分好坏，也就是说，区分什么使他们快乐和悲伤。

- advocated: 主张
- Jean-Jacques Rousseau: 让-雅克·卢梭
- return to nature: 回归自然
- arguing: 认为
- like Locke: 像洛克一样

- not in sin: 不是在罪恶中
- use: 使用
- innate sense: 天生的感觉
- instincts: 本能
- distinguish between: 区分
- good and bad: 好坏
- that is: 也就是说
- makes them happy and sad: 使他们快乐和悲伤

3. Illich advocated what he called the deschooling of society. Compulsory schooling, he pointed out, is a relatively recent invention, and there is no reason why it should be accepted as inevitable. (Giddens, 2009)

> 伊利奇主张他所谓的社会的去学校化。他指出，义务教育是一个相对较新的发明，没有理由应该将其视为不可避免的。

- advocated: 主张
- Illich: 伊利奇
- deschooling of society: 社会的去学校化
- compulsory schooling: 义务教育
- pointed out: 指出
- relatively recent invention: 相对较新的发明
- no reason: 没有理由
- accepted as inevitable: 被视为不可避免的

4. Hoping to blend the best of individualist and collectivist values, some social scientists—myself included—have advocated a communitarian synthesis that aims to balance individual rights with the collective right to communal well-being. (Myers, 2012)

> 希望融合个人主义和集体主义价值观的最佳部分，一些社会科学家——包括我自己——主张一种社群主义综合，旨在平衡个人权利与集体对社区福祉的权利。

- advocated: 主张

- best of: 最佳部分
- individualist values: 个人主义价值观
