1. Some recent authors argue that we should evaluate individuals' class location not only, or even mainly, in terms of economics and employment, but also in relation to cultural factors such as lifestyle and consumption patterns. (Giddens, 2009)

> 一些最近的作者认为，我们应该评估个人的阶级位置，不仅或甚至主要从经济和就业方面，而且还要从文化因素如生活方式和消费模式方面。

- evaluate: 评估
- some recent authors: 一些最近的作者
- argue that: 认为
- individuals' class location: 个人的阶级位置
- not only: 不仅
- even mainly: 甚至主要
- in terms of: 从...方面
- economics and employment: 经济和就业
- but also: 而且
- in relation to: 从...方面
- cultural factors: 文化因素
- lifestyle: 生活方式
- consumption patterns: 消费模式

2. Becoming a master required completing a masterpiece (work of art) that was evaluated by the leaders of the guild. Guilds not only controlled training but limited competition from artists outside their towns, investigated disputes among members, and saw to the social and economic needs of members, such as providing for burials, pensions, and the care of widows. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 成为大师需要完成一件由行会领袖评估的杰作（艺术作品）。行会不仅控制培训，还限制来自城镇外艺术家的竞争，调查成员之间的争端，并照顾成员的社会和经济需求，如提供葬礼、养老金和寡妇的照顾。

- evaluated: 被评估
- becoming a master: 成为大师
- required: 需要
- completing: 完成
- masterpiece: 杰作
- work of art: 艺术作品
- leaders of the guild: 行会领袖
- guilds: 行会
- not only controlled: 不仅控制
- training: 培训
- limited competition: 限制竞争
- artists outside their towns: 城镇外的艺术家
- investigated disputes: 调查争端
- among members: 成员之间
- saw to: 照顾
- social and economic needs: 社会和经济需求
- providing for: 提供
- burials: 葬礼
- pensions: 养老金
- care of widows: 寡妇的照顾

3. While Roman civilization left abundant visual and literary resources, the absence of surviving examples in music make it almost impossible to evaluate the Roman contribution in this domain. (Fiero, 2011)

> 虽然罗马文明留下了丰富的视觉和文学资源，但音乐中缺乏现存的例子使得几乎不可能评估罗马在这一领域的贡献。

- evaluate: 评估
- while: 虽然
- Roman civilization: 罗马文明
- left: 留下
- abundant visual and literary resources: 丰富的视觉和文学资源
- absence: 缺乏
- surviving examples: 现存的例子
- music: 音乐
- make it almost impossible: 使得几乎不可能
- contribution: 贡献
- domain: 领域

4. Sociology can help us to evaluate policies and proposals aimed at providing solutions to environmental problems. (Giddens, 2009)

> 社会学可以帮助我们评估旨在为环境问题提供解决方案的政策和建议。

- evaluate: 评估
- sociology: 社会学
- can help us to: 可以帮助我们
- policies: 政策
- proposals: 建议
- aimed at: 旨在
- providing solutions: 提供解决方案
- environmental problems: 环境问题
