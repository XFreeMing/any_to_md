1. Telling some people that "Maria played basketball" and others that "Mark played basketball" may make Maria seem more athletic than Mark (Biernat, 2003). Stereotypes therefore influence how we construe someone's behavior. (Myers, 2012)

> 告诉一些人"玛丽亚打篮球"，告诉其他人"马克打篮球"，可能使玛丽亚看起来比马克更运动（Biernat, 2003）。因此，刻板印象影响我们如何解释某人的行为。

- construe: 解释
- telling: 告诉
- some people: 一些人
- Maria played basketball: 玛丽亚打篮球
- others: 其他人
- Mark played basketball: 马克打篮球
- may make: 可能使
- seem more athletic: 看起来更运动
- stereotypes: 刻板印象
- therefore: 因此
- influence: 影响
- someone's behavior: 某人的行为

2. Chapter 1 noted a significant fact about the human mind: Our preconceptions guide how we perceive and interpret information. We construe the world through belief-tinted glasses. "Sure, preconceptions matter," people will agree; yet they fail to realize how great the effect is on themselves. (Myers, 2012)

> 第1章指出了关于人类思维的一个重要事实：我们的先入之见指导我们如何感知和解释信息。我们通过信念着色的眼镜来解释世界。"当然，先入之见很重要，"人们会同意；然而他们未能意识到对自己影响有多大。

- construe: 解释
- chapter 1: 第1章
- noted: 指出
- significant fact: 重要事实
- human mind: 人类思维
- preconceptions: 先入之见
- guide: 指导
- perceive: 感知
- interpret information: 解释信息
- world: 世界
- through: 通过
- belief-tinted glasses: 信念着色的眼镜
- preconceptions matter: 先入之见很重要
- people will agree: 人们会同意
- yet: 然而
- fail to realize: 未能意识到
- how great the effect is: 影响有多大
- themselves: 自己

3. A given social situation often affects different people differently. Because our minds do not see reality identically or objectively, we respond to a situation as we construe it. (Myers, 2012)

> 一个给定的社会情况往往对不同的人产生不同的影响。因为我们的思维不是相同或客观地看待现实，我们按照我们解释的方式来回应情况。

- construe: 解释
- given social situation: 给定的社会情况
- often affects: 往往影响
- different people: 不同的人
- differently: 不同地
- because: 因为
- minds: 思维
- do not see: 不是看待
- reality: 现实
- identically or objectively: 相同或客观地
- respond to: 回应
- situation: 情况
- as: 按照

4. Yet there is no reason why an acceptance that differences exist between men and women should imply that women are weaker: this is simply how we often construe the position. (Wolff, 2006)

> 然而，没有理由为什么接受男女之间存在差异应该意味着妇女更弱：这只是我们经常如何解释这个立场。

- construe: 解释
- yet: 然而
- no reason: 没有理由
- acceptance: 接受
- differences exist: 差异存在
- between men and women: 男女之间
- should imply: 应该意味着
- women are weaker: 妇女更弱
- simply: 只是
- how we often: 我们经常如何
- position: 立场
