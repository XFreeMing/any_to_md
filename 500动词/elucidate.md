1. During a period when literacy was rare and class-based, the book was a symbol of authority and dominance, an object that must have mystified many people as much as it elucidated matters for others. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在识字率低且基于阶级的时期，书是权威和支配的象征，一个对象，它一定让许多人感到困惑，就像它为其他人阐明问题一样。

- elucidated: 阐明
- during a period: 在...时期
- literacy: 识字率
- rare: 低的
- class-based: 基于阶级的
- book: 书
- symbol: 象征
- authority: 权威
- dominance: 支配
- object: 对象
- must have mystified: 一定让...感到困惑
- many people: 许多人
- as much as: 就像
- matters: 问题
- others: 其他人

2. The purpose of this chapter has been to explore the troublesome question of what atheism actually means, and to elucidate and justify the specific way in which it is being used in this volume. (Bullivant & Ruse, 2014)

> 本章的目的是探索无神论实际上意味着什么这个麻烦的问题，并阐明和证明它在本卷中使用的特定方式。

- elucidate: 阐明
- purpose: 目的
- this chapter: 本章
- explore: 探索
- troublesome question: 麻烦的问题
- what atheism actually means: 无神论实际上意味着什么
- justify: 证明
- specific way: 特定方式
- being used: 被使用
- this volume: 本卷

3. Durkheim saw sociology as a new science that could be used to elucidate traditional philosophical questions by examining them in an empirical manner. (Giddens, 2009)

> 涂尔干将社会学视为一门新科学，可以通过以经验方式检验传统哲学问题来阐明它们。

- elucidate: 阐明
- Durkheim: 涂尔干
- saw sociology as: 将社会学视为
- new science: 新科学
- could be used to: 可以用来
- traditional philosophical questions: 传统哲学问题
- examining: 检验
- empirical manner: 经验方式

4. The scholastic masters used reason not to challenge but to serve faith: to elucidate, clarify, and buttress it. They did not break with the central concern of Christianity: earning God's grace and achieving salvation. (Perry, 2010)

> 经院大师使用理性不是为了挑战而是为了服务信仰：阐明、澄清和支持它。他们没有背离基督教的核心关注：获得上帝的恩典和实现救赎。

- elucidate: 阐明
- scholastic masters: 经院大师
- used reason: 使用理性
- not to challenge: 不是为了挑战
- serve faith: 服务信仰
- clarify: 澄清
- buttress: 支持
- did not break with: 没有背离
- central concern: 核心关注
- Christianity: 基督教
- earning: 获得
- God's grace: 上帝的恩典
- achieving salvation: 实现救赎
