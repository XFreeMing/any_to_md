1. Sometimes a researcher will use more than one measure of central tendency to avoid giving a deceptive picture of the average. More often, he will calculate the standard deviation for the data in question. (Giddens, 2009)

> 有时，研究人员会使用不止一种集中趋势的测量方法，以避免给出平均值的误导性图景。更常见的是，他将计算相关数据的标准差。

- calculate: 计算
- sometimes: 有时
- researcher: 研究人员
- use more than one measure: 使用不止一种测量方法
- central tendency: 集中趋势
- avoid giving: 避免给出
- deceptive picture: 误导性图景
- average: 平均值
- more often: 更常见的是
- standard deviation: 标准差
- data in question: 相关数据

2. Another astronomer, Eratosthenes (c. 275--194 B.C.), determined that the earth was round and calculated the earth's circumference at 24,675 miles, within 200 miles of the actual figure. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 另一位天文学家，埃拉托色尼（约公元前275-194年），确定地球是圆的，并计算出地球的周长为24,675英里，与实际数字相差200英里以内。

- calculated: 计算出
- another astronomer: 另一位天文学家
- Eratosthenes: 埃拉托色尼
- determined that: 确定
- earth was round: 地球是圆的
- earth's circumference: 地球的周长
- 24,675 miles: 24,675英里
- within 200 miles: 200英里以内
- actual figure: 实际数字

3. Therefore the indirect utilitarian might calculate that the general happiness will best be served by ensuring that no one is punished unless they are proved guilty. (Wolff, 2006)

> 因此，间接功利主义者可能会计算出，通过确保没有人被惩罚，除非他们被证明有罪，将最好地服务于总体幸福。

- calculate: 计算出
- therefore: 因此
- indirect utilitarian: 间接功利主义者
- might: 可能会
- general happiness: 总体幸福
- best be served: 最好地服务于
- ensuring that: 确保
- no one is punished: 没有人被惩罚
- unless: 除非
- proved guilty: 被证明有罪

4. When committing a crime of passion, people don't pause to calculate the consequences (which include life in prison without parole as another potent deterrent). (Myers, 2012)

> 当犯下激情犯罪时，人们不会停下来计算后果（包括无假释的终身监禁作为另一个强有力的威慑）。

- calculate: 计算
- when committing: 当犯下
- crime of passion: 激情犯罪
- people: 人们
- don't pause to: 不会停下来
- consequences: 后果
- include: 包括
- life in prison: 终身监禁
- without parole: 无假释
- potent deterrent: 强有力的威慑
