3. Foreign intervention complicated the Spanish Civil War. Franco's forces were aided by arms, money, and men from the fascist regimes of Italy and Germany while the government was assisted by forty thousand foreign volunteers and trucks, planes, tanks, and military advisers from the Soviet Union. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 外国干预使西班牙内战变得复杂。佛朗哥的军队得到了来自意大利和德国法西斯政权的武器、金钱和人员的援助，而政府得到了四万名外国志愿者以及来自苏联的卡车、飞机、坦克和军事顾问的协助。

- fascist regimes: 法西斯政权

- trucks: 卡车
- planes: 飞机
- tanks: 坦克
