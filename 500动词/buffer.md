1. In their "terror management theory," Jeff Greenberg, Sheldon Solomon, and Tom Pyszczynski propose another reason why positive self-esteem is adaptive: It buffers anxiety, including anxiety related to our certain death. (Myers, 2012)

> 在他们的"恐怖管理理论"中，杰夫·格林伯格、谢尔登·所罗门和汤姆·皮兹辛斯基提出了另一个为什么积极的自尊是适应的原因：它缓冲了焦虑，包括与我们必然死亡相关的焦虑。

- buffers: 缓冲
- terror management theory: 恐怖管理理论
- Jeff Greenberg: 杰夫·格林伯格
- Sheldon Solomon: 谢尔登·所罗门
- Tom Pyszczynski: 汤姆·皮兹辛斯基
- propose: 提出
- another reason: 另一个原因
- positive self-esteem: 积极的自尊
- adaptive: 适应的
- anxiety: 焦虑
- including: 包括
- related to: 与...相关的
- certain death: 必然死亡

2. Idealization helped buffer conflict, bolster satisfaction, and turn self-perceived frogs into princes or princesses. (Myers, 2012)

> 理想化有助于缓冲冲突，增强满意度，并将自我感知的青蛙变成王子或公主。

- buffer: 缓冲
- idealization: 理想化
- helped: 有助于
- conflict: 冲突
- bolster: 增强
- satisfaction: 满意度
- turn: 将...变成
- self-perceived frogs: 自我感知的青蛙
- princes or princesses: 王子或公主

3. There is a second, more prosaic, reason why marriage promotes happiness, or at least buffers us from misery. Marriage offers the roles of spouse and parent, which can provide additional sources of self-esteem. (Myers, 2012)

> 有第二个、更平凡的原因，为什么婚姻促进幸福，或者至少缓冲我们免受痛苦。婚姻提供配偶和父母的角色，这可以提供额外的自尊来源。

- buffers: 缓冲
- second: 第二个
- more prosaic: 更平凡的
- reason: 原因
- marriage: 婚姻
- promotes happiness: 促进幸福
- at least: 至少
- misery: 痛苦
- offers: 提供
- roles: 角色
- spouse: 配偶
- parent: 父母
- provide: 提供
- additional sources: 额外的来源
- self-esteem: 自尊
