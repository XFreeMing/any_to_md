1. Frederick William strove to maintain a highly efficient bureaucracy of civil service workers. It had its own code in which obedience, honor, and service to the king were the highest duty. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 腓特烈·威廉努力维持一个高效的公务员官僚机构。它有自己的准则，其中服从、荣誉和为国王服务是最高职责。

- Frederick William: 腓特烈·威廉

- highly efficient bureaucracy: 高效的官僚机构
- civil service workers: 公务员
- its own code: 自己的准则
- obedience: 服从
- honor: 荣誉
- service to the king: 为国王服务
- highest duty: 最高职责

2. Plato claimed that Egyptian art had not changed in 10,000 years. The reality is more complex, but it is fair to say that most Egyptian artists did not strive for innovation or originality, but adhered instead to traditional formulations that expressed specific ideas. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 柏拉图声称埃及艺术在10,000年中没有改变。现实更加复杂，但可以说，大多数埃及艺术家并不努力追求创新或原创性，而是坚持表达特定思想的传统表述。

- strive for: 努力追求
- Plato: 柏拉图
- claimed that: 声称
- Egyptian art: 埃及艺术
- not changed: 没有改变
- 10,000 years: 10,000年
- reality: 现实
- more complex: 更加复杂
- fair to say: 可以说
- most Egyptian artists: 大多数埃及艺术家
- innovation: 创新
- originality: 原创性
- adhered instead to: 而是坚持
- traditional formulations: 传统表述
- expressed: 表达
- specific ideas: 特定思想

- strive: 努力追求
- striking: 令人震惊的
- economic growth: 经济增长
- affluent countries: 富裕国家
- failed to satisfy: 未能满足

- most for wealth: 最努力追求财富
- tend to live: 往往生活
- lower well-being: 较低的幸福感

4. But the text does strive to interpret and synthesize in order to provide students with a frame of reference with which to comprehend the principal events and eras in Western history.

- frame of reference: 参考框架
- comprehend: 理解

- eras: 时代
