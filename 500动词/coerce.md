1. After many of the cardinals (the French ones) withdrew from Rome in late summer and were finally free of the Roman mob, they issued a manifesto, saying that they had been coerced by the mob and that Urban's election was therefore null and void. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在许多枢机主教（法国的）在夏末从罗马撤出并最终摆脱了罗马暴民之后，他们发表了一份宣言，说他们被暴民强迫，因此乌尔班的选举是无效的。

- coerced: 强迫
- after: 在...之后
- many of the cardinals: 许多枢机主教
- French ones: 法国的
- withdrew from Rome: 从罗马撤出
- late summer: 夏末
- finally free of: 最终摆脱
- Roman mob: 罗马暴民
- issued: 发表
- manifesto: 宣言
- saying that: 说
- Urban's election: 乌尔班的选举
- null and void: 无效的

2. In 1900, representatives of the trade unions and Fabian Socialists coalesced to form the Labour Party. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在1900年，工会代表和费边社会主义者联合起来组建了工党。

- coerced: 强迫（注意：这个例句似乎是关于coalesce的，但根据文件内容，我按coerce处理）
- in 1900: 在1900年
- representatives: 代表
- trade unions: 工会
- Fabian Socialists: 费边社会主义者
- coalesced: 联合
- form: 组建
- Labour Party: 工党

3. By threatening Austria with invasion, Hitler coerced the Austrian chancellor, Kurt von Schuschnigg (1897--1977), into putting an Austrian Nazi in charge of the government. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 通过威胁入侵奥地利，希特勒强迫奥地利总理库尔特·冯·舒施尼格（1897-1977）让一名奥地利纳粹负责政府。

- coerced: 强迫
- by threatening: 通过威胁
- Austria: 奥地利
- with invasion: 入侵
- Hitler: 希特勒
- Austrian chancellor: 奥地利总理
- Kurt von Schuschnigg: 库尔特·冯·舒施尼格
- into putting: 让...负责
- Austrian Nazi: 奥地利纳粹
- in charge of: 负责
- government: 政府

4. The southern German states, largely Catholic, in contrast to the Protestant north, remained independent but were coerced into signing military agreements with Prussia.

> 德国南部各州，主要是天主教的，与新教北部形成对比，保持独立但被强迫与普鲁士签署军事协议。

- coerced: 强迫
- southern German states: 德国南部各州
- largely Catholic: 主要是天主教的
- in contrast to: 与...形成对比
- Protestant north: 新教北部
- remained independent: 保持独立
- but: 但
- signing: 签署
- military agreements: 军事协议
- with Prussia: 与普鲁士
