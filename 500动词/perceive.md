1. At Pompeii, wall painters sometimes used color to suggest deep space, a technique known as atmospheric or aerial perspective. This method recognizes the eye's inability to perceive color at great distances, and that specific colors become light blue or gray at the horizon line. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在庞贝，墙壁画家有时使用颜色来暗示深度空间，这是一种被称为大气或空中透视的技术。这种方法认识到眼睛无法在很远的距离感知颜色，以及特定颜色在地平线处变成浅蓝色或灰色。

- perceive: 感知
- Pompeii: 庞贝
- wall painters: 墙壁画家
- sometimes: 有时
- used color: 使用颜色
- suggest deep space: 暗示深度空间
- technique: 技术
- atmospheric or aerial perspective: 大气或空中透视
- method: 方法
- recognizes: 认识到
- eye's inability: 眼睛的无法
- great distances: 很远的距离
- specific colors: 特定颜色
- light blue or gray: 浅蓝色或灰色
- horizon line: 地平线

2. For Renaissance artists, the painting constituted a window on nature: the picture plane, that is, the two-dimensional surface of the panel or canvas, was conceived as a transparent glass or window through which one might perceive the three-dimensional world. (Fiero, 2011)

> 对文艺复兴艺术家来说，绘画构成了自然的窗口：画面，即面板或画布的两维表面，被设想为透明的玻璃或窗口，通过它人们可以感知三维世界。

- perceive: 感知
- Renaissance artists: 文艺复兴艺术家
- painting: 绘画
- constituted: 构成
- window on nature: 自然的窗口
- picture plane: 画面
- two-dimensional surface: 两维表面
- panel or canvas: 面板或画布
- conceived as: 被设想为
- transparent glass or window: 透明的玻璃或窗口
- through which: 通过它
- three-dimensional world: 三维世界

3. The greatest obstacle to the emergence of the general will that Rousseau sees is not individuals' failure to perceive it, but their failure to be sufficiently motivated to act upon it. (Wolff, 2006)

> 卢梭看到的公意出现的最大障碍不是个人未能感知它，而是他们未能有足够的动机来行动。

- perceive: 感知
- greatest obstacle: 最大障碍
- emergence: 出现
- general will: 公意
- Rousseau: 卢梭
- individuals' failure: 个人的失败
- sufficiently motivated: 足够的动机
- act upon it: 行动

4. Although social behaviour is guided to some extent by forces such as roles, norms and shared expectations, individuals perceive reality differently according to their backgrounds, interests and motivations.

> 虽然社会行为在某种程度上受到角色、规范和共同期望等力量的指导，但个人根据他们的背景、兴趣和动机以不同的方式感知现实。

- although: 虽然
- social behaviour: 社会行为

- forces: 力量
