1. We have now seen that a society's laws and, therefore, its behavior can have a strong influence on its racial attitudes. A danger lies in the possibility of employing the same idea for political socialization on a mass scale. (Myers, 2012)

> 我们现在已经看到，一个社会的法律，因此它的行为可以对其种族态度产生强烈影响。危险在于可能大规模地使用同样的想法进行政治社会化。

- employing: 使用
- society's laws: 社会的法律
- behavior: 行为
- strong influence: 强烈影响
- racial attitudes: 种族态度
- danger: 危险
- possibility: 可能
- same idea: 同样的想法
- political socialization: 政治社会化
- mass scale: 大规模

2. For example, he found that people who have been unemployed for long periods of time were more committed to the concept of work than those who were employed. (Giddens, 2009)

> 例如，他发现长期失业的人比那些被雇佣的人更致力于工作的概念。

- employed: 被雇佣
- for example: 例如
- found that: 发现
- people: 人们
- unemployed: 失业的
- long periods of time: 长期
- more committed to: 更致力于
- concept of work: 工作的概念
- those who were: 那些...的人

- employing: 使用
- often: 经常
- most sophisticated tools: 最复杂的工具
- remote-sensing: 遥感
- satellite photography: 卫星摄影
- move quickly: 快速移动
- unscrupulously: 肆无忌惮地
- site: 地点
- careless of: 不在乎
- destroy: 破坏
- search for treasure: 寻找宝藏

4. With so many men off fighting at the front, women were called on to take over jobs and responsibilities that had not been available to them before. They were now employed in jobs that had earlier been considered ''beyond the capacity of women'', including such occupations as chimney sweeps, truck drivers, farm laborers, and factory workers in heavy industry. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 由于许多男人在前线战斗，妇女被要求接管以前对她们不可用的工作和责任。她们现在被雇佣在以前被认为"超出妇女能力"的工作中，包括烟囱清扫工、卡车司机、农场工人和重工业工厂工人等职业。

- employed: 被雇佣
- so many men: 许多男人
- off fighting: 在前线战斗
- front: 前线
- women: 妇女
- called on to take over: 被要求接管
- jobs: 工作
- responsibilities: 责任
- not been available: 不可用
- before: 以前
- now: 现在
- earlier been considered: 以前被认为
- beyond the capacity of women: 超出妇女能力
- occupations: 职业
- chimney sweeps: 烟囱清扫工
- truck drivers: 卡车司机
- farm laborers: 农场工人
- factory workers: 工厂工人
- heavy industry: 重工业
