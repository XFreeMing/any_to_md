## 推进；前进

1. As the next step, we are going to advance south of the Caucasus and then help the rebels in Iran and Iraq against the English. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 作为下一步，我们将向高加索以南推进，然后帮助伊朗和伊拉克的叛军对抗英国人。

- advance: 推进，前进
- next step: 下一步
- south of: ...以南
- Caucasus: 高加索
- help: 帮助
- rebels: 叛军
- Iran: 伊朗
- Iraq: 伊拉克
- against: 对抗
- English: 英国人

2. Makeshift bridges enabled the Allies to cross the Rhine in some areas and advance deeper into Germany. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 临时桥梁使盟军能够在某些地区渡过莱茵河，并深入德国推进。

- advance: 推进，前进
- makeshift bridges: 临时桥梁
- enabled: 使能够
- Allies: 盟军
- cross: 渡过
- Rhine: 莱茵河
- some areas: 某些地区
- deeper into: 深入
- Germany: 德国

## 增进；提升

1. Plato advocates essentially a system of benevolent dictatorship. But even if the dictator wants to advance the interests of the people, how are those interests to be known? (Wolff, 2006)

> 柏拉图基本上主张一个仁慈独裁制度。但即使独裁者想要增进人民的利益，这些利益如何被知道？

- advance: 增进
- Plato: 柏拉图
- advocates: 主张
- essentially: 基本上
- system: 制度
- benevolent dictatorship: 仁慈独裁
- but: 但
- even if: 即使
- dictator: 独裁者
- wants to: 想要
- interests of the people: 人民的利益
- how are those interests to be known: 这些利益如何被知道

2. The excerpts below suggest that while humanism was an enterprise dominated by men, it provided women with the tools by which they might advance their intellectual status and voice their own complaints. (Fiero, 2011)

> 下面的摘录表明，虽然人文主义是一个由男性主导的事业，但它为女性提供了工具，通过它们她们可以提升她们的智力地位并表达自己的抱怨。

- advance: 提升
- excerpts: 摘录
- suggest that: 表明
- while: 虽然
- humanism: 人文主义
- enterprise: 事业
- dominated by men: 由男性主导
- provided women with: 为女性提供
- tools: 工具
- by which: 通过它们
- might: 可以
- intellectual status: 智力地位
- voice: 表达
- their own complaints: 自己的抱怨

3. We cannot conduct true experiments with large-scale social events. But we can identify the conceptual similarities between the large scale and the small, as the astronomers have between the planets and Newton's apple. That is why the games people play in our laboratory may advance our understanding of war, peace, and social justice. (Myers, 2012)

> 我们无法对大规模社会事件进行真正的实验。但我们可以识别大规模和小规模之间的概念相似性，就像天文学家在行星和牛顿的苹果之间所做的那样。这就是为什么人们在我们的实验室中玩的游戏可能会增进我们对战争、和平和社会正义的理解。

- advance: 增进
- cannot conduct: 无法进行
- true experiments: 真正的实验
- large-scale social events: 大规模社会事件
- but: 但
- identify: 识别
- conceptual similarities: 概念相似性
- between: 在...之间
- large scale and the small: 大规模和小规模
- astronomers: 天文学家
- planets: 行星
- Newton's apple: 牛顿的苹果
- that is why: 这就是为什么
- games: 游戏
- people play: 人们玩的
- laboratory: 实验室
- may: 可能会
- understanding: 理解
- war: 战争
- peace: 和平
- social justice: 社会正义
