1. Some have argued, for example, that the outlook he called 'the spirit of capitalism' can be discerned in the early Italian merchant cities in the twelfth century, long before Calvinism was ever heard of. (Giddens, 2009)

> 例如，一些人认为，他所谓的"资本主义精神"的展望可以在12世纪早期的意大利商业城市中被识别出来，远在加尔文主义被听说之前。

- discerned: 被识别出来
- some have argued: 一些人认为
- for example: 例如
- outlook: 展望
- spirit of capitalism: 资本主义精神
- early Italian merchant cities: 早期的意大利商业城市
- twelfth century: 12世纪
- long before: 远在...之前
- Calvinism: 加尔文主义
- ever heard of: 被听说

2. On one reading of the text we can discern at least four strands of argument in Locke's defense of initial appropriation of property. (Wolff, 2006)

> 在对文本的一种解读中，我们可以在洛克对财产初始占有的辩护中识别出至少四条论证线索。

- discern: 识别
- one reading: 一种解读
- text: 文本
- at least: 至少
- four strands: 四条线索
- argument: 论证
- Locke's defense: 洛克的辩护
- initial appropriation: 初始占有
- property: 财产

3. Although Marx refused to describe in detail how this transitionary society would be organized, its broad shape can be discerned from his admiration for the Paris Commune of 1871, which was a short-lived experiment in what approximated to direct democracy. (Heywood, 2013)

> 虽然马克思拒绝详细描述这个过渡社会将如何组织，但从他对1871年巴黎公社的钦佩中可以识别出它的大致形状，这是一个短暂的直接民主近似实验。

- discerned: 被识别出来
- although: 虽然
- Marx: 马克思
- refused to describe: 拒绝描述
- in detail: 详细地
- transitionary society: 过渡社会
- organized: 组织
- broad shape: 大致形状
- admiration for: 对...的钦佩
- Paris Commune: 巴黎公社
- 1871: 1871年
- short-lived experiment: 短暂实验
- approximated to: 近似于
- direct democracy: 直接民主

4. In the course of the fourteenth century, two general tendencies can be discerned in Italy: the replacement of republican governments by tyrants and the expansion of the larger city-states at the expense of the less powerful ones. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在14世纪的过程中，可以在意大利识别出两个一般趋势：共和政府被暴君取代，以及较大的城邦以牺牲较弱小的城邦为代价扩张。

- discerned: 被识别出来
- in the course of: 在...过程中
- fourteenth century: 14世纪
- two general tendencies: 两个一般趋势
- Italy: 意大利
- replacement: 取代
- republican governments: 共和政府
- tyrants: 暴君
- expansion: 扩张
- larger city-states: 较大的城邦
- at the expense of: 以...为代价
- less powerful ones: 较弱小的城邦
