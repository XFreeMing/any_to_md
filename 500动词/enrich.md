1. By mastering irrigation techniques, populations there exploited the rivers and their tributaries to enrich the fertile soil even further. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 通过掌握灌溉技术，那里的人口利用河流及其支流进一步丰富肥沃的土壤。

- enrich: 丰富
- by mastering: 通过掌握
- irrigation techniques: 灌溉技术
- populations: 人口
- there: 那里
- exploited: 利用
- rivers: 河流
- their tributaries: 它们的支流
- fertile soil: 肥沃的土壤
- even further: 进一步

2. Although preliminary abstract and concrete arguments are offered for the consideration of legal scholars and policymakers, at the conclusion of this chapter one can only hope that further research conducted by legal scholars will enrich the much-needed behavioral law and economics literature and make it more inclusive. (Zamir & Teichman,  2014)

> 虽然为法律学者和政策制定者的考虑提供了初步的抽象和具体论证，但在本章的结论中，人们只能希望法律学者进行的进一步研究将丰富急需的行为法律和经济学文献，并使其更具包容性。

- enrich: 丰富
- although: 虽然
- preliminary: 初步的
- abstract and concrete arguments: 抽象和具体论证
- offered for: 为...提供
- consideration: 考虑
- legal scholars: 法律学者
- policymakers: 政策制定者
- at the conclusion: 在结论中
- this chapter: 本章
- one can only hope: 人们只能希望
- further research: 进一步研究
- conducted by: 由...进行
- much-needed: 急需的
- behavioral law and economics literature: 行为法律和经济学文献
- make it more inclusive: 使其更具包容性

3. Ethnic diversity can greatly enrich societies. Multiethnic states are often vibrant and dynamic places that are strengthened by the varied contributions of their inhabitants. (Giddens, 2009)

> 种族多样性可以大大丰富社会。多民族国家往往是充满活力和动态的地方，通过其居民的各种贡献而得到加强。

- enrich: 丰富
- ethnic diversity: 种族多样性
- can greatly: 可以大大
- societies: 社会
- multiethnic states: 多民族国家
- often: 往往
- vibrant and dynamic places: 充满活力和动态的地方
- strengthened by: 通过...得到加强
- varied contributions: 各种贡献
- their inhabitants: 它们的居民

4. I write this text to offer social psychology's powerful, hard-wrought principles. They have, I believe, the power to expand your mind and enrich your life. (Myers, 2012)

> 我写这篇文章是为了提供社会心理学的强大、精心制定的原则。我相信，它们有能力扩展你的思维并丰富你的生活。

- enrich: 丰富
- write this text: 写这篇文章
- offer: 提供
- social psychology's: 社会心理学的
- powerful: 强大的
- hard-wrought principles: 精心制定的原则
- they have: 它们有
- I believe: 我相信
- power: 能力
- expand your mind: 扩展你的思维
- your life: 你的生活
