1. In doing so, he disregarded the church prohibition against the taxing of its property without papal permission. (Perry, 2010)

> 这样做时，他无视了教会禁止在没有教皇许可的情况下对其财产征税的规定。

- disregarded: 无视
- in doing so: 这样做时
- church prohibition: 教会禁止
- against: 禁止
- taxing: 征税
- property: 财产
- without papal permission: 没有教皇许可

2. Indeed, a judge's order to ignore testimony—"It must play no role in your consideration of the case. You have no choice but to disregard it"—can even boomerang, adding to the testimony's impact. (Wolf & Montgomery, 1972)

> 确实，法官要求忽略证词的命令——"它必须在你的案件考虑中不起任何作用。你别无选择，只能无视它"——甚至可能适得其反，增加了证词的影响。

- disregard: 无视
- indeed: 确实
- judge's order: 法官的命令
- ignore testimony: 忽略证词
- must play no role: 必须不起任何作用
- consideration of the case: 案件考虑
- no choice but to: 别无选择只能
- boomerang: 适得其反
- adding to: 增加
- testimony's impact: 证词的影响

3. Although warned about a plot against his life, Caesar chose to disregard it. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 虽然被警告有反对他生命的阴谋，但凯撒选择无视它。

- disregard: 无视
- although: 虽然
- warned about: 被警告
- plot: 阴谋
- against his life: 反对他生命
- Caesar: 凯撒
- chose to: 选择

4. The emperor, disregarding all considerations of expense, raised craftsmen from the whole world. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 皇帝无视所有费用考虑，从全世界招募工匠。

- disregarding: 无视
- emperor: 皇帝
- all considerations of expense: 所有费用考虑
- raised: 招募
- craftsmen: 工匠
- whole world: 全世界
