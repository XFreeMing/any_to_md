3. Social comparisons can also diminish our satisfaction. When we experience an increase in affluence, status, or achievement, we "compare upward"—we raise the standards by which we evaluate our attainments. When climbing the ladder of success, we tend to look up, not down; we compare ourselves with others doing even better. (Myers, 2012)

> 社会比较也会减少我们的满足感。当我们经历财富、地位或成就的增加时，我们"向上比较"——我们提高了评估我们成就的标准。当攀登成功的阶梯时，我们倾向于向上看，而不是向下看；我们把自己与做得更好的人比较。

- social comparisons: 社会比较
- also: 也

- not down: 而不是向下看

- others doing even better: 做得更好的人
