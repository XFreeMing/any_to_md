1. During actual community conflicts, like-minded people associate increasingly with one another, amplifying their shared tendencies. (Myers, 2012)

> 在实际的社区冲突中，志同道合的人越来越多地相互联系，放大他们共同的倾向。

- amplifying: 放大
- during actual community conflicts: 在实际的社区冲突中
- like-minded people: 志同道合的人
- associate increasingly: 越来越多地联系
- with one another: 相互
- shared tendencies: 共同的倾向

2. Much aggression is committed by groups. Circumstances that provoke individuals may also provoke groups. By diffusing responsibility and polarizing actions, group situations amplify aggressive reactions. (Myers, 2012)

> 许多攻击行为是由群体实施的。激怒个人的情况也可能激怒群体。通过分散责任和极化行动，群体情况放大了攻击性反应。

- amplify: 放大
- much aggression: 许多攻击行为
- committed by groups: 由群体实施的
- circumstances: 情况
- provoke individuals: 激怒个人
- may also provoke groups: 也可能激怒群体
- by diffusing responsibility: 通过分散责任
- polarizing actions: 极化行动
- group situations: 群体情况
- aggressive reactions: 攻击性反应

3. Sexual arousal and other forms of arousal, such as anger, can therefore amplify one another (Zillmann, 1989). Love is never so passionate as after a fight or a fright—one reason why it's so popular to take a hot date to a horror movie. (Myers, 2012)

> 因此，性唤起和其他形式的唤起，如愤怒，可以相互放大（Zillmann, 1989）。爱情从未像在战斗或惊吓之后那样充满激情——这就是为什么带热恋对象去看恐怖电影如此受欢迎的一个原因。

- amplify: 放大
- sexual arousal: 性唤起
- other forms of arousal: 其他形式的唤起
- such as: 如
- anger: 愤怒
- can therefore: 因此可以
- one another: 相互
- love: 爱情
- never so passionate: 从未如此充满激情
- after a fight or a fright: 在战斗或惊吓之后
- one reason: 一个原因
- why: 为什么
- so popular: 如此受欢迎
- take a hot date: 带热恋对象
- to a horror movie: 去看恐怖电影

4. Groups can amplify aggressive reactions partly by diffusing responsibility. (Myers, 2012)

> 群体可以通过分散责任来部分放大攻击性反应。

- amplify: 放大
- groups: 群体
- can: 可以
- aggressive reactions: 攻击性反应
- partly: 部分地
- by diffusing responsibility: 通过分散责任
