1. Conflicting parties have other ways to resolve their differences. When husband and wife, or labor and management, or nation X and nation Y disagree, they can bargain with each other directly. They can ask a third party to mediate by making suggestions and facilitating their negotiations. Or they can arbitrate by submitting their disagreement to someone who will study the issues and impose a settlement. (Myers, 2012)

> 冲突各方有其他方式来解决他们的分歧。当丈夫和妻子，或劳资双方，或国家 X 和国家 Y 不同意时，他们可以直接相互讨价还价。他们可以要求第三方通过提出建议和促进他们的谈判来调解。或者他们可以通过将分歧提交给将研究问题并强加解决方案的人来仲裁。

- conflicting parties: 冲突各方

- nation X and nation Y: 国家 X 和国家 Y
- disagree: 不同意

- directly: 直接地
- third party: 第三方
- making suggestions: 提出建议
- facilitating: 促进
- negotiations: 谈判
- arbitrate: 仲裁

2. When the two parties mistrust each other and communicate unproductively, a third-party mediator—a marriage counselor, a labor mediator, a diplomat— sometimes helps. Often the mediator is someone trusted by both sides. In the 1980s it took an Algerian Muslim to mediate the conflict between Iran and Iraq, and the pope to resolve a geographical dispute between Argentina and Chile (Myers, 2012)

> 当双方互不信任且沟通无效时，第三方调解人——婚姻顾问、劳工调解人、外交官——有时会有所帮助。通常调解人是双方都信任的人。在 20 世纪 80 年代，需要一位阿尔及利亚穆斯林来调解伊朗和伊拉克之间的冲突，需要教皇来解决阿根廷和智利之间的地理争端。

4. But the church held that the clergy were intermediaries between individuals and God and that, in effect, Christians reached eternal salvation through the clergy. For Luther, no priest, no ceremony, and no sacrament could mediate between the Creator and his creatures. Hope lay only in a personal relationship between the individual and God, as expressed through faith in God's mercy and grace. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 但教会认为，神职人员是个人和上帝之间的中介，实际上，基督徒通过神职人员获得永恒的救赎。对路德来说，没有牧师、没有仪式、没有圣礼可以在造物主和他的创造物之间进行调解。希望只在于个人和上帝之间的个人关系，正如通过对上帝的仁慈和恩典的信仰所表达的那样。
