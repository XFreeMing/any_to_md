1. Confucianism, it is argued, inculcates respect for one's elders and superiors, education, hard work and proven accomplishments as the key to advancement, as well as a willingness to sacrifice today to earn a greater reward tomorrow. (Giddens, 2009)

> 有人认为，儒家思想灌输对长辈和上级的尊重、教育、努力工作和已证明的成就作为进步的关键，以及愿意今天牺牲以获得明天更大回报的意愿。

- inculcates: 灌输
- Confucianism: 儒家思想
- it is argued: 有人认为
- respect for: 对...的尊重
- elders: 长辈
- superiors: 上级
- education: 教育
- hard work: 努力工作
- proven accomplishments: 已证明的成就
- key to advancement: 进步的关键
- willingness to sacrifice: 愿意牺牲
- greater reward: 更大回报

2. The Spartans learned only one craft, soldiering, and were inculcated with only one conception of excellence: fighting bravely for their city, and if needed, dying for it. (Perry, 2010)

> 斯巴达人只学习一种手艺，即当兵，并被灌输只有一种卓越的概念：为他们的城市勇敢战斗，如果需要，为它而死。

- inculcated with: 被灌输
- Spartans: 斯巴达人
- learned only one craft: 只学习一种手艺
- soldiering: 当兵
- only one conception: 只有一种概念
- excellence: 卓越
- fighting bravely: 勇敢战斗
- city: 城市
- if needed: 如果需要
- dying for it: 为它而死

3. The superego monitors human behavior according to principles inculcated by parents, teachers, and other authority figures. (Fiero, 2011)

> 超我根据父母、教师和其他权威人物灌输的原则监控人类行为。

- inculcated: 被灌输
- superego: 超我
- monitors: 监控
- human behavior: 人类行为
- according to: 根据
- principles: 原则
- parents: 父母
- teachers: 教师
- other authority figures: 其他权威人物

4. Most of the elementary schools also inculcated the middle-class virtues, such as hard work, thrift, sobriety, cleanliness, and respect for the family. For most students, elementary education led to apprenticeship and a job. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 大多数小学也灌输中产阶级的美德，如努力工作、节俭、节制、清洁和对家庭的尊重。对大多数学生来说，初等教育导致学徒和一份工作。

- inculcated: 灌输
- most of the elementary schools: 大多数小学
- middle-class virtues: 中产阶级的美德
- hard work: 努力工作
- thrift: 节俭
- sobriety: 节制
- cleanliness: 清洁
- respect for the family: 对家庭的尊重
- most students: 大多数学生
- elementary education: 初等教育
- led to: 导致
- apprenticeship: 学徒
- job: 工作
