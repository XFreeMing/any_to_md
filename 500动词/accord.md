1. But he [Copernicus] was a mathematician who felt that Ptolemy's geocentric system was too complicated and failed to accord with the observed motions of the heavenly bodies. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 但他[哥白尼]是一位数学家，他认为托勒密的地心系统太复杂，无法与观察到的天体运动一致。

- accord with: 与...一致
- Copernicus: 哥白尼
- mathematician: 数学家
- felt that: 认为
- Ptolemy's geocentric system: 托勒密的地心系统
- too complicated: 太复杂
- failed to: 无法
- observed motions: 观察到的运动
- heavenly bodies: 天体

2. National partiality is thus an extension of the near universal inclination to accord moral priority to those we know best, especially our families and close friends. (Heywood, 2013)

> 因此，民族偏袒是对那些我们最了解的人，特别是我们的家人和亲密朋友给予道德优先权的近乎普遍倾向的延伸。

- accord: 给予
- national partiality: 民族偏袒
- thus: 因此
- extension: 延伸
- near universal inclination: 近乎普遍倾向
- moral priority: 道德优先权
- those we know best: 那些我们最了解的人
- especially: 特别是
- families: 家人
- close friends: 亲密朋友

3. Claude succeeded in elevating the landscape genre, which had traditionally been accorded only a very low status. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 克劳德成功地提升了风景画类型，传统上它只被给予非常低的地位。

- accorded: 被给予
- Claude: 克劳德
- succeeded in: 成功地
- elevating: 提升
- landscape genre: 风景画类型
- traditionally: 传统上
- very low status: 非常低的地位

4. Rubin's research was carried out in the United States, but her findings accord closely with those of researchers in European countries. (Giddens, 2009)

> 鲁宾的研究是在美国进行的，但她的发现与欧洲国家研究人员的发现密切一致。

- accord with: 与...一致
- Rubin's research: 鲁宾的研究
- carried out: 进行
- United States: 美国
- findings: 发现
- closely: 密切地
- researchers: 研究人员
- European countries: 欧洲国家
