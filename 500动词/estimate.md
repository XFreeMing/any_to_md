1. Though much of the city remains unexcavated, archaeologists estimate that it covered an area of nearly a square mile, enclosed within an imposing mudbrick fortification wall. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 虽然城市的大部分仍未挖掘，但考古学家估计它覆盖了近一平方英里的面积，被包围在一座雄伟的泥砖防御墙内。

- estimate: 估计
- though: 虽然
- much of the city: 城市的大部分
- remains unexcavated: 仍未挖掘
- archaeologists: 考古学家
- covered an area: 覆盖面积
- nearly a square mile: 近一平方英里
- enclosed within: 被包围在...内
- imposing mudbrick fortification wall: 雄伟的泥砖防御墙

2. In the developing world, the greatest problem with domestic waste at the present time is the lack of refuse collection services. It has been estimated that 20- 50 percent of domestic waste in the developing world goes uncollected. (Giddens, 2009)

> 在发展中国家，目前家庭垃圾的最大问题是缺乏垃圾收集服务。据估计，发展中国家20-50%的家庭垃圾未被收集。

- estimated: 被估计
- developing world: 发展中国家
- greatest problem: 最大问题
- domestic waste: 家庭垃圾
- present time: 目前
- lack of: 缺乏
- refuse collection services: 垃圾收集服务
- 20-50 percent: 20-50%
- goes uncollected: 未被收集

3. The scale of the tragedy was not immediately apparent. By the end of the day on 26 December it was reported that 12,000 people had been killed. A few weeks later the United Nations estimated that more than 175,000 people had died. (Giddens, 2009)

> 悲剧的规模并不立即明显。到12月26日结束时，据报道有12,000人被杀。几周后，联合国估计有超过175,000人死亡。

- estimated: 估计
- scale of the tragedy: 悲剧的规模
- not immediately apparent: 并不立即明显
- by the end of the day: 到一天结束时
- 26 December: 12月26日
- reported that: 据报道
- 12,000 people: 12,000人
- killed: 被杀
- few weeks later: 几周后
- United Nations: 联合国
- more than 175,000 people: 超过175,000人
- died: 死亡

4. Under any circumstances, mosaic production was a costly enterprise; it has been estimated that it was at least four times more expensive than wall painting. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在任何情况下，马赛克生产都是一项昂贵的事业；据估计，它至少比壁画贵四倍。

- estimated: 被估计
- under any circumstances: 在任何情况下
- mosaic production: 马赛克生产
- costly enterprise: 昂贵的事业
- at least: 至少
- four times more expensive: 贵四倍
- wall painting: 壁画
