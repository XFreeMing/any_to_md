1. Should we query Mahmood and Hirschkind's claim that the end as well as the beginning of these projects is properly speaking ethical? (Skorupski, 2010)

> 我们应该质疑马哈茂德和赫希金德关于这些项目的结束和开始都恰当地说是伦理的主张吗？

- query: 质疑
- should we: 我们应该
- Mahmood and Hirschkind's claim: 马哈茂德和赫希金德的主张
- end as well as the beginning: 结束和开始
- these projects: 这些项目
- properly speaking: 恰当地说
- ethical: 伦理的

2. The second objection to the view that moral constraint is insufficiently grounded is to query the claim that it operates independently of, rather than through, our desires, interests, and affections. (Shafer-Landau, 2012)

> 对道德约束没有充分基础这一观点的第二个反对是质疑它独立于而不是通过我们的欲望、利益和情感运作的主张。

- query: 质疑
- second objection: 第二个反对
- view: 观点
- moral constraint: 道德约束
- insufficiently grounded: 没有充分基础
- claim: 主张
- operates independently of: 独立于...运作
- rather than: 而不是
- through: 通过
- desires: 欲望
- interests: 利益
- affections: 情感

3. On the other hand, followers of Næss's deep ecology were moved to rethink the notion of the ethical subject, querying what they saw as the individualistic and decontextualized nature of much western philosophical and moral theory. (Skorupski, 2010)

> 另一方面，奈斯深层生态学的追随者被推动重新思考伦理主体的概念，质疑他们认为许多西方哲学和道德理论的个人主义和去语境化性质。

- querying: 质疑
- on the other hand: 另一方面
- followers: 追随者
- Næss's deep ecology: 奈斯深层生态学
- moved to rethink: 被推动重新思考
- notion: 概念
- ethical subject: 伦理主体
- what they saw as: 他们认为
- individualistic: 个人主义的
- decontextualized nature: 去语境化性质
- western philosophical and moral theory: 西方哲学和道德理论

4. The first is to query the idea of constraint. Why should morality be seen as constraining our choices and actions? (Shafer-Landau, 2012)

> 第一个是质疑约束的概念。为什么道德应该被视为约束我们的选择和行动？

- query: 质疑
- first: 第一个
- idea of constraint: 约束的概念
- why should: 为什么应该
- morality: 道德
- seen as: 被视为
- constraining: 约束
- choices: 选择
- actions: 行动
