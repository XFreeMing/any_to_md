1. Their pictorial works share certain features: an approach to the imitation of nature that idealizes forms even as they are rendered to replicate nature; understanding of and reliance on the forms of antiquity; balance and clarity in their compositions; and emotional power. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 他们的绘画作品具有某些特征：一种模仿自然的方法，即使在渲染以复制自然时也理想化形式；对古代形式的理解和依赖；构图的平衡和清晰；以及情感力量。

- replicate: 复制
- pictorial works: 绘画作品
- share: 具有
- certain features: 某些特征
- approach: 方法
- imitation of nature: 模仿自然
- idealizes: 理想化
- forms: 形式
- even as: 即使当
- rendered to: 渲染以
- understanding: 理解
- reliance on: 对...的依赖
- forms of antiquity: 古代形式
- balance: 平衡
- clarity: 清晰
- compositions: 构图
- emotional power: 情感力量

2. Specifically, the social relationships of education——the relationships between administrators and teachers, teachers and students, and students and students, and students and their work——replicate the hierarchical divisions of labor. (Bowles and Gintis 1976: 131) (Giddens, 2009)

> 具体来说，教育的社会关系——管理者与教师、教师与学生、学生与学生以及学生与其工作之间的关系——复制了劳动的等级分工。

- replicate: 复制
- specifically: 具体来说
- social relationships: 社会关系
- of education: 教育的
- relationships between: ...之间的关系
- administrators: 管理者
- teachers: 教师
- students: 学生
- their work: 他们的工作
- hierarchical divisions: 等级分工
- of labor: 劳动的
