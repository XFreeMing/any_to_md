1. The fall in the number of deaths from heart disease is generally attributed to improvements in diet. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 心脏病死亡人数的下降通常归因于饮食的改善。

- attributed to: 归因于
- fall: 下降
- number of deaths: 死亡人数
- heart disease: 心脏病
- generally: 通常
- improvements: 改善
- diet: 饮食

2. Mesopotamian myths, for example, attributed personal misfortune and the catastrophes that afflicted a city to supernatural forces displeased with people's behavior. (Perry, 2010)

> 例如，美索不达米亚神话将个人不幸和困扰城市的灾难归因于对人们行为不满的超自然力量。

- attributed: 归因
- Mesopotamian myths: 美索不达米亚神话
- for example: 例如
- personal misfortune: 个人不幸
- catastrophes: 灾难
- afflicted: 困扰
- city: 城市
- supernatural forces: 超自然力量
- displeased with: 对...不满
- people's behavior: 人们的行为

3. The economic success of the East Asian NICs, particularly from the mid-1970s to the mid-1990s, can be attributed to a combination of factors. Some of these are historical, including those that stem from world political and economic shifts. Some are cultural. (Giddens, 2009)

> 东亚新兴工业化国家的经济成功，特别是从20世纪70年代中期到90年代中期，可以归因于多种因素的结合。其中一些是历史的，包括那些源于世界政治和经济转变的因素。一些是文化的。

- attributed to: 归因于
- economic success: 经济成功
- East Asian NICs: 东亚新兴工业化国家
- particularly: 特别是
- mid-1970s to mid-1990s: 20世纪70年代中期到90年代中期
- combination of factors: 多种因素的结合
- some of these: 其中一些
- historical: 历史的
- including: 包括
- stem from: 源于
- world political and economic shifts: 世界政治和经济转变
- cultural: 文化的

4. Cause and effect and the other categories of the mind permit us to attribute certainty to scientific knowledge. (Solomon & Higgins, 2009)

> 因果关系和心灵的其他范畴使我们能够将确定性归因于科学知识。

- attribute: 归因
- cause and effect: 因果关系
- other categories: 其他范畴
- mind: 心灵
- permit us to: 使我们能够
- certainty: 确定性
- scientific knowledge: 科学知识
