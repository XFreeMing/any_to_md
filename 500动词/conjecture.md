1. However, Saint-Denis's three portals are far larger and more richly carved than those at Saint-Étienne or any other Norman Romanesque church. From this we can conjecture that Abbot Suger attached considerable importance to the sculptural decoration of Saint-Denis, although his account of the church does not discuss it at length. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 然而，圣但尼的三个门户比圣艾蒂安或任何其他诺曼罗马式教堂的门户要大得多，雕刻也更丰富。由此我们可以推测，修道院院长苏热对圣但尼的雕塑装饰相当重视，尽管他对教堂的描述没有详细讨论它。

- conjecture: 推测
- however: 然而
- Saint-Denis's three portals: 圣但尼的三个门户
- far larger: 大得多
- more richly carved: 雕刻更丰富
- Saint-Étienne: 圣艾蒂安
- any other Norman Romanesque church: 任何其他诺曼罗马式教堂
- Abbot Suger: 修道院院长苏热
- attached considerable importance to: 相当重视
- sculptural decoration: 雕塑装饰
- account: 描述
- does not discuss it at length: 没有详细讨论它

2. The world has not turned out the way the philosophes wished and half expected that it would. Old fanaticisms have been more intractable, irrational forces more inventive than the philosophes were ready to conjecture in their darkest moments. (Perry, 2010)

> 世界并没有像哲学家们希望和半期望的那样发展。旧的狂热主义更加顽固，非理性力量比哲学家们在最黑暗时刻准备推测的更具创造性。

- conjecture: 推测
- world: 世界
- not turned out: 并没有发展
- way: 方式
- philosophes: 哲学家们
- wished and half expected: 希望和半期望
- old fanaticisms: 旧的狂热主义
- more intractable: 更加顽固
- irrational forces: 非理性力量
- more inventive: 更具创造性
- ready to: 准备
- darkest moments: 最黑暗时刻

3. Mutual attraction flourishes when each meets the other's unmet needs. In his 1665 book of Maxims, La Rochefoucauld conjectured, "Friendship is a scheme for the mutual exchange of personal advantages and favors whereby self-esteem may profit." (Myers, 2012)

> 当每个人都满足对方的未满足需求时，相互吸引就会蓬勃发展。在他1665年的《格言集》中，拉罗什富科推测，"友谊是一种个人优势和恩惠相互交换的方案，通过这种方式自尊可以获得利益。"

- conjectured: 推测
- mutual attraction: 相互吸引
- flourishes: 蓬勃发展
- each meets: 每个人都满足
- other's unmet needs: 对方的未满足需求
- 1665 book of Maxims: 1665年的《格言集》
- La Rochefoucauld: 拉罗什富科
- friendship: 友谊
- scheme: 方案
- mutual exchange: 相互交换
- personal advantages: 个人优势
- favors: 恩惠
- whereby: 通过这种方式
- self-esteem: 自尊
- profit: 获得利益

4. What is Cole and Ohanian's alternative explanation for the length of the recovery during the Great Depression? They conjecture that regulation was the culprit. (McConnell, Brue, & Flynn, 2011)

> 科尔和奥哈尼安对大萧条期间复苏长度的替代解释是什么？他们推测监管是罪魁祸首。

- conjecture: 推测
- Cole and Ohanian: 科尔和奥哈尼安
- alternative explanation: 替代解释
- length: 长度
- recovery: 复苏
- Great Depression: 大萧条
- regulation: 监管
- culprit: 罪魁祸首
