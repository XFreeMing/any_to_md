## 推广；宣传

1. Warhol has brilliantly expressed the indifference of the mass media, whose objective is to promote celebrities by saturating a thirsty public with their likenesses but which tells us nothing meaningful about them and shows no concern for them. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 沃霍尔巧妙地表达了大众媒体的冷漠，其目标是通过用名人的形象来满足渴望的公众来推广名人，但它没有告诉我们任何有意义的东西，也没有表现出对他们的关心。

- promote: 推广，宣传
- brilliantly expressed: 巧妙地表达
- indifference: 冷漠
- mass media: 大众媒体
- objective: 目标
- celebrities: 名人
- saturating: 满足
- thirsty public: 渴望的公众
- likenesses: 形象
- meaningful: 有意义的
- concern: 关心

2. They [Post-Impressionists] also maintained the anti-bourgeois, anti-academic attitude of the Impressionists, similarly turning to artists cooperatives and private galleries to promote their art. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 他们[后印象派]也保持了印象派的反资产阶级、反学院派的态度，同样转向艺术家合作社和私人画廊来推广他们的艺术。

- promote: 推广
- maintained: 保持
- anti-bourgeois: 反资产阶级的
- anti-academic: 反学院派的
- attitude: 态度
- Impressionists: 印象派
- similarly: 同样地
- turning to: 转向
- artists cooperatives: 艺术家合作社
- private galleries: 私人画廊
- art: 艺术

3. When the pope finally acted against Luther in 1520, it was too late; Luther had been given the needed time to promote his views. (Perry, 2010)

> 当教皇最终在1520年对路德采取行动时，已经太晚了；路德已经得到了推广他观点所需的时间。

- promote: 推广
- pope: 教皇
- finally: 最终
- acted against: 对...采取行动
- too late: 太晚了
- given: 给予
- needed time: 所需的时间
- views: 观点

4. His [Constantine's] choice to accept and promote Christianity was a turning point in history, as it resulted in the union of Christianity with the legacy of the Roman Empire. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 他[君士坦丁]接受并推广基督教的选择是历史上的一个转折点，因为它导致了基督教与罗马帝国遗产的结合。

- promote: 推广
- choice: 选择
- accept: 接受
- Christianity: 基督教
- turning point: 转折点
- history: 历史
- resulted in: 导致
- union: 结合
- legacy: 遗产
- Roman Empire: 罗马帝国

## 促进，提高

1. Even if they [utilitarians] agree that political philosophers should try to devise systems of rights, those rights, on the utilitarian view, are designed ultimately to promote happiness, not freedom and equality. (Wolff, 2006)

> 即使他们[功利主义者]同意政治哲学家应该尝试设计权利体系，那些权利，在功利主义观点看来，最终旨在促进幸福，而不是自由和平等。

- promote: 促进
- even if: 即使
- utilitarians: 功利主义者
- agree that: 同意
- political philosophers: 政治哲学家
- try to devise: 尝试设计
- systems of rights: 权利体系
- utilitarian view: 功利主义观点
- designed ultimately to: 最终旨在
- happiness: 幸福
- not freedom and equality: 而不是自由和平等

2. Studies confirm that systems of governing or managing people that promote personal control will indeed promote health and happiness. (Myers, 2012)

> 研究证实，促进个人控制的治理或管理系统确实会促进健康和幸福。

- promote: 促进
- studies: 研究
- confirm that: 证实
- systems of governing: 治理系统
- managing people: 管理人民
- personal control: 个人控制
- will indeed: 确实会
- health: 健康
- happiness: 幸福

3. As he [Mill] defines it, utilitarianism is the theory which: 'holds that actions are right in proportion as they tend to promote happiness, wrong as they tend to produce the reverse of happiness. (Wolff, 2006)

> 正如他[密尔]所定义的，功利主义是这样一个理论："认为行为在它们倾向于促进幸福的程度上是正确的，在它们倾向于产生幸福的反面时是错误的。"

- promote: 促进
- as he defines it: 正如他所定义的
- Mill: 密尔
- utilitarianism: 功利主义
- theory: 理论
- holds that: 认为
- actions: 行为
- right: 正确的
- in proportion as: 在...的程度上
- tend to: 倾向于
- happiness: 幸福
- wrong: 错误的
- produce: 产生
- reverse of happiness: 幸福的反面

4. The UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (2006) aims to promote equal rights for disabled people globally. (Giddens, 2009)

> 《联合国残疾人权利公约》（2006）旨在在全球范围内促进残疾人的平等权利。

- promote: 促进
- UN Convention: 联合国公约
- Rights of Persons with Disabilities: 残疾人权利
- aims to: 旨在
- equal rights: 平等权利
- disabled people: 残疾人
- globally: 在全球范围内

- far-reaching economic changes: 深远经济变化
- designed to: 旨在
- loosen: 放松
- restraints: 限制
- central planning: 中央计划
- market economy: 市场经济
- accompanied: 伴随着
- political reforms: 政治改革

- while: 而
- others: 其他国家
- attempt: 尝试
- collective forms: 集体形式

> 宾通过进口日本艺术和家具发了财，现在寻求推广日本的整体设计原则：内部空间的每个细节都应该整合到单一风格中。

- promote: 推广
- Bing: 宾

- Japanese art: 日本艺术
- furnishings: 家具
- sought to: 寻求

- every detail: 每个细节
- interior space: 内部空间
- integrated into: 整合到
- single style: 单一风格

- as well as: 以及

- among the elite: 在精英阶层中
