1. For all of their brilliant accomplishments, the Greeks were unable to rise above the divisions and rivalries that caused them to fight each other and undermine their own civilization. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 尽管他们取得了辉煌的成就，希腊人无法超越导致他们相互战斗并破坏自己文明的分裂和竞争。

- undermine: 破坏
- for all of: 尽管
- brilliant accomplishments: 辉煌的成就
- Greeks: 希腊人
- unable to rise above: 无法超越
- divisions: 分裂
- rivalries: 竞争
- caused them to: 导致他们
- fight each other: 相互战斗
- their own civilization: 自己的文明

2. The Assyrians would hammer a city's walls with heavy, wheeled siege towers and armored battering rams while sappers dug tunnels to undermine the walls' foundations and cause them to collapse. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 亚述人会用重型轮式攻城塔和装甲攻城槌锤击城市的墙壁，而工兵则挖掘隧道来破坏墙壁的基础并使其倒塌。

- undermine: 破坏
- Assyrians: 亚述人
- hammer: 锤击
- city's walls: 城市的墙壁
- heavy, wheeled siege towers: 重型轮式攻城塔
- armored battering rams: 装甲攻城槌
- while: 而
- sappers: 工兵
- dug tunnels: 挖掘隧道
- walls' foundations: 墙壁的基础
- cause them to collapse: 使其倒塌

3. The foundations of the authority of the paterfamilias over his family, which had already begun to weaken in the late Republic, were further undermined. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 家长对其家庭的权威基础，在共和国晚期已经开始削弱，进一步被破坏。

- undermined: 被破坏
- foundations: 基础
- authority: 权威
- paterfamilias: 家长
- over his family: 对其家庭的
- already begun to weaken: 已经开始削弱
- late Republic: 共和国晚期
- further: 进一步

4. Augustus thought that increased luxury had undermined traditional Roman frugality, and led to a loosening of morals. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 奥古斯都认为，增加的奢侈破坏了传统的罗马节俭，并导致道德的放松。

- undermined: 破坏
- Augustus: 奥古斯都
- thought that: 认为
- increased luxury: 增加的奢侈
- traditional Roman frugality: 传统的罗马节俭
- led to: 导致
- loosening of morals: 道德的放松
