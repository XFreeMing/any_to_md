1. Most important, art stimulates us to think. Even when it provokes and outrages us, it broadens our experience by making us question our values, attitudes, and worldview. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 最重要的是，艺术刺激我们思考。即使它激怒和激怒我们，它也通过让我们质疑我们的价值观、态度和世界观来拓宽我们的经验。

- broadens: 拓宽
- most important: 最重要的是
- art: 艺术
- stimulates us: 刺激我们
- to think: 思考
- even when: 即使当
- provokes: 激怒
- outrages us: 激怒我们
- our experience: 我们的经验
- by making us question: 通过让我们质疑
- our values: 我们的价值观
- attitudes: 态度
- worldview: 世界观

2. A first step toward socializing altruism is therefore to counter the natural ingroup bias favoring kin and tribe by personalizing and broadening the range of people whose well-being should concern us. (Myers, 2012)

> 因此，社交化利他主义的第一步是通过个性化和拓宽我们应该关心其福祉的人的范围来对抗有利于亲属和部落的自然内群体偏见。

- broadening: 拓宽
- first step: 第一步
- toward: 朝向
- socializing altruism: 社交化利他主义
- therefore: 因此
- counter: 对抗
- natural ingroup bias: 自然内群体偏见
- favoring: 有利于
- kin and tribe: 亲属和部落
- by personalizing: 通过个性化
- range of people: 人的范围
- whose well-being: 其福祉
- should concern us: 我们应该关心

3. The years following the Second World War witnessed a further powerful drive for the reform and expansion of the welfare system. Rather than concentrating solely on the destitute and ill, the focus of welfare was broadened to include all members of society. (Giddens, 2009)

> 第二次世界大战后的几年见证了福利制度改革和扩张的进一步强大推动力。福利的重点不是仅仅集中在贫困者和病人身上，而是扩大到包括社会的所有成员。

- broadened: 扩大
- years following: ...后的几年
- Second World War: 第二次世界大战
- witnessed: 见证
- further powerful drive: 进一步强大推动力
- reform and expansion: 改革和扩张
- welfare system: 福利制度
- rather than: 而不是
- concentrating solely on: 仅仅集中在
- destitute and ill: 贫困者和病人
- focus of welfare: 福利的重点
- include: 包括
- all members of society: 社会的所有成员

4. Indeed, we will later see reason to question whether Mill even thinks it is a necessary condition. To make further progress we must broaden our view. (Wolff, 2006)

> 确实，我们稍后会看到理由质疑密尔是否甚至认为这是一个必要条件。为了取得进一步进展，我们必须拓宽我们的视野。

- broaden: 拓宽
- indeed: 确实
- later see reason: 稍后会看到理由
- to question: 质疑
- whether: 是否
- Mill: 密尔
