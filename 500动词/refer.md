1. When we speak of the Judeo-Christian heritage of Western civilization, we refer not only to the concept of monotheism but also to ideas of law, morality, and social justice that have become important parts of Western culture. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 当我们谈到西方文明的犹太-基督教遗产时，我们不仅指一神论的概念，还指已成为西方文化重要组成部分的法律、道德和社会正义的观念。

- refer to: 指
- speak of: 谈到
- Judeo-Christian heritage: 犹太-基督教遗产
- Western civilization: 西方文明
- not only: 不仅
- concept: 概念
- monotheism: 一神论
- but also: 还
- ideas: 观念
- law: 法律
- morality: 道德
- social justice: 社会正义
- important parts: 重要组成部分
- Western culture: 西方文化

2. Some people refer to this activity as jihad, which they misleadingly interpret as "holy war". (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 有些人将这种活动称为圣战，他们误导性地将其解释为"圣战"。

- refer to: 将...称为
- some people: 有些人
- activity: 活动
- jihad: 圣战
- misleadingly: 误导性地
- interpret: 解释
- holy war: 圣战

3. The word university is derived from the Latin word universitas, meaning a corporation or guild, and referred to either a guild of teachers or a guild of students.

> 大学这个词来源于拉丁词universitas，意思是公司或行会，指的是教师行会或学生行会。

- referred to: 指的是
- word: 词
- university: 大学
- derived from: 来源于
- Latin word: 拉丁词
- universitas: 大学
- meaning: 意思是
- corporation: 公司
- guild: 行会
- either: 或者
- guild of teachers: 教师行会
- guild of students: 学生行会

4. The Chinese have long regarded the era of the Han as their classical age, and to this day refer to themselves as the "children of the Han". (Fiero, 2011b)

> 中国人长期以来将汉朝视为他们的古典时代，直到今天仍称自己为"汉人的孩子"。

- refer to: 称...为
- Chinese: 中国人
- long regarded: 长期以来将...视为
- era of the Han: 汉朝
- classical age: 古典时代
- to this day: 直到今天
- themselves: 他们自己
- children of the Han: 汉人的孩子
