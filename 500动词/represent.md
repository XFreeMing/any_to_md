1. If people do not always vote according to their preferences, we cannot represent the voting process as one which automatically reveals the preferences of the majority: So what does the vote reveal? (Wolff, 2006)

> 如果人们不总是根据他们的偏好投票，我们不能将投票过程表示为自动揭示多数人偏好的过程：那么投票揭示了什么？

- represent: 将...表示为
- if: 如果
- people: 人们
- do not always vote: 不总是投票
- according to: 根据
- preferences: 偏好
- voting process: 投票过程
- automatically reveals: 自动揭示
- majority: 多数人
- vote reveal: 投票揭示

2. Molded with noticeably large breasts and buttocks, these ''earth mothers'' perhaps symbolically represented the fertility of both ''mother earth'' and human mothers. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 这些"大地母亲"被塑造成明显的大胸部和臀部，也许象征性地代表了"大地母亲"和人类母亲的生育能力。

- represented: 代表
- molded with: 被塑造成
- noticeably large: 明显大的
- breasts: 胸部
- buttocks: 臀部
- earth mothers: 大地母亲
- perhaps: 也许
- symbolically: 象征性地
- fertility: 生育能力
- mother earth: 大地母亲
- human mothers: 人类母亲

3. As voting would no longer be voluntary, it could not possibly be represented as an act or sign of consent. (Wolff, 2006)

> 由于投票将不再是自愿的，它不可能被表示为同意行为或同意的标志。

- represented as: 被表示为
- as: 由于
- voting: 投票
- would no longer be voluntary: 将不再是自愿的
- could not possibly be: 不可能被
- act: 行为
- sign of consent: 同意的标志

4. And most participants are from WEIRD (Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, and Democratic) cultures that represent but 12 percent of humanity. (Myers, 2012)

> 大多数参与者来自WEIRD（西方、受过教育、工业化、富裕和民主）文化，这些文化只代表人类的12%。

- represent: 代表
- most participants: 大多数参与者
- WEIRD: WEIRD（西方、受过教育、工业化、富裕和民主）
- Western: 西方的
- Educated: 受过教育的
- Industrialized: 工业化的
- Rich: 富裕的
- Democratic: 民主的
- cultures: 文化
- but 12 percent: 只12%
- humanity: 人类
