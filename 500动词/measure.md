1. Masaccio's use of perspective gives the spectator all the data needed to measure the depth of this painted interior, to draw its plan, and to envision the structure in three dimensions. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 马萨乔对透视的使用给观众提供了测量这个绘画内部深度、绘制其平面图并在三个维度中设想结构所需的所有数据。

- Masaccio's use: 马萨乔的使用
- perspective: 透视
- gives: 提供
- spectator: 观众
- data needed: 所需数据
- depth: 深度
- painted interior: 绘画内部
- draw its plan: 绘制其平面图
- envision: 设想
- structure: 结构
- three dimensions: 三个维度

2. Building on Einstein's theories, Werner Heisenberg (1901–1976) theorized that since the very act of measuring subatomic phenomena altered them, the position and the velocity of a subatomic particle could not be measured simultaneously with absolute accuracy. (Fiero, 2011)

> 基于爱因斯坦的理论，维尔纳·海森堡（1901-1976）理论化说，由于测量亚原子现象的行为本身改变了它们，亚原子粒子的位置和速度不能同时以绝对精度测量。

- measuring: 测量
- building on: 基于
- Einstein's theories: 爱因斯坦的理论
- Werner Heisenberg: 维尔纳·海森堡
- theorized that: 理论化说
- since: 由于
- very act: 行为本身
- subatomic phenomena: 亚原子现象
- altered them: 改变了它们
- position: 位置
- velocity: 速度
- subatomic particle: 亚原子粒子
- could not be measured: 不能测量
- simultaneously: 同时
- absolute accuracy: 绝对精度

3. State policies, known as mercantilism, were also aimed at augmenting national wealth and power. According to mercantilist theory, wealth from trade was measured in gold and silver, of which there was believed to be a more or less fixed quantity. (Perry, 2010)

> 被称为重商主义的国家政策也旨在增加国家财富和权力。根据重商主义理论，来自贸易的财富以黄金和白银衡量，人们相信它们的数量或多或少是固定的。

- measured: 被衡量
- state policies: 国家政策
- known as: 被称为
- mercantilism: 重商主义
- aimed at: 旨在
- augmenting: 增加
- national wealth and power: 国家财富和权力
- according to: 根据
- mercantilist theory: 重商主义理论
- wealth from trade: 来自贸易的财富
- gold and silver: 黄金和白银
- believed to be: 被认为
- more or less fixed quantity: 或多或少固定的数量

4. Unlike a physician measuring heart rate, social psychologists never get a direct reading on attitudes. Rather, social psychologists measure expressed attitudes. (Myers, 2012)

> 与测量心率的医生不同，社会心理学家从未获得对态度的直接读数。相反，社会心理学家测量表达的态度。

- measure: 测量
- unlike: 与...不同
- physician: 医生
- measuring heart rate: 测量心率
- social psychologists: 社会心理学家
- never get: 从未获得
- direct reading: 直接读数
- attitudes: 态度
- rather: 相反
- expressed attitudes: 表达的态度
