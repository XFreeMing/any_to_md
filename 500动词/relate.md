1. We can analyze the religious beliefs and customs of a society, for example, by showing how they relate to other institutions within it, for the different parts of a society develop in close relation to one another. (Giddens, 2009)

> 例如，我们可以通过展示它们如何与其中的其他制度相关来分析一个社会的宗教信仰和习俗，因为社会的不同部分在密切关系中相互发展。

- relate to: 与...相关
- analyze: 分析
- religious beliefs: 宗教信仰
- customs: 习俗
- society: 社会
- for example: 例如
- showing: 展示
- other institutions: 其他制度
- within it: 其中的
- different parts: 不同部分
- develop: 发展
- close relation: 密切关系
- one another: 相互

2. The first education of men depends on the care of women. Thus, the whole education of women ought to relate to men. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 男人的最初教育取决于女人的照顾。因此，女人的整个教育应该与男人相关。

- relate to: 与...相关
- first education: 最初教育
- men: 男人
- depends on: 取决于
- care of women: 女人的照顾
- thus: 因此
- whole education: 整个教育
- women: 女人
- ought to: 应该

3. A provincial governor's staff was not large, so local city officials were expected to act as Roman agents in carrying out many government functions, especially those related to taxes. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 省长的职员不多，所以地方城市官员被期望作为罗马代理人在执行许多政府职能，特别是与税收相关的职能。

- related to: 与...相关的
- provincial governor's staff: 省长的职员
- not large: 不多
- local city officials: 地方城市官员
- expected to: 被期望
- act as: 作为
- Roman agents: 罗马代理人
- carrying out: 执行
- government functions: 政府职能
- especially: 特别是
- taxes: 税收

4. The process of global warming is closely related to the idea of the greenhouse effect - the build-up of heat-trapping greenhouse gases within the earth's atmosphere. (Giddens, 2009)

> 全球变暖的过程与温室效应的概念密切相关——地球大气层内吸热温室气体的积累。

- related to: 与...相关的
- process: 过程
- global warming: 全球变暖
- closely: 密切地
- idea: 概念
- greenhouse effect: 温室效应
- build-up: 积累
- heat-trapping: 吸热的
- greenhouse gases: 温室气体
- within: 在...内
- earth's atmosphere: 地球大气层
