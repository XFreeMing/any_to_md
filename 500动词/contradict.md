1. Their cosmology brought scientific understanding to sensory perception. By placing the sun, not the earth (and humanity), at the center of the universe, it contradicted what our eyes (and common sense) tell us: that the sun revolves around the earth. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 他们的宇宙学将科学理解带入了感官感知。通过将太阳而不是地球（和人类）置于宇宙的中心，它与我们眼睛（和常识）告诉我们的相矛盾：太阳围绕地球旋转。

- contradicted: 与...相矛盾
- cosmology: 宇宙学
- brought scientific understanding: 将科学理解带入
- sensory perception: 感官感知
- placing: 放置
- sun: 太阳
- earth: 地球
- humanity: 人类
- center of the universe: 宇宙的中心
- our eyes: 我们的眼睛
- common sense: 常识
- tell us: 告诉我们
- sun revolves around the earth: 太阳围绕地球旋转

2. Many scholars argue that the early books of the Bible, written centuries after the events described, preserve only what the Israelites came to believe about themselves and that recent archaeological evidence often contradicts the details of the biblical account. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 许多学者认为，圣经的早期书籍是在所描述的事件发生几个世纪后写成的，只保留了以色列人对自己形成的信念，而最近的考古证据往往与圣经记载的细节相矛盾。

- contradicts: 与...相矛盾
- many scholars: 许多学者
- argue that: 认为
- early books of the Bible: 圣经的早期书籍
- written centuries after: 在...几个世纪后写成的
- events described: 所描述的事件
- preserve only: 只保留
- Israelites: 以色列人
- came to believe: 形成的信念
- about themselves: 关于自己
- recent archaeological evidence: 最近的考古证据
- often: 往往
- details: 细节
- biblical account: 圣经记载

3. In his first law, Kepler contradicted Copernicus by showing that the orbits of the planets around the sun were not circular but elliptical, with the sun at one focus of the ellipse rather than at the center. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在他的第一定律中，开普勒通过表明行星围绕太阳的轨道不是圆形的而是椭圆形的，与哥白尼相矛盾，太阳位于椭圆的一个焦点而不是中心。

- contradicted: 与...相矛盾
- in his first law: 在他的第一定律中
- Kepler: 开普勒
- Copernicus: 哥白尼
- showing that: 表明
- orbits: 轨道
- planets: 行星
- around the sun: 围绕太阳
- not circular but elliptical: 不是圆形的而是椭圆形的
- sun at one focus: 太阳位于一个焦点
- ellipse: 椭圆
- rather than: 而不是
- center: 中心

4. Kepler's new physics advanced the idea of a universe in motion, contradicting the Aristotelian notion of a fixed and unchanging cosmos. (Fiero, 2011b)

> 开普勒的新物理学推进了运动宇宙的观念，与亚里士多德的固定不变宇宙的观念相矛盾。

- contradicting: 与...相矛盾
- Kepler's new physics: 开普勒的新物理学
- advanced: 推进
- idea: 观念
- universe in motion: 运动宇宙
- Aristotelian notion: 亚里士多德的观念
- fixed and unchanging cosmos: 固定不变宇宙
