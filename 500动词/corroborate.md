1. The arrangement recalls the façades of southwestern France and the carved portals of Burgundy. These correlations corroborate Suger's claim that his workforce included artists from many regions. Unhappily, the trumeau figure of St. Denis and the statue columns of the jambs were removed in 1770 and 1771, when the central portal was enlarged. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 这种安排让人想起法国西南部的立面和勃艮第的雕刻门户。这些相关性证实了苏热的说法，即他的劳动力包括来自许多地区的艺术家。不幸的是，圣但尼的三角楣雕像和门框的雕像柱在1770年和1771年被移除了，当时中央门户被扩大了。

- correlations: 相关性
- Suger's claim: 苏热的说法
- workforce: 劳动力
- included: 包括
- artists: 艺术家
- many regions: 许多地区

- St. Denis: 圣但尼

- 1770 and 1771: 1770年和1771年
- central portal: 中央门户
- enlarged: 被扩大
