1. Students can compare the traditional textbook map with an aerial view of the location today. It's an ideal resource for study, review, and research. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 学生可以将传统的教科书地图与今天该地点的鸟瞰图进行比较。这是学习、复习和研究的理想资源。

- compare: 比较
- students: 学生
- traditional textbook map: 传统的教科书地图
- aerial view: 鸟瞰图
- location: 地点
- today: 今天
- ideal resource: 理想资源
- study: 学习
- review: 复习
- research: 研究

2. Nevertheless, compared to the British forces, the Continental Army consisted of undisciplined amateurs whose terms of service were usually very brief. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 然而，与英国军队相比，大陆军由纪律松懈的业余人员组成，他们的服役期限通常很短。

- compared to: 与...相比
- nevertheless: 然而
- British forces: 英国军队
- Continental Army: 大陆军
- consisted of: 由...组成
- undisciplined amateurs: 纪律松懈的业余人员
- terms of service: 服役期限
- usually very brief: 通常很短

3. Not only does the theory require us to compare one person's happiness with another's, and say who has more, but to say how much more. (Wolff, 2006)

> 这个理论不仅要求我们比较一个人的幸福与另一个人的幸福，并说谁有更多，而且要说多多少。

- compare: 比较
- not only does: 不仅
- theory: 理论
- require us to: 要求我们
- one person's happiness: 一个人的幸福
- another's: 另一个人的
- say who has more: 说谁有更多
- but to say: 而且要说
- how much more: 多多少

4. Bergman compared filmmaking to composing music: a non-narrative and largely intuitive enterprise

> 伯格曼将电影制作比作作曲：一个非叙事且主要是直觉的事业。

- compared: 将...比作
- Bergman: 伯格曼
- filmmaking: 电影制作
- composing music: 作曲
- non-narrative: 非叙事的
- largely intuitive: 主要是直觉的
- enterprise: 事业
