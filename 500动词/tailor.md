1. These arguments, tailored to the needs of the recipient, illustrate the therapeutic approach adopted by our philosophers when they discuss fear, sorrow and vice. (Skorupski, 2012)

> 这些根据接受者需求定制的论点说明了我们的哲学家在讨论恐惧、悲伤和恶习时采用的治疗方法。

- tailored to: 根据...定制
- arguments: 论点
- needs of the recipient: 接受者的需求
- illustrate: 说明
- therapeutic approach: 治疗方法
- adopted by: 由...采用
- philosophers: 哲学家
- discuss: 讨论
- fear: 恐惧
- sorrow: 悲伤
- vice: 恶习

2. Tailoring his creative talents to suit special occasions and the tastes of his wealthy patron, he wrote operas, oratorios, solo concertos, sonatas, overtures, and liturgical music. (Fiero, 2011b)

- tailoring: 根据...定制
- creative talents: 创作才能
- suit: 适合
- special occasions: 特殊场合
- tastes: 品味
- wealthy patron: 富有的赞助人
- wrote: 创作
- operas: 歌剧
- oratorios: 清唱剧
- solo concertos: 独奏协奏曲
- sonatas: 奏鸣曲
- overtures: 序曲
- liturgical music: 礼仪音乐

3. Josquin tailored musical lines so that they followed the natural flow of the words, a device inspired perhaps by his appreciation of the classical kinship of song and text. (Fiero, 2011b)

> 若斯坎定制了音乐线条，使它们遵循词语的自然流动，这种手法可能受到他对歌曲和文本的古典亲缘关系的欣赏的启发。

- tailored: 定制
- Josquin: 若斯坎
- musical lines: 音乐线条
- so that: 以便
- followed: 遵循
- natural flow: 自然流动
- words: 词语
- device: 手法
- inspired perhaps by: 可能受到...的启发
- appreciation: 欣赏
- classical kinship: 古典亲缘关系
- song and text: 歌曲和文本

4. One of the most recent and influential approaches to the sociology of religion is tailored to Western societies, and particularly the United States, which offers many different faiths from which to pick and choose. (Giddens, 2009)

> 宗教社会学最新和最有影响力的方法之一是为西方社会，特别是美国量身定制的，它提供了许多不同的信仰供选择。

- tailored to: 为...量身定制
- most recent and influential approaches: 最新和最有影响力的方法
- sociology of religion: 宗教社会学
- Western societies: 西方社会
- particularly: 特别是
- United States: 美国
- offers: 提供
- many different faiths: 许多不同的信仰
- from which to pick and choose: 供选择
