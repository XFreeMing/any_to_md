1. We may stare at the intruder as if to say, 'Move away!' or elbow him aside. When people are forced into proximity closer than they deem desirable, they might create a kind of physical boundary; a reader at a crowded library desk might physically demarcate a private space by stacking books around its edges. (Giddens, 2009)

> 我们可能盯着入侵者，好像说"走开！"或者用肘把他推到一边。当人们被迫进入比他们认为理想的更近的距离时，他们可能会创造一种物理边界；在拥挤的图书馆桌子上的读者可能会通过在边缘堆放书籍来物理地划定一个私人空间。

- deem: 认为
- stare at: 盯着
- intruder: 入侵者
- as if to say: 好像说
- move away: 走开
- elbow him aside: 用肘把他推到一边
- forced into: 被迫进入
- proximity: 距离
- closer than: 比...更近
- desirable: 理想的
- create: 创造
- physical boundary: 物理边界
- reader: 读者
- crowded library desk: 拥挤的图书馆桌子
- physically demarcate: 物理地划定
- private space: 私人空间
- stacking books: 堆放书籍
- edges: 边缘

2. Governments influence quite personal activities and, in times of war, can even order us to lay down our lives for aims they deem necessary. (Giddens, 2009)

> 政府影响相当个人的活动，在战争时期，甚至可以命令我们为他们认为必要的目标献出生命。

- deem: 认为
- governments: 政府
- influence: 影响
- quite personal activities: 相当个人的活动
- times of war: 战争时期
- even order us: 甚至命令我们
- lay down our lives: 献出生命
- aims: 目标
- necessary: 必要的

3. The beauty of the script, with its contrasting thin and thick lines, written from right to left in an almost rhythmic visual cadence, was deemed appropriate for the poetic words of God himself. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 文字的美，以其对比的细线和粗线，从右到左以几乎有节奏的视觉韵律书写，被认为适合上帝自己的诗意话语。

- deemed: 被认为
- beauty: 美
- script: 文字
- contrasting: 对比的
- thin and thick lines: 细线和粗线
- written from right to left: 从右到左书写
- almost rhythmic visual cadence: 几乎有节奏的视觉韵律
- appropriate for: 适合
- poetic words: 诗意话语
- God himself: 上帝自己

4. Through strict control of political, economic, and cultural life, and by means of coercive measures such as censorship and terrorism, Soviet communists persecuted those whose activities they deemed threatening to the state.

> 通过对政治、经济和文化生活的严格控制，以及通过审查和恐怖主义等强制措施，苏联共产党人迫害那些他们认为威胁国家的活动的人。

- deemed: 认为
- through: 通过
- strict control: 严格控制
- political, economic, and cultural life: 政治、经济和文化生活
- by means of: 通过
- coercive measures: 强制措施
- censorship: 审查
- terrorism: 恐怖主义
- Soviet communists: 苏联共产党人
- persecuted: 迫害
- activities: 活动
- threatening to the state: 威胁国家的
