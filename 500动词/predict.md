1. Each prophet carries a scroll with texts that predict the death of Christ, and each bears an attribute that identifies him. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 每个先知都携带一个带有预测基督死亡的文本的卷轴，每个都带有识别他的属性。

- predict: 预测
- each prophet: 每个先知
- carries: 携带
- scroll: 卷轴
- texts: 文本
- death of Christ: 基督的死亡
- bears: 带有
- attribute: 属性
- identifies him: 识别他

2. If you were asked to predict how you would feel if you lost your eyesight, you would no doubt predict that it would very significantly reduce your subjective well-being. (Zamir & Teichman, 2014)

> 如果你被要求预测如果你失去视力你会感觉如何，你无疑会预测它会非常显著地降低你的主观幸福感。

- predict: 预测
- if: 如果
- asked to: 被要求
- how you would feel: 你会感觉如何
- lost your eyesight: 失去视力
- no doubt: 无疑
- very significantly reduce: 非常显著地降低
- subjective well-being: 主观幸福感

3. Decisions about educational qualifications and career paths can also feel risky: it is difficult to predict what skills will be valuable in an economy that is changing as rapidly as ours. (Giddens, 2009)

> 关于教育资格和职业道路的决定也可能感觉有风险：很难预测在像我们这样快速变化的经济中，什么技能将是有价值的。

- predict: 预测
- decisions: 决定
- educational qualifications: 教育资格
- career paths: 职业道路
- feel risky: 感觉有风险
- difficult to: 很难
- skills: 技能
- valuable: 有价值的
- economy: 经济
- changing as rapidly as: 像...一样快速变化
- ours: 我们的

4. Newtonian physics says that, given certain conditions, we can predict what will follow. (Perry, 2010)

> 牛顿物理学说，在给定某些条件下，我们可以预测接下来会发生什么。

- predict: 预测
- Newtonian physics: 牛顿物理学
- says that: 说
- given certain conditions: 在给定某些条件下
- what will follow: 接下来会发生什么
