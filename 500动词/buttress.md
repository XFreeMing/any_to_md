1. Derived from carefully observed facts, Kepler's laws of planetary motion buttressed Copernicanism, for they made sense only in a heliocentric universe. (Perry, 2010)

> 从仔细观察到的事实推导出来，开普勒的行星运动定律支持了哥白尼主义，因为它们只有在日心宇宙中才有意义。

- buttressed: 支持
- derived from: 从...推导出来
- carefully observed facts: 仔细观察到的事实
- Kepler's laws: 开普勒定律
- planetary motion: 行星运动
- Copernicanism: 哥白尼主义
- made sense: 有意义
- only in: 只有在
- heliocentric universe: 日心宇宙

2. Faith in market monitoring has not been buttressed by the failure of market prices to predict the 2007–9 Wnancial crisis. But this failure does not necessarily support special supervisory treatment of banks, for two reasons. (Berger, Molyneux, & Wilson, 2010)

> 对市场监控的信念并没有因为市场价格未能预测2007-9年金融危机而得到支持。但这种失败并不一定支持对银行的特殊监管处理，原因有两个。

- buttressed: 被支持
- faith in: 对...的信念
- market monitoring: 市场监控
- failure: 失败
- market prices: 市场价格
- predict: 预测
- 2007–9 financial crisis: 2007-9年金融危机
- but: 但
- does not necessarily support: 并不一定支持
- special supervisory treatment: 特殊监管处理
- banks: 银行
- two reasons: 两个原因

3. The advantages of this design were structural each house buttressing the next and defensive, since any attacker would have to scale the outer walls before facing resistance on the rooftops. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 这种设计的优势是结构性的——每座房子支撑着下一座——和防御性的，因为任何攻击者都必须先攀爬外墙，然后才能在屋顶上面对抵抗。

- buttressing: 支撑
- advantages: 优势
- design: 设计
- structural: 结构性的
- each house: 每座房子
- next: 下一座
- defensive: 防御性的
- since: 因为
- attacker: 攻击者
- scale: 攀爬
- outer walls: 外墙
- facing resistance: 面对抵抗
- rooftops: 屋顶

4. The architect created an artificial slope out of concrete, rising on radially disposed barrel vaults, which buttressed one another for a strong structure.

> 建筑师用混凝土创造了一个人工斜坡，建立在径向布置的桶形拱顶上，这些拱顶相互支撑以形成坚固的结构。

- buttressed: 支撑
- architect: 建筑师
- created: 创造
- artificial slope: 人工斜坡
- concrete: 混凝土
- rising on: 建立在...上
- radially disposed: 径向布置的
- barrel vaults: 桶形拱顶
- one another: 相互
- strong structure: 坚固的结构
