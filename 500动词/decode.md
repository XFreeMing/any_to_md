1. Using his own and his patients dreams as raw material, Freud decoded dream images into what he believed to be their true meaning, claiming sexual desires or concerns were often disguised as ordinary objects. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 使用他自己和病人的梦作为原始材料，弗洛伊德将梦的图像解码为他相信是它们的真实含义，声称性欲望或关注经常被伪装成普通物体。

- decoded: 解码
- using: 使用
- his own and his patients dreams: 他自己和病人的梦
- as raw material: 作为原始材料
- Freud: 弗洛伊德
- dream images: 梦的图像
- into: 为
- what he believed to be: 他相信是
- their true meaning: 它们的真实含义
- claiming: 声称
- sexual desires: 性欲望
- concerns: 关注
- often disguised as: 经常被伪装成
- ordinary objects: 普通物体

2. ... archaeologists found clay tablets inscribed with a second early writing system... . Architect and classical scholar Michael Ventris decoded the system in 1952. The tablets proved to have been inventories and archival documents, and an early form of Greek language. (Davies et al., 2015)

> ...考古学家发现了刻有第二种早期文字系统的泥板...。建筑师和古典学者迈克尔·文特里斯在1952年解码了这个系统。这些泥板被证明是清单和档案文件，以及早期形式的希腊语。

- decoded: 解码
- archaeologists: 考古学家
- found: 发现
- clay tablets: 泥板
- inscribed with: 刻有
- second early writing system: 第二种早期文字系统
- architect: 建筑师
- classical scholar: 古典学者
- Michael Ventris: 迈克尔·文特里斯
- system: 系统
- in 1952: 在1952年
- tablets: 泥板
- proved to have been: 被证明是
- inventories: 清单
- archival documents: 档案文件
- early form: 早期形式
- Greek language: 希腊语
