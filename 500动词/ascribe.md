1. Although medieval men generally regarded women as inferior and subordinate, courtly love poetry ascribed to noble ladies superior qualities of virtue. (Perry, 2010)

> 尽管中世纪的男人通常认为女人是低等和从属的，但宫廷爱情诗将优越的美德品质归因于贵族女士。

- ascribed to: 归因于
- although: 尽管
- medieval men: 中世纪的男人
- generally regarded: 通常认为
- women: 女人
- inferior: 低等的
- subordinate: 从属的
- courtly love poetry: 宫廷爱情诗
- noble ladies: 贵族女士
- superior qualities: 优越的品质
- virtue: 美德

2. Traditionally, failure had been ascribed to human wickedness or to God's plan. Now it was being attributed to an inferior hereditary endowment. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 传统上，失败被归因于人类的邪恶或上帝的计划。现在它被归因于低等的遗传天赋。

- ascribed to: 归因于
- traditionally: 传统上
- failure: 失败
- human wickedness: 人类的邪恶
- God's plan: 上帝的计划
- now: 现在
- attributed to: 归因于
- inferior hereditary endowment: 低等的遗传天赋

3. Russia also experienced a lowering of average life expectancy after the mid-1990s, which some analysts ascribe to the effects of increasing poverty and, in particular, widespread alcohol abuse. (Giddens, 2009)

> 俄罗斯在20世纪90年代中期后也经历了平均预期寿命的降低，一些分析家将其归因于日益严重的贫困的影响，特别是广泛的酗酒。

- ascribe to: 归因于
- Russia: 俄罗斯
- experienced: 经历
- lowering: 降低
- average life expectancy: 平均预期寿命
- after the mid-1990s: 20世纪90年代中期后
- some analysts: 一些分析家
- effects: 影响
- increasing poverty: 日益严重的贫困
- in particular: 特别是
- widespread alcohol abuse: 广泛的酗酒

4. If one ascribed a sufficient number of epicycles to a planet, the planet could seem to move in a perfectly circular orbit. (Perry, 2010)

> 如果一个人将足够数量的本轮归因于一颗行星，这颗行星可能看起来在完美的圆形轨道上移动。

- ascribed: 归因
- if: 如果
- sufficient number: 足够数量
- epicycles: 本轮
- planet: 行星
- could seem to: 可能看起来
- move: 移动
- perfectly circular orbit: 完美的圆形轨道
