1. It remains to be seen how much of the latter can be changed by piecemeal reform, rather than outright revolution. Thus, policies like parental leave and affirmative action might go a long way to equalize the positions of the sexes. (Wolff, 2006)

> 后者有多少可以通过渐进式改革而不是彻底革命来改变，还有待观察。因此，像育儿假和平权行动这样的政策可能在使两性地位平等方面大有帮助。

- equalize: 使平等
- remains to be seen: 还有待观察
- latter: 后者
- changed by: 通过...改变
- piecemeal reform: 渐进式改革
- rather than: 而不是
- outright revolution: 彻底革命
- thus: 因此
- policies: 政策
- parental leave: 育儿假
- affirmative action: 平权行动
- go a long way: 大有帮助
- positions of the sexes: 两性地位

2. Sustainable development links economic development to environmental protection and is concerned to equalize global inequalities to help achieve this. (Giddens, 2009)

> 可持续发展将经济发展与环境保护联系起来，并关注使全球不平等平等化以帮助实现这一目标。

- equalize: 使平等化
- sustainable development: 可持续发展
- links: 联系
- economic development: 经济发展
- environmental protection: 环境保护
- concerned to: 关注
- global inequalities: 全球不平等
- help achieve: 帮助实现

3. Later policies favored immigration for family reunification, which helped to equalize the proportions of men and women in many ethnic minority groups. (Giddens, 2009)

> 后来的政策支持家庭团聚移民，这有助于使许多少数民族群体中男女比例平等。

- equalize: 使平等
- later policies: 后来的政策
- favored: 支持
- immigration: 移民
- family reunification: 家庭团聚
- helped to: 有助于
- proportions: 比例
- men and women: 男女
- ethnic minority groups: 少数民族群体

4. One argument contends that if we are concerned to equalize liberty then we must also equalize property—for it seems obvious that the rich can do more than the poor, and hence have more liberty. (Wolff, 2006)

> 一个论点认为，如果我们关注使自由平等，那么我们也必须使财产平等——因为富人显然比穷人能做更多，因此拥有更多自由。

- equalize: 使平等
- argument: 论点
- contends that: 认为
- if: 如果
- concerned to: 关注
- liberty: 自由
- must also: 也必须
- property: 财产
- seems obvious: 显然
- rich: 富人
- can do more than: 比...能做更多
- poor: 穷人
- hence: 因此
- more liberty: 更多自由
