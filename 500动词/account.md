1. But no single factor theory could account for the diversity of human social development, from hunting and gathering and pastoral societies to traditional civilizations, and finally to the highly complex social systems of today. (Giddens, 2009)

> 但是，没有单一因素理论能够解释人类社会发展的多样性，从狩猎采集和游牧社会到传统文明，最后到今天高度复杂的社会系统。

- account for: 解释
- no single factor theory: 没有单一因素理论
- diversity: 多样性
- human social development: 人类社会发展
- hunting and gathering: 狩猎采集
- pastoral societies: 游牧社会
- traditional civilizations: 传统文明
- highly complex social systems: 高度复杂的社会系统
- today: 今天

2. As we have seen, industrial countries currently account for only about one-fifth of the world's population, yet they are responsible for over 75 per cent of the emissions that serve to pollute the atmosphere and hasten global warming. (Giddens, 2009)

> 正如我们所看到的，工业国家目前只占世界人口的大约五分之一，但它们却要对超过75%的排放负责，这些排放污染大气并加速全球变暖。

- account for: 占
- as we have seen: 正如我们所看到的
- industrial countries: 工业国家
- currently: 目前
- only about: 只大约
- one-fifth: 五分之一
- world's population: 世界人口
- yet: 然而
- responsible for: 对...负责
- over 75 per cent: 超过75%
- emissions: 排放
- serve to: 用于
- pollute: 污染
- atmosphere: 大气
- hasten: 加速
- global warming: 全球变暖

3. Some norms are culture-specific, others are universal. The force of culture appears in varying norms, whereas it is largely our genetic predispositions—our human nature—that account for the universality of some norms. Thus, we might think of nature as universal and nurture as culture-specific. (Myers, 2012)

> 一些规范是文化特定的，其他是普遍的。文化的力量出现在不同的规范中，而主要是我们的遗传倾向——我们的人性——解释了某些规范的普遍性。因此，我们可能认为自然是普遍的，而培养是文化特定的。

- account for: 解释
- some norms: 一些规范
- culture-specific: 文化特定的
- universal: 普遍的
- force of culture: 文化的力量
- appears in: 出现在
- varying norms: 不同的规范
- whereas: 而
- largely: 主要
- genetic predispositions: 遗传倾向
- human nature: 人性
- universality: 普遍性
- thus: 因此
- think of: 认为
- nature: 自然
- nurture: 培养

4. What accounts for the decline in the proportions of the population voting in elections in recent years? (Giddens, 2009)

> 什么解释了近年来选举中投票人口比例的下降？

- accounts for: 解释
- decline: 下降
- proportions: 比例
- population: 人口
- voting: 投票
- elections: 选举
- recent years: 近年来
