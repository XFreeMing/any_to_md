2. In Phase 1, people work to develop a greater awareness of their actual behavior, what instigates it, and what its results are. One of the best ways of doing this is to keep daily logs. By helping people redefine their problems in terms of their causes and results, these records can increase their feelings of control. (Zimbardo & Ruch, 1992)

> 在第一阶段，人们努力发展对他们实际行为的更大意识，什么引发了它，以及它的结果是什么。做到这一点的最佳方法之一是保持每日日志。通过帮助人们根据其原因和结果重新定义他们的问题，这些记录可以增加他们的控制感。

- instigates: 引发
- Phase 1: 第一阶段
- work to develop: 努力发展
- greater awareness: 更大的意识
- actual behavior: 实际行为
- results: 结果
- best ways: 最佳方法
- keep daily logs: 保持每日日志
- helping people redefine: 帮助人们重新定义
- problems: 问题
- in terms of: 根据
- causes: 原因
- records: 记录
- increase: 增加
- feelings of control: 控制感
