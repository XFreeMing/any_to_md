1. The Social Democrats, although a Marxist party, supported the Austrian government, fearful that the autonomy of the different nationalities would hinder industrial development and prevent improvements for workers. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 社会民主党人，虽然是马克思主义政党，但支持奥地利政府，担心不同民族的自治会阻碍工业发展并阻止工人的改善。

- hinder: 阻碍
- Social Democrats: 社会民主党人
- although: 虽然
- Marxist party: 马克思主义政党
- supported: 支持
- Austrian government: 奥地利政府
- fearful that: 担心
- autonomy: 自治
- different nationalities: 不同民族
- industrial development: 工业发展
- prevent: 阻止
- improvements: 改善
- workers: 工人

2. Hopes for global approaches to global problems have also been hindered by political, ethnic, and religious disputes. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 对全球问题采取全球方法的希望也受到政治、种族和宗教争端的阻碍。

- hindered: 阻碍
- hopes for: 对...的希望
- global approaches: 全球方法
- global problems: 全球问题
- also been: 也受到
- political: 政治的
- ethnic: 种族的
- religious disputes: 宗教争端

3. Saying that the presence of others sometimes facilitates performance and sometimes hinders it is about as satisfying as the typical Scottish weather forecast—predicting that it might be sunny but then again it might rain. (Myers, 2012)

> 说其他人的存在有时促进表现，有时阻碍它，就像典型的苏格兰天气预报一样令人满意——预测可能是晴天，但可能又会下雨。

- hinders: 阻碍
- saying that: 说
- presence of others: 其他人的存在
- sometimes: 有时
- facilitates: 促进
- performance: 表现
- about as satisfying as: 像...一样令人满意
- typical Scottish weather forecast: 典型的苏格兰天气预报
- predicting that: 预测
- might be sunny: 可能是晴天
- but then again: 但可能又
- might rain: 可能下雨

4. The Nazis hoped to drive women out of heavy industry or other jobs that might hinder women from bearing healthy children, as well as certain professions, including university teaching, law, and medicine, which were considered inappropriate for women, especially married women. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 纳粹希望将妇女赶出重工业或其他可能阻碍妇女生育健康孩子的工作，以及某些职业，包括大学教学、法律和医学，这些被认为不适合妇女，特别是已婚妇女。

- Nazis: 纳粹
- hoped to: 希望
- drive women out of: 将妇女赶出
- heavy industry: 重工业
- other jobs: 其他工作

- as well as: 以及

- medicine: 医学
- considered inappropriate: 被认为不适合
