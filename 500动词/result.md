1. Caesar also reorganized the administrative structures of cities in Italy in an attempt to create a sense of order in their government. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 凯撒还重组了意大利城市的行政结构，试图在政府中创造一种秩序感。

- reorganized: 重组
- Caesar: 凯撒
- also: 还
- administrative structures: 行政结构
- cities: 城市
- Italy: 意大利
- attempt: 试图
- create: 创造
- sense of order: 秩序感
- government: 政府

2. Renaissance artists also developed a new conception of visual space, which resulted in a naturalistic, three- dimensional rendering of the real world. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 文艺复兴艺术家还发展了一种新的视觉空间概念，这导致了现实世界的自然主义、三维渲染。

- resulted in: 导致
- Renaissance artists: 文艺复兴艺术家
- also: 还
- developed: 发展
- new conception: 新概念
- visual space: 视觉空间
- naturalistic: 自然主义的
- three-dimensional rendering: 三维渲染
- real world: 现实世界

3. In the more advanced industrial areas, the social organization of the workplace changed: the introduction of heavy equipment resulted in men replacing women and children in the factories. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在更先进的工业地区，工作场所的社会组织发生了变化：引入重型设备导致男性在工厂中取代了妇女和儿童。

- resulted in: 导致
- more advanced industrial areas: 更先进的工业地区
- social organization: 社会组织
- workplace: 工作场所
- changed: 变化
- introduction: 引入
- heavy equipment: 重型设备
- men: 男性
- replacing: 取代
- women and children: 妇女和儿童
- factories: 工厂

4. The nascent market economy was greatly aided by the expansion of trade and wealth resulting from the voyages of discovery and the conquest and colonization of the New World. (Perry, 2010)

> 新兴的市场经济得到了贸易和财富扩张的巨大帮助，这些扩张源于发现之旅以及新世界的征服和殖民化。

- resulting from: 源于
- nascent market economy: 新兴的市场经济
- greatly aided: 得到巨大帮助
- expansion: 扩张
- trade: 贸易
- wealth: 财富
- voyages of discovery: 发现之旅
- conquest: 征服
- colonization: 殖民化
- New World: 新世界
