1. The U.S. Constitution, framed in 1787 and ratified by popular vote in 1788–1789, articulated the mechanics of self-rule. First, it created a form of government new to the modern world: a system of representative government embodying the principle of "Republicanism," that is, government run by the elected representatives of the people. (Fiero, 2011)

> 美国宪法，于1787年制定，1788-1789年通过全民投票批准，阐述了自治的机制。首先，它创建了一种对现代世界来说是新的政府形式：一个体现"共和主义"原则的代议制政府系统，即由人民选举的代表管理的政府。

- of the people: 人民的

2. For centuries, the Hopi people of Arizona and the Zuni of New Mexico, both of the pueblo culture, have fabricated the small wooden figures known as kachinas. Literally "life-bringers," kachinas are spirit-beings that embody ancestral spirits. (Fiero, 2011)

3. During the sixteenth century, the role of a diplomat was distilled down to three broad areas of operation: representing and embodying his sovereign; negotiating his prince's relationship with the host court and protecting the prince's interests (and generally some of his own); and finally, gathering information by exploiting sources cultivated at court and beyond.

> 在十六世纪，外交官的角色被提炼为三个广泛的运作领域：代表和体现他的君主；谈判他的王子与东道国宫廷的关系并保护王子的利益（通常还有他自己的一些利益）；最后，通过利用在宫廷内外培养的资源收集信息。

4. With so many men off fighting at the front, women were called on to take over jobs and responsibilities that had not been available to them before. They were now employed in jobs that had earlier been considered ''beyond the capacity of women'', including such occupations as chimney sweeps, truck drivers, farm laborers, and factory workers in heavy industry. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 由于如此多的男人在前线战斗，妇女被要求接管以前对她们不可用的工作和责任。她们现在受雇于以前被认为"超出妇女能力"的工作，包括烟囱清洁工、卡车司机、农场工人和重工业工厂工人等职业。
