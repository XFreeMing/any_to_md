1. Moreover, a belief, once formed, is difficult to discredit, so going first could give voters ideas that would favorably bias how they perceive and interpret the second speech. (Myers, 2012)

> 此外，一旦形成，信念很难被质疑，所以先发言可能会给选民一些想法，这些想法会有利地影响他们如何感知和解释第二次演讲。

- bias: 影响
- moreover: 此外
- belief: 信念
- once formed: 一旦形成
- difficult to discredit: 很难被质疑
- going first: 先发言
- give voters ideas: 给选民想法
- favorably: 有利地
- perceive: 感知
- interpret: 解释
- second speech: 第二次演讲

2. A standard view is that while the sociologist's values should not be permitted to bias their conclusions, social research should be relevant to real-world concerns. (Giddens, 2009)

> 一个标准观点是，虽然社会学家的价值观不应该被允许影响他们的结论，但社会研究应该与现实世界的关注相关。

- bias: 影响
- standard view: 标准观点
- while: 虽然
- sociologist's values: 社会学家的价值观
- should not be permitted to: 不应该被允许
- conclusions: 结论
- social research: 社会研究
- relevant to: 与...相关
- real-world concerns: 现实世界的关注

3. To protect industry we could bias the ballot in favour either of the rich or (so Mill thinks) the educated. (Wolff, 2006)

> 为了保护工业，我们可以使投票偏向富人，或者（如密尔所认为的）受过教育的人。

- bias: 使...偏向
- protect industry: 保护工业
- ballot: 投票
- in favour of: 偏向
- rich: 富人
- Mill: 密尔
- educated: 受过教育的人

4. These experiments reveal that as before-the-fact judgments bias our perceptions and interpretations, so after-the-fact judgments bias our recall.

> 这些实验揭示，正如事前判断影响我们的感知和解释一样，事后判断也影响我们的回忆。

- bias: 影响
- experiments: 实验
- reveal that: 揭示
- before-the-fact judgments: 事前判断
- perceptions: 感知
- interpretations: 解释
- after-the-fact judgments: 事后判断
- recall: 回忆
