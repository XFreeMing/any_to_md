1. Western Europe emerged as a new community in the 1950s and the 1960s and staged a remarkable economic recovery, while Eastern Europe seemed to stagnate under the thumb of the Soviet Union. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 西欧在20世纪50年代和60年代作为一个新社区出现，并实现了显著的经济复苏，而东欧似乎在苏联的控制下停滞不前。

- stagnate: 停滞不前
- Western Europe: 西欧
- emerged as: 作为...出现
- new community: 新社区
- 1950s and the 1960s: 20世纪50年代和60年代
- staged: 实现
- remarkable economic recovery: 显著的经济复苏
- while: 而
- Eastern Europe: 东欧
- seemed to: 似乎
- under the thumb of: 在...的控制下
- Soviet Union: 苏联

2. In a despotic society, economic activity stagnates, for merchants, fearful that their goods will be conﬁscated by the state, lose their initiative. (Perry, 2010)

> 在专制社会中，经济活动停滞不前，因为商人担心他们的货物会被国家没收，失去了主动性。

- stagnates: 停滞不前
- despotic society: 专制社会
- economic activity: 经济活动
- for: 因为
- merchants: 商人
- fearful that: 担心
- goods: 货物
- confiscated by the state: 被国家没收
- lose their initiative: 失去主动性

3. Due to the lack of municipal direction, city streets were often used as sewers and open drains: ''In the centre of this street is a gutter, into which potato parings, the refuse of animal and vegetable matters of all kinds, the dirty water from the washing of clothes and of the houses, are all poured, and there they stagnate and putrefy. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 由于缺乏市政指导，城市街道经常被用作下水道和明沟："在这条街道的中心是一个排水沟，土豆皮、各种动植物垃圾、洗衣服和洗房子的脏水都被倒入其中，它们在那里停滞和腐烂。"

- stagnate: 停滞
- due to: 由于
- lack of municipal direction: 缺乏市政指导
- city streets: 城市街道
- often used as: 经常被用作
- sewers: 下水道
- open drains: 明沟
- centre of this street: 这条街道的中心
- gutter: 排水沟
- potato parings: 土豆皮
- refuse: 垃圾
- animal and vegetable matters: 动植物物质
- all kinds: 各种
- dirty water: 脏水
- washing of clothes: 洗衣服
- houses: 房子
- poured: 倒入
- putrefy: 腐烂

> 工业，特别是国有重工业，表现不佳，继续停滞不前。

- stagnate: 停滞不前
- industry: 工业
- especially: 特别是
- state-owned heavy industry: 国有重工业
- fared less well: 表现不佳
- continued to: 继续
