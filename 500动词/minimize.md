1. Their intensely pessimistic outlook leads them to magnify every bad experience and minimize every good one. (Myers, 2012)

> 他们强烈的悲观前景使他们放大每一个坏经历并最小化每一个好经历。

- minimize: 最小化
- intensely pessimistic outlook: 强烈的悲观前景
- leads them to: 使他们
- magnify: 放大
- every bad experience: 每一个坏经历
- every good one: 每一个好经历

2. Plato did not overlook this difficulty. His response is to argue that the guardians must be placed in a position where the opportunities for corruption are minimized. (Wolff, 2006)

> 柏拉图没有忽视这个困难。他的回应是论证守护者必须被置于腐败机会被最小化的位置。

- minimized: 最小化
- Plato: 柏拉图
- did not overlook: 没有忽视
- difficulty: 困难
- response: 回应
- argue that: 论证
- guardians: 守护者
- placed in a position: 被置于位置
- where: 在那里
- opportunities: 机会
- for corruption: 腐败的

3. We are especially likely to befriend dissimilar people when their outgroup identity is initially minimized. (Myers, 2012)

> 当他们的外群体身份最初被最小化时，我们特别可能与不同的人交朋友。

- minimized: 最小化
- especially likely to: 特别可能
- befriend: 与...交朋友
- dissimilar people: 不同的人
- when: 当
- outgroup identity: 外群体身份
- initially: 最初

> 费斯廷格认为，人们最小化不和谐的一种方式是通过选择性接触令人愉快的信息。

- minimize: 最小化
- one way: 一种方式
- people: 人们
- dissonance: 不和谐
- Festinger: 费斯廷格
- believed: 认为
- through: 通过
- selective exposure: 选择性接触
- agreeable information: 令人愉快的信息
