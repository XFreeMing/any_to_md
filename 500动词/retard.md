1. Governments now play a major role in stimulating, and sometimes retarding, rates of economic growth and in all industrial societies there is a high level of state intervention in production, the government being far and away the largest employer. (Giddens, 2009)

> 政府现在在刺激经济增长率方面发挥主要作用，有时也阻碍经济增长率，在所有工业社会中，国家对生产的干预水平很高，政府是迄今为止最大的雇主。

- retarding: 阻碍
- governments: 政府
- play a major role: 发挥主要作用
- stimulating: 刺激
- sometimes: 有时
- rates of economic growth: 经济增长率
- industrial societies: 工业社会
- high level: 高水平
- state intervention: 国家干预
- production: 生产
- far and away: 迄今为止
- largest employer: 最大的雇主

2. Government intervention, he [Adam Smith] said, retards economic progress; it reduces the real value of the annual produce of the nation's land and labor. (Perry, 2010)

> 他说[亚当·斯密]，政府干预阻碍经济进步；它减少了国家土地和劳动的年产出的实际价值。

- retards: 阻碍
- government intervention: 政府干预
- Adam Smith: 亚当·斯密
- economic progress: 经济进步
- reduces: 减少
- real value: 实际价值
- annual produce: 年产
- nation's land and labor: 国家土地和劳动

3. Barriers to effective communication can retard or distort the message and intention of the message being conveyed which may result in failure of the communication process or an effect that is undesirable. ("Communication," 2018)

> 有效沟通的障碍可能阻碍或扭曲正在传达的信息和信息意图，这可能导致沟通过程的失败或不良效果。

- retard: 阻碍
- barriers: 障碍
- effective communication: 有效沟通
- distort: 扭曲
- message: 信息
- intention: 意图
- being conveyed: 正在传达的
- result in: 导致
- failure: 失败
- communication process: 沟通过程
- effect: 效果
- undesirable: 不良的

> 卡里利、科因和利森（2008）也指出，政府创造和操纵社会资本的努力实际上可能阻碍和损害现有的社会资本。

- retard: 阻碍
- Carilli, Coyne, and Leeson: 卡里利、科因和利森
- pointed out that: 指出
- government efforts: 政府努力
- create: 创造
- manipulate: 操纵
- social capital: 社会资本
- actually: 实际上
- damage: 损害
- existing: 现有的
