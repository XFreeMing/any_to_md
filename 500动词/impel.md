1. Impelled by the images of romantic love she has read about in novels, she seeks the same thing for herself in adulterous affairs. Unfulfilled, she is ultimately driven to suicide, unrepentant to the end for her lifestyle. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 受到她在小说中读到的浪漫爱情形象的推动，她在通奸事务中为自己寻求同样的东西。未得到满足，她最终被推向自杀，对她的生活方式至死不悔。

- impelled by: 受到...推动
- images: 形象
- romantic love: 浪漫爱情
- read about: 读到
- novels: 小说
- seeks: 寻求
- same thing: 同样的东西
- herself: 自己
- adulterous affairs: 通奸事务
- unfulfilled: 未得到满足
- ultimately: 最终
- driven to suicide: 被推向自杀
- unrepentant: 不悔的
- to the end: 至死
- lifestyle: 生活方式

2. The early entrepreneurs had little awareness that they were helping to produce momentous changes in society; they were impelled above all by religious motives. The ascetic - that is, self-denying - lifestyle of the Puritans has subsequently become an intrinsic part of modern civilization. (Guinn, 2006)

> 早期企业家几乎没有意识到他们正在帮助产生社会中的重大变化；他们首先受到宗教动机的推动。清教徒的苦行——即自我否定——生活方式随后成为现代文明的内在部分。

- impelled by: 受到...推动
- early entrepreneurs: 早期企业家
- little awareness: 几乎没有意识到
- helping to produce: 帮助产生
- momentous changes: 重大变化
- society: 社会
- above all: 首先
- religious motives: 宗教动机
- ascetic: 苦行的
- self-denying: 自我否定的
- lifestyle: 生活方式
- Puritans: 清教徒
- subsequently: 随后
- become: 成为
- intrinsic part: 内在部分
- modern civilization: 现代文明

3. In the modern world, the Christian teaching that all persons are spiritually equal before God would impel some Christians to fight for the abolition of slavery. (Perry, 2010)

> 在现代世界，所有人在上帝面前精神平等的基督教教义会推动一些基督徒为废除奴隶制而战。

- impel: 推动
- modern world: 现代世界
- Christian teaching: 基督教教义
- all persons: 所有人
- spiritually equal: 精神平等
- before God: 在上帝面前
- some Christians: 一些基督徒
- fight for: 为...而战
- abolition of slavery: 废除奴隶制

4. The Protestant threat impelled the Roman Catholic church to institute reforms. (Perry, 2010)

> 新教威胁推动了罗马天主教会进行改革。

- impelled: 推动
- Protestant threat: 新教威胁
- Roman Catholic church: 罗马天主教会
- institute reforms: 进行改革
