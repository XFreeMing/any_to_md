1. Hence Augustine posited that secular government and authority were necessary for the pursuit of the true Christian life on earth; in doing so, he provided a justification for secular political authority that would play an important role in medieval thought. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 因此，奥古斯丁假设世俗政府和权威对于追求地球上真正的基督教生活是必要的；这样做，他为世俗政治权威提供了正当性，这将在中世纪思想中发挥重要作用。

- posited: 假设
- hence: 因此
- Augustine: 奥古斯丁
- secular government: 世俗政府
- authority: 权威
- necessary for: 对...必要的
- pursuit: 追求
- true Christian life: 真正的基督教生活
- on earth: 在地球上
- in doing so: 这样做
- provided: 提供
- justification: 正当性
- secular political authority: 世俗政治权威
- play an important role: 发挥重要作用
- medieval thought: 中世纪思想

2. Gislebertus is only one of a number of Romanesque sculptors with distinct artistic personalities who are known to us by name. His style is sufficiently individual to enable scholars to posit convincingly that he trained at Cluny before his elevation to master rank at Autun. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 吉斯勒贝尔图斯只是众多具有独特艺术个性的罗马式雕塑家之一，我们通过名字知道他们。他的风格足够个性化，使学者们能够令人信服地假设他在奥顿被提升为大师级别之前在克吕尼接受过训练。

- posit: 假设
- Gislebertus: 吉斯勒贝尔图斯
- only one of: 只是...之一
- number of: 众多
- Romanesque sculptors: 罗马式雕塑家
- distinct artistic personalities: 独特艺术个性
- known to us by name: 我们通过名字知道
- style: 风格
- sufficiently individual: 足够个性化
- enable: 使...能够
- scholars: 学者们
- convincingly: 令人信服地
- trained at: 在...接受过训练
- Cluny: 克吕尼
- before: 在...之前
- elevation to master rank: 提升为大师级别
- Autun: 奥顿

3. Control theories posit that crime occurs as a result of an imbalance between impulses towards criminal activity and the social or physical controls that deter it. (Giddens, 2009)

> 控制理论假设犯罪是作为朝向犯罪活动的冲动与阻止它的社会或物理控制之间不平衡的结果而发生的。

- posit: 假设
- control theories: 控制理论
- crime occurs: 犯罪发生
- as a result of: 作为...的结果
- imbalance: 不平衡
- between: 在...之间
- impulses: 冲动
- towards criminal activity: 朝向犯罪活动
- social or physical controls: 社会或物理控制
- deter it: 阻止它

4. Claiming to use reason as his sole guide to truth, Descartes posited a sharp distinction between mind and matter. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 声称使用理性作为他对真理的唯一指导，笛卡尔假设了心灵和物质之间的尖锐区别。

- posited: 假设
- claiming to use: 声称使用
- reason: 理性
- sole guide: 唯一指导
- truth: 真理
- Descartes: 笛卡尔
- sharp distinction: 尖锐区别
- between: 在...之间
- mind: 心灵
- matter: 物质
