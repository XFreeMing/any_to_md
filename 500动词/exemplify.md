3. Whenever two or more people, groups, or nations interact, their perceived needs and goals may conflict. Many social dilemmas arise as people pursue individual self-interest to their collective detriment. Two laboratory games, the Prisoner's Dilemma and the Tragedy of the Commons, exemplify such dilemmas. (Myers, 2012)

> 每当两个或更多的人、群体或国家互动时，他们感知的需求和目标可能会冲突。当人们追求个人自身利益而损害集体利益时，许多社会困境就会出现。两个实验室游戏，囚徒困境和公地悲剧，体现了这种困境。

- perceived needs: 感知的需求

- pursue: 追求

- collective detriment: 集体利益损害
