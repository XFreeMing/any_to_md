1. Indeed, Pozzo underlines the global ambitions of Roman Catholic evangelism by adding at the four corners of the ceiling the allegorical figures of Asia, Africa, Europe, and America. (Fiero, 2011)

> 确实，波佐通过在天花板的四个角落添加亚洲、非洲、欧洲和美洲的寓言人物来强调罗马天主教传教的全球野心。

- underlines: 强调
- indeed: 确实
- Pozzo: 波佐
- global ambitions: 全球野心
- Roman Catholic evangelism: 罗马天主教传教
- adding at: 在...添加
- four corners: 四个角落
- ceiling: 天花板
- allegorical figures: 寓言人物
- Asia: 亚洲
- Africa: 非洲
- Europe: 欧洲
- America: 美洲

2. These studies, together with other empirical evidence on the effects of traumatic events, underline the importance of long-term observation of health outcomes in traumatized individuals in facilitating appropriate intervention and rehabilitation programs beyond acute needs for help. (Nezu, Nezu, Geller, & Weiner, 2012)

> 这些研究，连同关于创伤事件影响的其他经验证据，强调了长期观察创伤个体健康结果的重要性，以便在急性帮助需求之外促进适当的干预和康复计划。

- underline: 强调
- these studies: 这些研究
- together with: 连同
- other empirical evidence: 其他经验证据
- effects: 影响
- traumatic events: 创伤事件
- importance: 重要性
- long-term observation: 长期观察
- health outcomes: 健康结果
- traumatized individuals: 创伤个体
- facilitating: 促进
- appropriate intervention: 适当的干预
- rehabilitation programs: 康复计划
- beyond: 在...之外
- acute needs: 急性需求
- help: 帮助

3. His vulnerability in turn underlines his mother's desperate grief at being unable to help her son. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 他的脆弱反过来强调了他母亲因无法帮助儿子而感到的绝望悲伤。

- underlines: 强调
- vulnerability: 脆弱
- in turn: 反过来
- mother's desperate grief: 母亲的绝望悲伤
- being unable to help: 无法帮助
- son: 儿子

4. Lee uses the camera inventively to underline social conflicts, as in his radical close-ups of faces caught in bitter, heated disputes. (Oxford English Dictionary, 1989)

> 李创新地使用相机来强调社会冲突，就像在他对陷入激烈、激烈争论的面孔的激进特写中一样。

- underline: 强调
- Lee: 李
- uses the camera: 使用相机
- inventively: 创新地
- social conflicts: 社会冲突
- radical close-ups: 激进特写
- faces: 面孔
- caught in: 陷入
- bitter: 激烈的
- heated disputes: 激烈争论
