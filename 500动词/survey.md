1. As the supreme overlord, the pharaoh oversaw an army of government officials, who collected taxes, supervised construction projects, checked the irrigation works, surveyed the land, kept records, conducted foreign trade, and supervised government warehouses, where grain was stored as insurance against a bad harvest. (Perry, 2010)

- supervised construction projects: 监督建设项目

- irrigation works: 灌溉工程
- land: 土地
- kept records: 保存记录
- conducted foreign trade: 进行对外贸易
- supervised: 监督
- government warehouses: 政府仓库
- grain: 粮食
- stored: 储存
- insurance against: 应对...的保险
- bad harvest: 歉收
