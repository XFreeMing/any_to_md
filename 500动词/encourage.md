1. Until the 1630s, it looked as if the Hapsburgs would be able to use the war to enhance their power and promote centralization. But the intervention of Protestant Sweden, led by Gustavus Adolphus and encouraged by France, wrecked Hapsburg ambitions. (Perry, 2010)

> 直到 17 世纪 30 年代，看起来哈布斯堡王朝将能够利用战争来增强他们的权力并促进中央集权。但由古斯塔夫·阿道夫领导并受到法国鼓励的新教瑞典的干预，破坏了哈布斯堡的野心。

- encouraged by: 受到...鼓励
- until the 1630s: 直到 17 世纪 30 年代
- looked as if: 看起来
- Hapsburgs: 哈布斯堡王朝
- would be able to use: 将能够利用
- war: 战争
- enhance: 增强
- power: 权力
- promote centralization: 促进中央集权
- but: 但
- intervention: 干预
- Protestant Sweden: 新教瑞典
- led by: 由...领导
- Gustavus Adolphus: 古斯塔夫·阿道夫
- France: 法国
- wrecked: 破坏
- Hapsburg ambitions: 哈布斯堡的野心

2. For liberal rights are egoistic rights of separation: rights which, according to Marx, encourage each individual to view others as limitations to his or her freedom. (Wolff, 2006)

> 因为自由权利是分离的利己权利：根据马克思的说法，这些权利鼓励每个人将他人视为对他或她自由的限制。

- encourage: 鼓励
- liberal rights: 自由权利
- egoistic rights: 利己权利
- separation: 分离
- according to: 根据
- Marx: 马克思
- each individual: 每个人
- view others as: 将他人视为
- limitations: 限制
- freedom: 自由

> 哈迪德的项目通常显示她对创造易于感知的流动空间的关注，这些空间鼓励人们进入并在她的结构中移动。

- encourage: 鼓励
- Hadid's projects: 哈迪德的项目
- generally: 通常
- show: 显示
- concern for: 对...的关注
- creating: 创造
- easily perceived fluid spaces: 易于感知的流动空间
- people: 人们
- come into: 进入
- move about: 在...中移动
- structures: 结构

- encouraged: 鼓励
- as: 随着
- economies: 经济
- Western European countries: 西欧国家
- revived: 复苏
- 1950s and 1960s: 20 世纪 50 年代和 60 年代
- severe labor shortage: 严重的劳动力短缺
- them: 它们
- rely on: 依赖
- foreign workers: 外国工人
