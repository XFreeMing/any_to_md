1. For him [Aristotle], Plato's two-world philosophy suffered from too much mystery, mysticism, and poetic fancy; moreover, Plato undervalued the world of facts and objects revealed through sight, hearing, and touch, a world that was important to Aristotle. (Perry, 2010)

> 对他来说[亚里士多德]，柏拉图的二元世界哲学遭受了太多的神秘、神秘主义和诗意幻想；此外，柏拉图低估了通过视觉、听觉和触觉揭示的事实和对象的世界，这对亚里士多德来说是一个重要的世界。

- undervalued: 低估

- suffered from: 遭受
- too much: 太多的
- mystery: 神秘
- mysticism: 神秘主义
- poetic fancy: 诗意幻想
- moreover: 此外
- world of facts and objects: 事实和对象的世界
- revealed through: 通过...揭示
- sight: 视觉
- hearing: 听觉
- touch: 触觉
- important to: 对...重要的

3. The romantic revolt against the Enlightenment had an important and enduring impact on European history. (Perry, 2010)

- important and enduring impact: 重要而持久的影响

- intuition: 直觉
- instinct: 本能

- romantics: 浪漫主义者
- shed light on: 揭示
- side of human nature: 人性的一面
