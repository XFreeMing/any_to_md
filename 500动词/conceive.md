1. Can we really conceive of a form of production which will produce enough to satisfy our needs, but will not depend on an alienating system of production? (Wolff, 2006)

> 我们真的能设想一种生产形式，它将生产足够的东西来满足我们的需求，但不会依赖于异化的生产系统吗？

- conceive of: 设想
- really: 真的
- form of production: 生产形式
- produce enough: 生产足够的东西
- satisfy our needs: 满足我们的需求
- depend on: 依赖于
- alienating system: 异化的系统
- production: 生产

2. Though the tradition supports many rights of people, these rights cannot be conceived apart from the demands of justice. Even so fundamental a right as the right to hold property may give way to the demands of justice seen in the needs of others. (Guinn, 2006)

> 虽然传统支持人们的许多权利，但这些权利不能脱离正义的要求来设想。即使是像持有财产权这样基本的权利，也可能让位于在他人需求中看到的正义要求。

- conceived: 被设想
- though: 虽然
- tradition: 传统
- supports: 支持
- many rights: 许多权利
- people: 人们
- apart from: 脱离
- demands of justice: 正义的要求
- even so fundamental a right: 即使是如此基本的权利
- right to hold property: 持有财产权
- may give way to: 可能让位于
- seen in: 在...中看到的
- needs of others: 他人的需求

3. Although the Greeks did not conceive of Western civilization as a cultural entity, their artistic, intellectual, and political contributions were crucial to the foundations of Western civilization. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 虽然希腊人没有将西方文明设想为一个文化实体，但他们的艺术、知识和政治贡献对西方文明的基础至关重要。

- conceive of: 将...设想为
- although: 虽然
- Greeks: 希腊人
- did not: 没有
- Western civilization: 西方文明
- cultural entity: 文化实体
- artistic: 艺术的
- intellectual: 知识的
- political contributions: 政治贡献
- crucial to: 对...至关重要
- foundations: 基础

4. In the commemorative marble sculpture, Forever Free (1867), a young slave who has broken his chains raises his arm in victory, while his female companion kneels in grateful prayer. The artist who conceived this remarkable work of art, Edmonia Lewis (1845–ca. 1885) was the daughter of an African-American father and a Chippawa mother.

> 在纪念性大理石雕塑《永远自由》（1867）中，一个打破锁链的年轻奴隶举起手臂庆祝胜利，而他的女性同伴跪在感恩的祈祷中。构思这件非凡艺术作品的艺术家埃德蒙尼亚·刘易斯（1845-约1885）是一位非裔美国父亲和奇帕瓦母亲的女儿。

- conceived: 构思
- commemorative marble sculpture: 纪念性大理石雕塑
- Forever Free: 《永远自由》
- 1867: 1867年
- young slave: 年轻奴隶
- broken his chains: 打破锁链
- raises his arm: 举起手臂
- victory: 胜利
- female companion: 女性同伴
- kneels: 跪
- grateful prayer: 感恩的祈祷
- remarkable work of art: 非凡艺术作品
- Edmonia Lewis: 埃德蒙尼亚·刘易斯
- daughter: 女儿
- African-American father: 非裔美国父亲
- Chippawa mother: 奇帕瓦母亲
