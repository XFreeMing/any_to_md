1. Mill accepts that power should be dispersed throughout the agencies of the state, to effect a system of 'checks and balances', so that the over- ambitious have little (Wolff, 2006)

> 密尔接受权力应该分散到国家的各个机构中，以实现"制衡"制度，以便过度雄心勃勃的人几乎没有...

- effect: 实现
- Mill: 密尔
- accepts that: 接受
- power: 权力
- should be dispersed: 应该分散
- throughout: 在...中
- agencies of the state: 国家的各个机构
- system of 'checks and balances': "制衡"制度
- so that: 以便
- over-ambitious: 过度雄心勃勃的
- have little: 几乎没有

2. Anne Maass (1998) became interested in how minorities could effect social change after growing up in postwar Germany and hearing her grandmother's personal accounts of fascism. (Myers, 2012)

> 安妮·马斯（1998）在战后德国长大并听到她祖母对法西斯主义的个人描述后，对少数群体如何能够实现社会变革产生了兴趣。

- effect: 实现
- Anne Maass: 安妮·马斯
- became interested in: 对...产生兴趣
- how: 如何
- minorities: 少数群体
- social change: 社会变革
- after: 在...之后
- growing up: 长大
- postwar Germany: 战后德国
- hearing: 听到
- grandmother's personal accounts: 祖母的个人描述
- fascism: 法西斯主义

3. By asserting that God was one, sovereign, transcendent, and good, the Hebrews effected a religious revolution that separated them entirely from the world-view held by the other peoples of the ancient Near East. (Perry, 2010)

> 通过断言上帝是唯一的、主权的、超验的和善良的，希伯来人实现了一场宗教革命，使他们完全脱离了古代近东其他民族所持有的世界观。

- effected: 实现
- asserting that: 断言
- God: 上帝
- one: 唯一的
- sovereign: 主权的
- transcendent: 超验的
- good: 善良的
- Hebrews: 希伯来人
- religious revolution: 宗教革命
- separated: 脱离
- entirely from: 完全从
- world-view: 世界观
- held by: 由...持有的
- other peoples: 其他民族
- ancient Near East: 古代近东

4. By the beginning of the first millennium C.E. , Rome had become the watchdog of the ancient world.  Roman imperialism, however, worked to effect changes within the Republic itself. (Fiero, 2011)

> 到公元第一个千年开始时，罗马已成为古代世界的守护者。然而，罗马帝国主义努力在共和国本身内部实现变革。

- effect: 实现
- beginning: 开始
- first millennium C.E.: 公元第一个千年
- Rome: 罗马
- watchdog: 守护者
- ancient world: 古代世界
- Roman imperialism: 罗马帝国主义
- however: 然而
- worked to: 努力
- changes: 变革
- within: 在...内
- Republic itself: 共和国本身
