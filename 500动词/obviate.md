1. The need to continue tissue archiving is paramount. Korn writes that advances in therapeutics, diagnostics, and understanding do not obviate the need for the continuation of tissue archiving and research. The most striking example of the need for this is to be seen in respect to the central nervous system and neuromuscular disorders. (Irving & Harris, 2009)

> 继续组织存档的需要是至关重要的。科恩写道，治疗、诊断和理解方面的进展并不能消除继续组织存档和研究的需要。这方面需要的最显著例子可以在中枢神经系统和神经肌肉疾病方面看到。

2. Children in an elementary-school class may engage in a diverse set of activities, but the fact that they generally do the same activities at the same time obviates scheduling conflicts. 3. A subject offered a large incentive (e.g., $20) for performing the act can use that fact to justify what he or she has done. Who wouldn't do something boring or even write an essay one doesn't believe if the price were right? The reward, in other words, obviates the psychological need to change one's feelings about what one has done.

> 为执行行为提供大激励（例如，20美元）的受试者可以使用这一事实来证明他或她所做的事情是合理的。如果价格合适，谁不会做一些无聊的事情，甚至写一篇自己不相信的文章？换句话说，奖励消除了改变一个人对自己所做的事情的感受的心理需要。

- obviates: 消除
- subject: 受试者
- offered: 提供
- large incentive: 大激励
- performing the act: 执行行为
- use that fact: 使用这一事实

- what he or she has done: 他或她所做的事情
- boring: 无聊的
- write an essay: 写一篇文章
- doesn't believe: 不相信
- if the price were right: 如果价格合适
- reward: 奖励
- in other words: 换句话说
- psychological need: 心理需要
- change one's feelings: 改变一个人的感受
- about what one has done: 关于一个人所做的事情
