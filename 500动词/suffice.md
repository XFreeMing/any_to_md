1. Most important, recent interpretations acknowledge that one explanation may not suffice for all times and places. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 最重要的是，最近的解释承认，一种解释可能不足以适用于所有时间和地点。

- suffice: 足够
- most important: 最重要的是
- recent interpretations: 最近的解释
- acknowledge: 承认
- one explanation: 一种解释
- may not: 可能不
- for all times and places: 适用于所有时间和地点

2. This is why we can usually ignore quantum mechanics when dealing with everyday objects; instead the classical description will suffice. ("Classical physics," 2018)

> 这就是为什么我们在处理日常物体时通常可以忽略量子力学；相反，经典描述就足够了。

- suffice: 足够
- this is why: 这就是为什么
- usually ignore: 通常忽略
- quantum mechanics: 量子力学
- when dealing with: 在处理...时
- everyday objects: 日常物体
- instead: 相反
- classical description: 经典描述
- will: 将

3. Working class women frequently worked in factories to ensure the family's survival, as the men's wages often did not suffice. ("Kitchen," 2018)

> 工人阶级妇女经常在工厂工作以确保家庭的生存，因为男人的工资往往不够。

- suffice: 足够
- working class women: 工人阶级妇女
- frequently worked: 经常工作
- in factories: 在工厂
- ensure: 确保
- family's survival: 家庭的生存
- as: 因为
- men's wages: 男人的工资
- often did not: 往往不

4. So many bodies were brought to the churches every day that the consecrated ground did not suffice to hold them. (Fiero, 2011)

> 每天有如此多的尸体被带到教堂，以至于神圣的土地不足以容纳它们。

- suffice: 足够
- so many bodies: 如此多的尸体
- brought to: 被带到
- churches: 教堂
- every day: 每天
- consecrated ground: 神圣的土地
- did not: 不
- to hold them: 容纳它们
