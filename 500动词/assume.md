1. The focus of the image is the tomb, a mausoleum for painters, as suggested by the title. We can assume the painters are German because the tomb is not painted but rendered in a large woodcut, a medium associated with German art since being widely used by northern European artists during the Renaissance as well as by the Expressionists in the early twentieth century. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 图像的焦点是坟墓，一个画家的陵墓，正如标题所暗示的。我们可以假设这些画家是德国人，因为坟墓不是画的，而是用大型木刻渲染的，这是一种与德国艺术相关的媒介，因为它在文艺复兴时期被北欧艺术家广泛使用，也在20世纪初被表现主义者使用。

3. Shy, anxious people overpersonalize situations, a tendency that breeds anxious concern and, in extreme cases, paranoia. They also overestimate the extent to which other people are watching and evaluating them. If their hair won't comb right or they have a facial blemish, they assume everyone else notices and judges them accordingly. (Myers, 2012)

> 害羞、焦虑的人过度个性化情况，这种倾向滋生焦虑担忧，在极端情况下，偏执。他们也高估了其他人观看和评估他们的程度。如果他们的头发梳不好或他们有面部瑕疵，他们假设其他所有人都注意到并相应地评判他们。

- shy: 害羞的

- situations: 情况
- tendency: 倾向

- facial blemish: 面部瑕疵

- notices: 注意到
