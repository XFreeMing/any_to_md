1. Customs officials spend a lot of their time tracking and attempting to intercept the movement of drugs across national borders. (Giddens, 2009)

> 海关官员花费大量时间追踪并试图拦截毒品跨越国界的流动。

- attempting to: 试图
- customs officials: 海关官员
- spend a lot of their time: 花费大量时间
- tracking: 追踪
- intercept: 拦截
- movement: 流动
- drugs: 毒品
- across national borders: 跨越国界

2. But, despite this, very many philosophers have attempted to solve these normative political problems, and they have not been short of things to say. (Wolff, 2006)

> 但是，尽管如此，许多哲学家都试图解决这些规范性政治问题，他们并不缺乏要说的话。

- attempted to: 试图
- but: 但是
- despite this: 尽管如此
- very many philosophers: 许多哲学家
- solve: 解决
- normative political problems: 规范性政治问题
- not been short of: 并不缺乏
- things to say: 要说的话

3. The government has attempted to control inflation, but failed. The inflation remained the same.

> 政府试图控制通货膨胀，但失败了。通货膨胀保持不变。

- attempted to: 试图
- government: 政府
- control inflation: 控制通货膨胀
- failed: 失败
- inflation: 通货膨胀
- remained the same: 保持不变

4. Learned helplessness often occurs when attempts to improve a situation have proven fruitless.

> 当改善情况的尝试被证明无效时，习得性无助经常发生。

- attempts to: 试图
- learned helplessness: 习得性无助
- often occurs: 经常发生
- improve a situation: 改善情况
- proven fruitless: 被证明无效
