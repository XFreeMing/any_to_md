1. To sustain the army, Frederick William established the General War Commissariat to levy taxes to support the army and oversee its growth. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 为了维持军队，腓特烈·威廉建立了总战争委员会来征税以支持军队并监督其增长。

- sustain: 维持
- army: 军队
- Frederick William: 腓特烈·威廉
- established: 建立
- General War Commissariat: 总战争委员会
- levy taxes: 征税
- support: 支持
- oversee: 监督
- growth: 增长

2. Biotechnology, according to Monsanto, will allow us to grow better-quality crops with higher yields, while at the same time sustaining and protecting the environment. (Giddens, 2009)

> 根据孟山都的说法，生物技术将使我们能够种植更高质量、更高产量的作物，同时维持和保护环境。

- sustaining: 维持
- biotechnology: 生物技术
- according to: 根据
- Monsanto: 孟山都
- allow us to: 使我们能够
- grow: 种植
- better-quality crops: 更高质量的作物
- higher yields: 更高产量
- while at the same time: 同时
- protecting: 保护
- environment: 环境

3. Those of us living in the industrialized societies depend on many raw materials and manufactured products from developing countries to sustain our lives. (Giddens, 2009)

> 我们这些生活在工业化社会中的人依赖来自发展中国家的许多原材料和制成品来维持我们的生活。

- sustain: 维持
- those of us: 我们这些人
- living in: 生活在
- industrialized societies: 工业化社会
- depend on: 依赖
- raw materials: 原材料
- manufactured products: 制成品
- developing countries: 发展中国家
- lives: 生活

4. As precipitation in a warming and wetter world falls more as rain and less as snow, the likely result will be rainy season floods and less dry season snow and ice melt to sustain rivers. (Longman Dictionary of Contemporary English, 2015)

> 随着在变暖和更潮湿的世界中降水更多地以雨的形式而不是雪的形式下降，可能的结果将是雨季洪水，以及更少的旱季雪和冰融化来维持河流。

- sustain: 维持
- precipitation: 降水
- warming and wetter world: 变暖和更潮湿的世界
- falls more as rain: 更多地以雨的形式下降
- less as snow: 更少地以雪的形式
- likely result: 可能的结果
- rainy season floods: 雨季洪水
- less dry season: 更少的旱季
- snow and ice melt: 雪和冰融化
- rivers: 河流
