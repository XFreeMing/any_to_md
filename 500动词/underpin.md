1. The most prominent feature of this process has been the worldwide advance of consumerism and of the materialistic values and appetites that underpin burgeoning global capitalism. (Heywood, 2013)

> 这一过程最突出的特征是世界范围内消费主义的进步，以及支撑新兴全球资本主义的物质主义价值观和欲望。

- underpin: 支撑
- most prominent feature: 最突出的特征
- process: 过程
- worldwide advance: 世界范围内的进步
- consumerism: 消费主义
- materialistic values: 物质主义价值观
- appetites: 欲望
- burgeoning global capitalism: 新兴全球资本主义

2. The concept of social capital was developed in the 1970s to highlight the social and cultural factors that underpin wealth creation. (Heywood, 2013)

> 社会资本的概念是在20世纪70年代发展起来的，以突出支撑财富创造的社会和文化因素。

- underpin: 支撑
- concept: 概念
- social capital: 社会资本
- developed: 发展
- 1970s: 20世纪70年代
- highlight: 突出
- social and cultural factors: 社会和文化因素
- wealth creation: 财富创造

3. A precise definition of the knowledge economy is difficult to formulate, but in general terms, it refers to an economy in which ideas, information and forms of knowledge underpin innovation and economic growth. (Giddens, 2009)

> 知识经济的精确定义很难制定，但一般来说，它指的是思想、信息和知识形式支撑创新和经济增长的经济。

- underpin: 支撑
- precise definition: 精确定义
- knowledge economy: 知识经济
- difficult to formulate: 很难制定
- in general terms: 一般来说
- refers to: 指的是
- economy: 经济
- ideas: 思想
- information: 信息
- forms of knowledge: 知识形式
- innovation: 创新
- economic growth: 经济增长

4. What are the conditions or processes that encourage them to see authority as rightful, and therefore underpin the stability of a regime? This reflects a shift from philosophy to sociology, but it also highlights the contested nature of the concept of legitimacy. (Heywood, 2013)

> 鼓励他们将权威视为正当的条件或过程是什么，从而支撑政权的稳定性？这反映了从哲学到社会学的转变，但它也突出了合法性概念的争议性。

- underpin: 支撑
- conditions or processes: 条件或过程
- encourage: 鼓励
- see authority as: 将权威视为
- rightful: 正当的
- therefore: 从而
- stability: 稳定性
- regime: 政权
- reflects: 反映
- shift: 转变
- philosophy: 哲学
- sociology: 社会学
- highlights: 突出
- contested nature: 争议性
- concept of legitimacy: 合法性概念
