1. Megasurveys of the visual arts held regularly in Venice, Shanghai, Miami, and elsewhere attract large audiences, invite the exchange of ideas, and stimulate a vigorous commercial art market. (Fiero, 2011)

- held regularly: 定期举行

- Shanghai: 上海
- Miami: 迈阿密
- elsewhere: 其他地方
- attract: 吸引
- large audiences: 大量观众

- exchange of ideas: 思想交流
- vigorous commercial art market: 活跃的商业艺术市场

- urban problems: 城市问题
- range of social movements: 一系列社会运动

- housing conditions: 住房条件

- artists: 艺术家

- fertility: 生育力
- in the wild: 在野外
