1. To paraphrase a recent writer," [t]here might not have been moral rights" (Coyle, 2002, p. 21) (Copp, 2011)

> 用最近一位作家的话来说，"可能没有道德权利"（Coyle, 2002, p. 21）。

- paraphrase: 用...的话来说，转述
- to paraphrase: 用...的话来说
- recent writer: 最近一位作家
- might not have been: 可能没有
- moral rights: 道德权利

2. His drawings, such as the Embryo in the Womb (fig. 16.2), combine his own vivid observations with the analytic clarity of diagrams or, to paraphrase Leonardo himself, sight and insight. (Davies et al., 2010)

> 他的绘画，如《子宫中的胚胎》（图 16.2），结合了他自己生动的观察和图表的分析清晰度，或者用列奥纳多自己的话来说，是视觉和洞察力。

- paraphrase: 用...的话来说
- his drawings: 他的绘画
- such as: 如
- Embryo in the Womb: 《子宫中的胚胎》
- combine: 结合
- his own vivid observations: 他自己生动的观察
- analytic clarity: 分析清晰度
- diagrams: 图表
- to paraphrase Leonardo himself: 用列奥纳多自己的话来说
- sight: 视觉
- insight: 洞察力

3. To paraphrase La Rochefoucauld's 1665 book of Maxims, it is not as difficult to find a person who has never succumbed to a given temptation as to find a person who has succumbed only once. (Myers, 2012)

> 用拉罗什富科 1665 年的《箴言集》的话来说，找到一个从未屈服于某种诱惑的人并不比找到一个只屈服过一次的人更困难。

- paraphrase: 用...的话来说
- La Rochefoucauld: 拉罗什富科
- 1665 book of Maxims: 1665 年的《箴言集》
- not as difficult: 并不更困难
- to find: 找到
- person: 人
- has never succumbed to: 从未屈服于
- given temptation: 某种诱惑
- as to find: 比找到
- has succumbed only once: 只屈服过一次

4. In Groundwork 2 Kant tries to draw out the presuppositions of the common idea of duty, and the mains steps can be paraphrased as follows. (Skorupski, 2012)

> 在《基础》2 中，康德试图引出义务这一共同观念的前提，主要步骤可以转述如下。

- paraphrased: 转述
- Groundwork 2: 《基础》2
- Kant: 康德
- tries to: 试图
- draw out: 引出
- presuppositions: 前提
- common idea: 共同观念
- duty: 义务
- main steps: 主要步骤
- can be: 可以
- as follows: 如下
