1. Studies such as this one demonstrate how microsociology is useful in illuminating the broad institutional patterns that are the content of macrosociology. (Giddens, 2009)

> 像这样的研究展示了微观社会学在阐明作为宏观社会学内容的广泛制度模式方面是如何有用的。

- demonstrate: 展示
- studies: 研究
- such as this one: 像这样的
- microsociology: 微观社会学
- useful in: 在...方面有用
- illuminating: 阐明
- broad institutional patterns: 广泛制度模式
- content: 内容
- macrosociology: 宏观社会学

2. "Large fractions of these students demonstrate their ability to extrapolate from what they know and apply their knowledge very creatively in novel situations," he said. (Giddens, 2009)

> "这些学生中的很大一部分展示了他们从已知知识中推断并在新情况下非常有创意地应用知识的能力，"他说。

- demonstrate: 展示
- large fractions: 很大一部分
- students: 学生
- ability: 能力
- extrapolate from: 从...推断
- what they know: 已知知识
- apply: 应用
- knowledge: 知识
- very creatively: 非常有创意地
- novel situations: 新情况
- he said: 他说

3. Literary realism evolved into naturalism when writers tried to demonstrate a causal relationship between human character and the social environment: that certain conditions of life produced predictable character traits in human beings. (Perry, 2010)

> 当作家试图展示人类性格与社会环境之间的因果关系时，文学现实主义演变为自然主义：某些生活条件在人类中产生了可预测的性格特征。

- demonstrate: 展示
- literary realism: 文学现实主义
- evolved into: 演变为
- naturalism: 自然主义
- writers: 作家
- tried to: 试图
- causal relationship: 因果关系
- between: 在...之间
- human character: 人类性格
- social environment: 社会环境
- certain conditions of life: 某些生活条件
- produced: 产生
- predictable character traits: 可预测的性格特征
- human beings: 人类

4. If a lord demanded more services or dues than was customary, or if he interfered with their right to cottages or strips of farmland, the peasants might demonstrate their discontent by refusing to labor for the lord. (Perry, 2010)

> 如果领主要求比习惯更多的服务或费用，或者如果他干涉他们对小屋或农田条块的权利，农民可能通过拒绝为领主劳动来展示他们的不满。

- demonstrate: 展示
- if: 如果
- lord: 领主
- demanded: 要求
- more services: 更多服务
- dues: 费用
- customary: 习惯的
- interfered with: 干涉
- right to: 对...的权利
- cottages: 小屋
- strips of farmland: 农田条块
- peasants: 农民
- might: 可能
- discontent: 不满
- refusing to labor: 拒绝劳动
- for the lord: 为领主
