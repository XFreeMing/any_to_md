1. Moreover, all those customs and franchises mentioned above which we have conceded in our kingdom, and which are to be fulfilled, as far as pertains to us, in respect to our men; all men of our kingdom as well as clergy as laymen, shall observe as far as pertains to them, in respect to their men. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 此外，上述所有我们在我们王国中已经承认的习俗和特许权，以及就我们而言，关于我们的人应该履行的；我们王国的所有人，无论是神职人员还是平信徒，就他们而言，关于他们的人应该遵守。

- pertains to: 关于
- moreover: 此外
- customs: 习俗
- franchises: 特许权
- mentioned above: 上述
- conceded: 承认
- kingdom: 王国
- fulfilled: 履行
- as far as: 就...而言
- in respect to: 关于
- our men: 我们的人
- all men: 所有人
- clergy: 神职人员
- laymen: 平信徒
- observe: 遵守
- their men: 他们的人
