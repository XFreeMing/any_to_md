1. Women were not generally encouraged to join the guild, which was a male preserve that reinforced the professional status of men. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 妇女通常不被鼓励加入行会，这是一个强化男性专业地位的男性保留地。

- reinforced: 强化
- women: 妇女
- not generally encouraged: 通常不被鼓励
- join the guild: 加入行会
- male preserve: 男性保留地
- professional status: 专业地位
- of men: 男性的

2. Long hair, beards, and outrageous clothes served to reinforce the individualism that young Romantics were trying to express. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 长发、胡须和出格的衣服用来强化年轻浪漫主义者试图表达的个人主义。

- reinforce: 强化
- long hair: 长发
- beards: 胡须
- outrageous clothes: 出格的衣服
- served to: 用来
- individualism: 个人主义
- young Romantics: 年轻浪漫主义者
- trying to express: 试图表达

3. As the person performs or behaves better, self-esteem is encouraged to rise, and the net effect will be to reinforce both good behavior and improvement.  (Myers, 2012)

> 随着个人表现或行为更好，自尊被鼓励上升，净效果将是强化良好行为和改进。

- reinforce: 强化
- as: 随着
- person: 个人
- performs: 表现
- behaves better: 行为更好
- self-esteem: 自尊
- encouraged to rise: 被鼓励上升
- net effect: 净效果
- will be to: 将是
- both: 两者
- good behavior: 良好行为
- improvement: 改进

4. When these three factors interact, they reinforce and intensify one another. (Giddens, 2009)

> 当这三个因素相互作用时，它们相互强化和加剧。

- reinforce: 强化
- when: 当
- these three factors: 这三个因素
- interact: 相互作用
- intensify: 加剧
- one another: 相互
