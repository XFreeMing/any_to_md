1. To reference the name of the sponsoring group, Rosso has given a great deal of emphasis to the Cross itself. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 为了引用赞助团体的名称，罗索对十字架本身给予了很大的强调。

- reference: 引用
- name of the sponsoring group: 赞助团体的名称
- Rosso: 罗索
- given a great deal of emphasis: 给予了很大的强调
- Cross itself: 十字架本身

2. Her (Hannah Höch's) disjunctive Cut with the Kitchen Knife through the Last Weimar Beer Belly Cultural Epoch of Germany consists of bits and pieces clipped from newspapers and magazines: the wheels and cogs of military technology, the faces of German celebrities, and androgynous figures that reference her own bisexuality. (Fiero, 2011)

> 她（汉娜·赫希）的《用厨房刀切穿德国最后一个魏玛啤酒肚文化时代》由从报纸和杂志剪下的片段组成：军事技术的轮子和齿轮、德国名人的面孔，以及引用她自己双性恋的雌雄同体形象。

- reference: 引用
- Hannah Höch: 汉娜·赫希
- disjunctive: 分离的
- Cut with the Kitchen Knife: 《用厨房刀切穿》
- Last Weimar Beer Belly Cultural Epoch of Germany: 德国最后一个魏玛啤酒肚文化时代
- consists of: 由...组成
- bits and pieces: 片段
- clipped from: 从...剪下
- newspapers and magazines: 报纸和杂志
- wheels and cogs: 轮子和齿轮
- military technology: 军事技术
- faces: 面孔
- German celebrities: 德国名人
- androgynous figures: 雌雄同体形象
- own bisexuality: 自己的双性恋

3. When shown an underwater scene, Americans focus on the biggest fish. Asians are more likely to reference the background, such as the plants, bubbles, and rocks.

> 当展示水下场景时，美国人关注最大的鱼。亚洲人更可能引用背景，如植物、气泡和岩石。

- reference: 引用
- when shown: 当展示
- underwater scene: 水下场景
- Americans: 美国人
- focus on: 关注
- biggest fish: 最大的鱼
- Asians: 亚洲人
- more likely to: 更可能
- background: 背景
- such as: 如
- plants: 植物
- bubbles: 气泡
- rocks: 岩石
