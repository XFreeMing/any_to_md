1. Post-structuralism emphasizes that all ideas and concepts are expressed in language which itself is enmeshed in complex relations of power. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 后结构主义强调，所有思想和概念都在语言中表达，而语言本身陷入复杂的权力关系中。

- enmeshed in: 陷入
- Post-structuralism: 后结构主义
- emphasizes that: 强调
- all ideas: 所有思想
- concepts: 概念
- expressed in: 在...中表达
- language: 语言
- itself: 本身
- complex relations: 复杂关系
- power: 权力

2. The first difficulty that leaders face is that modern societies have perhaps become so complex and enmeshed with global influences that politicians find it almost impossible to get things done. (Heywood, 2013)

> 领导人面临的第一个困难是，现代社会可能变得如此复杂，并与全球影响纠缠在一起，以至于政治家发现几乎不可能完成事情。

- enmeshed with: 与...纠缠
- first difficulty: 第一个困难
- leaders: 领导人
- face: 面临
- modern societies: 现代社会
- perhaps: 可能
- become so complex: 变得如此复杂
- global influences: 全球影响
- politicians: 政治家
- find it almost impossible: 发现几乎不可能
- get things done: 完成事情

3. The things of the spirit were good because they were created by God, the source of light; the things of the world were evil because they were created by Satan, the prince of darkness. Humans, too, were enmeshed in dualism. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 精神的东西是好的，因为它们是由上帝创造的，光的源泉；世界的东西是邪恶的，因为它们是由撒旦创造的，黑暗的王子。人类也陷入了二元论。

- enmeshed in: 陷入
- things of the spirit: 精神的东西
- good: 好的
- because: 因为
- created by God: 由上帝创造的
- source of light: 光的源泉
- things of the world: 世界的东西
- evil: 邪恶的
- Satan: 撒旦
- prince of darkness: 黑暗的王子
- humans: 人类
- too: 也
- dualism: 二元论

4. It is widely accepted that, through a combination of social and technological changes, the media have become increasingly powerful political actors and, in some respects, more deeply enmeshed in the political process. (Heywood, 2013)

> 人们普遍认为，通过社会和技术变化的结合，媒体已经成为越来越强大的政治行为者，在某些方面，更深地陷入政治进程中。

- enmeshed in: 陷入
- widely accepted: 普遍认为
- through: 通过
- combination: 结合
- social and technological changes: 社会和技术变化
- media: 媒体
- become increasingly: 变得越来越
- powerful political actors: 强大的政治行为者
- in some respects: 在某些方面
- more deeply: 更深地
- political process: 政治进程
