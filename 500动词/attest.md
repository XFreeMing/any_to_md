1. The Encyclopedia had been undertaken in Paris during the 1740s as a monumental effort to bring together all human knowledge and to propagate Enlightenment ideas. Its numerous articles on science and technology and its limited coverage of theological questions attest to the new interests of eighteenth-century intellectuals. (Perry, 2010)

> 《百科全书》在18世纪40年代在巴黎开始，作为汇集所有人类知识和传播启蒙思想的巨大努力。它关于科学和技术的众多文章以及对神学问题的有限覆盖证明了18世纪知识分子的新兴趣。

- Encyclopedia: 《百科全书》

- Paris: 巴黎
- 1740s: 18世纪40年代
- monumental effort: 巨大努力
- bring together: 汇集
- all human knowledge: 所有人类知识
- propagate: 传播
- Enlightenment ideas: 启蒙思想
- numerous articles: 众多文章
- science and technology: 科学技术
- limited coverage: 有限覆盖
- theological questions: 神学问题
- new interests: 新兴趣
- eighteenth-century intellectuals: 18世纪知识分子

2. The bulk of our knowledge about Etruscan culture comes from art, and especially from their monumental tombs. These structures provide information about Etruscan building practices, and artists often painted them with scenes that reveal a glimpse of Etruscan life. Objects found within them attest to the Etruscans reputation as fine sculptors and metalworkers. (Fiero, 2011)

> 我们关于伊特鲁里亚文化的大部分知识来自艺术，特别是来自他们巨大的坟墓。这些结构提供了关于伊特鲁里亚建筑实践的信息，艺术家经常用揭示伊特鲁里亚生活一瞥的场景来绘制它们。在其中发现的物品证明了伊特鲁里亚人作为优秀雕塑家和金属工人的声誉。

- bulk of our knowledge: 我们的大部分知识
- Etruscan culture: 伊特鲁里亚文化
- comes from: 来自
- art: 艺术

- monumental tombs: 巨大的坟墓
- structures: 结构
- provide information: 提供信息
- building practices: 建筑实践

- objects found within them: 在其中发现的物品
- reputation: 声誉
- fine sculptors: 优秀雕塑家
- metalworkers: 金属工人
