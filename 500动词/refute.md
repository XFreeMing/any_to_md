1. With the help of Reason, Righteousness, and Justice, who appear to her in a vision, Christine refutes these antifeminist attacks. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在理性、正义和公正的帮助下，它们在她的异象中出现，克里斯蒂娜驳斥了这些反女权主义的攻击。

- refutes: 驳斥
- with the help of: 在...的帮助下
- Reason: 理性
- Righteousness: 正义
- Justice: 公正
- appear to her: 出现在她面前
- in a vision: 在异象中
- Christine: 克里斯蒂娜
- antifeminist attacks: 反女权主义的攻击

2. "Gulliver's Travels" is an anatomy of human nature, often criticised for its apparent misanthropy. It asks its readers to refute it, to deny that it has adequately characterised human nature and society. ("Jonathan Swift," 2018)

> 《格列佛游记》是对人性的剖析，经常因其明显的厌世而受到批评。它要求读者驳斥它，否认它充分描述了人性和社会。

- refute: 驳斥
- Gulliver's Travels: 《格列佛游记》
- anatomy: 剖析
- human nature: 人性
- often criticised for: 经常因...而受到批评
- apparent misanthropy: 明显的厌世
- asks its readers to: 要求读者
- deny that: 否认
- adequately characterised: 充分描述
- society: 社会

3. A challenge to one's views, if refuted, is more likely to solidify one's position than to undermine it, particularly if the threatening material can be examined with like-minded others.  (Giddens, 2009)

> 对一个人观点的挑战，如果被驳斥，更可能巩固一个人的立场而不是破坏它，特别是如果威胁性的材料可以与志同道合的人一起审查。

- refuted: 被驳斥
- challenge: 挑战
- one's views: 一个人的观点
- more likely to: 更可能
- solidify: 巩固
- position: 立场
- than to: 而不是
- undermine: 破坏
- particularly if: 特别是如果
- threatening material: 威胁性的材料
- examined with: 与...一起审查
- like-minded others: 志同道合的人

4. The Presumption is usually refuted by the most astonishing, agreed-upon, and dramatic finding of developmental psychology. (Giddens, 2009)

> 这个假设通常被发展心理学最令人惊讶、一致同意和戏剧性的发现所驳斥。

- refuted: 被驳斥
- Presumption: 假设
- usually: 通常
- most astonishing: 最令人惊讶的
- agreed-upon: 一致同意的
- dramatic finding: 戏剧性的发现
- developmental psychology: 发展心理学
