2. Traditionally, media studies have 'timed' events by using the day that the story was on the front page of a major newspaper such as the New York Times. Theoretically, we expect the market will react much more quickly, so we need a more fine-grained measure. The Drudge Report provides that; it is constantly updated and is archived every 15 minutes. (O'Hagan & West, 2010)

> 传统上，媒体研究通过使用故事出现在《纽约时报》等主要报纸头版的那一天来"计时"事件。理论上，我们期望市场反应更快，所以我们需要更细粒度的测量。德拉吉报告提供了这一点；它不断更新，每15分钟归档一次。

- archived: 被归档
- traditionally: 传统上
- media studies: 媒体研究
- timed events: 计时事件
- day: 天
- story: 故事
- front page: 头版
- major newspaper: 主要报纸
- New York Times: 《纽约时报》
- theoretically: 理论上
- expect: 期望
- market: 市场
- react much more quickly: 反应更快
- fine-grained measure: 细粒度的测量
- Drudge Report: 德拉吉报告
- provides that: 提供这一点
- constantly updated: 不断更新
- every 15 minutes: 每15分钟
