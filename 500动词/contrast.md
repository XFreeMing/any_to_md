1. Johnson's easy charm contrasted sharply with the prickliness of his boss.

> 约翰逊的轻松魅力与他老板的易怒形成鲜明对比。

- contrasted: 形成对比
- Johnson's easy charm: 约翰逊的轻松魅力
- sharply: 鲜明地
- prickliness: 易怒
- boss: 老板

2. The operating principles of the experiential system were described and contrasted with those of the rational system. Although the systems are independent in the sense that they operate by different rules, they nevertheless are highly interactive. (Nezu, Nezu, Geller, & Weiner, 2012)

> 经验系统的操作原则被描述并与理性系统的操作原则进行对比。尽管这些系统在它们按不同规则运作的意义上是独立的，但它们仍然是高度互动的。

- contrasted: 进行对比
- operating principles: 操作原则
- experiential system: 经验系统
- described: 被描述
- those of the rational system: 理性系统的操作原则
- although: 尽管
- systems: 系统
- independent: 独立的
- in the sense that: 在...的意义上
- operate by different rules: 按不同规则运作
- nevertheless: 然而
- highly interactive: 高度互动的

3. The approach to learning at this school contrasts sharply with the traditional methods used at other schools nearby.

> 这所学校的学习方法与附近其他学校使用的传统方法形成鲜明对比。

- contrasts: 形成对比
- approach to learning: 学习方法
- this school: 这所学校
- sharply: 鲜明地
- traditional methods: 传统方法
- used at: 在...使用的
- other schools nearby: 附近其他学校

4. The rising wealth of a small segment of the population came to contrast with widespread poverty and old aristocratic privilege. (Perry, 2010)

> 一小部分人口不断增长的财富开始与广泛的贫困和旧的贵族特权形成对比。

- contrast: 形成对比
- rising wealth: 不断增长的财富
- small segment: 一小部分
- population: 人口
- came to: 开始
- widespread poverty: 广泛的贫困
- old aristocratic privilege: 旧的贵族特权
