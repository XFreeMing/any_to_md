1. As the Lindisfarne Gospels document a process of synthesis between northern and Mediterranean elements, it helps us appreciate the remarkable contribution to European art made by migration styles and the skill of Hiberno-Saxon artists. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 由于《林迪斯法恩福音书》记录了北方和地中海元素之间的综合过程，它帮助我们欣赏由移民风格和希伯诺-撒克逊艺术家的技能对欧洲艺术做出的显著贡献。

- document: 记录
- Lindisfarne Gospels: 《林迪斯法恩福音书》
- process of synthesis: 综合过程
- between: 在...之间
- northern and Mediterranean elements: 北方和地中海元素
- helps us appreciate: 帮助我们欣赏
- remarkable contribution: 显著贡献
- European art: 欧洲艺术
- migration styles: 移民风格
- skill: 技能
- Hiberno-Saxon artists: 希伯诺-撒克逊艺术家

2. It is the product of an age that depended on realistic narrative relief—as we today depend on photography and film—to document (and immortalize) key historical events. (Fiero, 2011)

> 它是一个时代的产物，这个时代依赖于现实主义的叙事浮雕——就像我们今天依赖摄影和电影——来记录（并使其不朽）关键的历史事件。

- document: 记录
- product: 产物
- age: 时代
- depended on: 依赖于
- realistic narrative relief: 现实主义的叙事浮雕
- as we today depend on: 就像我们今天依赖
- photography and film: 摄影和电影
- immortalize: 使其不朽
- key historical events: 关键的历史事件

3. Rather than assuming that social reality objectively exists, social constructivists work to document and analyse the processes through which social reality is constructed, such that the construction then serves to confirm its own status as social reality. (Giddens, 2009)

> 与其假设社会现实客观存在，社会建构主义者致力于记录和分析社会现实被建构的过程，这样建构然后服务于确认其自身作为社会现实的地位。

- document: 记录
- rather than: 与其
- assuming that: 假设
- social reality: 社会现实
- objectively exists: 客观存在
- social constructivists: 社会建构主义者
- work to: 致力于
- analyse: 分析
- processes: 过程
- through which: 通过...过程
- constructed: 被建构
- such that: 这样
- construction: 建构
- serves to confirm: 服务于确认
- status: 地位

4. Others used the camera to document the factual realities of their time and place. (Fiero, 2011)

- document: 记录
- others: 其他人
- used the camera: 使用相机
- factual realities: 真实现实
- their time and place: 他们时代和地点
