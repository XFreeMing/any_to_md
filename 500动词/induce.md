1. All these reasons therefore induced the good Aristotle to blame women—the principal among them, I believe, being the envy he bore them. (Perry, 2010)

> 所有这些原因因此促使善良的亚里士多德责备妇女——我相信，其中主要的是他对她们的嫉妒。

- induced: 促使
- all these reasons: 所有这些原因
- therefore: 因此
- good Aristotle: 善良的亚里士多德
- blame women: 责备妇女
- principal among them: 其中主要的
- believe: 相信
- envy: 嫉妒
- bore them: 对她们的

2. Note that cognitive dissonance theory focuses not on the relative effectiveness of rewards punishments administered after the act but, rather, on what induces a desired action. (Myers, 2012)

> 注意，认知失调理论关注的不是行为后给予的奖励惩罚的相对有效性，而是什么促使了期望的行为。

- induces: 促使
- note that: 注意
- cognitive dissonance theory: 认知失调理论
- focuses not on: 关注的不是
- relative effectiveness: 相对有效性
- rewards punishments: 奖励惩罚
- administered after the act: 行为后给予的
- but rather on: 而是
- desired action: 期望的行为

3. In a mass society, the leisure industry was used to induce appropriate values amongst the public: leisure was no longer a break from work, but a preparation for it. (Giddens, 2009)

> 在大众社会中，休闲产业被用来在公众中诱导适当的价值观：休闲不再是工作的休息，而是为工作做准备。

- induce: 诱导
- mass society: 大众社会
- leisure industry: 休闲产业
- used to: 被用来
- appropriate values: 适当的价值观
- amongst the public: 在公众中
- leisure: 休闲
- no longer: 不再是
- break from work: 工作的休息
- preparation for it: 为工作做准备

4. In addition, the high levels of stress induced by poverty and unemployment may lead to more violence within families. (Myers, 2012)

> 此外，由贫困和失业引起的高水平压力可能导致家庭内更多的暴力。

- induced by: 由...引起
- in addition: 此外
- high levels of stress: 高水平压力
- poverty: 贫困
- unemployment: 失业
- may lead to: 可能导致
- more violence: 更多的暴力
- within families: 家庭内
