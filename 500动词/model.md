1. In the West, the Early Christian church building was modeled on the Roman basilica. (Fiero, 2011)

> 在西方，早期基督教教堂建筑是以罗马巴西利卡为模型的。

- modeled on: 以...为模型
- West: 西方
- Early Christian church building: 早期基督教教堂建筑
- Roman basilica: 罗马巴西利卡

2. The earliest Egyptian tombs—homes for the dead—were probably modeled on Egypt's domestic dwellings. (Fiero, 2011)

> 最早的埃及坟墓——死者的家园——可能是以埃及的住宅为模型的。

- modeled on: 以...为模型
- earliest Egyptian tombs: 最早的埃及坟墓
- homes for the dead: 死者的家园
- probably: 可能
- Egypt's domestic dwellings: 埃及的住宅

3. The Platonic and Aristotelian pictures are much more difficult, but it is for good reason that many philosophers consider virtually all philosophy done in the past twenty-five hundred years to be modeled after Plato, after Aristotle, or after both. (Solomon & Higgins, 2009)

> 柏拉图和亚里士多德的图景要困难得多，但许多哲学家认为过去2500年所做的几乎所有哲学都是以柏拉图、亚里士多德或两者为模型的，这是有充分理由的。

- modeled after: 以...为模型
- Platonic and Aristotelian pictures: 柏拉图和亚里士多德的图景
- much more difficult: 要困难得多
- for good reason: 有充分理由
- many philosophers: 许多哲学家
- consider: 认为
- virtually all philosophy: 几乎所有哲学
- done in: 在...中做的
- past twenty-five hundred years: 过去2500年
- Plato: 柏拉图
- Aristotle: 亚里士多德
- both: 两者

4. Prehistoric artists also carved and modeled sculptures in a variety of materials. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 史前艺术家也用各种材料雕刻和塑造雕塑。

- modeled: 塑造
- prehistoric artists: 史前艺术家
- also: 也
- carved: 雕刻
- sculptures: 雕塑
- variety of materials: 各种材料
