1. The fire also spared the west façade and portals, and the decision to conserve these architectural features which, at the time of the fire, were nearly 50 years old and certainly out of fashion is worth noting. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 火灾也幸免了西立面和门户，决定保护这些建筑特征（在火灾时已近50年，肯定已经过时）值得注意。

- conserve: 保护
- fire: 火灾
- spared: 幸免
- west façade: 西立面
- portals: 门户
- decision: 决定
- architectural features: 建筑特征
- at the time of the fire: 在火灾时
- nearly 50 years old: 近50年
- out of fashion: 过时
- worth noting: 值得注意

2. Anyone who recycles their plastic, paper and glass, conserves water or tries to use their car less is probably aware that they too are trying to put into practice the idea of sustainability. (Giddens, 2009)

> 任何回收塑料、纸张和玻璃、节约用水或试图少用汽车的人可能都意识到，他们也在努力实践可持续性的理念。

- conserves: 节约
- anyone: 任何人
- recycles: 回收
- plastic: 塑料
- paper: 纸张
- glass: 玻璃
- water: 水
- tries to use their car less: 试图少用汽车
- probably aware: 可能意识到
- put into practice: 实践
- idea of sustainability: 可持续性的理念

3. For instance, demands on Indonesia to conserve its rainforests could be seen as unfair, because Indonesia has a greater need than the industrialized countries for the revenue it must forgo by accepting conservation. (Giddens, 2009)

> 例如，要求印度尼西亚保护其雨林可能被视为不公平，因为印度尼西亚比工业化国家更需要它通过接受保护而必须放弃的收入。

- conserve: 保护
- for instance: 例如
- demands on: 要求
- Indonesia: 印度尼西亚
- rainforests: 雨林
- seen as unfair: 被视为不公平
- because: 因为
- greater need: 更大的需求
- industrialized countries: 工业化国家
- revenue: 收入
- must forgo: 必须放弃
- accepting conservation: 接受保护

4. If you are an American, would you favor higher gasoline and oil taxes to help conserve resources and restrain climate change? (Longman Dictionary of Contemporary English, 2015)

> 如果你是美国人，你会支持更高的汽油和石油税来帮助保护资源和抑制气候变化吗？

- conserve: 保护
- if: 如果
- American: 美国人
- favor: 支持
- higher gasoline and oil taxes: 更高的汽油和石油税
- help: 帮助
- resources: 资源
- restrain: 抑制
- climate change: 气候变化
