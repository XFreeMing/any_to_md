1. Be they mental, written, or on the computer making lists of what you want to accomplish can help you prioritize and keep you focused on what needs to get done.

> 无论是心理的、书面的，还是在电脑上，列出你想完成的事情可以帮助你确定优先级，并让你专注于需要完成的事情。

- prioritize: 确定优先级
- be they: 无论是
- mental: 心理的
- written: 书面的
- computer: 电脑
- making lists: 列出
- want to accomplish: 想完成
- help you: 帮助你
- keep you focused on: 让你专注于
- needs to get done: 需要完成

2. Let the list be your guide for what you prioritize during your working hours, making the first one on the list your most important thing.

> 让列表成为你在工作时间内确定优先级的指南，使列表上的第一个成为你最重要的事情。

- prioritize: 确定优先级
- list: 列表
- guide: 指南
- during your working hours: 在工作时间内
- making: 使
- first one: 第一个
- most important thing: 最重要的事情

3. In other words, your goal with time management is to effectively compress, organize and prioritize activities for maximum efficiency. (Williamson, 2013)

> 换句话说，你时间管理的目标是有效地压缩、组织和确定活动的优先级，以实现最大效率。

- prioritize: 确定优先级
- in other words: 换句话说
- goal: 目标
- time management: 时间管理
- effectively: 有效地
- compress: 压缩
- organize: 组织
- activities: 活动
- maximum efficiency: 最大效率

4. Prioritize your own wants rather than constantly thinking about others. (McConnell, Brue, & Flynn, 2014)

> 优先考虑你自己的需求，而不是不断考虑他人。

- prioritize: 优先考虑
- your own wants: 你自己的需求
- rather than: 而不是
- constantly thinking about: 不断考虑
- others: 他人
