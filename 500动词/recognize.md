1. In many cases, vase painters had such distinctive styles that scholars can recognize their work even without a signature, and use modern names to identify them. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 在许多情况下，花瓶画家有如此独特的风格，以至于学者们即使没有签名也能识别他们的作品，并使用现代名称来识别他们。

- recognize: 识别
- many cases: 许多情况
- vase painters: 花瓶画家
- distinctive styles: 独特的风格
- scholars: 学者们
- work: 作品
- even without a signature: 即使没有签名
- use modern names: 使用现代名称
- identify them: 识别他们

2. As you look at it [a face], your brain breaks the visual information into subdimensions such as color, depth, movement, and form and works on each aspect simultaneously before reassembling the components. Finally, using automatic processing, your brain compares the perceived image with previously stored images. Voilà! Instantly and effortlessly, you recognize your grandmother. (Myers, 2012)

> 当你看着它[一张脸]时，你的大脑将视觉信息分解为子维度，如颜色、深度、运动和形式，并同时处理每个方面，然后重新组装组件。最后，使用自动处理，你的大脑将感知到的图像与先前存储的图像进行比较。瞧！瞬间且毫不费力地，你认出了你的祖母。

- recognize: 认出
- look at: 看着
- brain: 大脑
- breaks: 分解
- visual information: 视觉信息
- subdimensions: 子维度
- color: 颜色
- depth: 深度
- movement: 运动
- form: 形式
- works on: 处理
- each aspect: 每个方面
- simultaneously: 同时
- reassembling: 重新组装
- components: 组件
- finally: 最后
- automatic processing: 自动处理
- compares: 比较
- perceived image: 感知到的图像
- previously stored images: 先前存储的图像
- instantly: 瞬间
- effortlessly: 毫不费力地
- grandmother: 祖母

3. As Jesus raises his hand to bless the bread and wine, his two disciples recognize him as the Christ. (Fiero, 2011)

> 当耶稣举起手祝福面包和酒时，他的两个门徒认出他是基督。

- recognize: 认出
- Jesus: 耶稣
- raises his hand: 举起手
- bless: 祝福
- bread and wine: 面包和酒
- two disciples: 两个门徒
- Christ: 基督

4. The Christians did not recognize other gods and therefore abstained from public festivals honoring these divinities. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 基督徒不承认其他神，因此不参加纪念这些神明的公共节日。

- recognize: 承认
- Christians: 基督徒
- did not: 不
- other gods: 其他神
- therefore: 因此
- abstained from: 不参加
- public festivals: 公共节日
- honoring: 纪念
- divinities: 神明
