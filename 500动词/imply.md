1. Moreover, upright megaliths taper upward, with a central bulge, visually implying the weight they bear, and capturing an energy that gives life to the stones. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 此外，直立的巨石向上逐渐变细，中央凸起，视觉上暗示它们承受的重量，并捕捉到赋予石头生命的能量。

- implying: 暗示
- moreover: 此外
- upright megaliths: 直立的巨石
- taper upward: 向上逐渐变细
- central bulge: 中央凸起
- visually: 视觉上
- weight they bear: 它们承受的重量
- capturing: 捕捉
- energy: 能量
- gives life to: 赋予...生命
- stones: 石头

2. The contrast is often stated in terms that imply an essential character difference between nature-loving Minoans and warmongering Mycenaeans. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 这种对比通常用暗示热爱自然的米诺斯人和好战的迈锡尼人之间本质性格差异的术语来表述。

- contrast: 对比
- often stated: 通常表述
- in terms that: 用...术语
- essential character difference: 本质性格差异
- between: 在...之间
- nature-loving Minoans: 热爱自然的米诺斯人
- warmongering Mycenaeans: 好战的迈锡尼人

3. The use of European Paleolithic terms to imply both cultural similarity and migrations of people is most explicit in the Russian Far East. For example, Derevianko speaks of the pebble-tool sites of southern Siberia as "the wave of the earliest human populations from the west, the bearers of the Olduvai industry, and it must have reached southern Siberia not later than 300,000 years ago". (Cunliffe, Joyce, & Gosden, 2009)

> 使用欧洲旧石器时代的术语来暗示文化相似性和人口迁移在俄罗斯远东最为明确。例如，德列维扬科谈到西伯利亚南部的砾石工具遗址时说："来自西方的最早人类群体的浪潮，奥杜瓦伊工业的承载者，它一定不晚于30万年前到达西伯利亚南部"。

- imply: 暗示
- use: 使用
- European Paleolithic terms: 欧洲旧石器时代的术语
- both: 两者
- cultural similarity: 文化相似性
- migrations of people: 人口迁移
- most explicit: 最为明确
- Russian Far East: 俄罗斯远东
- for example: 例如
- Derevianko: 德列维扬科
- speaks of: 谈到
- pebble-tool sites: 砾石工具遗址
- southern Siberia: 西伯利亚南部
- wave: 浪潮
- earliest human populations: 最早人类群体
- from the west: 来自西方
- bearers: 承载者
- Olduvai industry: 奥杜瓦伊工业
- must have reached: 一定到达
- not later than: 不晚于
- 300,000 years ago: 30万年前
