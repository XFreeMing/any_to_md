1. These results cannot, however, be extrapolated to other patient groups. (Perry, 2010)

> 然而，这些结果不能外推到其他患者群体。

- extrapolated to: 外推到
- these results: 这些结果
- cannot: 不能
- however: 然而
- other patient groups: 其他患者群体

2. Extrapolating from his latest findings, he reckons about 80% of these deaths might be attributed to smoking. (Giddens, 2009)

> 从他最新的发现外推，他估计这些死亡中约80%可能归因于吸烟。

- extrapolating from: 从...外推
- latest findings: 最新的发现
- reckons: 估计
- about 80%: 约80%
- these deaths: 这些死亡
- might be attributed to: 可能归因于
- smoking: 吸烟

3. "Large fractions of these students demonstrate their ability to extrapolate from what they know and apply their knowledge very creatively in novel situations," he said. (Giddens, 2009)

> "这些学生中的很大一部分展示了他们从已知知识外推并在新情况下非常有创意地应用知识的能力，"他说。

- extrapolate from: 从...外推
- large fractions: 很大一部分
- students: 学生
- demonstrate: 展示
- ability: 能力
- what they know: 已知知识
- apply: 应用
- knowledge: 知识
- very creatively: 非常有创意地
- novel situations: 新情况
- he said: 他说

4. It is possible to extrapolate future developments from current trends. (Giddens, 2009)

> 从当前趋势外推未来发展是可能的。

- extrapolate: 外推
- possible: 可能的
- future developments: 未来发展
- from current trends: 从当前趋势
