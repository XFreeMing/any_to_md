1. Love develops depending on how much each partner is prepared to reveal concerns and needs and to be vulnerable to the other. (Giddens, 2009)

> 爱情的发展取决于每个伴侣准备在多大程度上揭示关注和需求，并对另一方脆弱。

- reveal: 揭示
- love: 爱情
- develops: 发展
- depending on: 取决于
- how much: 多大程度
- each partner: 每个伴侣
- prepared to: 准备
- concerns: 关注
- needs: 需求
- vulnerable to: 对...脆弱
- other: 另一方

2. The defender of democracy replies that experts need to know the people's interests, and only a vote will show what these are. The response to this is that it is not only false that only a vote will reveal people's interests, but that opinion-polling may well do a more effective job. (Wolff, 2006)

> 民主的捍卫者回答说，专家需要知道人民的利益，只有投票才能显示这些是什么。对此的回应是，不仅只有投票才能揭示人民利益的说法是错误的，而且民意调查可能做得更有效。

- reveal: 揭示
- defender of democracy: 民主的捍卫者
- replies that: 回答说
- experts: 专家
- need to know: 需要知道
- people's interests: 人民的利益
- only a vote: 只有投票
- show: 显示
- response to this: 对此的回应
- not only false: 不仅错误
- opinion-polling: 民意调查
- may well do: 可能做得
- more effective job: 更有效的工作

3. Through gestures and movement, Leonardo hoped to reveal a person's inner life. (Fiero, 2011)

> 通过手势和动作，列奥纳多希望揭示一个人的内心生活。

- reveal: 揭示
- through: 通过
- gestures: 手势
- movement: 动作
- Leonardo: 列奥纳多
- hoped to: 希望
- person's inner life: 一个人的内心生活

4. Do males and females differ in how they experience passionate love? Studies of men and women falling in and out of love reveal some surprises. (Myers, 2012)

> 男性和女性在如何体验激情爱情方面有差异吗？对男女坠入和失恋的研究揭示了一些惊喜。

- reveal: 揭示
- males and females: 男性和女性
- differ in: 在...方面有差异
- how: 如何
- experience: 体验
- passionate love: 激情爱情
- studies: 研究
- men and women: 男女
- falling in and out of love: 坠入和失恋
- some surprises: 一些惊喜
