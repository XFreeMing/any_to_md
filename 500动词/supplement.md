1. In this manner she could on occasion supplement and lighten her father's occupation. (Fiero, 2011)

> 以这种方式，她可以偶尔补充和减轻她父亲的职业。

- supplement: 补充
- in this manner: 以这种方式
- she: 她
- could: 可以
- on occasion: 偶尔
- lighten: 减轻
- her father's occupation: 她父亲的职业

2. Social signals can be used to supplement or even replace regulation when we can use social context to encourage individuals and households to behave more prosocially. (Zamir & Teichman, 2014)

> 当我们能够使用社会背景来鼓励个人和家庭表现得更加亲社会时，社会信号可以用来补充甚至取代监管。

- supplement: 补充
- social signals: 社会信号
- can be used to: 可以用来
- or even replace: 甚至取代
- regulation: 监管
- when: 当
- use social context: 使用社会背景
- encourage: 鼓励
- individuals: 个人
- households: 家庭
- behave more prosocially: 表现得更加亲社会

3. I started reading palms when I was in my teens as a way to supplement my income from doing magic and mental shows. (Myers, 2012)

> 我在十几岁时开始看手相，作为补充我从做魔术和心理表演获得的收入的一种方式。

- supplement: 补充
- started: 开始
- reading palms: 看手相
- when I was in my teens: 我在十几岁时
- as a way to: 作为...的一种方式
- income: 收入
- from doing: 从做...获得
- magic and mental shows: 魔术和心理表演

4. Art historians often use contemporaneous written texts to supplement their understandings of art; prehistoric art, however, dates to a time before writing, for which works of art are among our only evidence. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 艺术史学家经常使用同时代的书面文本来补充他们对艺术的理解；然而，史前艺术可以追溯到文字之前的时代，对于那个时代，艺术作品是我们唯一的证据。

- supplement: 补充
- art historians: 艺术史学家
- often use: 经常使用
- contemporaneous written texts: 同时代的书面文本
- understandings of art: 对艺术的理解
- prehistoric art: 史前艺术
- however: 然而
- dates to: 追溯到
- time before writing: 文字之前的时代
- for which: 对于那个时代
- works of art: 艺术作品
- among our only evidence: 是我们唯一的证据
