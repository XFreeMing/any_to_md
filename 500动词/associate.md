1. The most important literary movement associated with the Renaissance is humanism. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 与文艺复兴相关的最重要的文学运动是人文主义。

- associated with: 与...相关的
- most important: 最重要的
- literary movement: 文学运动
- Renaissance: 文艺复兴
- humanism: 人文主义

2. In the Classical Greek world, the happiness of individuals and the meaning of life were closely associated with the life of the polis. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在古典希腊世界中，个人的幸福和生活的意义与城邦的生活密切相关。

- associated with: 与...相关的
- Classical Greek world: 古典希腊世界
- happiness: 幸福
- individuals: 个人
- meaning of life: 生活的意义
- closely: 密切地
- life of the polis: 城邦的生活

3. As the concept of Western civilization has evolved over the centuries, so have the values and unique features associated with it. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 随着西方文明的概念在几个世纪中演变，与之相关的价值观和独特特征也在演变。

- associated with: 与...相关的
- as: 随着
- concept: 概念
- Western civilization: 西方文明
- evolved: 演变
- over the centuries: 在几个世纪中
- values: 价值观
- unique features: 独特特征

4. Although Western civilization did not yet exist, its origins can be traced back to the ancient Near East, where people in Southwest Asia and in Egypt, in northeastern Africa, developed organized societies, invented writing, and created the ideas and institutions that we associate with civilization. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 尽管西方文明还不存在，但它的起源可以追溯到古代近东，在那里，西南亚和埃及（在非洲东北部）的人们发展了有组织的社会，发明了文字，并创造了我们与文明联系起来的观念和制度。

- associate: 联系
- although: 尽管
- Western civilization: 西方文明
- did not yet exist: 还不存在
- origins: 起源
- traced back to: 追溯到
- ancient Near East: 古代近东
- Southwest Asia: 西南亚
- Egypt: 埃及
- northeastern Africa: 非洲东北部
- developed: 发展
- organized societies: 有组织的社会
- invented: 发明
- writing: 文字
- created: 创造
- ideas: 观念
- institutions: 制度
- civilization: 文明
