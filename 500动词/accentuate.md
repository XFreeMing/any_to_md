1. The Renaissance was one avenue to modernity; another was the Reformation. By dividing Europe into Catholic and Protestant, the Reformation ended medieval religious unity. It also accentuated the importance of the individual person, a distinctive feature of the modern outlook. (Perry, 2010)

> 文艺复兴是通往现代性的一条途径；另一条是宗教改革。通过将欧洲分为天主教和新教，宗教改革结束了中世纪的宗教统一。它也强调了个人人的重要性，这是现代观点的独特特征。

- accentuated: 强调
- Renaissance: 文艺复兴
- avenue to modernity: 通往现代性的途径
- Reformation: 宗教改革
- dividing Europe into: 将欧洲分为
- Catholic and Protestant: 天主教和新教
- ended: 结束
- medieval religious unity: 中世纪的宗教统一
- importance: 重要性
- individual person: 个人人
- distinctive feature: 独特特征
- modern outlook: 现代观点

2. Television's modeling of the lifestyles of the wealthy also serves to accentuate feelings of "relative deprivation" and desires for more. (Myers, 2012)

> 电视对富人生活方式的模仿也有助于强调"相对剥夺"的感觉和对更多的渴望。

- accentuate: 强调
- television's modeling: 电视的模仿
- lifestyles of the wealthy: 富人的生活方式
- serves to: 有助于
- feelings: 感觉
- relative deprivation: 相对剥夺
- desires for more: 对更多的渴望

3. I (Vicki S. Helgeson) have also integrated the research on GLBT persons into the text rather than having separate sections devoted to GLBT persons or GLBT relationships, which only served to accentuate differences. (Helgeson, 2011)

> 我（Vicki S. Helgeson）也将关于GLBT人士的研究整合到文本中，而不是有专门针对GLBT人士或GLBT关系的单独部分，这只会强调差异。

- accentuate: 强调
- integrated: 整合
- research: 研究
- GLBT persons: GLBT人士
- text: 文本
- rather than: 而不是
- separate sections: 单独部分
- devoted to: 专门针对
- GLBT relationships: GLBT关系
- only served to: 只会
- differences: 差异

4. A stairway and a colonnaded portico accentuate the single entranceway and give the building a frontal "focus" usually lacking in Greek temples. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 楼梯和柱廊门廊强调了单一的入口，并给建筑一个通常希腊神庙缺乏的正面"焦点"。

- accentuate: 强调
- stairway: 楼梯
- colonnaded portico: 柱廊门廊
- single entranceway: 单一的入口
- give the building: 给建筑
- frontal focus: 正面焦点
- usually lacking: 通常缺乏
- Greek temples: 希腊神庙
