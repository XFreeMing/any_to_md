1. An important project in the new anatomy of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries was the attempt to illustrate the human body and skeleton. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 16和17世纪新解剖学的一个重要项目是试图说明人体和骨骼。

- illustrate: 说明
- important project: 重要项目
- new anatomy: 新解剖学
- sixteenth and seventeenth centuries: 16和17世纪
- attempt: 试图
- human body: 人体
- skeleton: 骨骼

2. Facts have been carefully selected to illustrate key relationships and concepts and to avoid overwhelming students with unrelated and disconnected data. (Perry, 2010)

> 事实已被仔细选择以说明关键关系和概念，并避免用不相关和断开的数据压倒学生。

- illustrate: 说明
- facts: 事实
- carefully selected: 仔细选择
- key relationships: 关键关系
- concepts: 概念
- avoid: 避免
- overwhelming: 压倒
- students: 学生
- unrelated and disconnected data: 不相关和断开的数据

3. People are especially keen on reading information that supports their political, religious, and ethical views—a phenomenon that most of us can illustrate from our own favorite news and blog sources. (Myers, 2012)

> 人们特别热衷于阅读支持他们政治、宗教和伦理观点的信息——一种我们大多数人可以从我们自己最喜欢的新闻和博客来源中说明的现象。

- illustrate: 说明
- people: 人们
- especially keen on: 特别热衷于
- reading information: 阅读信息
- supports: 支持
- political, religious, and ethical views: 政治、宗教和伦理观点
- phenomenon: 现象
- most of us: 我们大多数人
- favorite news and blog sources: 最喜欢的新闻和博客来源

4. Philosophers even referred to works of art to illustrate their theories. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 哲学家甚至参考艺术作品来说明他们的理论。

- illustrate: 说明
- philosophers: 哲学家
- even: 甚至
- referred to: 参考
- works of art: 艺术作品
- theories: 理论
