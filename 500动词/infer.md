1. For example, if you observe someone who makes repeated snide comments, you may infer that this person has a nasty disposition, and then you might try to avoid the person. (Myers, 2012)

> 例如，如果你观察到某人反复做出刻薄的评论，你可能推断这个人有恶劣的性情，然后你可能试图避开这个人。

- infer: 推断
- for example: 例如
- if: 如果
- observe: 观察
- someone: 某人
- makes repeated snide comments: 反复做出刻薄的评论
- may: 可能
- person: 人
- nasty disposition: 恶劣的性情
- then: 然后
- might try to avoid: 可能试图避开

2. Like a paleontologist inferring the appearance of a dinosaur from bone fragments, we reconstruct our distant past by using our current feelings and expectations to combine information fragments. (Myers, 2012)

> 就像古生物学家从骨骼碎片推断恐龙的外观一样，我们通过使用我们当前的感觉和期望来组合信息片段来重建我们遥远的过去。

- inferring: 推断
- like: 就像
- paleontologist: 古生物学家
- appearance: 外观
- dinosaur: 恐龙
- from bone fragments: 从骨骼碎片
- reconstruct: 重建
- distant past: 遥远的过去
- using: 使用
- current feelings: 当前的感觉
- expectations: 期望
- combine: 组合
- information fragments: 信息片段

3. Lukes's point here is that this 'ideological' exercise of power is not explicitly observable or measurable, but can be inferred when people act in ways that are against their own interests. (Giddens, 2009)

> 卢克斯这里的观点是，这种"意识形态的"权力行使不是明确可观察或可测量的，但当人们以违背自己利益的方式行动时可以被推断。

- inferred: 被推断
- Lukes's point: 卢克斯的观点
- ideological exercise of power: 意识形态的权力行使
- not explicitly observable: 不是明确可观察
- measurable: 可测量的
- but: 但
- can be: 可以被
- when: 当
- people: 人们
- act in ways: 以...方式行动
- against their own interests: 违背自己利益

4. One of the main tasks of sociological research - in combination with theoretical thinking - is to identify causes and effects. Causation cannot be directly inferred from correlation. (Giddens, 2009)

> 社会学研究的主要任务之一——结合理论思维——是识别原因和结果。因果关系不能直接从相关性推断。

- inferred from: 从...推断
- one of the main tasks: 主要任务之一
- sociological research: 社会学研究
- in combination with: 结合
- theoretical thinking: 理论思维
- identify: 识别
- causes and effects: 原因和结果
- causation: 因果关系
- cannot be directly: 不能直接
- correlation: 相关性
