- Marsden Hartley: 马斯登·哈特利

- created out of: 出于...而创造
- spiritual necessity: 精神需要

- French art: 法国艺术
- superficial: 肤浅的
- lacking soul: 缺乏灵魂
- went to: 前往
- Berlin: 柏林
- 1913: 1913年

3. To test for a possible recency effect, Miller and Campbell gave another group of students one block of testimony to read. A week later, the researchers had them read the second block and then immediately state their opinions. The results were the reverse of the other condition—a recency effect. (Myers, 2012)

> 为了测试可能的近因效应，米勒和坎贝尔给另一组学生一块证词阅读。一周后，研究人员让他们阅读第二块，然后立即陈述他们的意见。结果与其他条件相反——近因效应。

- state: 陈述
- test for: 测试
- possible recency effect: 可能的近因效应
- Miller and Campbell: 米勒和坎贝尔
- another group of students: 另一组学生
- one block of testimony: 一块证词
- read: 阅读
- week later: 一周后
- researchers: 研究人员
- second block: 第二块
- immediately: 立即
- opinions: 意见
- results: 结果
- reverse of: 与...相反
- other condition: 其他条件
- recency effect: 近因效应

4. In 1628, it (Parliament) refused to grant Charles I tax revenues unless he agreed to the Petition of Right, which stated that the king could not collect taxes without Parliament's consent or imprison people without a specific charge.

> 1628年，它（议会）拒绝授予查理一世税收收入，除非他同意《权利请愿书》，该请愿书规定国王未经议会同意不能征税，也不能在没有具体指控的情况下监禁人民。

- stated: 规定
- 1628: 1628年
- Parliament: 议会
- refused to grant: 拒绝授予
- Charles I: 查理一世
- tax revenues: 税收收入

- agreed to: 同意
- Petition of Right: 《权利请愿书》
- king: 国王
- could not collect taxes: 不能征税
- without Parliament's consent: 未经议会同意
- imprison people: 监禁人民
- specific charge: 具体指控
