1. Rousseau's view that society will function better when fewer decisions need to be made by the people as a whole begins to look much more attractive. (Wolff, 2006)

> 卢梭的观点，即当需要由人民整体做出的决定更少时，社会将更好地运作，开始看起来更有吸引力。

- function: 运作
- Rousseau's view: 卢梭的观点
- society: 社会
- better: 更好地
- fewer decisions: 更少的决定
- need to be made: 需要被做出
- people as a whole: 人民整体
- begins to look: 开始看起来
- much more attractive: 更有吸引力

2. Art historians are also studying how works of art functioned among late medieval populations to forge bonds among social groups and encourage group identity. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 艺术史学家也在研究艺术作品在中世纪晚期人群中如何发挥作用，以在社会群体之间建立联系并鼓励群体认同。

- functioned: 发挥作用
- art historians: 艺术史学家
- studying: 研究
- works of art: 艺术作品
- late medieval populations: 中世纪晚期人群
- forge bonds: 建立联系
- social groups: 社会群体
- encourage: 鼓励
- group identity: 群体认同

3. Television conveyed to Chinese audiences that other social systems seemed to function more smoothly and offer greater freedom than their own. (Giddens, 2009)

> 电视向中国观众传达，其他社会制度似乎比他们自己的运作更顺畅，提供更大的自由。

- function: 运作
- television: 电视
- conveyed to: 向...传达
- Chinese audiences: 中国观众
- other social systems: 其他社会制度
- seemed to: 似乎
- more smoothly: 更顺畅
- offer: 提供
- greater freedom: 更大的自由
- their own: 他们自己的

4. To understand his reasoning we must take a brief look at how the free market can be expected to function. (Wolff, 2006)

> 为了理解他的推理，我们必须简要看一下自由市场可以预期如何运作。

- function: 运作
- understand: 理解
- reasoning: 推理
- take a brief look: 简要看一下
- free market: 自由市场
- can be expected to: 可以预期
