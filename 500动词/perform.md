1. Although women also worked in the fields, many remained behind to care for the children, weave clothes, and perform other tasks that required labor close to home. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 虽然妇女也在田里工作，但许多人留在家里照顾孩子、织衣服和执行其他需要在家附近劳动的任务。

- perform: 执行
- although: 虽然
- women: 妇女
- also worked: 也工作
- fields: 田地
- remained behind: 留在家里
- care for: 照顾
- children: 孩子
- weave clothes: 织衣服
- other tasks: 其他任务
- required labor: 需要劳动
- close to home: 在家附近

2. Structural functionalism analyses societies as social systems in which various social institutions perform specific functions ensuring the smooth operation of the system as a whole. (Giddens, 2009)

> 结构功能主义将社会分析为社会系统，其中各种社会制度执行特定功能，确保整个系统的顺利运行。

- perform: 执行
- structural functionalism: 结构功能主义
- analyses: 分析
- societies: 社会
- social systems: 社会系统
- various social institutions: 各种社会制度
- specific functions: 特定功能
- ensuring: 确保
- smooth operation: 顺利运行
- system as a whole: 整个系统

3. To perform one's duty as an active citizen is time-consuming, if one is both to keep oneself well informed, and attend the public forum or assembly. (Wolff, 2006)

> 作为一个积极公民履行自己的职责是耗时的，如果一个人既要保持自己充分了解情况，又要参加公共论坛或集会。

- perform: 履行
- duty: 职责
- active citizen: 积极公民
- time-consuming: 耗时的
- keep oneself well informed: 保持自己充分了解情况
- attend: 参加
- public forum: 公共论坛
- assembly: 集会

4. The Play of Adam—a twelfth-century medieval drama of a type that was traditionally performed in churches—probably served as the source for the choice and arrangement of figures at Notre-Dame-la-Grande. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 《亚当剧》——一种12世纪的中世纪戏剧，传统上在教堂中演出——可能作为大圣母院人物选择和安排的来源。

- performed: 被演出
- Play of Adam: 《亚当剧》
- twelfth-century: 12世纪的
- medieval drama: 中世纪戏剧
- type: 类型
- traditionally: 传统上
- churches: 教堂
- probably served as: 可能作为
- source: 来源
- choice and arrangement: 选择和安排
- figures: 人物
- Notre-Dame-la-Grande: 大圣母院
