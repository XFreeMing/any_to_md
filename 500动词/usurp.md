1. Although Luther condemned the violence and brutality of the Peasant Revolts, social unrest and ideological warfare had only just begun. His denunciation of the lower-class rebels brought many of the German princes to his side; and some used their new religious allegiance as an excuse to seize and usurp church properties and revenues within their own domains. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 尽管路德谴责了农民起义的暴力和残暴，但社会动荡和意识形态战争才刚刚开始。他对下层阶级反叛者的谴责使许多德国王子站在他一边；一些人利用他们新的宗教忠诚作为借口，在自己的领域内夺取和篡夺教会财产和收入。

- usurp: 篡夺
- although: 尽管
- Luther: 路德
- condemned: 谴责
- violence: 暴力
- brutality: 残暴
- Peasant Revolts: 农民起义
- social unrest: 社会动荡
- ideological warfare: 意识形态战争
- only just begun: 才刚刚开始
- denunciation: 谴责
- lower-class rebels: 下层阶级反叛者
- brought many of the German princes: 使许多德国王子
- to his side: 站在他一边
- used: 利用
- new religious allegiance: 新的宗教忠诚
- excuse: 借口
- seize: 夺取
- church properties: 教会财产
- revenues: 收入
- within their own domains: 在自己的领域内

2. There were a couple of attempts to usurp. (Durant, 2010)

> 有几次篡夺的尝试。

- usurp: 篡夺
- there were: 有
- couple of attempts: 几次尝试

3. Local control is being usurped by central government, therefore all the local affairs have to be handed to the central government, which is insufficient and time-wasting. (Goodwin, 1976)

> 地方控制正在被中央政府篡夺，因此所有地方事务都必须交给中央政府，这是不足的且浪费时间的。

- usurped: 被篡夺
- local control: 地方控制
- central government: 中央政府
- therefore: 因此
- local affairs: 地方事务
- have to be handed to: 必须交给
- insufficient: 不足的
- time-wasting: 浪费时间的

4. Industrialism beganto usurp the production and distribution functions of the family. (McConnell, Brue, & Flynn, 2014)

> 工业主义开始篡夺家庭的生产和分配功能。

- usurp: 篡夺
- industrialism: 工业主义
- began to: 开始
- production and distribution functions: 生产和分配功能
- family: 家庭
