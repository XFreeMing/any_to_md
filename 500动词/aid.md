1. The development of Roman prose was greatly aided by the practice of oratory. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 罗马散文的发展在很大程度上得到了演讲实践的帮助。

- aided: 帮助
- development: 发展
- Roman prose: 罗马散文
- greatly: 在很大程度上
- practice of oratory: 演讲实践

2. The army, the government bureaucracy, and the priests and priestesses all aided the kings in their rule. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 军队、政府官僚机构和男女祭司都帮助国王进行统治。

- aided: 帮助
- army: 军队
- government bureaucracy: 政府官僚机构
- priests and priestesses: 男女祭司
- all: 都
- kings: 国王
- their rule: 他们的统治

3. Various techniques aided artists in the task of recreating the illusion of reality. (Fiero, 2011b)

> 各种技术帮助艺术家完成重现现实幻觉的任务。

- aided: 帮助
- various techniques: 各种技术
- artists: 艺术家
- task: 任务
- recreating: 重现
- illusion of reality: 现实幻觉

4. The structure of the Roman Empire itself aided the growth of Christianity. Christian missionaries, including some of Jesus' original twelve disciples, known as apostles, used Roman roads to travel throughout the empire spreading their ''good news.'' (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 罗马帝国本身的结构帮助了基督教的发展。基督教传教士，包括耶稣最初的十二门徒中的一些，被称为使徒，使用罗马道路在整个帝国旅行，传播他们的"好消息"。

- aided: 帮助
- structure: 结构
- Roman Empire: 罗马帝国
- itself: 本身
- growth: 发展
- Christianity: 基督教
- Christian missionaries: 基督教传教士
- including: 包括
- Jesus' original twelve disciples: 耶稣最初的十二门徒
- known as apostles: 被称为使徒
- used: 使用
- Roman roads: 罗马道路
- travel throughout: 在整个...旅行
- empire: 帝国
- spreading: 传播
- good news: 好消息
