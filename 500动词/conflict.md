1. This is starkly confirmed in response to a later question: when responsibility to oneself and responsibility to others conflict, how should one choose? (Wolff, 2006)

> 这在回答后面的问题时得到了明确确认：当对自己的责任和对其他人的责任冲突时，一个人应该如何选择？

- conflict: 冲突
- starkly confirmed: 明确确认
- in response to: 在回答...时
- later question: 后面的问题
- responsibility to oneself: 对自己的责任
- responsibility to others: 对其他人的责任
- how should one choose: 一个人应该如何选择

2. Despite her high degree of critical acumen, however, Wollstonecraft seems to have been deeply conflicted by her own personal efforts to reconcile her sexual passions, her need for independence, and her free-spirited will. (Fiero, 2011b)

> 尽管她有高度的批判敏锐度，然而，沃斯通克拉夫特似乎被她自己调和性激情、独立需要和自由意志的个人努力深深困扰。

- conflicted: 困扰
- despite: 尽管
- high degree: 高度
- critical acumen: 批判敏锐度
- however: 然而
- Wollstonecraft: 沃斯通克拉夫特
- seems to have been: 似乎
- deeply: 深深地
- personal efforts: 个人努力
- reconcile: 调和
- sexual passions: 性激情
- need for independence: 独立需要
- free-spirited will: 自由意志

3. The point, however, is that the moral systems of both Socratic philosophy and Christianity were suppressed because they conflicted with established views 'known for certain' to be true. (Wolff, 2006)

> 然而，重点是苏格拉底哲学和基督教的道德体系都被压制了，因为它们与"已知为确定"真实的既定观点冲突。

- conflicted: 冲突
- point: 重点
- however: 然而
- moral systems: 道德体系
- Socratic philosophy: 苏格拉底哲学
- Christianity: 基督教
- suppressed: 被压制
- because: 因为
- established views: 既定观点
- known for certain: 已知为确定
- to be true: 真实的

4. The theory of heliocentricity itself conflicted with the Bible— where, for example, the Hebrew hero Joshua is described as making the sun stand still, a miraculous event that could have occurred only if the sun normally moved around the earth.

> 日心说理论本身与圣经冲突——例如，希伯来英雄约书亚被描述为使太阳静止，这是一个只有在太阳通常围绕地球旋转时才能发生的奇迹事件。

- conflicted: 冲突
- theory of heliocentricity: 日心说理论
- itself: 本身
- Bible: 圣经
- Hebrew hero: 希伯来英雄
- Joshua: 约书亚
- described as: 被描述为
- making the sun stand still: 使太阳静止
- miraculous event: 奇迹事件
- could have occurred only if: 只有在...时才能发生
- sun normally moved around the earth: 太阳通常围绕地球旋转
