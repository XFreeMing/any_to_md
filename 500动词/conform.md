1. These disparate styles beautifully merge in a fifth-century statue (FIG. 6-13) of the Buddha from Sarnath. The statue's smooth, unadorned surfaces conform to the Indian notion of perfect body form and emphasize the figure's spirituality. (Kleiner, 2015)

> 这些不同的风格在来自萨尔纳特的五世纪佛像（图 6-13）中美丽地融合。雕像光滑、未装饰的表面符合印度对完美身体形态的概念，并强调了人物的灵性。

- conform to: 符合
- disparate styles: 不同的风格
- beautifully merge: 美丽地融合
- fifth-century statue: 五世纪雕像
- Buddha: 佛像
- Sarnath: 萨尔纳特
- smooth, unadorned surfaces: 光滑、未装饰的表面
- Indian notion: 印度概念
- perfect body form: 完美身体形态
- emphasize: 强调
- figure's spirituality: 人物的灵性

2. Newton had already paved the way by showing how reason enabled enlightened people to discover the natural laws to which all institutions should conform. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 牛顿已经通过展示理性如何使开明的人发现所有制度都应该遵守的自然法则来铺平了道路。

- conform: 遵守
- Newton: 牛顿
- paved the way: 铺平了道路
- showing: 展示
- reason: 理性
- enabled: 使
- enlightened people: 开明的人
- discover: 发现
- natural laws: 自然法则
- all institutions: 所有制度
- should: 应该

3. Even if it is true that in a closely unified, highly equal society a general will can be formed and relatively easily perceived, it is not true that, contemporary societies conform to this ideal; nor is it desirable that they should. (Wolff, 2006)

> 即使在紧密统一、高度平等的社会中可以形成并相对容易感知普遍意志，但当代社会符合这一理想并不是真的；也不应该期望它们这样做。

- conform to: 符合
- even if: 即使
- closely unified: 紧密统一的
- highly equal society: 高度平等的社会
- general will: 普遍意志
- formed: 形成
- relatively easily perceived: 相对容易感知
- contemporary societies: 当代社会
- ideal: 理想
- desirable: 应该期望的

4. Experiments reveal two reasons people conform. Normative influence results from a person's desire for acceptance: We want to be liked. The tendency to conform more when responding publicly reflects normative influence. Informational influence results from others' providing evidence about reality. The tendency to conform more on difficult decision-making tasks reflects informational influence: We want to be right. (Myers, 2012)

> 实验揭示了人们从众的两个原因。规范影响源于一个人对接受的渴望：我们想被喜欢。在公开回应时更倾向于从众反映了规范影响。信息影响源于他人提供关于现实的证据。在困难的决策任务上更倾向于从众反映了信息影响：我们想是对的。

- conform: 从众
- experiments: 实验
- reveal: 揭示
- two reasons: 两个原因
- normative influence: 规范影响
- results from: 源于
- desire for acceptance: 对接受的渴望
- want to be liked: 想被喜欢
- tendency: 倾向
- responding publicly: 公开回应
- reflects: 反映
- informational influence: 信息影响
- providing evidence: 提供证据
- about reality: 关于现实的
- difficult decision-making tasks: 困难的决策任务
- want to be right: 想是对的
