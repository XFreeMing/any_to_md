1. Media consumers are not 'cultural dopes' who can be manipulated effortlessly by corporate interests; as the scope and volume of media forms and content expand, individuals are becoming more, not less, skilled in interpreting and evaluating the messages and material they encounter. (Giddens, 2009)

> 媒体消费者不是可以被企业利益轻松操纵的"文化傻瓜"；随着媒体形式和内容的范围和数量扩大，个人在解释和评估他们遇到的信息和材料方面变得越来越熟练，而不是越来越不熟练。

- manipulated: 被操纵
- media consumers: 媒体消费者
- cultural dopes: 文化傻瓜
- effortlessly: 轻松地
- corporate interests: 企业利益
- scope and volume: 范围和数量
- media forms: 媒体形式
- content: 内容
- expand: 扩大
- individuals: 个人
- becoming more: 变得越来越
- skilled in: 在...方面熟练
- interpreting: 解释
- evaluating: 评估
- messages: 信息
- material: 材料
- encounter: 遇到

3. By the 1850s, the most prevalent use of photography was for recording the world: people, sights, and objects. These pictures were generally viewed as fact, which is ironic since, as we shall see, photographers could manipulate images in various ways, including the selection of motifs and objects to be included in, or excluded from, a photograph. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 到19世纪50年代，摄影最普遍的用途是记录世界：人、风景和物体。这些图片通常被视为事实，这是具有讽刺意味的，因为正如我们将看到的，摄影师可以以各种方式操纵图像，包括选择要包含在照片中或从照片中排除的主题和物体。

- manipulate: 操纵
- by the 1850s: 到19世纪50年代
- most prevalent use: 最普遍的用途
- photography: 摄影
- recording the world: 记录世界
- people: 人
- sights: 风景
- objects: 物体
- pictures: 图片
- viewed as fact: 被视为事实
- ironic: 具有讽刺意味的
- photographers: 摄影师
- images: 图像
- various ways: 各种方式
- selection: 选择
- motifs: 主题
- included in: 包含在
- excluded from: 从...中排除
- photograph: 照片

4. We can sometimes act ourselves into a frame of mind. Manipulated into a smiling expression, people feel better; when they scowl, the whole world seems to scowl back. So put on a happy face. (Myers, 2012)

> 我们有时可以让自己进入一种心态。被操纵成微笑的表情，人们感觉更好；当他们皱眉时，整个世界似乎都在皱眉回应。所以戴上快乐的面孔。

- frame of mind: 心态
