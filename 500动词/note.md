1. The insight of indirect utilitarianism is to note that, instead of setting out a single law—maximize happiness—the utilitarian legislator might do much better, in terms of the general happiness, to set out a larger body of law, which guarantees and respects secure rights of individuals. (Wolff, 2006)

> 间接功利主义的洞察是注意到，与其制定单一的法律——最大化幸福——功利主义立法者在总体幸福方面可能会做得更好，制定更大的法律体系，保证和尊重个人的安全权利。

- indirect utilitarianism: 间接功利主义
- instead of: 与其
- setting out: 制定
- single law: 单一法律

- utilitarian legislator: 功利主义立法者
- might do much better: 可能会做得更好
- in terms of: 在...方面
- general happiness: 总体幸福
- larger body of law: 更大的法律体系
- guarantees: 保证
- respects: 尊重
- secure rights: 安全权利
- individuals: 个人

2. Hazel Markus and colleagues (Inglehart & others, 1989; Markus & Nurius, 1986) note that our possible selves include our visions of the self we dream of becoming—the rich self, the thin self, the passionately loved and loving self. (Myers, 2012)

- Hazel Markus: 黑兹尔·马库斯

- possible selves: 可能的自我

- visions: 愿景
- self: 自我

3. Social psychologists Jacquie Vorauer and Stacey Sasaki (2010, 2011) note that multiculturalism's focus on differences, which can be positive in the absence of conflict (making intergroup exchanges seem interesting and stimulating), sometimes comes at a cost. (Myers, 2012)

> 社会心理学家杰基·沃劳尔和斯泰西·佐佐木（2010，2011）注意到，多元文化主义对差异的关注，在没有冲突的情况下可能是积极的（使群体间交流显得有趣和刺激），有时是有代价的。

- note: 注意到
- social psychologists: 社会心理学家

- making intergroup exchanges seem: 使群体间交流显得

4. Durkheim found that certain categories of people were more likely to commit suicide than others. He discovered, for example, that there were more suicides amongst men than amongst women, more Protestants than Catholics, more wealthy than poor, and more single people than married people. Durkheim also noted that suicide rates tended to be lower during times of war and higher during times of economic change or instability. (Myers, 2012)

> 涂尔干发现某些类别的人比其他人更可能自杀。例如，他发现男性中的自杀比女性多，新教徒比天主教徒多，富人比穷人多，单身者比已婚者多。涂尔干还注意到，自杀率在战争期间往往较低，在经济变化或不稳定期间往往较高。

- noted: 注意到
- Durkheim: 涂尔干
- found that: 发现
- certain categories: 某些类别
- people: 人们
- more likely to commit suicide: 更可能自杀
- discovered: 发现
- for example: 例如
- more suicides amongst: 在...中更多的自杀
- men than amongst women: 男性比女性
- Protestants than Catholics: 新教徒比天主教徒
- wealthy than poor: 富人比穷人
- single people than married people: 单身者比已婚者
- also: 还
- suicide rates: 自杀率
- tended to be lower: 往往较低
- times of war: 战争期间
- higher: 较高
- times of economic change or instability: 经济变化或不稳定期间
