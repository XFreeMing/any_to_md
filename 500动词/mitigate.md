1. Social reformers looked for ways to mitigate and solve such problems, which led them to carry out research and gather evidence on the extent and nature of the problems to reinforce their case for change. (Giddens, 2009)

> 社会改革者寻找减轻和解决这些问题的方法，这导致他们进行研究并收集关于问题的程度和性质的证据，以加强他们要求改变的理由。

- mitigate: 减轻
- social reformers: 社会改革者
- looked for ways to: 寻找...的方法
- solve: 解决
- such problems: 这些问题
- led them to: 导致他们
- carry out research: 进行研究
- gather evidence: 收集证据
- extent and nature: 程度和性质
- problems: 问题
- reinforce: 加强
- their case for change: 他们要求改变的理由

2. Durkheim was concerned with the way in which nineteenth-century French society was becoming increasingly individualistic and looked for ways to mitigate its possible harmful effects. (Giddens, 2009)

> 涂尔干关注19世纪法国社会变得越来越个人主义的方式，并寻找减轻其可能有害影响的方法。

- mitigate: 减轻
- Durkheim: 涂尔干
- concerned with: 关注
- way: 方式
- nineteenth-century: 19世纪
- French society: 法国社会
- becoming increasingly individualistic: 变得越来越个人主义
- looked for ways to: 寻找...的方法
- possible harmful effects: 可能有害影响

3. The code prescribed death for housebreaking, kidnapping, aiding the escape of slaves, receiving stolen goods, and bearing false witness, but being forgiven by the wronged party could mitigate the penalty. For example, a wife who committed adultery could be spared execution if she was pardoned by her husband. (Giddens, 2009)

> 法典规定对入室盗窃、绑架、帮助奴隶逃跑、接收赃物和作伪证处以死刑，但被受害方宽恕可以减轻惩罚。例如，犯通奸罪的妻子如果被丈夫宽恕，可以免于处决。

- code: 法典
- prescribed: 规定
- death: 死刑
- for: 对
- housebreaking: 入室盗窃
- kidnapping: 绑架
- aiding the escape of slaves: 帮助奴隶逃跑
- receiving stolen goods: 接收赃物
- bearing false witness: 作伪证
- but: 但
- being forgiven by: 被...宽恕
- wronged party: 受害方
- could: 可以
- penalty: 惩罚
- for example: 例如
- wife: 妻子
- committed adultery: 犯通奸罪
- spared execution: 免于处决
- if: 如果
- pardoned by: 被...宽恕
- her husband: 她的丈夫

- mitigate: 减轻
- attempts: 尝试
- explain: 解释
- Black Death: 黑死病
- harshness: 严酷性
- led to: 导致
- extreme sorts of behavior: 极端的行为
