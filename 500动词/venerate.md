1. Believing in a changeless universe, the Egyptians did not value change or development— what we call progress—but venerated the institutions, traditions, and authority that embodied permanence. (Perry, 2010)

> 相信一个不变的宇宙，埃及人不重视变化或发展——我们称之为进步——而是崇敬体现永恒的制度、传统和权威。

- venerated: 崇敬
- believing in: 相信
- changeless universe: 不变的宇宙
- Egyptians: 埃及人
- did not value: 不重视
- change or development: 变化或发展
- progress: 进步
- institutions: 制度
- traditions: 传统
- authority: 权威
- embodied: 体现
- permanence: 永恒

2. All these towers are burial places of chiefs, and the chapels therein are each dedicated to the idol which he venerated. (Fiero, 2011)

> 所有这些塔都是酋长的埋葬地，其中的小教堂都供奉着他所崇敬的偶像。

- venerated: 崇敬
- all these towers: 所有这些塔
- burial places: 埋葬地
- chiefs: 酋长
- chapels: 小教堂
- therein: 其中
- each dedicated to: 每个都供奉
- idol: 偶像

3. Valera believed that his Inca ancestors venerated a "true God" who was an approximation of the Christian God and whose worship the devil directed towards less worthy objects. (Scott, 2018)

> 瓦莱拉相信他的印加祖先崇敬一个"真正的上帝"，他是基督教上帝的近似，魔鬼将对他的崇拜引向不那么值得的对象。

- venerated: 崇敬
- Valera: 瓦莱拉
- believed that: 相信
- Inca ancestors: 印加祖先
- true God: 真正的上帝
- approximation: 近似
- Christian God: 基督教上帝
- worship: 崇拜
- devil: 魔鬼
- directed towards: 引向
- less worthy objects: 不那么值得的对象

4. The philosophes venerated reason; it had enabled Newton to unravel nature's mysteries and would permit people to achieve virtue and reform society. (Perry, 2010)

> 哲学家们崇敬理性；它使牛顿能够解开自然的奥秘，并将允许人们实现美德和改革社会。

- venerated: 崇敬
- philosophes: 哲学家们
- reason: 理性
- enabled: 使...能够
- Newton: 牛顿
- unravel: 解开
- nature's mysteries: 自然的奥秘
- permit: 允许
- achieve virtue: 实现美德
- reform society: 改革社会
