1. They directly seek a cure for some human disease or condition. After identifying a potential animal model of the human disease, they may give the animal a drug or excise a growth, or see if implanting stem cells alters that condition. If the intervention cures the animals or attenuates the disease, then others may try the same intervention in a small number of humans—first to see if it is relatively safe (it doesn't cause any significant adverse effects), then to see if it is efficacious. (Beauchamp & Frey, 2014)
