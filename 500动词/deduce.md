1. They did not reject those Christian beliefs that were beyond the grasp of human reason and therefore could not be deduced by rational argument. (Perry, 2010)

> 他们没有拒绝那些超出人类理性把握范围、因此无法通过理性论证推导出的基督教信仰。

- deduced: 推导出
- they: 他们
- did not reject: 没有拒绝
- Christian beliefs: 基督教信仰
- beyond the grasp of: 超出...的把握范围
- human reason: 人类理性
- therefore: 因此
- could not be: 无法被
- rational argument: 理性论证

2. Bacon's new foundation—a correct scientific method—was to be built on inductive principles. Rather than beginning with assumed first principles from which logical conclusions could be deduced he urged scientists to proceed from the particular to the general. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 培根的新基础——一个正确的科学方法——将建立在归纳原则之上。与其从可以推导出逻辑结论的假设第一原则开始，他敦促科学家从特殊到一般进行。

- deduced: 推导出
- Bacon's new foundation: 培根的新基础
- correct scientific method: 正确的科学方法
- built on: 建立在...之上
- inductive principles: 归纳原则
- rather than: 与其
- beginning with: 从...开始
- assumed first principles: 假设第一原则
- from which: 从...中
- logical conclusions: 逻辑结论
- could be: 可以被
- urged: 敦促
- scientists: 科学家
- proceed from: 从...进行
- particular: 特殊
- general: 一般

3. Our use of the availability heuristic highlights a basic principle of social thinking: People are slow to deduce particular instances from a general truth, but they are remarkably quick to infer general truth from a vivid instance. (Myers, 2012)

> 我们对可用性启发式的使用突出了社会思维的一个基本原则：人们从一般真理推导出特殊实例很慢，但他们从生动实例推断一般真理非常快。

- deduce: 推导出
- our use of: 我们对...的使用
- availability heuristic: 可用性启发式
- highlights: 突出
- basic principle: 基本原则
- social thinking: 社会思维
- people: 人们
- slow to: 很慢
- particular instances: 特殊实例
- from a general truth: 从一般真理
- but: 但
- remarkably quick: 非常快
- infer: 推断
- vivid instance: 生动实例

4. From his first postulate, Descartes deduced an additional principle, the separation of mind and matter. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 从他的第一个假设，笛卡尔推导出了一个额外的原则，即心灵和物质的分离。

- deduced: 推导出
- from his first postulate: 从他的第一个假设
- Descartes: 笛卡尔
- additional principle: 额外的原则
- separation: 分离
- mind and matter: 心灵和物质
