1. Burchell et al.'s study found a strong correlation between job insecurity and poor overall health. This link is substantiated by data from the British Household Panel Survey, which showed that people's mental and physical health continues to deteriorate with episodes of prolonged job insecurity. (Giddens, 2009)

> 伯切尔等人的研究发现工作不安全感与整体健康状况不佳之间存在强相关性。这种联系得到了英国家庭面板调查数据的证实，该调查显示，人们的心理和身体健康随着长期工作不安全感的发作而继续恶化。

- substantiated: 被证实
- Burchell et al.'s study: 伯切尔等人的研究
- found: 发现
- strong correlation: 强相关性
- between: 在...之间
- job insecurity: 工作不安全感
- poor overall health: 整体健康状况不佳
- link: 联系
- data: 数据
- British Household Panel Survey: 英国家庭面板调查
- showed that: 显示
- people's mental and physical health: 人们的心理和身体健康
- continues to deteriorate: 继续恶化
- episodes: 发作
- prolonged job insecurity: 长期工作不安全感

2. Darwin, however, substantiated the theory of evolution by explaining the process by which evolution occurs. (Fiero, 2011)

> 然而，达尔文通过解释进化发生的过程证实了进化论。

- substantiated: 证实
- Darwin: 达尔文
- however: 然而
- theory of evolution: 进化论
- explaining: 解释
- process: 过程
- evolution occurs: 进化发生

3. Breen and Goldthorpe provide an alternative analysis of the same data used by Saunders and produce radically different findings, which substantiate their own argument that class barriers are important to social mobility. (Giddens, 2009)

> 布林和戈德索普对桑德斯使用的相同数据提供了另一种分析，并产生了根本不同的发现，这些发现证实了他们自己的论点，即阶级障碍对社会流动性很重要。

- substantiate: 证实
- Breen and Goldthorpe: 布林和戈德索普
- provide: 提供
- alternative analysis: 另一种分析
- same data: 相同数据
- used by: 被...使用
- Saunders: 桑德斯
- produce: 产生
- radically different findings: 根本不同的发现
- their own argument: 他们自己的论点
- class barriers: 阶级障碍
- important to: 对...重要
- social mobility: 社会流动性

4. These numbers exist for any reason other than to substantiate a fragile argument. (Oxford English Dictionary, 1989)

> 这些数字存在的任何原因都不是为了证实一个脆弱的论证。

- substantiate: 证实
- these numbers: 这些数字
- exist for: 存在为了
- any reason other than: 任何原因除了
- fragile argument: 脆弱的论证
