1. Life in Russia has changed dramatically after the fall of communism. Older people have sometimes found it difficult to adapt, which has led to nostalgia for former communist leaders, such as Stalin and Lenn. (Giddens, 2009)

- older people: 老年人

- led to: 导致
- nostalgia: 怀旧
- former communist leaders: 前共产党领导人
- Stalin: 斯大林
- Lenin: 列宁

2. 3. The design on the back of a mirror created soon after 400 BCE is of a different genre, and shows how the Etruscans adapted Greek traditions to their own ends. Within an undulating wreath of vines stands a winged old man, one foot raised upon a rock. An inscription identifies him as the seer Chalchas, an Etruscan version of the Greek figure known from Homer's Iliad. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 公元前 400 年后不久创建的镜子背面的设计属于不同的类型，并显示了伊特鲁里亚人如何将希腊传统适应到他们自己的目的。在一个起伏的葡萄藤花环中站着一个有翅膀的老人，一只脚抬在岩石上。一个铭文将他识别为预言家卡尔卡斯，这是来自荷马《伊利亚特》的希腊人物的伊特鲁里亚版本。

- adapted: 适应
- design: 设计
- back of a mirror: 镜子背面
- created soon after 400 BCE: 公元前 400 年后不久创建的
- different genre: 不同的类型
- shows: 显示
- Etruscans: 伊特鲁里亚人
- Greek traditions: 希腊传统
- their own ends: 他们自己的目的
- undulating wreath: 起伏的花环
- vines: 葡萄藤
- winged old man: 有翅膀的老人
- one foot raised: 一只脚抬起
- rock: 岩石
- inscription: 铭文
- identifies: 识别
- seer Chalchas: 预言家卡尔卡斯
- Etruscan version: 伊特鲁里亚版本
- Greek figure: 希腊人物
- Homer's Iliad: 荷马的《伊利亚特》
