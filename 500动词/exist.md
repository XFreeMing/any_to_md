1. Various state-enforced monopolies exist (the post office, for example) which prevent newcomers from entering a particular industry. (Wolff, 2006)

> 存在各种国家强制的垄断（例如邮局），这些垄断阻止新来者进入特定行业。

- exist: 存在
- various: 各种
- state-enforced monopolies: 国家强制的垄断
- post office: 邮局
- for example: 例如
- prevent: 阻止
- newcomers: 新来者
- entering: 进入
- particular industry: 特定行业

2. According to Plato, a higher world of eternal, unchanging Ideas or Forms has always existed. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 根据柏拉图的说法，一个永恒的、不变的观念或形式的更高世界一直存在。

- existed: 存在
- according to: 根据
- Plato: 柏拉图
- higher world: 更高世界
- eternal: 永恒的
- unchanging Ideas or Forms: 不变的观念或形式
- always: 一直

3. Reason could say only that God probably exists and that he probably endowed human beings with an immortal soul; it could not prove these propositions with certainty. (Perry, 2010)

> 理性只能说上帝可能存在，他可能赋予人类不朽的灵魂；它不能确定地证明这些命题。

- exists: 存在
- reason: 理性
- could say only that: 只能说
- God: 上帝
- probably: 可能
- endowed: 赋予
- human beings: 人类
- immortal soul: 不朽的灵魂
- could not prove: 不能证明
- propositions: 命题
- with certainty: 确定地

4. Law enforcement exists in all countries, but comparative empirical studies of police forces are needed to reveal their similarities and differences. (Oxford English Dictionary, 1989)

> 执法在所有国家都存在，但需要警察部队的比较经验研究来揭示它们的相似性和差异。

- exists: 存在
- law enforcement: 执法
- all countries: 所有国家
- but: 但
- comparative empirical studies: 比较经验研究
- police forces: 警察部队
- needed to: 需要
- reveal: 揭示
- similarities: 相似性
- differences: 差异
