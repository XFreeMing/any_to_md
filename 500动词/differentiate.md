1. The Fourth Lateran Council in 1215 decreed that Jews must wear distinguishing marks, such as ribbons, yellow badges, and special veils and cloaks, to differentiate themselves from Christians. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 1215 年的第四次拉特兰会议规定，犹太人必须佩戴区分标记，如丝带、黄色徽章和特殊面纱和斗篷，以将自己与基督徒区分开来。

- differentiate: 区分
- Fourth Lateran Council: 第四次拉特兰会议
- in 1215: 在 1215 年
- decreed that: 规定
- Jews: 犹太人
- must wear: 必须佩戴
- distinguishing marks: 区分标记
- such as: 如
- ribbons: 丝带
- yellow badges: 黄色徽章
- special veils and cloaks: 特殊面纱和斗篷
- themselves: 自己
- Christians: 基督徒

2. A "cluster" of traits (race, language, physical appearance, and religious values) that form one's self-image, ethnicity also differentiates the self from mass culture. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 作为形成一个人自我形象的"一组"特征（种族、语言、外貌和宗教价值观），族裔也将自我与大众文化区分开来。

- differentiates: 区分
- cluster: 一组
- traits: 特征
- race: 种族
- language: 语言
- physical appearance: 外貌
- religious values: 宗教价值观
- form: 形成
- self-image: 自我形象
- ethnicity: 族裔
- self: 自我
- mass culture: 大众文化

3. Until the age of about four months or so, infants cannot differentiate themselves from their environment. (Giddens, 2009)

> 直到大约四个月左右，婴儿无法将自己与环境区分开来。

- differentiate: 区分
- until: 直到
- age of about four months: 大约四个月
- or so: 左右
- infants: 婴儿
- cannot: 无法
- themselves: 自己
- environment: 环境

4. In traditional societies, city and countryside were clearly differentiated. For example, they were different in the number of populations, the means of transportation and so on. (Guinn, 2006)

> 在传统社会中，城市和乡村被明确区分。例如，它们在人口数量、交通方式等方面不同。

- differentiated: 区分
- traditional societies: 传统社会
- city: 城市
- countryside: 乡村
- clearly: 明确地
- for example: 例如
- different in: 在...方面不同
- number of populations: 人口数量
- means of transportation: 交通方式
- and so on: 等等
