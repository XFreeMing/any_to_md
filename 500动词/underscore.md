1. The sense of meaninglessness that inspired the Theater of the Absurd also underscored the philosophy of existentialism of Albert Camus (1913--1960) and Jean Paul Sartre (1905--1980). (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 激发荒诞剧场的无意义感也强调了阿尔贝·加缪（1913-1960）和让-保罗·萨特（1905-1980）的存在主义哲学。

- underscored: 强调
- sense of meaninglessness: 无意义感
- inspired: 激发
- Theater of the Absurd: 荒诞剧场
- philosophy of existentialism: 存在主义哲学
- Albert Camus: 阿尔贝·加缪
- Jean Paul Sartre: 让-保罗·萨特

2. To underscore the importance of these issues, I use statistics to show that industrial farming has caused more pain and suffering than did the atrocious behavior that produced the Holocaust. (Rachels, 2011)

> 为了强调这些问题的重要性，我使用统计数据来表明，工业化农业造成的痛苦和苦难比产生大屠杀的残暴行为更多。

- underscore: 强调
- importance: 重要性
- issues: 问题
- use statistics: 使用统计数据
- show that: 表明
- industrial farming: 工业化农业
- caused: 造成
- pain and suffering: 痛苦和苦难
- atrocious behavior: 残暴行为
- produced: 产生
- Holocaust: 大屠杀

3. Because marriage and family play a fundamental role in any society, wedding rituals are especially significant. Whether private or public, sacred or secular, weddings reveal, confirm, and underscore important cultural values. (Haviland, Prints, Walrath, & McBride, 2010)

> 因为婚姻和家庭在任何社会中都发挥着根本作用，婚礼仪式特别重要。无论是私人的还是公共的，神圣的还是世俗的，婚礼都揭示、确认和强调重要的文化价值观。

- underscore: 强调
- because: 因为
- marriage and family: 婚姻和家庭
- play a fundamental role: 发挥根本作用
- any society: 任何社会
- wedding rituals: 婚礼仪式
- especially significant: 特别重要
- whether: 无论
- private or public: 私人的还是公共的
- sacred or secular: 神圣的还是世俗的
- weddings: 婚礼
- reveal: 揭示
- confirm: 确认
- important cultural values: 重要的文化价值观

4. Comparison of the two manuscripts underscores how Western Europe had been transformed from an oral to a written culture, in part a result of Charlemagne's ambitions and policies. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 对这两份手稿的比较强调了西欧如何从口头文化转变为书面文化，部分原因是查理曼的野心和政策。

- underscores: 强调
- comparison: 比较
- two manuscripts: 两份手稿
- Western Europe: 西欧
- transformed from: 从...转变
- oral to a written culture: 口头文化到书面文化
- in part: 部分
- result of: ...的结果
- Charlemagne's ambitions: 查理曼的野心
- policies: 政策
