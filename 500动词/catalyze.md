1. The successful July Revolution in France served to catalyze change in Britain. The Industrial Revolution had led to an expanding group of industrial leaders who objected to the corrupt British electoral system, which excluded them from political power. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 法国成功的七月革命促进了英国的变革。工业革命导致了一个不断扩大的工业领袖群体，他们反对腐败的英国选举制度，该制度将他们排除在政治权力之外。

- catalyze: 促进
- successful July Revolution: 成功的七月革命
- France: 法国
- served to: 用于
- change: 变革
- Britain: 英国
- Industrial Revolution: 工业革命
- led to: 导致
- expanding group: 不断扩大的群体
- industrial leaders: 工业领袖
- objected to: 反对
- corrupt British electoral system: 腐败的英国选举制度
- excluded them from: 将他们排除在...之外
- political power: 政治权力

2. A culture rewarding ingenuity and success will catalyze a new and international wave of economic growth, boosting the GDP of the country go higher. (McConnell, Brue, & Flynn, 2014)

> 奖励独创性和成功的文化将促进新的国际经济增长浪潮，推动该国的GDP上升。

- catalyze: 促进
- culture: 文化
- rewarding: 奖励
- ingenuity: 独创性
- success: 成功
- new and international wave: 新的国际浪潮
- economic growth: 经济增长
- boosting: 推动
- GDP: 国内生产总值
- country: 国家
- go higher: 上升

3. During the 1960s, feminism resurfaced dramatically. Catalyzed by the publication of Betty Friedan's The Feminine Mystique, many women began to look critically at the sources of their "problems with no names," and the family was seen as a major culprit. (Strong, DeVault, & Cohen, 2010)

> 在20世纪60年代，女权主义戏剧性地重新出现。在贝蒂·弗里丹的《女性的奥秘》出版的推动下，许多妇女开始批判性地审视她们"没有名字的问题"的根源，家庭被视为主要罪魁祸首。

- catalyzed by: 在...的推动下
- during the 1960s: 在20世纪60年代
- feminism: 女权主义
- resurfaced dramatically: 戏剧性地重新出现
- publication: 出版
- Betty Friedan's The Feminine Mystique: 贝蒂·弗里丹的《女性的奥秘》
- many women: 许多妇女
- began to: 开始
- look critically at: 批判性地审视
- sources: 根源
- problems with no names: 没有名字的问题
- family: 家庭
- seen as: 被视为
- major culprit: 主要罪魁祸首

> 那种自我审视是你需要的，以便促进个人改变和改进。

- catalyze: 促进
- that kind of: 那种
- self-examination: 自我审视
- what you need: 你需要的
- in order to: 以便
- personal change: 个人改变
- improvement: 改进
