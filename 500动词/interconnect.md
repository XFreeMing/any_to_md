1. The period sociologists call modernity has witnessed a much more rapid globalization of social life, connecting large-scale societies together in a whole variety of ways, from long-range economic exchanges and international political agreements to global tourism, electronic communications technology and more fluid migration patterns. In all these ways, people across the world have interconnected more and become interdependent than in previous times. (Giddens, 2009)

> 社会学家称为现代性的时期见证了社会生活的更快速全球化，以各种方式将大规模社会连接在一起，从长期经济交流和国际政治协议到全球旅游、电子通信技术和更流动的移民模式。在所有这些方式中，世界各地的人们比以往更加相互连接并变得相互依存。

- interconnected: 相互连接
- period: 时期
- sociologists: 社会学家
- call modernity: 称为现代性
- witnessed: 见证
- much more rapid: 更快速
- globalization: 全球化
- social life: 社会生活
- connecting: 连接
- large-scale societies: 大规模社会
- whole variety of ways: 各种方式
- long-range economic exchanges: 长期经济交流
- international political agreements: 国际政治协议
- global tourism: 全球旅游
- electronic communications technology: 电子通信技术
- fluid migration patterns: 流动的移民模式
- all these ways: 所有这些方式
- people across the world: 世界各地的人们
- become interdependent: 变得相互依存
- previous times: 以往

- policy areas: 政策领域
