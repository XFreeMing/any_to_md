1. In recent German experiments in neural consciousness, patients wearing electrodes on their scalps modulate electrical signals to choose letters from a video screen—thus communicating with nothing but their own brains. (Fiero, 2011b)

> 在最近的德国神经意识实验中，在头皮上佩戴电极的患者调节电信号从视频屏幕选择字母——因此只用他们自己的大脑进行交流。

- modulate: 调节
- recent German experiments: 最近的德国实验
- neural consciousness: 神经意识
- patients: 患者
- wearing electrodes: 佩戴电极
- on their scalps: 在头皮上
- electrical signals: 电信号
- choose letters: 选择字母
- video screen: 视频屏幕
- communicating: 交流
- nothing but: 只用
- their own brains: 自己的大脑

2. Where is the line to be drawn between animals that evince morality in this limited sense and those that do not? Bekoff and Pierce suggest that the line is shifting as more empirical evidence becomes available and as our philosophical understanding of what it means to be moral is modulated by reflection on the scientific data. (Beauchamp & Frey, 2014)

> 在在这个有限意义上表现出道德感的动物和那些没有表现出道德感的动物之间，界限应该划在哪里？贝科夫和皮尔斯认为，随着更多经验证据的出现，以及我们对道德意味着什么的哲学理解通过对科学数据的反思而得到调节，这条界限正在变化。

- modulated: 调节
- line to be drawn: 应该划的界限
- between: 在...之间
- animals: 动物
- evince morality: 表现出道德感
- limited sense: 有限意义
- those that do not: 那些没有的
- Bekoff and Pierce: 贝科夫和皮尔斯
- suggest that: 认为
- line is shifting: 界限正在变化
- more empirical evidence: 更多经验证据
- becomes available: 出现
- philosophical understanding: 哲学理解
- what it means to be moral: 道德意味着什么
- reflection on: 对...的反思
- scientific data: 科学数据

4. Sympathy is separated into two categories of response, the attempt made by an observer to understand and to some extent reconstruct the suffering (or joy) of another subject, and that of the sufferer to modulate his feelings to make it possible for the observer to share them. (Berry, Paganelli, & Smith, 2016)

> 同情被分为两类反应，观察者试图理解并在某种程度上重建另一个主体的痛苦（或快乐），以及受苦者调节他的感受以使观察者能够分享它们。

- modulate: 调节
- sympathy: 同情
- separated into: 被分为
- two categories: 两类
- response: 反应
- attempt: 试图
- observer: 观察者
- understand: 理解
- to some extent: 在某种程度上
- reconstruct: 重建
- suffering: 痛苦
- joy: 快乐
- another subject: 另一个主体
- sufferer: 受苦者
- feelings: 感受
- make it possible: 使...成为可能
- share them: 分享它们
