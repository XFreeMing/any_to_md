1. Neither knowledge nor human foresight availed against it, though the city was cleansed of much filth by chosen officers in charge and sick persons were forbidden to enter it, while advice was broadcast for the preservation of health. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 知识和人类的远见都无法对抗它，尽管城市被选定的负责官员清理了大量污秽，病人被禁止进入，同时为了保持健康而广播了建议。

- availed: 起作用
- neither...nor: 既不...也不
- knowledge: 知识
- human foresight: 人类的远见
- against it: 对抗它
- though: 尽管
- city: 城市
- cleansed: 清理
- much filth: 大量污秽
- chosen officers: 选定的官员
- in charge: 负责的
- sick persons: 病人
- forbidden to enter: 被禁止进入
- advice: 建议
- broadcast: 广播
- preservation of health: 保持健康

2. Kant's injunction forbids using another person as a "mere" means, not using another person as a means at all. Human beings use each other as means, in the sense that we avail ourselves of one another's services, all the time. (Beauchamp & Frey, 2014)

> 康德的禁令禁止将另一个人仅仅作为手段使用，而不是完全禁止将另一个人作为手段使用。人类相互作为手段使用，在这个意义上，我们一直在利用彼此的服务。

- avail ourselves of: 利用
- Kant's injunction: 康德的禁令
- forbids: 禁止
- using another person: 将另一个人
- mere means: 仅仅作为手段
- not...at all: 完全不
- human beings: 人类
- use each other: 相互使用
- as means: 作为手段
- in the sense that: 在这个意义上
- one another's services: 彼此的服务
- all the time: 一直

3. Guests should feel at liberty to avail themselves of your facilities. (Oxford English Dictionary, 1989)

> 客人应该可以自由使用您的设施。

- avail themselves of: 使用
- guests: 客人
- should feel at liberty: 应该可以自由
- facilities: 设施
