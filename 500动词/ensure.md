1. What held the system together was personal loyalty to the king, who was strong enough to ensure loyalty by force when necessary. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 将系统维系在一起的是对国王的个人忠诚，国王足够强大，在必要时可以通过武力确保忠诚。

- ensure: 确保
- held the system together: 将系统维系在一起
- personal loyalty: 个人忠诚
- king: 国王
- strong enough: 足够强大
- by force: 通过武力
- when necessary: 在必要时

2. Montesquieu, a proponent of constitutional monarchy, advanced the idea of a separation of powers among the executive, legislative, and judicial agencies of government, advising that each monitor the activities of the others in order to ensure a balanced system of government. (Fiero, 2011)

> 孟德斯鸠，立宪君主制的支持者，提出了在政府的行政、立法和司法机构之间分权的想法，建议每个机构监督其他机构的活动，以确保平衡的政府系统。

- ensure: 确保
- Montesquieu: 孟德斯鸠
- proponent: 支持者
- constitutional monarchy: 立宪君主制
- advanced: 提出
- idea: 想法
- separation of powers: 分权
- executive: 行政的
- legislative: 立法的
- judicial agencies: 司法机构
- government: 政府
- advising that: 建议
- monitor: 监督
- activities: 活动
- balanced system: 平衡的系统

3. The emphasis on the reproductive features has suggested to many that she [the sculpture of Woman of Willendorf] may have been a fertility object; the intention may have been to ensure a successful birth outcome rather than an increase in the number of pregnancies. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 对生殖特征的强调使许多人认为她[维伦多夫的维纳斯雕塑]可能是一个生育对象；意图可能是确保成功的出生结果，而不是增加怀孕次数。

- ensure: 确保
- emphasis: 强调
- reproductive features: 生殖特征
- suggested to many: 使许多人认为
- sculpture of Woman of Willendorf: 维伦多夫的维纳斯雕塑
- fertility object: 生育对象
- intention: 意图
- successful birth outcome: 成功的出生结果
- rather than: 而不是
- increase: 增加
- number of pregnancies: 怀孕次数

4. We need only think of the murder rate in any large city, and the precautions ordinary citizens feel they have to take in order to ensure their personal safety.

> 我们只需要想想任何大城市的谋杀率，以及普通公民觉得他们必须采取的预防措施，以确保他们的个人安全。

- ensure: 确保
- need only think of: 只需要想想
- murder rate: 谋杀率
- any large city: 任何大城市
- precautions: 预防措施
- ordinary citizens: 普通公民
- feel they have to take: 觉得他们必须采取
- personal safety: 个人安全
