1. As popular culture and its economic support system became increasingly intertwined, industries of leisure emerged. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 随着流行文化及其经济支持系统变得越来越交织，休闲产业出现了。

- intertwined: 交织
- popular culture: 流行文化
- economic support system: 经济支持系统
- became increasingly: 变得越来越
- industries of leisure: 休闲产业
- emerged: 出现

2. By the 1980s and '90s things were changing, as knowledge of global environmental problems emerged and it became much clearer that the fate of the "natural" and "social" worlds was inevitably intertwined. (Giddens, 2009)

> 到20世纪80年代和90年代，事情正在发生变化，随着全球环境问题知识的出现，越来越清楚的是，"自然"和"社会"世界的命运不可避免地交织在一起。

- intertwined: 交织
- by the 1980s and '90s: 到20世纪80年代和90年代
- things were changing: 事情正在发生变化
- knowledge: 知识
- global environmental problems: 全球环境问题
- emerged: 出现
- became much clearer: 越来越清楚
- fate: 命运
- natural: 自然的
- social worlds: 社会世界
- inevitably: 不可避免地

- intertwined: 交织
- Elias: 埃利亚斯
- facial communication: 面部交流
- emotions: 情感
- demonstrates that: 表明
- human beings: 人类
- natural: 自然的
