1. The purpose of these paintings is hard to determine. They may record and perpetuate activities the deceased once enjoyed, or depict rituals observed at the funeral (the games, in fact, are probably the forerunners of Roman gladiatorial contests). (Davies et al., 2015)

2. Why is divorce becoming more common? Several factors are involved, to do with wider social changes. Except for a very small proportion of wealthy people, marriage today no longer has much connection with the desire to perpetuate property and status from generation to generation. (Giddens, 2009)

> 为什么离婚变得越来越普遍？涉及几个因素，与更广泛的社会变化有关。除了很小比例的富人，今天的婚姻不再与代代相传财产和地位的愿望有很大联系。

- becoming more common: 变得越来越普遍
- several factors: 几个因素

- very small proportion: 很小比例

- marriage today: 今天的婚姻
- no longer has: 不再有
- much connection with: 与...有很大联系

4. Many people who give a presentation report not just feeling anxious, but anxious that others will notice their anxiety. And if they feel their knees shaking and hands trembling, their worry that others are noticing may compound and perpetuate their anxiety. (Myers, 2012)

- perpetuate: 延续
- many people: 许多人
- give a presentation: 做演讲
- report: 报告
- not just feeling anxious: 不仅感到焦虑

- others will notice: 别人会注意到

- if they feel: 如果他们感到
- knees shaking: 膝盖颤抖
- hands trembling: 手发抖
- worry: 担忧
- others are noticing: 别人正在注意

- their anxiety: 他们的焦虑
