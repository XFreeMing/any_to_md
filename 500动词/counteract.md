1. Like many environmental issues, acid rain is difficult to counteract because it is transnational in its origins and consequences. (Giddens, 2009)

> 像许多环境问题一样，酸雨很难抵消，因为它在起源和后果上都是跨国的。

- counteract: 抵消
- like many environmental issues: 像许多环境问题一样
- acid rain: 酸雨
- difficult to: 很难
- because: 因为
- transnational: 跨国的
- origins: 起源
- consequences: 后果

- counteract: 抵消
- mid-1950s: 20世纪50年代中期
- administration: 政府
- President Dwight D. Eisenhower: 德怀特·D·艾森豪威尔总统
- adopted: 采取
- policy of massive retaliation: 大规模报复政策
- advocated: 主张
- full use: 充分利用
- American nuclear bombs: 美国核弹
- even: 甚至
- Soviet ground attack: 苏联地面攻击
- in Europe: 在欧洲

3. Despite this crowding, a golden light unites the whole image, illuminating the bodies of the animals, the faces of the humans, and parts of the landscape. - crowding: 拥挤
- golden light: 金色的光线
- unites: 统一
- whole image: 整个图像

- strong flattening effect: 强烈扁平化效果
- gold: 黄金
