1. As the church stepped up efforts to curb socially destructive fighting in the twelfth century, tournaments began to be organized. Initially, tournaments consisted of the melee, in which warriors on horseback fought with blunted weapons in free-for-all combat. By late in the century, the melee was preceded by the joust, individual combat between two knights. Gradually, the joust became the main part of the tournament. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 随着教会在12世纪加强努力遏制对社会破坏性的战斗，锦标赛开始组织起来。最初，锦标赛由混战组成，其中骑马的战士在自由混战中使用钝器战斗。到该世纪后期，混战之前是马上长矛比武，两个骑士之间的个人战斗。逐渐地，马上长矛比武成为锦标赛的主要部分。

- warriors on horseback: 骑马的战士

- free-for-all combat: 自由混战

- joust: 马上长矛比武
- individual combat: 个人战斗
- two knights: 两个骑士
- gradually: 逐渐地
- main part: 主要部分

2. For Marx political emancipation—liberalism—is a great advance over the hierarchical, discriminatory state that preceded it. But it is a long way from his ideal, a communist society in which emancipation extends all the way down to civil society. (Wolff, 2006)

> 对马克思来说，政治解放——自由主义——是对先于它的等级制、歧视性国家的巨大进步。但它离他的理想还很远，一个共产主义社会，其中解放一直延伸到公民社会。

4. Economist Hyman Minksy believed that severe recessions are often preceded by asset-price bubbles——periods during which euphoria and debt-fueled speculation cause the price of one or more financial assets to irrationally skyrocket before collapsing down to more realistic levels.

> 经济学家海曼·明斯基认为，严重的经济衰退往往先于资产价格泡沫——在这些时期，兴奋和债务推动的投机导致一个或多个金融资产的价格非理性地飙升，然后崩溃到更现实的水平。
