1. Habermas argues that it is still possible to envisage a political community beyond individual nationstates in which issues can be operily debated and where public opinion will influence governments. (Giddens, 2009)

> 哈贝马斯认为，仍然可以设想一个超越个别民族国家的政治社区，在这个社区中，问题可以公开辩论，公众舆论将影响政府。

- envisage: 设想
- Habermas: 哈贝马斯
- argues that: 认为
- still possible: 仍然可能
- political community: 政治社区
- beyond: 超越
- individual nationstates: 个别民族国家
- issues: 问题
- openly debated: 公开辩论
- public opinion: 公众舆论
- influence: 影响
- governments: 政府

2. At present, there is no sizeable electorate or mass movement able to envisage forms of economic and political organization beyond that of the market and liberal democlacy. (Giddens, 2009)

> 目前，没有相当大的选民或群众运动能够设想超越市场和自由民主的经济和政治组织形式。

- envisage: 设想
- at present: 目前
- sizeable electorate: 相当大的选民
- mass movement: 群众运动
- able to: 能够
- forms: 形式
- economic and political organization: 经济和政治组织
- beyond: 超越
- market: 市场
- liberal democracy: 自由民主

3. In an infamous phrase from a 1962 speech in Birmingham, Enoch Powell, a Conservative front-bench minister at the time, envisaged an extraordinary growth in the non-white population in Britain: 'Like the Romans, I seem to see "the River Tiber flowing with much blood".' (Giddens, 2009)

> 在1962年伯明翰演讲的一个臭名昭著的短语中，当时的保守党前座议员伊诺克·鲍威尔设想了英国非白人群体的非凡增长："就像罗马人一样，我似乎看到'台伯河血流成河'。"

- envisaged: 设想
- infamous phrase: 臭名昭著的短语
- 1962 speech: 1962年演讲
- Birmingham: 伯明翰
- Enoch Powell: 伊诺克·鲍威尔
- Conservative front-bench minister: 保守党前座议员
- at the time: 当时
- extraordinary growth: 非凡增长
- non-white population: 非白人群体的
- Britain: 英国
- Like the Romans: 就像罗马人一样
- River Tiber flowing with much blood: 台伯河血流成河

4. Although utopias of various kinds can be envisaged, most are characterized by the abolition of want, the absence of conflict, and the avoidance of violence and oppression. (Heywood, 2013)

> 虽然可以设想各种类型的乌托邦，但大多数都以消除匮乏、没有冲突以及避免暴力和压迫为特征。

- envisaged: 被设想
- although: 虽然
- utopias: 乌托邦
- various kinds: 各种类型
- most: 大多数
- characterized by: 以...为特征
- abolition of want: 消除匮乏
- absence of conflict: 没有冲突
- avoidance: 避免
- violence: 暴力
- oppression: 压迫
