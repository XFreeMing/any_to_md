1. Temples and pyramids suggest that an ancient society had an authority structure capable of marshaling the labor needed to build such monuments.

> 寺庙和金字塔表明，一个古代社会有一个能够动员建造这些纪念碑所需劳动力的权威结构。

- suggest: 表明
- temples: 寺庙
- pyramids: 金字塔
- ancient society: 古代社会
- authority structure: 权威结构
- capable of: 能够
- marshaling: 动员
- labor: 劳动力
- needed to build: 建造所需的
- monuments: 纪念碑

2. Ancient Egyptians had a very positive attitude toward daily life on earth and followed the advice of the wisdom literature, which suggested that people marry young and establish a home and family. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 古埃及人对地球上的日常生活有非常积极的态度，并遵循智慧文学的建议，该建议建议人们早婚并建立家庭。

- suggested: 建议
- ancient Egyptians: 古埃及人
- very positive attitude: 非常积极的态度
- daily life: 日常生活
- on earth: 在地球上
- followed: 遵循
- advice: 建议
- wisdom literature: 智慧文学
- marry young: 早婚
- establish: 建立
- home and family: 家庭
