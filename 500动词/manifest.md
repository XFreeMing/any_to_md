1. Influenced by Islamic verse, troubadour poems generally manifest a positive, even joyous, response to physical nature and the world of the senses. (Fiero, 2011)

> 受伊斯兰诗歌的影响，游吟诗人的诗歌通常表现出对物理自然和感官世界的积极，甚至欢乐的回应。

- manifest: 表现
- influenced by: 受...影响
- Islamic verse: 伊斯兰诗歌
- troubadour poems: 游吟诗人的诗歌
- generally: 通常
- positive: 积极的
- even joyous: 甚至欢乐的
- response: 回应
- physical nature: 物理自然
- world of the senses: 感官世界

2. For, although one's status was known only by God, Christians might manifest that they were among the "elect" by a show of moral rectitude. (Fiero, 2011)

> 因为，虽然一个人的地位只有上帝知道，但基督徒可能通过展示道德正直来表明他们属于"选民"。

- manifest: 表明
- for: 因为
- although: 虽然
- one's status: 一个人的地位
- known only by God: 只有上帝知道
- Christians: 基督徒
- might: 可能
- elect: 选民
- show of moral rectitude: 展示道德正直

3. Ninhursaga began as a goddess associated with soil, mountains, and vegetation. Eventually, however, she was worshiped as a mother goddess, a ''mother of all children,'' who manifested her power by giving birth to kings and conferring the royal insignia on them. (Perry, 2010)

> 宁胡尔萨加最初是一个与土壤、山脉和植被相关的女神。然而，最终，她被崇拜为母亲女神，"所有孩子的母亲"，她通过生下国王并授予他们皇家徽章来展现她的力量。

- manifested: 展现
- Ninhursaga: 宁胡尔萨加
- began as: 最初是
- goddess: 女神
- associated with: 与...相关
- soil: 土壤
- mountains: 山脉
- vegetation: 植被
- eventually: 最终
- however: 然而
- worshiped as: 被崇拜为
- mother goddess: 母亲女神
- mother of all children: 所有孩子的母亲
- power: 力量
- giving birth to: 生下
- kings: 国王
- conferring: 授予
- royal insignia: 皇家徽章

4. It did not manifest itself as in the East, where if a man bled at the nose he had certain warning of inevitable death. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 它没有像在东方那样表现出来，在东方，如果一个人流鼻血，他就有必然死亡的某种警告。

- manifest itself: 表现出来
- did not: 没有
- as in the East: 像在东方那样
- where: 在那里
- if: 如果
- man: 人
- bled at the nose: 流鼻血
- certain warning: 某种警告
- inevitable death: 必然死亡
