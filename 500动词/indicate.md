1. Studies indicate that, apart from the odd showpiece project, providing incentives and expecting private enterprise to do the job is ineffective as a way of tackling the fundamental social problems generated by the central cities. (Giddens, 2009)

> 研究表明，除了偶尔的展示项目外，提供激励并期望私营企业完成这项工作作为解决中心城市产生的根本社会问题的方法是无效的。

- indicate: 表明
- studies: 研究
- apart from: 除了
- odd showpiece project: 偶尔的展示项目
- providing incentives: 提供激励
- expecting: 期望
- private enterprise: 私营企业
- do the job: 完成这项工作
- ineffective: 无效的
- way of tackling: 解决...的方法
- fundamental social problems: 根本社会问题
- generated by: 由...产生
- central cities: 中心城市

2. The often arbitrarily cut-off figures, the patterns of light splotches, and the blurriness of the images in this and other Degas works indicate the artist's interest in reproducing single moments. (Kleiner, 2015)

> 这幅和其他德加作品中经常任意截断的人物、光斑图案和图像的模糊性表明艺术家对再现单一时刻的兴趣。

- often arbitrarily cut-off figures: 经常任意截断的人物
- patterns: 图案
- light splotches: 光斑
- blurriness: 模糊性
- images: 图像
- Degas works: 德加作品
- artist's interest: 艺术家的兴趣
- reproducing: 再现
- single moments: 单一时刻

3. One thought is that consent is communicated via the ballot-box. In voting for the government we give it our consent. And it is not wholly implausible that even those who vote against the government nevertheless indicate their consent to the system as a whole through voting. (Heywood, 2013)

> 一种想法是，同意通过投票箱传达。在投票支持政府时，我们给予它我们的同意。甚至那些投票反对政府的人通过投票表明他们对整个系统的同意，这并非完全不可信。

- indicate: 表明
- one thought: 一种想法
- consent: 同意
- communicated via: 通过...传达
- ballot-box: 投票箱
- voting for: 投票支持
- government: 政府
- give it our consent: 给予它我们的同意
- not wholly implausible: 并非完全不可信
- even those who: 甚至那些
- vote against: 投票反对
- nevertheless: 然而
- system as a whole: 整个系统
- through voting: 通过投票

4. Correlations indicate a relationship, but that relationship is not necessarily one of cause and effect. Correlational research allows us to predict, but it cannot tell us whether changing one variable (such as social status) will cause changes in another (such as health). (Myers, 2012)

> 相关性表明一种关系，但这种关系不一定是因果关系。相关研究使我们能够预测，但它不能告诉我们改变一个变量（如社会地位）是否会导致另一个变量（如健康）的变化。

- indicate: 表明
- correlations: 相关性
- relationship: 关系
- not necessarily: 不一定
- cause and effect: 因果关系
- correlational research: 相关研究
- allows us to predict: 使我们能够预测
- cannot tell us: 不能告诉我们
- whether: 是否
- changing one variable: 改变一个变量
- social status: 社会地位
- cause changes: 导致变化
- health: 健康

5. This rather banal example of economic life shows the remarkable powers of markets. First, the price system is a way of signalling and transmitting information. The fact that the price of a good rises indicates that the good is in short supply; if the price falls then it is oversupplied.

> 这个相当平凡的经济生活例子显示了市场的显著力量。首先，价格系统是发出信号和传递信息的一种方式。商品价格上涨的事实表明商品供应短缺；如果价格下跌，则供应过剩。

- indicates: 表明
- rather banal example: 相当平凡的例子
- economic life: 经济生活
- shows: 显示
- remarkable powers: 显著力量
- markets: 市场
- first: 首先
- price system: 价格系统
- way of signalling: 发出信号的方式
- transmitting information: 传递信息
- fact: 事实
- price of a good rises: 商品价格上涨
- good is in short supply: 商品供应短缺
- price falls: 价格下跌
- oversupplied: 供应过剩
