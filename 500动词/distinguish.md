1. About 100 miles before it empties into the Mediterranean, the river splits into two major branches, forming the delta, a triangular-shaped territory called Lower Egypt to distinguish it from Upper Egypt, the land upstream to the south. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在它流入地中海之前约 100 英里处，这条河分成两个主要分支，形成三角洲，一个被称为下埃及的三角形领土，以区别于上埃及，即上游向南的土地。

- distinguish: 区分
- about 100 miles: 约 100 英里
- before: 在...之前
- empties into: 流入
- Mediterranean: 地中海
- river: 河流
- splits into: 分成
- two major branches: 两个主要分支
- forming: 形成
- delta: 三角洲
- triangular-shaped territory: 三角形领土
- Lower Egypt: 下埃及
- Upper Egypt: 上埃及
- land upstream: 上游土地
- to the south: 向南

2. In some provinces a difference in dress distinguishes the Jews or Saracens [Muslims] from the Christians, but in certain others such a confusion has grown up that they cannot be distinguished by any difference. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 在一些省份，服装的差异将犹太人或撒拉逊人[穆斯林]与基督徒区分开来，但在某些其他省份，这种混乱已经发展到他们无法通过任何差异来区分。

- distinguishes: 区分
- in some provinces: 在一些省份
- difference in dress: 服装的差异
- Jews: 犹太人
- Saracens: 撒拉逊人
- Muslims: 穆斯林
- Christians: 基督徒
- but: 但
- certain others: 某些其他省份
- confusion: 混乱
- grown up: 发展
- cannot be distinguished: 无法区分
- by any difference: 通过任何差异

3. Montesquieu distinguished three basic kinds of governments: the republic, suitable for small states and based on citizen involvement; monarchy, appropriate for midsized states and grounded in the ruling class's adherence to law; and despotism, apt for large empires and dependent on fear to inspire obedience. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 孟德斯鸠区分了三种基本的政府类型：共和国，适合小国，基于公民参与；君主制，适合中等国家，基于统治阶级对法律的遵守；专制主义，适合大帝国，依赖恐惧来激发服从。

- distinguished: 区分
- Montesquieu: 孟德斯鸠
- three basic kinds: 三种基本类型
- governments: 政府
- republic: 共和国
- suitable for: 适合
- small states: 小国
- based on: 基于
- citizen involvement: 公民参与
- monarchy: 君主制
- appropriate for: 适合
- midsized states: 中等国家
- grounded in: 基于
- ruling class's adherence: 统治阶级的遵守
- law: 法律
- despotism: 专制主义
- apt for: 适合
- large empires: 大帝国
- dependent on: 依赖
- fear: 恐惧
- inspire obedience: 激发服从

4. As one knight explained, "A knight cannot distinguish himself in [war] if he has not trained for it in tourneys" (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 正如一位骑士所解释的，"如果骑士没有在锦标赛中训练过，他就无法在[战争中]表现突出"。

- distinguish: 表现突出

- knight: 骑士
- cannot: 无法
- in war: 在战争中

- has not trained for it: 没有为它训练过
- in tourneys: 在锦标赛中
