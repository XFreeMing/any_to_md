1. I have written this brief history of Western civilization to assist a new generation of students in learning more about the past that has shaped them and the world in which they live. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 我写了这部西方文明的简史，以帮助新一代学生更多地了解塑造了他们和他们所生活的世界的过去。

- shaped: 塑造
- written: 写
- brief history: 简史
- Western civilization: 西方文明
- assist: 帮助
- new generation of students: 新一代学生
- learning more about: 更多地了解
- past: 过去
- world in which they live: 他们所生活的世界

2. Fernand Léger's art (1881–1955) is usually classed with that of the Cubists, but it was the French artist's wartime experience that actually shaped his long and productive career. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 费尔南德·莱热的艺术（1881-1955）通常与立体派艺术归为一类，但实际上是这位法国艺术家的战时经历塑造了他漫长而多产的职业生涯。

- shaped: 塑造
- Fernand Léger's art: 费尔南德·莱热的艺术
- usually classed with: 通常与...归为一类
- Cubists: 立体派
- French artist: 法国艺术家
- wartime experience: 战时经历
- actually: 实际上
- long and productive career: 漫长而多产的职业生涯

3. Without knowledge of history, men and women cannot fully know themselves, for all human beings have been shaped by institutions and values inherited from the past. (Perry, 2010)

> 没有历史知识，男人和女人无法完全了解自己，因为所有人类都被从过去继承的制度和价值观所塑造。

- shaped: 塑造
- without knowledge of history: 没有历史知识
- men and women: 男人和女人
- cannot fully know: 无法完全了解
- themselves: 自己
- for: 因为
- all human beings: 所有人类
- institutions: 制度
- values: 价值观
- inherited from the past: 从过去继承的

4. As the world adjusted to the transformation from Cold War to post-Cold War sensibilities, other changes shaped the Western outlook. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 随着世界适应从冷战到后冷战敏感性的转变，其他变化塑造了西方的观点。

- shaped: 塑造
- as: 随着
- world: 世界
- adjusted to: 适应
- transformation: 转变
- from Cold War: 从冷战
- post-Cold War sensibilities: 后冷战敏感性
- other changes: 其他变化
- Western outlook: 西方的观点
