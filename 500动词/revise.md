1. Discoveries in Palestine and other neighboring regions within the last century have forced scholars to revise their estimation of the quantity and quality of Jewish art produced in antiquity. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 过去一个世纪在巴勒斯坦和其他邻近地区的发现迫使学者们修正他们对古代生产的犹太艺术数量和质量的估计。

- revise: 修正
- discoveries: 发现
- Palestine: 巴勒斯坦
- neighboring regions: 邻近地区
- last century: 过去一个世纪
- forced scholars to: 迫使学者们
- estimation: 估计
- quantity and quality: 数量和质量
- Jewish art: 犹太艺术
- produced in antiquity: 古代生产的

2. In the summer of 1787, fifty-five delegates attended a convention in Philadelphia to revise the Articles of Confederation. The convention's delegates —— wealthy, politically experienced, and well educated —— rejected revision and decided to devise a new constitution. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 1787年夏天，55名代表在费城参加了一次会议，以修订《邦联条例》。会议的代表——富有、政治经验丰富、受过良好教育——拒绝了修订，决定制定一部新宪法。

- revise: 修订
- summer of 1787: 1787年夏天
- fifty-five delegates: 55名代表
- attended: 参加
- convention: 会议
- Philadelphia: 费城
- Articles of Confederation: 《邦联条例》
- wealthy: 富有的
- politically experienced: 政治经验丰富的
- well educated: 受过良好教育的
- rejected revision: 拒绝了修订
- decided to devise: 决定制定
- new constitution: 新宪法

3. In 1870, the Church of England established a Revising Committee to revise and update the biblical texts; but, as Stanton pointed out, the committee contained not a single woman. (Giddens, 2009)

> 1870年，英国国教建立了一个修订委员会来修订和更新圣经文本；但是，正如斯坦顿指出的，该委员会没有一名女性。

- revise: 修订
- 1870: 1870年
- Church of England: 英国国教
- established: 建立
- Revising Committee: 修订委员会
- update: 更新
- biblical texts: 圣经文本
- but: 但是
- Stanton: 斯坦顿
- pointed out: 指出
- committee: 委员会
- contained not a single woman: 没有一名女性

4. To the extent that other forms of 'intelligence' such as emotional intelligence or interpersonal intelligence are brought into our understanding of people's abilities, then we will have to revise our ideas to include the diverse range of factors that contribute towards success in life. (Giddens, 2009)

> 在将其他形式的"智力"（如情商或人际智力）纳入我们对人们能力的理解的程度内，我们将不得不修正我们的想法，以包括有助于生活成功的各种因素。

- revise: 修正
- to the extent that: 在...程度内
- other forms: 其他形式
- intelligence: 智力
- emotional intelligence: 情商
- interpersonal intelligence: 人际智力
- brought into: 纳入
- understanding: 理解
- people's abilities: 人们的能力
- then: 那么
- ideas: 想法
- include: 包括
- diverse range of factors: 各种因素
- contribute towards: 有助于
- success in life: 生活成功
