1. Oil companies have been accused of acquiescing in the pollution of the ocean. (Giddens, 2009)

> 石油公司被指控默许海洋污染。

- acquiescing in: 默许
- oil companies: 石油公司
- accused of: 被指控
- pollution of the ocean: 海洋污染

2. Informal sanctions emphasize cultural control and are diffuse in nature, involving spontaneous expressions of approval or disapproval by members of the group or community. They are, nonetheless, very effective in enforcing a large number of seemingly unimportant customs. Because most people want to be accepted, they are willing to acquiesce to the rules that govern dress, eating, and conversation, even in the absence of actual laws. (Haviland, Prints, Walrath, & McBride, 2010)

> 非正式制裁强调文化控制，本质上是分散的，涉及群体或社区成员自发的赞同或不赞同表达。然而，它们在执行大量看似不重要的习俗方面非常有效。因为大多数人希望被接受，他们愿意默许管理着装、饮食和谈话的规则，即使在没有实际法律的情况下。

- acquiesce to: 默许
- informal sanctions: 非正式制裁
- emphasize: 强调
- cultural control: 文化控制
- diffuse in nature: 本质上是分散的
- spontaneous expressions: 自发的表达
- approval or disapproval: 赞同或不赞同
- members: 成员
- group or community: 群体或社区
- nonetheless: 然而
- very effective: 非常有效
- enforcing: 执行
- seemingly unimportant customs: 看似不重要的习俗
- most people: 大多数人
- want to be accepted: 希望被接受
- willing to: 愿意
- rules: 规则
- govern: 管理
- dress: 着装
- eating: 饮食
- conversation: 谈话
- even in the absence of: 即使在没有...的情况下
- actual laws: 实际法律

3. The cardinal acquiesces, and Portuguese troops are sent to take over the missions. (Copp, 2005)

> 红衣主教默许了，葡萄牙军队被派去接管传教团。

- acquiesces: 默许
- cardinal: 红衣主教
- Portuguese troops: 葡萄牙军队
- sent to: 被派去
- take over: 接管
- missions: 传教团
