1. The prime minister pushed through a law in 1911 that restricted the ability of the House of Lords to impede legislation enacted by the House of Commons. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 首相在1911年推动通过了一项法律，限制了上议院阻碍下议院制定的立法的能力。

- impede: 阻碍
- prime minister: 首相
- pushed through: 推动通过
- law: 法律
- in 1911: 在1911年
- restricted: 限制
- ability: 能力
- House of Lords: 上议院
- legislation: 立法
- enacted by: 由...制定的
- House of Commons: 下议院

2. Even in households where men contribute, an egalitarian division of household labour between spouses is greatly impeded when the couple have children - children require constant attention, and their care schedules are often unpredictable. (Giddens, 2009)

> 即使在男性贡献的家庭中，当夫妇有孩子时，配偶之间家务劳动的平等分配也受到很大阻碍——孩子需要持续关注，他们的照顾时间表往往不可预测。

- impeded: 阻碍
- even in: 即使在
- households: 家庭
- men contribute: 男性贡献
- egalitarian division: 平等分配
- household labour: 家务劳动
- between spouses: 配偶之间
- greatly: 很大地
- couple have children: 夫妇有孩子
- children: 孩子
- require: 需要
- constant attention: 持续关注
- care schedules: 照顾时间表
- often unpredictable: 往往不可预测

3. Inequality seems to impede 'fair' outcomes (based on ability and effort) and in order to produce a genuine meritocracy, it will also be necessary to reduce inequalities. (Giddens, 2009)

> 不平等似乎阻碍了"公平"的结果（基于能力和努力），为了产生真正的精英统治，也有必要减少不平等。

- impede: 阻碍
- inequality: 不平等
- seems to: 似乎
- fair outcomes: 公平的结果
- based on: 基于
- ability: 能力
- effort: 努力
- in order to: 为了
- produce: 产生
- genuine meritocracy: 真正的精英统治
- necessary to: 有必要
- reduce: 减少
- inequalities: 不平等

4. Market-oriented theories also fail to take into account the various economic ties between poor countries and wealthy ones - ties that can impede economic growth under some conditions and enhance it under others. (Giddens, 2009)

> 以市场为导向的理论也未能考虑到穷国和富国之间的各种经济联系——这些联系在某些条件下可能阻碍经济增长，在其他条件下可能促进经济增长。

- impede: 阻碍
- market-oriented theories: 以市场为导向的理论
- fail to take into account: 未能考虑到
- various economic ties: 各种经济联系
- between: 在...之间
- poor countries: 穷国
- wealthy ones: 富国
- ties: 联系
- can: 可能
- economic growth: 经济增长
- under some conditions: 在某些条件下
- enhance: 促进
- under others: 在其他条件下
