3. Pictographs, small symbols based on abstract representations of concepts, encode further information: In the falcon and papyrus group, the falcon represents Horus, whom the Egyptians believed the king incarnated, while the human-headed papyrus stand represents Lower Egypt, where papyrus grew abundantly. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 象形文字，基于概念抽象表示的小符号，编码了进一步的信息：在猎鹰和纸莎草组中，猎鹰代表荷鲁斯，埃及人相信国王化身于他，而人头的纸莎草架代表下埃及，那里纸莎草大量生长。
