2. Gentileschi's favorite subjects were biblical heroines— she painted the Judith story some seven times. The violence she brought to these depictions may be said to reflect a personal sense of victimization: at the age of eighteen, she was raped by her drawing teacher and (during the sensational trial of her assailant) subjected to torture as a test of the truth of her testimony. (Fiero, 2011)

> 真蒂莱斯基最喜欢的主题是圣经女英雄——她画了朱迪思的故事大约七次。她给这些描绘带来的暴力可以说反映了一种个人受害感：在18岁时，她被她的绘画老师强奸，并在（在她攻击者的轰动审判期间）遭受酷刑作为对她证词真实性的测试。

- violence: 暴力
- brought to: 给...带来
- depictions: 描绘
- reflect: 反映
- personal sense: 个人感觉
- victimization: 受害
- age of eighteen: 18岁
- raped by: 被...强奸

- sensational trial: 轰动审判
