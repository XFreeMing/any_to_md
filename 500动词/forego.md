1. It is proper to state that I forego any advantage which could be derived to my argument from the idea of abstract right, as a thing independent of utility' (On Liberty, 136). (Wolff, 2006)

> 应该说明，我放弃任何可以从抽象权利的概念中为我的论点带来的优势，作为独立于效用的东西（《论自由》，136）。

- forego: 放弃
- proper to state: 应该说明
- advantage: 优势
- derived to: 为...带来
- argument: 论点
- idea of abstract right: 抽象权利的概念
- independent of utility: 独立于效用

2. A change in the perceived riskiness of banks. If consumers and firms perceive that holding a banking deposit is a more risky proposition, for example if they think that banks could fail and they might lose their deposits, then consumers and firms may forego dealing with the banking system and simply conduct transactions using currency. (Williamson, 2013)

> 银行感知风险的变化。如果消费者和公司认为持有银行存款是一个更冒险的提议，例如，如果他们认为银行可能失败，他们可能会失去存款，那么消费者和公司可能会放弃与银行系统打交道，而只是使用货币进行交易。

- forego: 放弃
- change: 变化
- perceived riskiness: 感知风险
- banks: 银行
- consumers and firms: 消费者和公司
- perceive that: 认为
- holding a banking deposit: 持有银行存款
- risky proposition: 冒险的提议
- for example: 例如
- think that: 认为
- banks could fail: 银行可能失败
- might lose: 可能会失去
- deposits: 存款
- dealing with: 与...打交道
- banking system: 银行系统
- simply: 只是
- conduct transactions: 进行交易
- using currency: 使用货币

- somber precision: 严肃的精确性

4. Man-the-sovereign will provide woman-the-liege with material protection and will undertake the moral justification of her existence; thus she can evade at once both economic risk and the metaphysical risk of a liberty in which ends and aims must be contrived without assistance. Indeed, along with the ethical urge of  each individual to affirm his subjective existence, there is also the temptation to forego liberty and become a thing. (Fiero, 2011)

> 作为主权者的男人将为作为臣属的女人提供物质保护，并将承担她存在的道德正当性；因此，她可以同时逃避经济风险和自由的形而上学风险，在这种自由中，目的和目标必须在没有帮助的情况下设计。确实，除了每个人肯定其主观存在的伦理冲动外，还有放弃自由并成为物的诱惑。

- man-the-sovereign: 作为主权者的男人
- provide: 提供
- woman-the-liege: 作为臣属的女人
- material protection: 物质保护
- undertake: 承担
- moral justification: 道德正当性
- existence: 存在
- thus: 因此
- evade: 逃避
- at once: 同时
- economic risk: 经济风险
- metaphysical risk: 形而上学风险
- liberty: 自由
- ends and aims: 目的和目标
- contrived: 设计
- without assistance: 没有帮助
- indeed: 确实
- along with: 除了
- ethical urge: 伦理冲动
- each individual: 每个人
- affirm: 肯定
- subjective existence: 主观存在
- temptation: 诱惑
- become a thing: 成为物
