1. In India, for instance, religion is grounded in pantheism, the belief that an all-pervading divine spirit infuses all aspects of the universe. (Fiero, 2011)

> 例如，在印度，宗教基于泛神论，即相信一个遍及一切的神圣精神渗透到宇宙的所有方面。

- grounded in: 基于
- India: 印度
- for instance: 例如
- religion: 宗教
- pantheism: 泛神论
- belief: 信仰
- all-pervading divine spirit: 遍及一切的神圣精神
- infuses: 渗透
- all aspects: 所有方面
- universe: 宇宙

2. Marx's viewpoint was grounded in what he called the materialist conception of history. According to this view, it is not the ideas or values which human beings hold that are the main sources of social change; rather, social change is prompted primarily by economic influences. (Giddens, 2009)

> 马克思的观点基于他所谓的唯物主义历史观。根据这一观点，不是人类持有的思想或价值观是社会变革的主要来源；相反，社会变革主要是由经济影响推动的。

- grounded in: 基于
- Marx's viewpoint: 马克思的观点
- materialist conception of history: 唯物主义历史观
- according to: 根据
- this view: 这一观点
- ideas or values: 思想或价值观
- human beings hold: 人类持有的
- main sources: 主要来源
- social change: 社会变革
- rather: 相反
- prompted primarily by: 主要由...推动
- economic influences: 经济影响

- facts: 事实
- debates: 辩论
- relating to: 与...相关
- policy questions: 政策问题
- resource pricing: 资源定价

- Kant: 康德
- tries to: 试图
- draw out: 引出
- presuppositions: 前提
- common idea: 共同观念
