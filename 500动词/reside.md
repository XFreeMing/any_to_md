1. Rather, the idea is that political obligations are universal in the sense that they apply to all people who reside within the borders of the state. (Wolff, 2006)

> 相反，这个想法是，政治义务在适用于居住在国家边界内的所有人的意义上是普遍的。

- reside: 居住
- rather: 相反
- idea: 想法
- political obligations: 政治义务
- universal: 普遍的
- sense: 意义
- apply to: 适用于
- all people: 所有人
- within the borders: 在边界内
- state: 国家

2. The crowd wear masks, which reveal as opposed to hide the greed, corruption, and immorality that resides behind the false face of contemporary society. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 人群戴着面具，这些面具揭示而不是隐藏隐藏在当代社会虚假面孔背后的贪婪、腐败和不道德。

- resides: 存在于
- crowd: 人群
- wear masks: 戴着面具
- reveal: 揭示
- as opposed to: 而不是
- hide: 隐藏
- greed: 贪婪
- corruption: 腐败
- immorality: 不道德
- behind: 在...背后
- false face: 虚假面孔
- contemporary society: 当代社会

3. Along with the brushwork, color seems to reside uniformly on the surface of the image, reflecting Matisse's careful study of Cézanne's paintings. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 与笔触一起，颜色似乎均匀地存在于图像表面，反映了马蒂斯对塞尚绘画的仔细研究。

- reside: 存在于
- along with: 与...一起
- brushwork: 笔触
- color: 颜色
- seems to: 似乎
- uniformly: 均匀地
- surface of the image: 图像表面
- reflecting: 反映
- Matisse's careful study: 马蒂斯的仔细研究
- Cézanne's paintings: 塞尚的绘画

4. The state accepts the responsibility of protecting everyone who resides within its borders from illegitimate violence. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 国家接受保护居住在其边界内的每个人免受非法暴力的责任。

- resides: 居住
- state: 国家
- accepts: 接受
- responsibility: 责任
- protecting: 保护
- everyone: 每个人
- within its borders: 在其边界内
- illegitimate violence: 非法暴力
