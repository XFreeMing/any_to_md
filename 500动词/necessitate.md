1. By lowering the price of goods, fast transportation caused markets to grow, and this increased demand necessitated more factories and more machinery, thereby reinforcing the self-sustaining nature of the Industrial Revolution. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 通过降低商品价格，快速运输导致市场增长，这种增加的需求需要更多的工厂和更多的机械，从而加强了工业革命的自持性质。

- necessitated: 需要
- by lowering: 通过降低
- price of goods: 商品价格
- fast transportation: 快速运输
- caused: 导致
- markets to grow: 市场增长
- increased demand: 增加的需求
- more factories: 更多的工厂
- more machinery: 更多的机械
- thereby: 从而
- reinforcing: 加强
- self-sustaining nature: 自持性质
- Industrial Revolution: 工业革命

2. Environmental issues are hybrids of environment and society, which marks them out as different from many other sociological issues and social problems. This necessitates interdisciplinary understanding and, potentially, collaboration. All societies are now faced by increasingly global environmental problems and international cooperation is needed in order to find workable solutions. (Giddens, 2009)

> 环境问题是环境与社会的混合体，这使它们与许多其他社会学问题和社会问题不同。这需要跨学科的理解，并可能需要合作。所有社会现在都面临日益严重的全球环境问题，需要国际合作以找到可行的解决方案。

- necessitates: 需要
- environmental issues: 环境问题
- hybrids: 混合体
- environment and society: 环境与社会
- marks them out as: 使它们成为
- different from: 与...不同
- many other sociological issues: 许多其他社会学问题
- social problems: 社会问题
- interdisciplinary understanding: 跨学科的理解
- potentially: 可能
- collaboration: 合作
- all societies: 所有社会
- faced by: 面临
- increasingly global environmental problems: 日益严重的全球环境问题
- international cooperation: 国际合作
- needed in order to: 需要以便
- find: 找到
- workable solutions: 可行的解决方案

3. If temperatures increase by the expected 28 to 48 Celsius this century, the resulting changes in water availability, agriculture, disaster risk, and sea level will necessitate massive resettlement. (Myers, 2012)

> 如果本世纪温度按预期的 2.8 到 4.8 摄氏度增加，由此产生的水资源可用性、农业、灾害风险和海平面的变化将需要大规模重新安置。

- necessitate: 需要
- if: 如果
- temperatures: 温度
- increase by: 增加
- expected: 预期的
- 28 to 48 Celsius: 2.8 到 4.8 摄氏度

- resulting changes: 由此产生的变化

- agriculture: 农业
- disaster risk: 灾害风险
- sea level: 海平面
- massive resettlement: 大规模重新安置

- from: 从
- small domestic fires: 小型家庭火
- used for: 用于

- cooking food: 烹饪食物
- all the way to: 一直到
- modern central heating systems: 现代中央供暖系统
- large power plants: 大型发电厂
- gradual expansion: 逐渐扩展
- fire-making: 制火
- more complex forms: 更复杂的形式
- social organization: 社会组织
