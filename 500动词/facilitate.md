1. To facilitate understanding of cultural movements, illustrations of artistic works discussed in the text are placed near the discussions. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 为了促进对文化运动的理解，文本中讨论的艺术作品的插图被放置在讨论附近。

- facilitate: 促进
- understanding: 理解
- cultural movements: 文化运动
- illustrations: 插图
- artistic works: 艺术作品
- discussed in the text: 文本中讨论的
- placed near: 放置在...附近
- discussions: 讨论

2. Although both empires-built roads chiefly for military purposes, these arteries also came to be used to facilitate trade. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 尽管两个帝国建造道路主要是为了军事目的，但这些动脉也开始被用来促进贸易。

- facilitate: 促进
- although: 尽管
- both empires: 两个帝国
- built roads: 建造道路
- chiefly for: 主要是为了
- military purposes: 军事目的
- arteries: 动脉
- came to be used: 开始被使用
- trade: 贸易

3. The increase in agricultural productivity combined with improvements in transportation facilitated the shipment of food supplies from areas of surplus to regions with poor harvests. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 农业生产力的提高与交通的改善相结合，促进了从剩余地区向收成不佳地区运送食品供应。

- facilitated: 促进
- increase: 提高
- agricultural productivity: 农业生产力
- combined with: 与...相结合
- improvements: 改善
- transportation: 交通
- shipment: 运送
- food supplies: 食品供应
- areas of surplus: 剩余地区
- regions: 地区
- poor harvests: 收成不佳

4. Saint Stephen, king of Hungary from 997 to 1038, facilitated the acceptance of Christianity by his people. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 圣斯蒂芬，997至1038年的匈牙利国王，促进了他的人民对基督教的接受。

- facilitated: 促进
- Saint Stephen: 圣斯蒂芬
- king of Hungary: 匈牙利国王
- from 997 to 1038: 997至1038年
- acceptance: 接受
- Christianity: 基督教
- by his people: 由他的人民
