1. The boundaries of each nome were now settled precisely, and the obligations of the nomes to the state were clearly delineated. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 每个诺姆的边界现在都精确地确定了，诺姆对国家的义务也被清楚地界定了。

- delineated: 被界定
- boundaries: 边界
- each nome: 每个诺姆
- settled precisely: 精确地确定
- obligations: 义务
- nomes: 诺姆
- state: 国家
- clearly: 清楚地

2. Even the white lines that delineate the table, high-backed chair, and wall, for example, and suggest recession and thus space, ironically reinforce the two-dimensionality of the image, for they are not painted lines. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 例如，即使是界定桌子、高背椅和墙壁的白线，并暗示后退和空间，讽刺地强化了图像的二维性，因为它们不是画的线。

- delineate: 界定
- even: 即使
- white lines: 白线
- table: 桌子
- high-backed chair: 高背椅
- wall: 墙壁
- for example: 例如
- suggest: 暗示
- recession: 后退
- space: 空间
- ironically: 讽刺地
- reinforce: 强化
- two-dimensionality: 二维性
- image: 图像
- painted lines: 画的线

3. However, with the newly developed red-figured style, artists might delineate physical details on the buff-colored surface, thereby making the human form appear more lifelike. (Fiero, 2011)

> 然而，随着新开发的红绘风格，艺术家可能在浅黄色表面上界定身体细节，从而使人体形式显得更加逼真。

- delineate: 界定
- however: 然而
- newly developed: 新开发的
- red-figured style: 红绘风格
- artists: 艺术家
- might: 可能
- physical details: 身体细节
- buff-colored surface: 浅黄色表面
- thereby: 从而
- making: 使
- human form: 人体形式
- appear more lifelike: 显得更加逼真

4. Each apple has a powerful physical presence as it is built up out of slablike brushstrokes, its form also carefully delineated with a distinct line.

> 每个苹果都有强大的物理存在感，因为它是由板状笔触构建的，其形式也用清晰的线条仔细界定。

- delineated: 界定
- each apple: 每个苹果
- powerful physical presence: 强大的物理存在感
- built up out of: 由...构建
- slablike brushstrokes: 板状笔触
- form: 形式
- carefully: 仔细地
- distinct line: 清晰的线条
