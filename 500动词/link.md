1. Aeneas, the son of Anchises of Troy, survives the destruction of Troy and eventually settles in Latium; hence Roman civilization is linked to Greek history. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 埃涅阿斯，特洛伊安基塞斯的儿子，在特洛伊的毁灭中幸存下来，最终在拉丁姆定居；因此，罗马文明与希腊历史联系在一起。

- Latium: 拉丁姆

- Roman civilization: 罗马文明
- Greek history: 希腊历史

3. One area of research where there is substantial agreement as to whether a sex difference exists is aggression. Even aggression, however, may be partly due to biological sex and partly due to gender role, that is, our encouragement of aggression among males and discouragement of aggression among females. Features of the male gender role have been linked to aggression. (Helgeson, 2011)

> 一个研究领域，关于是否存在性别差异有实质性一致的是攻击性。然而，即使攻击性也可能部分归因于生物性别，部分归因于性别角色，也就是说，我们鼓励男性中的攻击性，不鼓励女性中的攻击性。男性性别角色的特征与攻击性联系在一起。

- linked to: 与...联系在一起
- area of research: 研究领域
- substantial agreement: 实质性一致

- aggression: 攻击性

- biological sex: 生物性别
- gender role: 性别角色
- encouragement: 鼓励
- males: 男性
- discouragement: 不鼓励
- females: 女性
- features: 特征
- male gender role: 男性性别角色
