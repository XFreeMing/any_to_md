1. Although Cohen's typology is a simplification (which he readily admits) and may therefore be criticized for being imprecise for the analysis he undertakes, the study is valuable because it shows how the meaning of diaspora is not static, but relates to the ongoing processes of maintaining collective identities and preserving ethnic cultures in the context of a rapid period of globalization. (Giddens, 2009)

> 虽然科恩的类型学是一种简化（他欣然承认），因此可能因对他进行的分析不精确而受到批评，但这项研究是有价值的，因为它显示了流散的含义不是静态的，而是与在快速全球化时期维持集体身份和保存民族文化的持续过程相关。

- although: 虽然
- Cohen's typology: 科恩的类型学
- simplification: 简化

- criticized for: 因...而受到批评
- imprecise: 不精确的
- analysis: 分析
- study: 研究
- valuable: 有价值的
- shows how: 显示
- meaning of diaspora: 流散的含义
- not static: 不是静态的
- relates to: 与...相关
- ongoing processes: 持续过程
- maintaining collective identities: 维持集体身份
- preserving ethnic cultures: 保存民族文化
- context: 背景
- rapid period of globalization: 快速全球化时期

- social psychologists: 社会心理学家
