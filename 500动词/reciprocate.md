1. In Japan, they conclude, "there is little pressure to reciprocate the smile of the confederate because there is no relationship with the confederate and no obligation to respond." (Myers, 2012)

> 在日本，他们得出结论，"几乎没有压力来回报同伙的微笑，因为与同伙没有关系，也没有回应的义务。"

- reciprocate: 回报
- Japan: 日本
- conclude: 得出结论
- little pressure: 几乎没有压力
- smile: 微笑
- confederate: 同伙
- because: 因为
- no relationship: 没有关系
- no obligation: 没有义务
- respond: 回应

2. Each person we encounter has some expertise that exceeds our own and thus has something to teach us. As we connect, we can hope to learn from this person and to reciprocate by sharing our knowledge. (Myers, 2012)

> 我们遇到的每个人都有一些超过我们自己的专业知识，因此有东西可以教我们。当我们连接时，我们可以希望从这个人身上学习，并通过分享我们的知识来回报。

- reciprocate: 回报
- each person: 每个人
- encounter: 遇到
- expertise: 专业知识
- exceeds: 超过
- our own: 我们自己的
- thus: 因此
- something to teach us: 有东西可以教我们
- connect: 连接
- hope to learn: 希望学习
- sharing: 分享
- knowledge: 知识

3. Asians, for whom social ties and the reciprocity norm are stronger than for North Americans, are therefore more likely to refuse a gift from a casual acquaintance to avoid the felt need to reciprocate. (Myers, 2012)

> 对亚洲人来说，社会联系和互惠规范比北美人更强，因此更可能拒绝来自偶然熟人的礼物，以避免感到需要回报。

- reciprocate: 回报
- Asians: 亚洲人
- social ties: 社会联系
- reciprocity norm: 互惠规范
- stronger than: 比...更强
- North Americans: 北美人
- therefore: 因此
- more likely to: 更可能
- refuse: 拒绝
- gift: 礼物
- casual acquaintance: 偶然熟人
- avoid: 避免
- felt need: 感到需要

4. The peasants reciprocated this contempt for their so-called social superiors. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 农民回报了这种对他们所谓的社会上级的蔑视。

- reciprocated: 回报
- peasants: 农民
- contempt: 蔑视
- so-called: 所谓的
- social superiors: 社会上级
