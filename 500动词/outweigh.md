- decrease: 下降

2. What would a utilitarian theory of political obligation look like? According to Jeremy Bentham, we should obey our rulers as long as the benefits of doing so outweigh the costs. This, then, sounds like the theory that I should obey the law if, but only if, my obedience will lead to the greater happiness of society than my disobedience. (Wolff, 2006)

> 功利主义的政治义务理论会是什么样子？根据杰里米·边沁的说法，只要这样做的收益超过成本，我们就应该服从我们的统治者。那么，这听起来像是这样的理论：我应该遵守法律，但只有当我的服从将导致比我的不服从更大的社会幸福时。

- outweigh: 超过
- utilitarian theory: 功利主义理论
- political obligation: 政治义务
- according to: 根据
- Jeremy Bentham: 杰里米·边沁
- should obey: 应该服从
- rulers: 统治者
- as long as: 只要
- benefits: 收益
- costs: 成本
- sounds like: 听起来像
- theory: 理论
- obey the law: 遵守法律
- only if: 只有当
- obedience: 服从
- lead to: 导致
- greater happiness: 更大的幸福
- society: 社会
- disobedience: 不服从

3. Huge agricultural subsidies by Western countries to their small farm populations far outweigh the aid given to developing countries. The rich countries have repeatedly pledged to reduce the size of their farm supports. So far the amount of such subsidies has changed little in 20 years, while the amount of aid has declined. (Giddens, 2009)

> 西方国家对其小农场人口的巨大农业补贴远远超过给予发展中国家的援助。富裕国家一再承诺减少其农业支持的规模。到目前为止，这种补贴的数额在20年中变化不大，而援助的数额已经下降。

- huge agricultural subsidies: 巨大的农业补贴

- far: 远远

- size: 规模

- amount: 数额
- such subsidies: 这种补贴

- declined: 下降
