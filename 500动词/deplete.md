1. Yet if the consumption of renewable resources gets out of balance or is too extreme, there is a danger that they will be depleted altogether. (Giddens, 2009)

> 然而，如果可再生资源的消费失去平衡或过于极端，它们将完全耗尽的危险。

- depleted: 耗尽
- yet: 然而
- if: 如果
- consumption: 消费
- renewable resources: 可再生资源
- gets out of balance: 失去平衡
- too extreme: 过于极端
- danger: 危险
- altogether: 完全

2. We deplete our natural resources because the immediate personal benefits of, for instance, taking a long, hot shower outweigh the seemingly inconsequential costs. (Myers, 2012)

> 我们耗尽我们的自然资源，因为例如洗一个长时间热水澡的即时个人利益超过了看似无关紧要的成本。

- deplete: 耗尽
- we: 我们
- natural resources: 自然资源
- because: 因为
- immediate personal benefits: 即时个人利益
- for instance: 例如
- taking a long, hot shower: 洗一个长时间热水澡
- outweigh: 超过
- seemingly inconsequential costs: 看似无关紧要的成本

3. Although the self's energy can be temporarily depleted, our self-concepts do influence our behavior (Graziano & others, 1997). Given challenging tasks, people who imagine themselves as hardworking and successful outperform those who imagine themselves as failures (Ruvolo & Markus, 1992). Envision our positive possibilities and you become more likely to plan and enact a successful strategy. (Myers, 2012)

> 虽然自我的能量可以暂时耗尽，但我们的自我概念确实影响我们的行为（Graziano等人，1997）。面对具有挑战性的任务，那些想象自己勤奋和成功的人比那些想象自己是失败者的人表现更好（Ruvolo & Markus, 1992）。设想我们的积极可能性，你更可能计划并制定成功的策略。

- depleted: 耗尽
- although: 虽然
- self's energy: 自我的能量
- temporarily: 暂时
- self-concepts: 自我概念
- influence: 影响
- behavior: 行为
- given: 面对
- challenging tasks: 具有挑战性的任务
- imagine themselves as: 想象自己是
- hardworking and successful: 勤奋和成功的
- outperform: 表现更好
- failures: 失败者
- envision: 设想
- positive possibilities: 积极可能性
- more likely to: 更可能
- plan: 计划
- enact: 制定
- successful strategy: 成功的策略

- both campaigns: 两次战役
- German manpower: 德国的人力
- while: 而
- Americans: 美国人
- arriving in great numbers: 大量到达
- strengthen: 加强
- Allied lines: 盟军战线
