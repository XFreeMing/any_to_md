1. Zola maintained that the artist must analyze and dissect life as a biologist would a living organism. (Spielvogel, 2010)

> 左拉坚持认为，艺术家必须像生物学家解剖活体一样分析和解剖生活。

- analyze: 分析
- Zola: 左拉
- maintained that: 坚持认为
- artist: 艺术家
- must: 必须
- dissect: 解剖
- life: 生活
- biologist: 生物学家
- living organism: 活体

2. Some artists, such as Picasso and Georges Braque, in a sense emulated scientists, treating their studios like laboratories in which to analyze the very language of painting and where  each creative breakthrough served as a steppingstone to the next as they sought to develop a new model of visual perception. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 一些艺术家，如毕加索和乔治·布拉克，在某种意义上模仿科学家，将他们的工作室当作实验室，在其中分析绘画的语言，每个创造性突破都作为下一个的垫脚石，因为他们寻求开发一种新的视觉感知模式。

- analyze: 分析
- some artists: 一些艺术家
- such as: 如
- Picasso: 毕加索
- Georges Braque: 乔治·布拉克
- in a sense: 在某种意义上
- emulated: 模仿
- scientists: 科学家
- treating: 将...当作
- studios: 工作室
- laboratories: 实验室
- very language of painting: 绘画的语言
- each creative breakthrough: 每个创造性突破
- served as: 作为
- steppingstone: 垫脚石
- next: 下一个
- sought to develop: 寻求开发
- new model: 新模式
- visual perception: 视觉感知

3. Art was also something that could not be bought and sold, something so temporary that it could be seen only for a brief time, making it difficult for scholars and critics to study, analyze,  and write about it. (Davies et al., 2015)

> 艺术也是不能买卖的东西，如此短暂以至于只能在短时间内看到，这使得学者和评论家很难研究、分析和写作它。

- analyze: 分析
- art: 艺术
- something: 东西
- could not be bought and sold: 不能买卖
- temporary: 短暂的
- seen only for a brief time: 只能在短时间内看到
- making it difficult: 使得...困难
- scholars: 学者
- critics: 评论家
- study: 研究
- write about it: 写作它

4. Persuasion is also enhanced by a distraction that inhibits counterarguing. Political ads often use this technique. The words promote the candidate, and the visual images keep us occupied so we don't analyze the words. (Myers, 2012)

> 说服也通过抑制反驳的分心而得到增强。政治广告经常使用这种技术。文字宣传候选人，视觉图像让我们忙碌，所以我们不分析文字。

- analyze: 分析
- persuasion: 说服
- enhanced by: 通过...得到增强
- distraction: 分心
- inhibits: 抑制
- counterarguing: 反驳
- political ads: 政治广告
- often use: 经常使用
- technique: 技术
- words: 文字
- promote: 宣传
- candidate: 候选人
- visual images: 视觉图像
- keep us occupied: 让我们忙碌
- so: 所以
